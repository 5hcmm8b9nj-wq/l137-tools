TITLE: Walking in Divine Power
SCRIPTURE: Romans 8:19
DATE: 2026-08-11

WALKING IN DIVINE POWER.

For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God.
Romans 8:19.

With power, you become an enviable personality and will not be ashamed to be called the child of God. You must desire to walk in power. The first thing Jesus restored was power. Power is one of the proofs that Jesus Christ is real; your prosperity is tied to it. The level you will reach in life is based on the power at work in you.

To fulfill destiny, you need to be empowered and you need divine power to actualize divine plan. For every open door, there is an adversary. God told Moses, "tell Pharaoh let my people go" but Pharaoh was skeptical until God showed His power.

Forces will challenge you but be equal to the task. That God said you will be a great man is not automatic; you need power to overcome challenges of life. The enemy will always stand in the way of divine plan; that is why you must grow in divine power.

I declare in the Name of Jesus Christ, that every "rod of the wicked" opposing your inheritance and breakthrough is destroyed now by the power of God. Amen.
