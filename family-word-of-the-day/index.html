Today's Word

Benefits of Thanksgiving and Praise

"But he giveth more grace. Wherefore he saith, God resisteth the proud, but giveth grace unto the humble."

— James 4:6 (KJV)
Read the full prophetic word from Prophet Prince

Thanksgiving opens the door of abundant grace (2 Corinthians 4:15). When you give God glory, He gives you grace; God engraces the thanksgiver. Thanksgiving ushers you to grace, and grace is the secret of ease. Thanksgiving is a sign of humility.

Thanksgiving establishes us in the will and plan of God for our lives (1 Thessalonians 5:18). Inside everything, look for something to thank God for. When you thank God, you mess up the devil, and God will usher you into His will for your life.

When you thank God permanently, He will marry your life to His will. The doorway to the will of God is thanksgiving.

Father, I come to You with a heart full of joy. Thank You for Your gift of life, endless love, and daily grace. You are my strength, my shield, and my provider. I praise Your Holy Name for all the great things You have done in my life. Amen.

God is not impressed by loud people; He gives grace to the humble, and a thankful heart is a humble heart. When you thank God on purpose, heaven backs you and the enemy's noise gets confused.
