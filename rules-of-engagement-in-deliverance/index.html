Rules of Engagement in Deliverance

A Scripture-first study on how the believer fights, and how the believer wins

Every rule in this study is drawn from Scripture and quoted in full from the King James Version — no paraphrase, no snippets, no added doctrine. Each lesson teaches the same truth several different ways so that visual learners, listeners, new believers, children, teenagers, older adults, and anyone who struggles with long passages can all follow it.

Before the rules

The order of engagement

Eight steps, in this order, every time. Never skip a step and never reorder them.

1
Praise and Worship
You do not begin a battle by talking about the battle. You begin by magnifying the One who fights it.
2
Thanksgiving
Thanksgiving settles the heart and reminds you of every victory God has already given.
3
Confession of Sins
Unconfessed sin is legal ground. Confession removes it before a single word is spoken to the enemy.
4
Forgive Others
Unforgiveness is the one door the Lord Himself tells us to shut before we pray.
5
Invite the Holy Spirit
Deliverance is not a technique. It is the Spirit of God working through a yielded believer.
6
Teaching
People must understand what is happening to them. Understanding is part of the deliverance.
7
Deliverance Prayers and Declarations
Now you speak — with authority, in the name of Jesus, against what the Word has exposed.
8
Sealing
Cover everything that has been cleansed with the blood of Jesus and fill the house.
The study

The eleven rules

Know Your Enemy and Know the Battlefield
Never fight a spiritual war with human weapons.
Rule 1
Fight From Victory, Not For Victory
You engage from a seated position — the war was won at the cross.
Rule 2
No Borrowed Authority
You cannot use the name of Jesus without belonging to Jesus.
Rule 3
Clean Hands — Close the Door Before You Command
Deal with legal ground first. Unconfessed sin and unforgiveness are open doors.
Rule 4
Never Step Onto the Field Undressed
The whole armour — not a favourite piece of it.
Rule 5
The Weapons That Actually Work
The blood, the Word, the name, and praise — in that confidence.
Rule 6
Speak To It — Authority, Not Volume
Deliverance is commanded, not begged. But the authority is Christ's, never yours.
Rule 7
Discern Before You Engage
Test the spirits. Not everything spiritual is from God, and not everything wrong is a demon.
Rule 8
Some Kinds Require Prayer and Fasting
Not every fight yields at the same level. Some ground is taken on your knees over time.
Rule 9
Rules of Restraint — Never Rail, Never Fear, Never Freelance
Even Michael did not bring a railing accusation. Stay under authority and in order.
Rule 10
Hold the Ground — Fill the House
Deliverance is not the end. An empty house invites a worse return.

Salvation and rededication

No one engages in this fight on borrowed authority. If you have never given your life to Jesus Christ, or you want to come back to Him, pray this aloud now.

Pray aloud

Lord Jesus, I come to You today. I confess that I am a sinner and I ask You to forgive me.

I believe that You died for me and that God raised You from the dead.

I confess You now as my Lord and my Saviour. Come into my heart and make me new.

Thank You for saving me. From today I belong to You. Amen.
