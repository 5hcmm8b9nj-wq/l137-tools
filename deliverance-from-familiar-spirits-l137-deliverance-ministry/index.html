Deliverance From Familiar Spirits

Identify and Destroy the Familiar Spirit

Main scripture · Deuteronomy 18:10-12 · KJV

“There shall not be found among you any one that maketh his son or his daughter to pass through the fire, or that useth divination, or an observer of times, or an enchanter, or a witch. Or a charmer, or a consulter with familiar spirits, or a wizard, or a necromancer. For all that do these things are an abomination unto the LORD: and because of these abominations the LORD thy God doth drive them out from before thee.”

A biblical deliverance teaching by Pastor Kiky Ocansey on identifying and destroying familiar spirits — the spirit guides, monitoring spirits and ancestral spirits that counterfeit the Holy Spirit, gather information, and misdirect a believer's steps. The service moves from thanksgiving and repentance into full teaching, then into deliverance prayers, the blood of Jesus, breaking of soul ties and covenants, and the sealing of every deliverance.

What’s in this teaching

Ten teaching modules

Every module carries the scripture in full, a visual explanation, four reading levels, a key point, an activity, flashcards, a knowledge check, a prayer, a declaration and a recall question.

Module 01

What a Familiar Spirit Is

A familiar spirit is a demonic spirit guide — a counterfeit of the Holy Spirit — sent by the enemy to guide your steps into wrong places, wrong people and wrong decisions.

Open module 1 Module 02

What God Says About Familiar Spirits

God has forbidden consultation with familiar spirits and declared them an abomination. He is not neutral about them — He hates them, and He does not want His people troubled by them.

Open module 2 Module 03

How Familiar Spirits Operate

Familiar spirits work by information. They monitor, gather and file — then they use what they collected to manipulate, misdirect and destroy. They are the power behind mediums and false prophets.

Open module 3 Module 04

Doors of Entry

Familiar spirits do not arrive without a door. Inheritance, mediums, ungodly material, wrong hands laid on you, trauma, wrong friendships and careless announcements are the doors named in this teaching.

Open module 4 Module 05

The Damage They Cause

Familiar spirits cause delay, poverty patterns, destroyed marriages, wrong choices, strange sicknesses, mental torment and premature death. Naming the damage is how you begin to fight it.

Open module 5 Module 06

Why Women Are Especially Targeted

Satan fights the woman because of what the woman carries. Revelation 12 shows the dragon pursuing the woman who carried the Saviour — and the same hatred continues.

Open module 6 Module 07

The Holy Spirit — The Only Spirit Guide You Need

God has already given His people a Guide. The Holy Spirit teaches, reminds, comforts, leads, intercedes and delivers — so no believer needs a familiar spirit.

Open module 7 Module 08

God Reveals In Order To Redeem

A familiar spirit reveals in order for the evil to happen. God reveals so that you can pray and it does not happen. Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and the sentence was reversed.

Open module 8 Module 09

How To Come Out — The Deliverance

Surrender, repent, renounce, break every covenant and soul tie, apply the blood of Jesus, cast the spirit out, divorce every spiritual marriage, and breathe in the Holy Spirit.

Open module 9 Module 10

After Deliverance — Doors Are Being Shut

Every visitation of the Lord shuts a door the enemy opened. Do not quit because you cannot see the one you were focused on — keep going, and let salvation and the word secure what deliverance opened.

Open module 10

Before you begin

Set the atmosphere

This is the order the service followed, and the order to follow at home. Never skip a step and never reorder them.

Step 1

Praise and worship

Emmanuel — God is with me. Yahweh, we lift you high.

Step 2

Thanksgiving

Psalm 121 read aloud as a family; gratitude for life, protection and provision.

Step 3

Confession of sins

1 John 1:8-10 — every sin that blocked testimony, elevation, grace and favour confessed and washed.

Step 4

Forgive others

Every offence released; hearts opened so nothing blocks the deliverance.

Step 5

Invite the Holy Spirit

John 14:26 and Acts 2:38 — the true Spirit Guide invited to take position and possession.

Step 6

Teaching

Identify and destroy the familiar spirit — definition, operation, doors of entry, damage, and the way out.

Step 7

Deliverance prayers and declarations

Renunciation, breaking covenants and soul ties, the blood of Jesus, casting out, seven breathings of the Holy Spirit.

Step 8

Sealing

Every deliverance sealed and covered with the blood of Jesus; doors shut so nothing returns.

About the teacher

Pastor Kiky Ocansey & L137

Pastor Kiky Ocansey teaches and ministers deliverance through L137 Deliverance Ministry International. The ministry gathers on Clubhouse for teaching, corporate prayer, deliverance and the sealing of every deliverance in the blood of Jesus.

L137 stands for Luke 1:37 — For with God nothing shall be impossible. That verse is the ministry’s foundation: no bloodline, no covenant, no altar and no familiar spirit is beyond the reach of the finished work of Jesus Christ.

Every teaching becomes a permanent study resource — an interactive course, a workbook, a prayer manual and a full scripture companion — so members can study it for years, pray it in their homes, and teach it to their families.
