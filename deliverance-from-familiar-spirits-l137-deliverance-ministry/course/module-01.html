What a Familiar Spirit Is

A familiar spirit is a demonic spirit guide — a counterfeit of the Holy Spirit — sent by the enemy to guide your steps into wrong places, wrong people and wrong decisions.

A familiar spirit is a bad spirit that follows a person around and whispers wrong directions. God gives His children the Holy Spirit instead. The Holy Spirit is the only Guide we need.

Think of a familiar spirit as a fake GPS. It knows your name, your family, your habits and your secrets — and it deliberately gives you the wrong route: wrong friends, wrong relationship, wrong church, wrong choices. The Holy Spirit is the real Guide, and He never misdirects you.

Pastor Kiky defines the familiar spirit first by its function and then by its name. Functionally it is a spirit guide — a demonic escort assigned to direct a person's steps. Etymologically, familiar means well known: this spirit has long-term, intimate knowledge of a person and a bloodline. That combination — intimate knowledge plus directive influence — is what makes it so effective at deception.

Scripture consistently pairs familiar spirits with divination, necromancy and mediumship (Deuteronomy 18:9-12; Leviticus 19:31; Leviticus 20:27; Isaiah 8:19; Isaiah 29:4). The Hebrew term rendered familiar spirit (’ôb) is used both of the spirit and of the medium who hosts it, which is why Leviticus 20:27 speaks of a man or woman that hath a familiar spirit. The biblical category is therefore not neutral spiritual sensitivity but an unlawful channel of information — a counterfeit of the prophetic ministry of the Holy Spirit, who alone brings all things to remembrance (John 14:26).

The teaching

A familiar spirit is a supernatural being that is said to assist witches, sorcerers, wizards and others in their practice of magic and divination. They are known as familiars, familiar animals or spirit guides.

They are the spirits that direct you wrongly — to the wrong people, wrong churches, wrong friends, wrong everything in your life.

They know your ancestors. They know your grandparents. They know your great-grandparents. They know your family lineage and everyone in it, the ones who have passed and the ones still alive.

The Latin word behind familiar spirit is familiaris — household servant. He serves the household, and what he serves the household with is destruction and iniquity.

When a person willingly or ignorantly forms a relationship with an evil spirit, he or she has a familiar spirit. Anyone who becomes a channel of communication for an evil spirit has a familiar spirit. Having a familiar spirit is to personally identify with a demon spirit — like a demon guardian.
