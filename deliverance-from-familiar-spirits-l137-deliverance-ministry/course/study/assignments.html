The seven-day weekly assignment

Day One

Thanksgiving and the Keeper of Israel

  • Read Psalm 121 aloud in your home, out loud, as the service did.
  • Write ten things God has done for you that you never thanked Him for.
  • Pray the Thanksgiving section of the Prayer Point Manual.
  • Declare: my going out and my coming in are preserved by the Lord.
Day Two

Repentance and shutting the doors

  • Read 1 John 1:8-10 and confess every known sin by name.
  • Work the door list from Module Four and mark every door that has been open.
  • Renounce each marked door aloud and shut it with the blood of Jesus.
  • Pray the Repentance and Renunciation sections of the Prayer Point Manual.
Day Three

Forgiveness

  • Name before God every person you are still holding something against.
  • Release each one aloud, and hand every case of vengeance over to God.
  • Pray the Forgiveness section of the Prayer Point Manual.
  • Ask the Lord to heal the place the offence wounded.
Day Four

The Holy Spirit — the only Guide you need

  • Read John 14:26 and Acts 2:38 slowly, twice.
  • Invite the Holy Spirit to take position and possession of your day before any decision.
  • Pray in the Spirit, and worship at home for at least fifteen minutes.
  • Memorise 1 John 4:4.
Day Five

Renouncing and breaking covenants

  • Read the Declaration Card sets one and two aloud.
  • Break every soul tie and covenant with familiar spirits by the blood of Jesus.
  • Anoint your ring finger and divorce every spiritual marriage, as the service instructed.
  • Pray the Breaking Covenants and Breaking Altars sections of the Prayer Point Manual.
Day Six

Deliverance and healing

  • Pray the Deliverance and Healing sections of the Prayer Point Manual.
  • Take communion if you have it, remembering the finished work of Christ.
  • Command every deposit out and ask the Holy Spirit to fill every emptied place.
  • Write down every change you have noticed since the service.
Day Seven

Sealing, salvation and rest

  • Confess Romans 10:9 aloud and settle the question of salvation.
  • Seal every deliverance with the blood of Jesus and declare that nothing returns.
  • Read 2 Corinthians 5:17, 1 Peter 2:9 and Romans 8:15 over yourself.
  • Plan the week ahead: what you will read, when you will pray, where you will fellowship.

Prophetic directions

Prayer schedule
  • Pray every day and every night against familiar spirits in your family, your home and over your children — this was given as a continual assignment, not a one-off prayer.
  • Speak the words of the Psalms aloud in your atmosphere; when the Psalms were sung over Saul, the distressing spirit departed.
  • Pray in the Spirit daily, and invite the Holy Spirit to take position and possession before you make decisions.
  • Join the ministry's gatherings: Dominion Hour in the mornings at 7:00 AM EST / 12 noon GMT, the evening deliverance service, and the Monday commanding-the-week prayers.
Communion and the blood
  • Take communion wine as the blood of Jesus; where you have none, the service permitted a red drink or a cup of water.
  • Look directly into the cup as you pray, standing on 1 John 5:7-8 — the Spirit, the water and the blood agree in one.
  • Declare Revelation 12:11 as you drink: they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony.
  • Mark yourself with the blood of Jesus — when they see the blood, they pass over.
  • Take the elements only through Christ's finished work; there is no power in the element itself.
Anointing the ring finger
  • Take anointing oil and anoint your ring finger.
  • Remove every invisible ring placed on your finger, one by one, as you pray.
  • Say aloud: you familiar spirit, I divorce you now in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • Keep saying it until the divorce is settled in your heart; some will feel the release at the finger.
  • This is a prophetic act of severance under Christ's authority, not a ritual with power of its own.
The seven breathings
  • Breathe in the Holy Spirit deeply and hold the breath; do not breathe out until the prayer calls for it.
  • As you breathe out, command every deposit, tomb, habitation, network and communication system of familiar spirits to come out.
  • The service did this seven times, naming something different each round — mind control, leviathan and serpent spirits, foundational spirits from the mother's side and the father's side.
  • On the last round, name for yourself whatever you know is operating in your life, and let it come out.
  • Finish by asking the Holy Spirit to fill every place that has been emptied.
Scriptures to meditate on this week
  • Deuteronomy 18:9-12 — the forbidden list and God's verdict on it.
  • Isaiah 8:19 — should not a people seek unto their God?
  • John 14:26 — the Comforter teaches all things and brings all things to remembrance.
  • 1 John 4:4 — greater is He that is in you.
  • Revelation 12:11 — the blood of the Lamb and the word of their testimony.
  • 2 Corinthians 5:17 — old things are passed away.
  • Psalm 121 — the Lord is your keeper, and He does not slumber nor sleep.
Worship
  • Open every prayer time with praise and worship until the atmosphere is saturated.
  • Sing I Surrender and hand over everything — your sorrows, your tears, your pain, your marriage, your children.
  • Declare Emmanuel — God is with me, He is here before me, who can be against me.
  • Keep worship in the home, not only in the service.
Shutting the doors one by one
  • Understand that deliverance is progressive: if there are a hundred doors, they are shut one by one.
  • Do not measure your deliverance only by the one issue you came for — other doors were shut while you prayed.
  • Keep going; every visitation of the Lord shuts another door the enemy opened, and He gives rest when all are shut.
  • Where you know a door is still open, name it, renounce it, and shut it with the blood of Jesus.
Guard your mouth and your company
  • Be careful how you voice out what you are building; some familiar spirits do not know your plan until they hear you say it.
  • Finish the house and let the finished house announce itself; build the business and let the business announce itself.
  • Be careful of the friends you take, and be careful of the preachers and prophets you draw close to.
  • Guard what you read and what you watch — words are spirit and they are alive.
Weekly assignment
  • Work the seven-day plan: thanksgiving, repentance, forgiveness, the Holy Spirit, renouncing and breaking covenants, deliverance and healing, sealing and salvation.
  • Pray one section of the Prayer Point Manual each day rather than praying at random.
  • Learn the six memory verses and say them aloud daily.
  • Write down every change you notice, and bring your remaining questions back to the next service.
  • Stay locked in to the ministry platform so you do not walk this out alone.
