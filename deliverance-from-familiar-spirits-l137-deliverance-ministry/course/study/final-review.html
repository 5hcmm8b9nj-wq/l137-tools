Summary of the teaching

A familiar spirit is a demonic spirit guide — a counterfeit of the Holy Spirit — with intimate, long-standing knowledge of a person and their bloodline, sent to monitor, misdirect and destroy. God forbids every consultation with such spirits and has already given His people the Holy Spirit as their true Guide. Deliverance comes by surrender, repentance, renunciation, the breaking of every covenant, the blood of Jesus, casting out, filling with the Holy Spirit, and sealing.

A biblical deliverance teaching by Pastor Kiky Ocansey on identifying and destroying familiar spirits — the spirit guides, monitoring spirits and ancestral spirits that counterfeit the Holy Spirit, gather information, and misdirect a believer's steps. The service moves from thanksgiving and repentance into full teaching, then into deliverance prayers, the blood of Jesus, breaking of soul ties and covenants, and the sealing of every deliverance.

Key lessons

Remember these
  • Familiar means well known — the spirit's power is intimate knowledge used to misdirect.
  • God calls consultation with familiar spirits an abomination and forbids every channel of divination.
  • They fight with information: they monitor, gather, file and manipulate; they are the voice behind mediums and false prophets.
  • Contact with the dead is false — the dead know not any thing, and it is appointed unto men once to die.
  • Every familiar spirit came through a door: inheritance, mediums, ungodly material, wrong hands, trauma, wrong company, careless announcing.
  • The damage shows as a repeating bloodline pattern — delay, poverty, broken marriages, wrong choices, sickness, mental torment.
  • God reveals in order to redeem; a familiar spirit reveals so that the evil will happen.
  • Deliverance is an exchange: evict the counterfeit, fill with the Holy Spirit, seal the door, and keep it shut by salvation and a renewed mind.

Real-life application

Apply this week
  • Name every door that has been open in your life and shut it with the blood of Jesus.
  • Renounce every familiar spirit aloud, break every covenant and soul tie, and divorce every spiritual marriage.
  • Invite the Holy Spirit to take position and possession before every decision this week.
  • Test every revelation by its purpose — does it drive you to prayer, or to fear?
  • Finish what you build before you announce it, and review the company you keep.
  • Seal the deliverance, settle salvation, renew the mind, and keep praying — the doors are still being shut.

Discussion questions

  1. What does the word familiar mean, and why does that meaning make these spirits effective?
  2. Deuteronomy 18:10-12 names nine practices. Which of them still appear in modern dress today?
  3. Why does the teaching insist that accurate private knowledge is not evidence that God is speaking?
  4. Which of the doors of entry is most common in your context, and why?
  5. How do you distinguish a repeating bloodline pattern from ordinary difficulty without becoming fearful?
  6. Revelation 12 shows both a pursuit and a provision. How do you keep both in view when you are under attack?
  7. How does the Holy Spirit answer, function by function, every counterfeit named in this teaching?
  8. Why did the service end with breathing in the Holy Spirit, salvation and sealing rather than with casting out?
  9. What would you say to someone who leaves a deliverance service disappointed because the presenting issue has not changed?

Closing prayer

Pray aloud · Sealing

Nothing is left open. Every deliverance is sealed with the blood of Jesus, every emptied place is filled with the Holy Spirit, and every door is shut.

Pray

  • By the blood of Jesus, I seal and shut every door, every portal and every entry that familiar spirits were using to gain access to me, in the name of Jesus.
  • I seal my deliverance with the blood of Jesus — whatever has left me will never return, in the name of Jesus.
  • Holy Spirit, fill every place that has been vacated in me, in the name of Jesus.
  • Let every door the enemy opened in my life be shut one by one, until the Lord gives me my rest, in the name of Jesus.
  • Father, strengthen me with might by Your Spirit in the inner man, and let Christ dwell in my heart by faith, in the name of Jesus.
  • I am a new creature — old things are passed away, and I will not pick them up again, in the name of Jesus.
  • Renew my mind, O Lord, so that the old pattern finds no place in me, in the name of Jesus.
  • Let the peace of God guard my mind, my home and my sleep tonight, in the name of Jesus.
  • Whom the Son sets free is free indeed — I am free and I remain free, in the name of Jesus.

Final declarations

Declare out loud · Set Seven — The Prayer of Salvation

Declare

  • Dear Lord Jesus.
  • Thank you for dying on the cross for my sin.
  • Please forgive me.
  • Come into my life.
  • I receive you as my Lord and Saviour.
  • Now help me to live for you.
  • For the rest of my life.
  • In Jesus' name I pray, Amen.
