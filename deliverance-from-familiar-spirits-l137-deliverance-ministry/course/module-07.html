The Holy Spirit — The Only Spirit Guide You Need

God has already given His people a Guide. The Holy Spirit teaches, reminds, comforts, leads, intercedes and delivers — so no believer needs a familiar spirit.

God does not leave His children without help. He gives us the Holy Spirit. He teaches us, He reminds us of God's word, He comforts us, and He keeps us safe. We do not need any other spirit.

You were never offered a choice between guidance and no guidance. The choice is between a guide who wants you dead and a Guide who died for you. One is a counterfeit; the other is the Comforter the Father sends in Jesus' name.

Pastor Kiky frames the entire teaching as a substitution rather than a subtraction. Deliverance is not merely the eviction of a false guide; it is the installation of the true one. That is why the service invited the Holy Spirit before the teaching, prayed in the Spirit before the warfare, and closed with seven breathings of the Holy Spirit rather than only with casting out.

John 14-16 gives the fullest description of the Spirit's ministry: He abides for ever (14:16), teaches and reminds (14:26), testifies of Christ (15:26), convicts the world (16:8), and guides into all truth while showing things to come (16:13). Every one of those functions has a demonic counterfeit — a false comforter, a false memory, a false witness, false conviction, false guidance, false foreknowledge. This is precisely Pastor Kiky's point that familiar spirits counterfeit the works of the Holy Spirit. The test is never the accuracy of information but the source, the fruit, and agreement with the written word.

The teaching

John 14:26 names the office: the Comforter, the Holy Ghost, whom the Father sends in the name of Jesus, teaches all things and brings all things to remembrance.

Acts 2:38 names the order: repent, be baptised in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and receive the gift of the Holy Ghost. Repentance first; then the indwelling Guide.

The Holy Spirit's work in this service was practical, not abstract: He fights other presences, He comforts homes and children, He orders steps, He opens doors, He heals, He gives utterance, He baptises with fire.

1 John 4:4 supplies the settled confidence — greater is He that is in you than he that is in the world. The believer is not matching strength with the familiar spirit; a greater Person indwells them.

1 Corinthians 2:12 draws the contrast exactly: we have received the Spirit which is of God — not the spirit of the world — that we might know the things freely given to us of God.

Therefore the exchange the whole service calls for is this: renounce the counterfeit guide, and invite the true One to take position and possession.
