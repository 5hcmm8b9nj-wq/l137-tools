Why Women Are Especially Targeted

Satan fights the woman because of what the woman carries. Revelation 12 shows the dragon pursuing the woman who carried the Saviour — and the same hatred continues.

Pastor Kiky Ocansey · L137 Deliverance Ministry International

The devil fought the woman in the Bible because she was carrying Jesus. He still fights women because of what God has put in their hands — children, homes and the future. God protects the woman.

In Revelation 12 the dragon does not chase the throne, he chases the woman. Why? Because of what she carries. If the enemy is targeting you, it is not because you are weak — it is because of what is inside you that he cannot afford to be born.

This module is pastoral rather than polemical. Pastor Kiky treats the intensity of the attack as evidence of assignment, not of inferiority: the woman is pursued because of what she carries and the influence she exercises over the next generation. The application she draws is discernment in friendship and persistence in prayer.

Revelation 12 belongs to a biblical pattern of the seed being attacked: the serpent and the woman's seed (Genesis 3:15), the Hebrew male children (Exodus 1), the children of Bethlehem (Matthew 2). Each assault targets the vessel through which the promise comes. Note also that in Revelation 12 God Himself provides the protection — the earth helps the woman, the wilderness place is prepared for her, and she is nourished there. The believer's confidence rests on God's provision, not on the absence of attack.

The teaching

Revelation 12 records the dragon persecuting the woman, casting out a flood after her, and the earth helping the woman — and then making war with the remnant of her seed. Pastor Kiky asks the diagnostic question: why the woman? Because the woman carried the Saviour.

Satan's agenda against women, as she names it, is to make them prostitute, destitute, useless, possessed, obsessed and oppressed, and to kill them off as quickly as possible.

The woman is the womb of man and is called the weaker vessel; she carries virtue, she influences the cradle, the womb and the home. That influence is exactly why she is targeted.

Because of this, women are warned specifically about friendships. Some presenting as friends are monitoring spirits and evil eyes sent to watch and to stop.

The instruction is not fear but warfare: you either fight, or you perish. Women are urged to pray hard and continually concerning these spirits.
