How Familiar Spirits Operate

Familiar spirits work by information. They monitor, gather and file — then they use what they collected to manipulate, misdirect and destroy. They are the power behind mediums and false prophets.

Pastor Kiky Ocansey · L137 Deliverance Ministry International

Understand it · Choose your level

Simple, Teen, Adult or Deeper

Familiar spirits are like spies. They watch, they listen, they write things down, and then they use what they know to hurt you. When someone claims to talk to a dead relative, they are really talking to a spy spirit that knew that relative.

They run surveillance. They build a file on you — habits, weaknesses, passwords to your behaviour — and then feed that file to whoever is working against you. It is why a false prophet can tell you something private and still be operating by the wrong power. Accurate information is not proof of God.

Pastor Kiky's central insight is that the power of these spirits is informational, not sovereign. They cannot read the mind of God, and she notes that some do not even know your plans until you announce them. What they have is accumulated intelligence and access. Remove the access and the intelligence goes stale — which is why the deliverance later in the service targets networks, communication systems, transportation systems and monitoring devices specifically.

The account in 1 Samuel 28 shows the medium of Endor operating by an ’ôb — a familiar spirit — and Scripture records God's judgement on Saul for enquiring of it (1 Chronicles 10:13). Ecclesiastes 9:5 states that the dead know not anything, and Hebrews 9:27 fixes a single death followed by judgement, which together rule out ongoing communication with the departed. The conclusion is therefore not that mediums are always frauds, but something more serious: the voice is real, and it is demonic.

The teaching

Familiar spirits counterfeit the works of the Holy Spirit and they defile the vessel. They are the key enemy spirits in our battle against deception, because they are experts at counterfeiting situations and the move of the Spirit.

They collect details: personal talents, neighbours, habits, routines, resources, friends, enemies, materials, mental abilities, weaknesses, tendencies, educational abilities — so that they know the person inside out.

They then maneuver and manipulate the victim, transferring the files they have gathered to their agents.

Contact with the dead is false. Hebrews 9:27 settles it: it is appointed unto men once to die, and after that the judgment. So what mediums invoke and speak with are familiar spirits — spirits familiar with the dead person and with your lineage.

Always seeing the dead is evidence that a person is being troubled by familiar spirits. And when you leave a medium, you do not leave alone — you leave with those spirits, and even more of them.

They are the medium spirits behind false prophets. Many prophecies from false prophets are from familiar spirits. They are behind the signs and wonders of false prophets.

They monitor through what they plant: that bird that follows you, that cockroach, that mouse, monitoring beds, creaks in the wall, creaks on the steps, spiders, lizards, insects, bees, flies. They also monitor through people they send into your life, and they follow what you post and announce.
