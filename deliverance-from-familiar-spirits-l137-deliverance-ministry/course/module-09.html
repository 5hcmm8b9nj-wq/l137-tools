How To Come Out — The Deliverance

Surrender, repent, renounce, break every covenant and soul tie, apply the blood of Jesus, cast the spirit out, divorce every spiritual marriage, and breathe in the Holy Spirit.

Give your whole life to Jesus. Say sorry for sin. Tell the bad spirit to go in Jesus' name. Use the blood of Jesus. Breathe in the Holy Spirit and let the bad thing out. Then let Jesus lock the door.

This is the practical part, in order: surrender, repent, renounce, break the contract, apply the blood, uproot the planting, cast it out, divorce the spirit-marriage, breathe in the Holy Spirit, seal the door. Do not skip steps and do not stop halfway.

The order matters. Pastor Kiky never begins with confrontation; she begins with surrender and repentance, because legal ground must be removed before authority is exercised. Renunciation revokes consent, the breaking of covenants and soul ties dissolves agreement, the blood provides the legal covering, casting out enforces the eviction, and the seven breathings fill the vacated space with the Holy Spirit so nothing returns.

The pattern reflects Matthew 12:43-45, where the evicted spirit returns to an empty house and brings seven others. Deliverance that only evacuates and never fills is unfinished, which is why this service ends with inhaling the Holy Spirit, with salvation, and with sealing. Compare also 2 Corinthians 10:4-5 (strongholds and thoughts), Ephesians 4:22-24 (put off, be renewed, put on), and Colossians 2:14-15 (the handwriting of ordinances blotted out — the legal basis for revoking a covenant).

The teaching

1. Surrender totally. Hand over the marriage, the home, the children, the court case, the torment — vengeance belongs to God (Psalm 94:1), and what you hand to Him He resolves.

2. Repent from every known sin. Without forgiveness there is no deliverance. 1 John 1:8-10 was read for this purpose at the start of the service.

3. Renounce the spirit by name. Renounce every familiar spirit that masquerades as family members, friends, coworkers, neighbours and familiar faces.

4. Break every covenant and soul tie. Every covenant between you and any familiar spirit, every soul tie made knowingly and unknowingly, is broken by the power in the blood of Jesus.

5. Apply the blood. 1 John 5:7-8 — the Spirit, the water and the blood agree in one. Revelation 12:11 — they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony. When they see the blood, they pass over.

6. Uproot every planting. Matthew 15:13 — every plant which the heavenly Father has not planted shall be rooted up. Every deposit, mark, remote control, cage and habitation is destroyed and commanded out.

7. Cast out and resist. By air or liquid, through every opening of the body — cough it out, yawn it out, vomit it out. James 4:7 — resist the devil and he will flee from you.

8. Divorce every spiritual marriage. Anoint the ring finger, remove every invisible ring, and divorce the familiar spirit in the name of Jesus.

9. Breathe in the Holy Spirit — seven times. Breathe in the Holy Spirit, hold it, and as you breathe out let every deposit, tomb, habitation, network and communication system of familiar spirits come out.

10. Seal it. Whatever has left is sealed out; the door is closed; the deliverance is covered with the blood of Jesus.

Pray this module through

Pray aloud

Pray

  • Father, in the name of Jesus, I surrender everything — my soul, my mind, my heart, my body, my home, my children, my life.
  • By the blood of Jesus I break every soul tie made with familiar spirits, knowingly and unknowingly.
  • Every plant which my heavenly Father has not planted in my life, be rooted up now in the name of Jesus.
  • Every deposit of familiar spirits in my body, by air or by liquid, through every opening of my body, come out now in the name of Jesus.
  • Holy Spirit, fill every place that has been vacated, and seal my deliverance with the blood of Jesus.
Declare out loud

Declare

  • I renounce every familiar spirit that masquerades as family members, friends, coworkers, neighbours and other familiar faces that I see on a regular basis.
  • I break every soul tie made with familiar spirits, knowingly and unknowingly, by the power in the blood of Jesus.
  • I silence, I destroy and I blind every familiar spirit — you will not see or hear me in the name of Jesus.
  • You demon have no legal rights over my life and destiny through the blood of Jesus.
  • I walk out free with angelic assistance right now, and no one can stop me.
