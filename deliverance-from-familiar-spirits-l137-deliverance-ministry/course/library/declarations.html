Declaration Library

The seven declaration sets of this service, exactly as they were spoken. Declare them out loud.

Pastor Kiky Ocansey · L137 Deliverance Ministry International

These are the corporate declarations of the service, preserved word for word. Declare Speak every line out loud.

Declaration set 1 · Declare out loud

Set One — Renouncing and Drinking the Blood

  • In the name of Jesus.
  • I renounce every familiar spirit that masquerades as family members and the ones that are alive, friends, coworkers, neighbours, and other familiar faces that I see on a regular basis.
  • I renounce every familiar spirit that is in my spiritual house.
  • I declare that whenever these spirits try to approach me in my dream, by the blood of Jesus, I shut them down immediately.
  • And they are destroyed by the blood of Jesus and by the power of the Holy Ghost.
  • Lord, wash me clean with Your blood from the crown of my head to the soles of my feet.
  • And cleanse me from every unclean deposit and evil food that I have eaten from the hands of the devil in my dreams and in my waking life.
  • I decree and I declare that the fire of God, as I drink the blood of Jesus, runs through my veins and every chip of my vessel, and cleanses me of all sicknesses and death that may have been planted inside of me by familiar spirits.
  • Jesus said, every plant that my heavenly Father has not planted will be uprooted by the roots. I stand on Matthew chapter fifteen verse thirteen.
  • Lord, Your word is always relevant and never changes.
  • I break every soul tie made with familiar spirits, knowingly and unknowingly, by the power in the blood of Jesus.
  • I decree and I declare that I cast every monitoring spirit into their dry places, and through the Holy Spirit that lives inside of me, I demand that they do not return.
  • By the power in the blood of Jesus, as I drink the blood of Jesus, I silence and I destroy. I blind every familiar spirit.
  • You will not see or hear me in the name of Jesus.
  • You demon have no legal rights, through the blood of Jesus, over my life and destiny.
  • I pour out by the blood of Jesus the wrath of God upon you.
  • Jesus became a curse for me to set me free.
  • So I declare that I stand on that freedom, and I refuse to allow any demon, any familiar spirit, to take my blessings, my elevation, my grace, my favour away from me.
  • As I drink the blood of Jesus, Father, deliver me from every familiar spirit and my strong enemies.
  • Father, set me free!
  • By the power of the Holy Ghost, as I drink the blood of Jesus, cancel every initiation of familiar spirits against my destiny.
  • Every spiritual marriage with familiar spirits, by the blood of Jesus, be destroyed.
  • I reverse every evil pattern of familiar spirits in my destiny.
  • By the power of the Holy Ghost, every cage of familiar spirits held in my life and my destiny, held in my joy and my laughter, by the blood of Jesus, I command you to be destroyed.
  • Spirits in my blood, in my body, in my eyes, in my ears, in my mouth, in my stomach, in my feet, in my body system — as I drink the blood, I command you to come out.
  • By air or liquid, every opening in my body, in the name of Jesus.
Declaration set 2 · Declare out loud

Set Two — Sealing and Shutting Every Door

  • In the name of Jesus.
  • Father, in the name of Jesus, by the blood of Jesus, I seal and shut every door, every portal, and every entry the familiar spirits are using to gain access to me.
  • By the blood of Jesus, I destroy them right now.
  • I smash all monitoring communication devices, and I destroy them into pieces.
  • By the blood of Jesus, I burn them to ashes.
  • By the fire of God, I dethrone every familiar spirit, and I declare that they receive the fire of God tonight.
  • Father, destroy their deposits inside my body by the blood of Jesus.
  • By the blood, by the fire of God, I destroy into pieces and I burn into ashes every transportation system used by familiar spirits.
  • I plead the blood of Jesus over every altar used by familiar spirits tonight.
  • Deliver me, Father. Separate me from every power of familiar spirits in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • I declare that every remote control and device that they have planted into my life and into my body, by the blood of Jesus that I have taken, let it be destroyed right now.
  • I declare in the mighty name of Jesus, their plans, their attempts, their assignments and their efforts against my destiny are frustrated right now.
  • I speak death to their habitation — their habitation in my body, their habitation in my home, their habitation at my workplace.
  • Let it become desolate right now by the blood of Jesus.
  • I command every familiar spirit hovering around my atmosphere, I command you out — out right now in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • I command every familiar spirit deposit in my body, I command you out right now in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • I will cough you out. I will vomit you out. I will gag you out.
  • Get out by air or liquid, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • Every trap that they prepared for me, fall into themselves right now.
  • Let the wicked fall into their nets right now by the power of the Holy Ghost.
  • I declare that they fight among themselves right now, and they destroy each other right now, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.
Declaration set 3 · Declare out loud

Set Three — Every Chain and Every Padlock

  • Every chain! Every chain! Every chain!
  • Every padlock!
  • Every other restricting device that they have used against my life, I command it to be broken right now in the name of Jesus.
  • I walk out free with angelic assistance right now, and no one can stop me.
  • Just like when Peter was delivered from the prison by an angel, and no one knew how that happened so miraculously.
  • I am coming out. I am coming out.
  • By air or liquid, by every opening in my body, I will cough you out, I will burp you out, I will vomit you out now in the name of Jesus.
Declaration set 4 · Declare out loud

Set Four — Divorcing the Spiritual Marriage

  • You familiar spirit, I divorce you now in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • I divorce you now.
  • As I anoint my hands with this oil, I divorce you. I divorce you.
  • Go now in the name of Jesus.
Declaration set 5 · Declare out loud

Set Five — I Am Free

  • I am free. I am free. I am free.
  • Father, I am free from this spirit in the name of Jesus.
  • I receive it in the name of Jesus.
  • Break — break, break, break in the name of Jesus. Break by the power of the Holy Ghost.
Declaration set 6 · Declare out loud

Set Six — After the Deliverance

  • Father, by the power of the Holy Ghost and in the mighty name of Jesus, everything that I ask of You will be done unto me.
  • Whatever these demons were stopping me from doing, it will come to pass.
  • My faith, my glory, everything in my life, O Lord — every blessing will come in alignment in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • Every open door will come in alignment.
  • Let the blessings of the Lord rest upon me, Father, in the name of Jesus.
  • I call out my peace today. I call out my favour today. I call out my grace today.
  • I will not fail in my life, O Father. You will move me forward in the name of Jesus.
  • My destiny will not be aborted. My life will not be cut short. I will not be destroyed in any way.
  • Blessings will come to me, elevation will come to me, my peace will come to me. I will celebrate in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • I will find my path again. I will find my path again.
Declaration set 7 · Declare out loud

Set Seven — The Prayer of Salvation

  • Dear Lord Jesus.
  • Thank you for dying on the cross for my sin.
  • Please forgive me.
  • Come into my life.
  • I receive you as my Lord and Saviour.
  • Now help me to live for you.
  • For the rest of my life.
  • In Jesus' name I pray, Amen.
