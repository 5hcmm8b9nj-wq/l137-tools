Pray it

Prayer Library

Every prayer point from the service, organised in eleven sections from repentance through to sealing. Pray them out loud.

Pastor Kiky Ocansey · L137 Deliverance Ministry International

Section 01 · Pray aloud

Repentance

Without the forgiveness of the Lord there will be no deliverance, so the service began here — every sin that blocked a testimony, an elevation, a grace or a favour confessed and washed away by the blood.

Pray

  • Father, if we say that we have no sin we deceive ourselves; I confess my sins to You now, and I thank You that You are faithful and just to forgive me and to cleanse me from all unrighteousness, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, forgive me of every sin that has blocked my testimony, my elevation, my grace and my favour, in the name of Jesus.
  • Forgive me, O Lord, of every transgression, and remove from me every iniquity, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, cleanse me with Your blood from my head to my toe, in the name of Jesus.
  • Father, forgive every sin that gave the enemy power to destroy and to keep troubling my life, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, renew a right spirit within me and renew a right presence within me, in the name of Jesus.
  • Father, help me to turn away from every wicked way tonight, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every addiction of sin that has become a torment in my life, be destroyed by the blood of Jesus.
  • Lord, do not let the consequences of sin overwhelm my life and my destiny, in the name of Jesus.
  • Sins of my fathers and sins of my mothers, be washed away by the blood of Jesus.
  • Father, give me a new page, give me a new life, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, set my heart free from all fear and all doubt, in the name of Jesus.
Section 02 · Pray aloud

Forgiveness

Hearts are opened and every offence released, so that nothing held against another person stands in the way of the deliverance about to be received.

Pray

  • Father, I release from my heart every person who has offended me, wounded me or betrayed me, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, I forgive those who opened doors of destruction over my life through what they did to me, in the name of Jesus.
  • Father, take from me every root of bitterness, resentment and unforgiveness, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, heal my heart where the offence is still sore, in the name of Jesus.
  • Father, forgive me where I have held a grudge and called it strength, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, I refuse to carry vengeance — vengeance belongs to God, and I hand every case over to You, in the name of Jesus.
  • Father, deliver me from every evil tongue and from every desire to repay evil for evil, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, let no unforgiveness in my heart block my deliverance tonight, in the name of Jesus.
  • Father, give me a heart that forgives quickly and a mouth that blesses, in the name of Jesus.
Section 03 · Pray aloud

Renunciation

Renunciation revokes consent. Pastor Kiky led the congregation to renounce the familiar spirit by name and by every disguise it wears.

Pray

  • In the name of Jesus, I renounce every familiar spirit that masquerades as family members, friends, coworkers, neighbours and other familiar faces that I see on a regular basis.
  • I renounce every familiar spirit that is in my spiritual house, in the name of Jesus.
  • I renounce every evil spirit that came to me through mediums, diviners, witch doctors and false prophets, in the name of Jesus.
  • I renounce every spirit guide, every monitoring spirit and every ancestral spirit assigned to direct my steps, in the name of Jesus.
  • I renounce every familiar spirit burial and every familiar spirit connection, in the name of Jesus.
  • I renounce every occult book, every ungodly literature and every defiled material I ever took into my mind, in the name of Jesus.
  • I renounce every deposit given to me through food, through a word, or through hands laid on me, in the name of Jesus.
  • I renounce every agreement I made knowingly or ignorantly with a familiar spirit, in the name of Jesus.
  • Deliver my soul, Lord, from everything that makes me familiar to these spirits, in the name of Jesus.
  • I renounce, I denounce and I will have no dealing with these spirits from this point on, in the name of Jesus.
Section 04 · Pray aloud

Breaking Covenants

Every covenant, soul tie, spiritual marriage and initiation is dissolved by the power in the blood of Jesus.

Pray

  • Every covenant between me and any familiar spirit, break by the power of the Holy Ghost, in the name of Jesus.
  • I break every soul tie made with familiar spirits, knowingly and unknowingly, by the power in the blood of Jesus.
  • Every strange covenant binding me to a familiar spirit — wherever I went, wherever my mother went, wherever my father went — be broken, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every spiritual marriage with a familiar spirit, by the blood of Jesus, be destroyed.
  • Cancel every initiation of familiar spirits against my destiny, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every invisible ring placed on my finger, I remove you and I divorce that spirit, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every ancestral curse over my life and over my destiny, be destroyed, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every curse that found its way into my financial life, be destroyed, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every spell and every enchantment spoken against my life by familiar spirits, be destroyed, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every identification mark placed on me by a familiar spirit, I wipe it off with the blood of Jesus.
  • You demon have no legal rights over my life and my destiny, through the blood of Jesus.
Section 05 · Pray aloud

Breaking Altars

The altars, thrones, networks and habitations that sustain a familiar spirit are pulled down so that the spirit has nowhere to sit and nothing to speak from.

Pray

  • Every altar used by familiar spirits against my life, I plead the blood of Jesus over you tonight, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every seat of familiar spirits in my life, receive the fire of the Lord, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every throne of familiar spirits, be dismantled right now, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every stronghold of familiar spirits, be pulled down by the blood of Jesus.
  • Let the habitation of familiar spirits in my body, in my home and at my workplace become desolate, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every network of familiar spirits, be dismantled by the blood of Jesus.
  • Every communication system of familiar spirits, be destroyed right now, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every transportation system of familiar spirits, I burn you to ashes by the fire of God, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every monitoring system, every monitoring bed and every monitoring device planted against me, be destroyed, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every satanic sacrifice assigned to steal my laughter, be destroyed by the blood of Jesus.
  • Every altar of sorrow raised against my marriage, my finances and my business, be destroyed, in the name of Jesus.
  • Let the weapons of familiar spirits jam against them and turn back on their senders, in the name of Jesus.
Section 06 · Pray aloud

Deliverance

The eviction itself: by the finger of God, by the blood of Jesus and by the fire of the Holy Ghost, every familiar spirit is commanded out.

Pray

  • By the finger of God, I cast out every evil spirit from my soul and from my home, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every plant which my heavenly Father has not planted in my life, be rooted up, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every deposit of familiar spirits in my blood, my body, my eyes, my ears, my mouth, my stomach and my feet, come out by air or by liquid, through every opening of my body, in the name of Jesus.
  • I silence, I destroy and I blind every familiar spirit — you will not see or hear me, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every cage of familiar spirits holding my life, my destiny, my joy and my laughter, be destroyed by the blood of Jesus.
  • Every remote control and device planted into my life and into my body, be destroyed by the blood of Jesus.
  • Every connection controlling my thoughts, be disconnected by the blood of Jesus.
  • Every familiar spirit from my mother's side and from my father's side, be disconnected from my life and my destiny, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every chain, every padlock and every restricting device used against my life, be broken right now, in the name of Jesus.
  • I resist the devil, and he must flee from me, in the name of Jesus.
  • Holy Spirit, lay Your hand upon me and set me free, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every familiar spirit hovering around my atmosphere, I command you out right now, in the name of Jesus.
Section 07 · Pray aloud

Healing

What the spirit planted in the body is uprooted, and the Lord who went about healing all that were oppressed of the devil is asked to restore health of mind and body.

Pray

  • Let the blood of Jesus run through my veins and cleanse me of every sickness and death planted inside me by familiar spirits, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every strange sickness and every inherited disease propagated by familiar spirits, be destroyed, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every affliction, every plague and every infirmity, loose me now, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every mental torment caused by familiar spirits, be lifted, and let me receive a sound mind, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, heal my heart where the wound of what was done to me is still open, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every scale placed on my eyes, be removed — I will see properly, I will choose properly, I will think properly, in the name of Jesus.
  • Father, resurrect my strength and heal every area of my life, in the name of Jesus.
  • Let the Holy Spirit work in my body, my mind and my ears, and complete what He has begun, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every sleeplessness and every night torment, be destroyed, and let me sleep in peace, in the name of Jesus.
Section 08 · Pray aloud

Restoration

After the eviction comes the return of what was stolen — peace, favour, grace, finances, elevation, direction and rest.

Pray

  • Father, restore every virtue that has come out of me, in the name of Jesus.
  • I call out my peace today, I call out my favour today, I call out my grace today, in the name of Jesus.
  • Let my blessings, my elevation, my grace and my favour that were withheld be released to me now, in the name of Jesus.
  • I withdraw my life and my blessings from every strong room of familiar spirits, in the name of Jesus.
  • Let my finances be released, and let my toiling be over, in the name of Jesus.
  • Let every open door come into alignment, and let advancement come to my workplace, my marriage and my business, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, loose upon me the spirit of grace, miracles, victory, excellence, wisdom and supernatural breakthrough, in the name of Jesus.
  • I will not be lost, I will not get stuck, I will not be stranded — I will find my path again, in the name of Jesus.
  • The Lord will order my steps and I will not run another person's race, in the name of Jesus.
  • My destiny will not be aborted and my life will not be cut short, in the name of Jesus.
Section 09 · Pray aloud

Thanksgiving

The service opened with gratitude from Psalm 121 and closed with thanksgiving for the deliverance received; the grateful heart keeps the door shut.

Pray

  • Father, I thank You that I did not wake because an alarm rang — I woke because You breathed life into me, in the name of Jesus.
  • I thank You, Lord, that my going out and my coming in are in Your hands, in the name of Jesus.
  • I thank You that You do not slumber nor sleep, and that You watch over me at all times, in the name of Jesus.
  • I thank You, Father, for the seen and unseen battles You have fought for me, in the name of Jesus.
  • I thank You for the forgiveness of my sins and for the blood that washed me, in the name of Jesus.
  • I thank You for revelation, because my people perish for lack of knowledge, in the name of Jesus.
  • I thank You, Father, for my deliverance — whatever left me tonight, I thank You that it is gone, in the name of Jesus.
  • I thank You for the Holy Ghost, for Your angels, for strength and for ability, in the name of Jesus.
  • Not unto us, O Lord, but unto Your name be all glory, honour, power, dominion and majesty for evermore, in the name of Jesus.
Section 10 · Pray aloud

Protection

Barricade the life with the blood of Jesus against any future attack, and ask for the covering that God Himself provided for the woman in Revelation 12.

Pray

  • I barricade my life with the blood of Jesus against every future attack of the familiar spirit, in the name of Jesus.
  • Father, mark me and my household as a passover to every entity poised to destroy and to confuse, in the name of Jesus.
  • Let angels of deliverance encamp around my home, my car and my workplace, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every monitoring eye and every evil eye watching my life through friendship, be blinded, in the name of Jesus.
  • Let no false prophet see anything concerning me, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every trap prepared for me, let the wicked fall into their own nets, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, hide me in the path which no fowl knoweth and which the vulture's eye hath not seen, in the name of Jesus.
  • Father, prepare my place and nourish me there, as You did for the woman in Revelation 12, in the name of Jesus.
  • Cover my children, my marriage, my finances and my ministry with the blood of Jesus.
  • The Lord shall preserve me from all evil; He shall preserve my soul, my going out and my coming in, in the name of Jesus.
Section 11 · Pray aloud

Sealing

Nothing is left open. Every deliverance is sealed with the blood of Jesus, every emptied place is filled with the Holy Spirit, and every door is shut.

Pray

  • By the blood of Jesus, I seal and shut every door, every portal and every entry that familiar spirits were using to gain access to me, in the name of Jesus.
  • I seal my deliverance with the blood of Jesus — whatever has left me will never return, in the name of Jesus.
  • Holy Spirit, fill every place that has been vacated in me, in the name of Jesus.
  • Let every door the enemy opened in my life be shut one by one, until the Lord gives me my rest, in the name of Jesus.
  • Father, strengthen me with might by Your Spirit in the inner man, and let Christ dwell in my heart by faith, in the name of Jesus.
  • I am a new creature — old things are passed away, and I will not pick them up again, in the name of Jesus.
  • Renew my mind, O Lord, so that the old pattern finds no place in me, in the name of Jesus.
  • Let the peace of God guard my mind, my home and my sleep tonight, in the name of Jesus.
  • Whom the Son sets free is free indeed — I am free and I remain free, in the name of Jesus.
