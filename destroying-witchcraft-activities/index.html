Welcome

How this study works

Every original word of this prophetic direction is kept exactly as spoken, in the ivory panels marked SAY. Around those words, this study adds four layers you can open at your own pace: a word study from the original Hebrew and Greek, commentary from reputable, named Christian sources, a plain-language explanation, and a practical, modern-life example.

Before you begin

The power in this direction is never the blood as a physical liquid, the words as a formula, or the ground itself. Every scripture behind this study — Hebrews 12:24, Revelation 12:11 — points to what Jesus Christ already accomplished at the cross. Pouring the blood on the ground is your act of obedient faith in that finished work, not a ritual that generates power on its own.

How To Pray This

A short order of prayer

A short, faith-filled order of prayer for the front door, the driveway, or any piece of ground the Lord leads you to.

  1. Read the four KJV scriptures aloud.
  2. Pray the twenty prayers aloud, one by one, as you pour the blood of Jesus on the earth.
  3. Close with the sealing prayer and Amen.
  4. Pour the blood of Jesus on the ground.

Teacher's Corner

Practical Discernment For Everyday Life

Six real-life scenarios that show how witchcraft's fingerprints can show up in ordinary situations — a missed promotion, a jamming computer, money that never stays — and how to respond with both prayer and practical wisdom.

A balanced approach

Not every setback is spiritual attack, and not every spiritual attack looks the same twice. Scripture calls believers to prayer and to wisdom, diligence, and practical care (Proverbs 24:3-4; James 1:5) — not to blame every ordinary difficulty on witchcraft, and not to ignore the spiritual dimension either. Where a pattern is repeated, targeted, and timed against your progress, these prayers speak directly to it.

Summary

Four scriptures anchor this direction: God's decisive removal of sorcery (Micah 5:12), the powerlessness of enchantment against covenant people (Numbers 23:23), the three real, non-magical keys to victory (Revelation 12:11), and the finished, better-speaking blood of Christ's mediation (Hebrews 12:24). Twenty specific prayers apply the blood of Jesus to twenty distinct patterns of household and territorial witchcraft, and one sealing prayer gathers them into a single, rested petition.

🔥 Closing Declaration

Repeat after me: I overcome by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of my testimony. I love not my life unto compromise. As I have poured the blood of Jesus on the earth, I stand delivered from every witchcraft power, in Jesus' Name. Amen.

🙏 Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank You for the blood that speaks better things than Abel's. Thank You that no enchantment and no divination can stand against those who are Yours. Seal what I have prayed today, and let my life testify of what You have wrought. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
