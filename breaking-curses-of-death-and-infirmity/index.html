Main Scripture

The tongue speaks; the Lord answers.

Proverbs 18:21 · KJV

“Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof.”

This teaching examines where curses come from, the seven mouths that carry weight in Scripture, and the curse a believer can speak over themselves. It closes with the prayer of deliverance in the exact order Pastor Kiky Ocansey led the service — praise, thanksgiving, confession, forgiveness, invitation of the Holy Spirit, the teaching, the deliverance prayers and declarations, and sealing in the blood of Jesus.

The Order of the Service

The prayer of deliverance follows Pastor Kiky’s order.

Do not skip a step. The order keeps you from beginning in fear. It brings you back to the Lord, back to the Cross, and back to the authority of Jesus Christ.

Editorial Note

Two matters for Pastor Kiky’s confirmation.

Pending confirmation

The manuscript preserves the teaching exactly as delivered. Two matters have been left open for Pastor Kiky’s confirmation and are listed at the back of the book in the Editorial Notes:

  1. Elijah / Elisha. The recording introduces 2 Kings 2:23–24 as “Elijah,” but the passage names Elisha. The KJV text is quoted in full; the introduction is flagged rather than silently harmonised.
  2. Six passages where the recording is partly unclear (altar references in Chapter 14; communion and water instruction in Chapter 15; a corporate declaration after “Inherited curses”; the anointing-oil declaration; a “covenant” passage; an altar prayer point). Wording defaults to Christ-centred, KJV-anchored language and each spot is marked “Recording unclear — Pastor Kiky to confirm.”
