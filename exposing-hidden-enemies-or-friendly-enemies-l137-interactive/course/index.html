What Is a Friendly Enemy?

The definition Pastor Kiky gave, and why this teaching matters

Tonight, in the mighty name of Jesus, we are exposing hidden enemies or friendly enemies.

The definition

Beloved, a friendly enemy is someone whose actions consistently seek to harm your life, or seek to mislead your life, or seek to undermine your life.

Guardrail Pastor Kiky set from the start

Since we cannot always judge motives accurately, we should ask God for wisdom, discernment and protection rather than making assumptions about others. This teaching is not a licence to accuse people. It is a call to pray for discernment.

There are people in our lives who appear to be friendly and helpful, but in reality they are enemies of our lives. Spiritual wisdom and discernment enable us to know and to expose who they are.

Why it matters

  • They are the reasons why we suffer.
  • They are the reasons why we cannot move forward.
  • And they are so close to us.
  • These people can often be close to us, and they know a lot about us.
  • They may appear friendly, but they have envy and resentment over your life.
  • They try to put blockages and hindrances in front of our breakthroughs.

We want a breakthrough — but there is a friendly enemy. We want a miracle — but there is a friendly enemy. We want that favour — but there is a friendly enemy. They are the obstacles in our way of greatness. They are the obstacles in our way of receiving. They are the reasons why there are certain doors in our life that are closed.

Love them — and still watch

Jesus said, “Love your enemies.” Yes, we should. But we also need to watch out for them, because of the damage they can do in our life and in our destiny.

Case file one — King Saul

He employed David and pretended that he loved him, yet all the while he was looking for an opportunity to kill David. He plotted against David several times, but God frustrated all his plans.

The pattern

  • Verse 17 — the offer looked like promotion; the motive was death.
  • Verse 21 — the gift was designed to be a snare.
  • Verse 22 — he used other people to carry the message.
  • Verse 25 — he set a task he expected to kill David.
  • Verse 29 — the friendliness ended; the enmity had been there all along.

The deliverance principle

A friendly enemy will hand you an opportunity that is actually a trap. Saul’s smile and Saul’s sword were the same man. God frustrated every plan — and David behaved himself more wisely (v.30). Wisdom, not warfare only, preserved him.

Discernment — The Greatest Gift

What discernment is, and what it is not

Beloved, the problem with many Christians is that we lack the power of spiritual discernment. So we open the door for people to hurt us.

One of the greatest gifts God gives us is discernment. If we have discernment, if we operate in discernment, beloved, we will have our peace of mind.

Pastor Kiky’s definition

Discernment is the ability, by the Holy Spirit, to recognize truth from error — and genuine motive from hidden motive.

Discernment is NOT

  • Suspicion
  • Fear
  • Assumption

Discernment IS

  • Spiritual wisdom that helps us make godly decisions
  • Protection from deception
  • Protection from evil, destruction and lies

Until we cry out for revelation, a trusted friend who has a bad agenda against us will keep coming close to harm us.

Wisdom After Revelation

How to carry yourself once God has shown you

Beloved, we need discernment. We need the Lord to lead us; otherwise we are praying amiss.

We pray, the Lord is ready to release, but there are hindrances. There are some friendly enemies who are hindering the blessings and the progress of our lives. And sadly, some of them are family members. Some of them are best friends. Some of them are too close to us — too close for comfort.

Do you know the amount of things that God has prepared for you? Some people complain, “I have had so many promises, but they are not coming to pass.” They are there — but there are hindrances from these people in our life. If they are exposed to you, that is the beginning of your freedom.

What changes when you know

1

You learn how to live around them

You begin to keep quiet about certain things; certain conversations you no longer have with them.

2

You do not announce that you know

When you discover them, you do not even allow them to know that you know them. Just the way they were pretending and playing in your face, you do the same thing.

3

You guard your information

There are certain things you would not say to them. There are certain breakthroughs you would not tell them. Even the church that you attend, you do not say. Some of them, do not even tell them your address.

4

You refuse bitterness

Ask the Lord to expose any harmful plan against you, your family, and your calling — while also asking Him to help you avoid becoming suspicious or bitter.

The heart guardrail

This is the safeguard Pastor Kiky repeated: ask Him to give you discernment… while also asking Him to help you avoid becoming suspicious or bitter. Revelation without love turns a believer into an accuser. Guard your heart while you guard your information.

The Holy Spirit’s part

  • The Holy Spirit brings understanding to expose their deception.
  • The Holy Spirit protects us from them.
  • The Holy Spirit leads us into all truth.
  • When we walk closely with God, He guards us from being overcome by evil and enables us to respond with wisdom.

Is it right for a Christian to separate?

As a Christian I was thinking, “Lord, is it okay to stop communicating with these sort of people?” And the Lord said to me: when the devil misbehaved in heaven, I threw him out. I did not allow him to stay. The devil was in heaven — not just destroying other angels, polluting other angels, he wanted to come against God Himself. So I said, if the Lord Himself cast away the devil, who am I?

And that was the scripture God gave me — to cut them off from my life, to stop communication with them, to mind my business, and to pray my prayers and get close to my God who saved my life. For with God nothing shall be impossible.

“The Witch Must Die” — What It Means

A clarification Pastor Kiky gave in the service

A woman of God asked: “We say the witch must die, but the witch is still living. They do not die.”

Pastor Kiky’s answer

It is their spirits that die — the spirit that is working against you. You kill that spirit. So whatever they throw at you, it will not work anymore. It does not touch you anymore. That means that spirit is dead.

The human being is a human being. That man is a man. But that man has a spirit called witchcraft. So when you say “the witch must die,” you are not killing the man or the woman — you are killing their spirits. They will still plot against you, but it will not come to you, because the witch must die. You have killed the witchcraft spirit that attacks you.

Why this correction matters

This clarification matters. L137 prayer is warfare against spirits, never against people. We pray for the person’s repentance and salvation; we war against the spirit operating through them.

8. Teaching Clarification — "The Witch Must Die"

(This section is preserved because it corrects a common misunderstanding in deliverance prayer.)

A woman of God asked: "We say the witch must die, but the witch is still living. They do not die."

I said: it is their spirits that die — the spirit that is working against you. You kill that spirit. So whatever they throw at you, it will not work anymore. It does not touch you anymore. That means that spirit is dead.

The human being is a human being. That man is a man. But that man has a spirit called witchcraft. So when you say "the witch must die," you are not killing the man or the woman — you are killing their spirits. They will still plot against you, but it will not come to you, because the witch must die. You have killed the witchcraft spirit that attacks you.

Any witchcraft spirit that is hindering your progress in your life, let it die by fire. By the power of the Holy Ghost, any enemy that is a witch, any witchcraft that has vowed that you will not move forward in life — let it die in the name of Jesus, so you can move forward.
