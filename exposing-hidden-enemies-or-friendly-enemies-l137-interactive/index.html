The heart of the teaching

Nothing covered shall stay covered

A friendly enemy is someone whose actions consistently seek to harm your life, or seek to mislead your life, or seek to undermine your life.

Pastor Kiky opened this service with a guardrail before she opened the subject: since we cannot always judge motives accurately, we should ask God for wisdom, discernment and protection rather than making assumptions about others. This teaching is not permission to accuse anybody. It is a call to pray until God Himself brings the hidden thing into the light — and to walk in wisdom once He does.

Prayer of salvation — repeat after me
