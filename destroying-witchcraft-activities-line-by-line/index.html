Section 1

The opening line, word by word

Before we get to the scriptures and prayers, Pastor Kiky gives a very short opening. Every word of it carries weight.

“Take the blood of Jesus. Go outside. Read the scriptures. Say the prayers. Pour the blood of Jesus on the ground.”

Take
“Take the blood of Jesus.”

Pastor Kiky is not asking you to imagine the blood. She is telling you to actively receive it and apply it. In deliverance ministry the blood of Jesus is a real weapon (Revelation 12:11). To “take” it is a faith action — you take it by your mouth in prayer, by declaring it, and by pouring on the ground as a prophetic act.

Go outside
“Go outside.”

The ground is important. You are not praying at a shrine or an altar of witchcraft — you are turning the earth itself into an altar of testimony (Hebrews 12:24). Going outside means to the front door, the driveway, the compound, or any patch of ground the Lord leads you to. Witchcraft powers work in secret; you are stepping into the open and confronting them where they operate.

Read the scriptures
“Read the scriptures.”

Scripture comes first — before any prayer point is prayed. The Word of God is the sword of the Spirit (Ephesians 6:17). Reading the four scriptures aloud sets a legal, biblical foundation for everything that follows.

Say the prayers
“Say the prayers.”

Say them out loud. Deliverance prayers are not silent thoughts — they are spoken commands. Faith comes by hearing (Romans 10:17), and your own ears must hear your mouth agree with the Word.

Pour the blood of Jesus on the ground
“Pour the blood of Jesus on the ground.”

This is the prophetic action Pastor Kiky is instructing. As you speak, you pour — physically, on the earth — as a prophetic act of covering that ground with the blood of the everlasting covenant. Hebrews 12:24 says the blood of Jesus speaketh better things than that of Abel. Abel’s blood cried for vengeance; Jesus’ blood cries mercy, redemption, and testimony over that ground.

Section 2

How to pray this — the order, line by line

Pastor Kiky gives a short, faith-filled order of prayer. Each line is one instruction.

“Read the four KJV scriptures aloud.”

The four scriptures are read first because they establish the covenant ground you are standing on. Read slowly, read aloud, and let your ears hear the Word before your mouth prays.

“Pray the twenty prayers aloud, one by one, as you pour the blood of Jesus on the earth.”

Twenty prayers — pray each one out loud, one by one. Do not skim. As you pray, pour a little water or wine that has been prayed over as the blood of Jesus, or simply pour by faith. The pouring is a prophetic act, not a magical formula. The power is in Christ’s finished work, not in the liquid.

“Close with the sealing prayer and Amen.”

Never leave a deliverance session open-ended. The sealing prayer covers you, your household, and the ground you just prayed on with the blood of Jesus and closes the session under His authority.

“Pour the blood of Jesus on the ground.”

This is repeated on purpose. It is the last action Pastor Kiky wants you to remember — after the scriptures, after the prayers, after the sealing prayer — the final act is a physical pouring on the ground.

Ministry note. The physical element (the water or wine used as a symbol of the blood) is only meaningful through the finished work of Christ on the cross. It is not a ritual on its own. Your faith is in Jesus’ blood, not in the substance you pour.
Section 3 · READ

Four KJV scriptures, verse by verse

Read each verse slowly and aloud before you begin. Then read the explanation.

Micah 5:12 · KJV
And I will cut off witchcrafts out of thine hand; and thou shalt have no more soothsayers.
Tap to reveal explanation

“And I will cut off” — the LORD Himself is the one doing the cutting. This is not you fighting witchcraft in your own strength; God is severing it.

“witchcrafts out of thine hand” — “out of thine hand” means any witchcraft that has been operating in, through, or around you — inherited, invited, or imposed. God cuts it off from where it has been working.

“thou shalt have no more soothsayers” — soothsayers are diviners, fortune-tellers, consulters of familiar spirits. God is promising you will have no more of them influencing your life, your family, or your future.

This verse is the covenant reason we can pour the blood on the ground. God has spoken; we are enforcing what He has already said.

Numbers 23:23 · KJV
Surely there is no enchantment against Jacob, neither is there any divination against Israel: according to this time it shall be said of Jacob and of Israel, What hath God wrought!
Tap to reveal explanation

“Surely there is no enchantment against Jacob” — enchantments are witchcraft spells and incantations. Balaam was hired to curse Israel and could not. If you are in Christ, you are grafted into the covenant of Jacob and Israel (Romans 11); no witchcraft spell can legally stand against you.

“neither is there any divination against Israel” — divination is consulting evil spirits for information or power. God says none of it stands against His people.

“according to this time it shall be said of Jacob and of Israel, What hath God wrought!” — instead of witchcraft testimony, there will be God’s testimony. People will look at your life and say, “Look what God has done.”

This verse is the confidence scripture — you pray from victory, not toward victory.

Revelation 12:11 · KJV
And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.
Tap to reveal explanation

“And they overcame him” — “him” is Satan, the accuser of the brethren (verse 10). Overcoming him is not optional; it is our inheritance in Christ.

“by the blood of the Lamb” — this is why the blood is central to this prophetic direction. The blood of Jesus is the first weapon of overcoming.

“and by the word of their testimony” — the second weapon is your mouth. You must speak. Silent Christians do not overthrow witchcraft. That is why every one of the twenty prayers is said aloud.

“and they loved not their lives unto the death” — the third weapon is total surrender. You are not begging witchcraft to spare your comfort; you are declaring that Jesus is Lord, whatever the cost.

Hebrews 12:24 · KJV
And to Jesus the mediator of the new covenant, and to the blood of sprinkling, that speaketh better things than that of Abel.
Tap to reveal explanation

“And to Jesus the mediator of the new covenant” — Jesus is the go-between of a better covenant. Every prayer of the blood is legal because Jesus is the mediator.

“and to the blood of sprinkling” — this is exactly what Pastor Kiky is having you do. Sprinkling — pouring — the blood on the ground is a biblical picture, not something invented.

“that speaketh better things than that of Abel” — Abel’s blood cried for vengeance from the ground (Genesis 4:10). Jesus’ blood cries mercy, forgiveness, redemption, and testimony from the ground. When you pour the blood of Jesus on the ground, you are silencing every voice of witchcraft, generational curses, and shed innocent blood that has been speaking against your life, and you are letting the blood of Jesus speak instead.

Section 4 · SAY

Twenty prayers of the blood, explained one by one

Pray each prayer aloud. As you pray, pour the blood of Jesus on the earth. Do not rush. Let each word come out with faith. Then, if you want to understand the prayer more deeply, tap it below.

Prayer 1

As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, I cut myself off from every territorial spirit, in Jesus’ Name.

Tap to reveal explanation

Territorial spirits are demonic principalities assigned to a location — a city, a family compound, a village, a workplace, or a piece of ground (Daniel 10:13; Ephesians 6:12).

“I cut myself off” — you are severing any legal claim any territorial spirit has been using against you because of your address, your origin, or your inheritance.

By pouring the blood on the earth, you are marking the ground and saying: this territory answers to Jesus now, not to any principality.

Prayer 2

Any association of domestic demon responsible for my present battles, O God, arise and destroy them by fire, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Domestic demon = a household spirit. It gains access through family sin, family covenants, family idols, or bloodline patterns.

“Association” — these spirits rarely operate alone; they operate as a network.

“Arise and destroy them by fire” — echoes Psalm 68:1–2: Let God arise, let his enemies be scattered. Fire in scripture is a symbol of God’s consuming judgment (Hebrews 12:29).

Prayer 3

Every household power praying for me to fall and never to rise up again, die, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Household power — a spirit or a person in your bloodline using witchcraft prayers against you.

“Falling and never rising” is the exact witchcraft agenda spoken of in scripture (Micah 7:8 promises the opposite: when I fall, I shall arise).

This prayer returns their evil prayers back to sender and cancels their assignment.

Prayer 4

I loose myself from curses that activate prolonged poverty and lack, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Prolonged poverty — poverty that never seems to end, that follows you from job to job, family member to family member.

Galatians 3:13 — Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law. This prayer enforces that redemption over your finances.

Prayer 5

Whatever ancestral and household enemies have destroyed in my life and marriage, be restored by fire, in Jesus’ Name.

Tap to reveal explanation

Ancestral enemies — spirits operating along the bloodline. Household enemies — spirits operating within the family unit.

“Be restored by fire” — Joel 2:25, I will restore to you the years that the locust hath eaten. Restoration in deliverance ministry is often violent because it must break the hold the enemy had.

Prayer 6

As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, Lord, let the anointing of the Holy Ghost break every yoke of failure at the edge of breakthroughs caused by ancestral witchcraft powers, in Jesus’ Name.

Tap to reveal explanation

Failure at the edge of breakthrough — the pattern of coming very close to victory (job, marriage, healing, finances) and then losing it just before it manifests.

Isaiah 10:27 — the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing. The anointing does not negotiate; it breaks.

Prayer 7

Altar of household wickedness in my family, catch fire now as I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth.

Tap to reveal explanation

Altar — any place, agreement, or covenant where sacrifice or dedication has been made to demonic powers, especially by ancestors.

Altars speak (Hebrews 12:24). If they were not silenced by the blood of Jesus, they are still speaking sickness, poverty, delay, and death over the bloodline.

“Catch fire” — see Elijah in 1 Kings 18:38. The fire of God consumes the sacrifice, the wood, the stones, the dust, and the water in the trench.

Prayer 8

O God, let all those using affliction to pursue my intended blessings — I command it to disappear by fire, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Affliction is often used as a diversion — the enemy afflicts your body, marriage, or mind so you cannot pursue the blessing God intended.

Nahum 1:9 — affliction shall not rise up the second time.

Prayer 9

Everything that household enemies have stolen or destroyed in my life, be restored now, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Proverbs 6:31 — if he be found, he shall restore sevenfold. Command the return of what was stolen: time, opportunities, health, money, relationships, peace, virginity of purpose, spiritual gifts.

Prayer 10

Evil network of my household witchcraft, scatter by the thunder of God, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Network — witchcraft rarely operates alone; it operates as a coordinated network of people, spirits, altars, and covenants.

Psalm 18:14 — he sent out his arrows, and scattered them; and he shot out lightnings, and discomfited them.

Prayer 11

As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, every weapon of household enemies targeted against me, turn against your owners, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Esther 7:10 — Haman was hanged on the very gallows he built for Mordecai.

Psalm 7:15–16 — he made a pit, and digged it, and is fallen into the ditch which he made… his mischief shall return upon his own head.

You are not cursing anyone; you are asking God to send back to the sender what was sent against you.

Prayer 12

Every evil leg and evil hand walking about for my sake, catch fire, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Witchcraft agents “walk” to shrines, herbalists, and evil altars to fetch power against people. Evil hands “handle” evil objects and materials.

You are asking God to consume the tools of the assignment, not the persons — vengeance is the Lord’s (Romans 12:19).

Prayer 13

Every stubborn strongman trading with my goodness, submit your power and perish, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Strongman — Mark 3:27, Jesus’ teaching that you must bind the strongman before you can spoil his house.

“Trading with my goodness” — the strongman has been exchanging your blessings for his profit. This prayer commands him to submit his power (surrender it to Christ) and perish (be judged).

Prayer 14

As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, evil eyes monitoring my life, go blind now by fire, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Evil monitoring — the practice of scanning someone’s life by demonic means (in dreams, mirrors, water, ritual objects) to track them and attack.

Acts 13:11 — Elymas the sorcerer was struck blind for opposing the gospel. You are asking for that same judgment on any monitoring assignment.

Prayer 15

Demonic reverse gears installed to hinder my progress and success, roast now by fire, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Reverse gear — the pattern where every step forward is followed by two steps back.

Isaiah 43:19 — Behold, I will do a new thing… I will even make a way in the wilderness. This prayer removes the mechanism the enemy has been using to keep you going backwards.

Prayer 16

Every witchcraft handwriting in any area of my life, by the blood of Jesus, be washed off, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Handwriting — Colossians 2:14, Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross.

Any covenant, contract, dedication, or blood oath that anyone (including your ancestors) signed against your life — the blood of Jesus washes it off.

Prayer 17

Every altar of household enemies speaking hardship into my life, catch fire and scatter, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Altars speak — for good (Hebrews 12:24) or for evil. Altars that speak hardship must be silenced. Fire consumes them; scattering breaks their organized authority.

Prayer 18

Satanic keepers of my instruments of promotion and advancement, release them by fire, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Instruments of promotion — the gifts, talents, opportunities, favor, and open doors God had assigned to you. Sometimes they are held in demonic custody.

Psalm 68:18 — Thou hast ascended on high, thou hast led captivity captive: thou hast received gifts for men. Jesus already collected them; this prayer enforces the release.

Prayer 19

As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, Blood of Jesus, deliver me from every power determined to destroy me because of the wickedness of my ancestors, in the Name of Jesus.

Tap to reveal explanation

Exodus 20:5 speaks of iniquity visiting the third and fourth generations. Ezekiel 18:20 tells us the soul that sins shall die and that the son does not bear the iniquity of the father when he turns to God.

The blood of Jesus is what enforces Ezekiel 18 in your life. You are stepping out from under generational judgment and standing under the blood.

Prayer 20

Household enemies, carry your load and go. (Name the evil load one by one: sickness, poverty, sorrow, loneliness, demotion, backwardness.)

Tap to reveal explanation

“Carry your load and go” — you refuse to keep carrying what does not belong to you. The load is returned to its senders.

Pastor Kiky instructs you to name the load one by one: sickness, poverty, sorrow, loneliness, demotion, backwardness. Be specific. Deliverance is precise, not vague.

Matthew 11:28–30 — Jesus carries your yoke. Anything the household enemy laid on you does not have to stay.

Section 5 · SEAL

The sealing prayer, word by word

Every deliverance session ends with sealing. This is not optional.

Sealing Prayer

As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, Lord, deliver me from every witchcraft powers, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth

Once more, the physical act. You are still on your feet, still pouring, still active in faith.

Lord

You address the Lord — not the witch, not the spirit, not the ancestor. Deliverance is not a conversation with demons; it is a petition to God.

Deliver me from every witchcraft powers

“Deliver me” is the very language Jesus taught in the Lord’s Prayer (Matthew 6:13, deliver us from evil). “Every witchcraft powers” closes every loophole — nothing is excluded, no source of witchcraft is exempt.

In Jesus’ Name. Amen.

You close the prayer under the authority of the Name that is above every name (Philippians 2:9–11). Amen means so be it — it is the covenant seal on what you just prayed.

“Now pour the blood of Jesus on the ground.”

The last action. The prophetic act closes the session, marks the ground, and testifies that Jesus is Lord over that territory.

The L137 tagline, explained

Luke 1:37 · KJV
For with God nothing shall be impossible.
L137

L137 is the ministry acronym for Luke 1:37. Every scripture, every prayer, every declaration in this direction rests on this promise: nothing is impossible with God — not the destruction of witchcraft, not the restoration of what was stolen, not the deliverance of your household.
