This interactive study walks you through a complete deliverance flow for mental attacks. It follows Pastor Kiky's standard L137 order: Praise → Thanksgiving → Confession → Forgiveness → Invite the Holy Spirit → Teaching → Deliverance Prayers & Declarations → Sealing.

Pastoral note. This is a spiritual manual. If you or a loved one is dealing with severe or ongoing mental symptoms — suicidal thoughts, psychosis, uncontrollable panic, or self-harm — please stand in prayer and seek qualified clinical care and trusted pastoral covering. God works through both the altar and the physician (Colossians 4:14).

The Teaching: Seven Doorways the Enemy Uses Against the Mind

Main text.

2 Corinthians 10:3–5 (KJV)For though we walk in the flesh, we do not war after the flesh: (For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds;) Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ.
Ephesians 6:12 (KJV)For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.

What "mental attacks" means in Scripture. Mental attacks are the enemy's assault on the mind — the seat of thought, imagination, memory, will, and belief. The Bible calls this a battle, and the battle is spiritual before it is emotional.

Common signatures. Sudden intrusive thoughts against God, yourself, family, or covering; looping thoughts that will not stop; night-time torment; condemnation after repentance; whispered lies in your own voice; panic without cause; confusion and forgetfulness; word-twisting inside your own head; suicidal or violent suggestions; and post-service counterattack after worship, communion, or a strong prayer meeting. None of this defines you — it exposes what must bow to Jesus.

Doorway 1 · Fear and Torment
2 Timothy 1:7 (KJV)For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.
1 John 4:18 (KJV)There is no fear in love; but perfect love casteth out fear: because fear hath torment. He that feareth is not made perfect in love.

Fear is a spirit — not merely a mood or personality trait — and it can be commanded to leave in the Name of Jesus.

Doorway 2 · Condemnation and Accusation
Romans 8:1 (KJV)There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.
Revelation 12:10 (KJV)And I heard a loud voice saying in heaven, Now is come salvation, and strength, and the kingdom of our God, and the power of his Christ: for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night.

After you have repented, any voice still accusing you of that sin is the accuser — not the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit convicts to restore; the enemy accuses to condemn.

Doorway 3 · Confusion and Mental Fog
1 Corinthians 14:33 (KJV)For God is not the author of confusion, but of peace, as in all churches of the saints.
James 1:8 (KJV)A double minded man is unstable in all his ways.

Confusion is not from God. Where there is confusion in your mind, there is a door that must be closed and a Word that must be planted.

Doorway 4 · Pride and Twisting (Leviathan)
Job 41:1 (KJV)Canst thou draw out leviathan with an hook? or his tongue with a cord which thou lettest down?
Proverbs 16:18 (KJV)Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall.

Leviathan twists words, exalts pride, brings accusation, and pressures relationships. Much mental attack is Leviathan's speech turned inward — self-exalting arguments, distortion of what people actually said, and the constant urge to defend and justify.

Doorway 5 · Depression, Heaviness, Spirit of Death
Isaiah 61:3 (KJV)To appoint unto them that mourn in Zion, to give unto them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness; that they might be called trees of righteousness, the planting of the Lord, that he might be glorified.
Psalm 42:5 (KJV)Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted in me? hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him, for the help of my countenance.

Heaviness is exchanged for the garment of praise. When suicidal or "everything is over" thoughts strike, seek pastoral covering and qualified clinical care in the Name of Jesus.

Doorway 6 · Occult and Psychic Streams
Deuteronomy 18:10–12 (KJV)There shall not be found among you any one that maketh his son or his daughter to pass through the fire, or that useth divination, or an observer of times, or an enchanter, or a witch, Or a charmer, or a consulter with familiar spirits, or a wizard, or a necromancer. For all that do these things are an abomination unto the Lord: and because of these abominations the Lord thy God doth drive them out from before thee.
Acts 19:19 (KJV)Many of them also which used curious arts brought their books together, and burned them before all men: and they counted the price of them, and found it fifty thousand pieces of silver.

Mind-control, mind-binding, mind-idolatry, and familiar spirits enter through any prior exposure to horoscopes, tarot, ouija, palm reading, mediums, ancestral rituals, spells, chanting, or "harmless" curiosity. Every door must be renounced.

Doorway 7 · Word Curses and Inner Vows
Proverbs 18:21 (KJV)Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof.
Numbers 30:2 (KJV)If a man vow a vow unto the Lord, or swear an oath to bind his soul with a bond; he shall not break his word, he shall do according to all that proceedeth out of his mouth.

Negative words spoken over you — and vows you spoke over yourself ("I'm stupid," "I'll never be free," "I always mess up") — become footholds until they are renounced.

Deliverance Prayer Flow

Speak these prayers aloud. The enemy hears your mouth, not your feelings. Move through the eight steps in order.

Step 1 · Praise and Worship
Psalm 22:3But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel.
Psalm 8:2Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength because of thine enemies, that thou mightest still the enemy and the avenger.
Father, I lift up Your Name above every voice in my mind. You are holy, You are worthy, You are enthroned. I worship You before I fight. Every enemy voice is silenced in the presence of my King. In Jesus' Name.
Step 2 · Thanksgiving
1 Thessalonians 5:18In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.
Father, thank You for the blood of Jesus. Thank You that I am not alone in this battle. Thank You that You have already given me the mind of Christ (1 Corinthians 2:16). Thank You that You are renewing me day by day. Thank You for every deliverance already won and every one still coming. In Jesus' Name.
Step 3 · Confession of Sins
1 John 1:9If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.
Psalm 51:10Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me.
Father, I confess every sin the enemy is using as a legal foothold in my mind. I confess pride, self-hatred, unbelief, fear, agreement with lies, meditating on evil, entertaining bitterness, wrong media, occult curiosity, and every ungodly word I have spoken over myself and others. Wash me in the blood of Jesus. Cleanse my conscience. Renew a right spirit within me. In Jesus' Name.
Step 4 · Forgive Others
Mark 11:25And when ye stand praying, forgive, if ye have ought against any: that your Father also which is in heaven may forgive you your trespasses.
Father, I forgive every person who wounded my mind — parents, family, spouse, ex-partners, teachers, pastors, employers, strangers, church members, those who mocked me, betrayed me, silenced me, lied on me, rejected me, or spoke curses over me. I release them into Your hands. I refuse bitterness. I forgive myself for the years I agreed with the lie. I receive Your forgiveness by the blood of Jesus. In Jesus' Name.
Step 5 · Invite the Holy Spirit
John 14:26But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.
Holy Spirit, come. Fill every room in my mind. Search me, know me, lead me. Bring to remembrance every open door, every vow, every word curse, every trauma the enemy has been using. Give me courage to renounce it. In Jesus' Name.
Step 6 · Renouncing Every Open Door
In the Name of Jesus, I renounce every open door I have given the enemy against my mind — every act of unbelief, every consultation with the occult, every horoscope I read, every card I pulled, every psychic I visited, every ancestral ritual I participated in, every drug that altered my mind, every pornographic image I fed my imagination, every ungodly soul tie, every vow of self-hatred, every agreement with fear. I close every one of those doors by the blood of Jesus and I forbid the enemy to use them again. In Jesus' Name.
Step 7 · Breaking Word Curses and Generational Streams
Isaiah 54:17No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the Lord, and their righteousness is of me, saith the Lord.
Galatians 3:13Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree.
In the Name of Jesus, I break every word curse spoken over my mind — from my mother's womb, from my father's mouth, from teachers, siblings, spouses, ex-partners, church leaders, employers, witches, enemies, and from my own mouth. I break every inner vow: "I am stupid," "I am forgotten," "I cannot recover," "I am my mother's madness," "I am my father's anger," "I will never be free." I renounce them all. I cancel their assignment. Every word planted against my mind, dry up from the root, by the blood of Jesus. I plead the blood of Jesus over my bloodline back to Adam. I sever every generational chain — fear, depression, anxiety, suicide, addiction, madness, occult inheritance, ancestral pacts, chronic worry. I am redeemed by the blood of the Lamb. In Jesus' Name.
Step 8 · Casting Out Spirits Assigned Against the Mind
Mark 16:17And these signs shall follow them that believe; In my name shall they cast out devils; they shall speak with new tongues.
Luke 10:19Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you.

In the Name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I command every unclean spirit assigned against my mind to come out and go now:

  • Spirit of fear, come out and go.
  • Spirit of torment, come out and go.
  • Spirit of condemnation, come out and go.
  • Spirit of confusion, come out and go.
  • Spirit of double-mindedness, come out and go.
  • Spirit of forgetfulness, come out and go.
  • Spirit of mental fog, come out and go.
  • Spirit of heaviness and depression, come out and go.
  • Spirit of hopelessness and suicide, come out and go.
  • Spirit of anxiety and panic, come out and go.
  • Spirit of insomnia and night torment, come out and go.
  • Spirit of pride, come out and go.
  • Spirit of Leviathan and twisting, come out and go.
  • Spirit of accusation, come out and go.
  • Every occult, familiar, psychic, mind-control, mind-binding, mind-idolatry spirit, come out and go.
  • Every inherited spirit from my father's line and my mother's line, come out and go.
  • I command you to leave now, never to return, sealed by the blood of Jesus.
Step 9 · Sealing with the Blood of Jesus
Revelation 12:11And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.
Father, I seal this deliverance with the blood of Jesus. I cover my mind, my thoughts, my imagination, my memory, my dreams, my sleep, my speech, my emotions, my will, and every gate of my head — eyes, ears, mouth — with the blood of the Lamb. Fill every place vacated by the enemy with the Holy Spirit, the Word of God, and the mind of Christ. Post Your angels around me according to Psalm 34:7. What You have delivered, no devil can reclaim. In Jesus' Name. Amen.

Aftercare — Keeping Your Mind Free

Freedom is a lifestyle, not one prayer. Return to this study whenever a counterattack rises.

Joshua 1:8This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.
Psalm 101:3I will set no wicked thing before mine eyes: I hate the work of them that turn aside; it shall not cleave to me.
Hebrews 10:25Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some is; but exhorting one another: and so much the more as ye see the day approaching.
  • Daily Bible intake — feed your mind KJV Scripture aloud, morning and night.
  • Guard your gates — what you watch, listen to, and read either builds or breaks your renewal.
  • Fellowship — isolation is where the enemy re-enters; stay under godly covering.
  • Speak, don't only think — the enemy attacks in thoughts; you answer in spoken Word.
  • Sleep with worship or the Word — atmosphere over sleeping minds matters.
  • Renew inner vows — the moment the Holy Spirit shows you one, renounce it.
  • Fast periodically as the Lord leads (Matthew 17:21).
  • Return to this study after strong ministry, worship, or communion — counterattack is real, and so is your authority in Christ.
  • Clinical care where appropriate — for severe, chronic, or dangerous mental symptoms, walk with a Bible-believing pastor and a qualified clinician.
