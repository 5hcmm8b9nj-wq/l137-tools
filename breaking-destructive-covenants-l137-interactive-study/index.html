How to use this study

Order of service

The standard L137 deliverance flow, as it ran in this service. Tick each stage as you walk through it.

Teaching modules

Fourteen modules covering the service from the opening thanksgiving to the closing charge. Open each one, pray the prayers aloud, then mark it complete.

Guided deliverance session

Walk the service through in order, exactly as Pastor Kiky Ocansey led it. Do not skip or reorder the stages.

Before you begin — gather these
  • Your Bible (King James Version)
  • Communion wine if you have it — if you do not have the blood, take water that represents the blood of Jesus
  • Your anointing oil
  • A piece of paper and a pen for the names
As given in the teaching

"You cannot just wake up and break it by yourself and declare that the covenant is no longer binding on your life… For you to break the covenant you must involve Jesus, and a deliverance minister that is skilled in that area to help you by the power of the Holy Ghost."

The direction, as given

"When you leave here tonight, you take a piece of paper and you write down certain names — of certain partners, old husbands, old boyfriends, old girlfriends, whoever in your life. Even the ones that you asked, and you sent money for them to go and do certain things for you… And as that agreement is working over your life, disconnect yourself."
