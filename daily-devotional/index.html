Today's Word

The God Who Breaks Protocols

"And it was so, when the king saw Esther the queen standing in the court, that she obtained favour in his sight: and the king held out to Esther the golden sceptre that was in his hand. So Esther drew near, and touched the top of the sceptre."

— Esther 5:2 (KJV)
  1. God is not limited by man-made systems. When rules, traditions, and closed doors seem to hinder your destiny, God has power to bypass every one of them to fulfill His purpose in your life.
  2. God is the author of divine exceptions. What men say is impossible, He makes possible. Protocols are created by men, but miracles are orchestrated by God.
  3. Any man-made law closing doors against your destiny is broken by the power of the Holy Ghost. When God steps in, age-old traditions collapse and divine order manifests.
