Then in ElevenLabs

  1. Open ElevenLabs and go to Text to Speech.
  2. Tap the text box and hold your finger down until Paste appears, then tap it.
  3. Set the voice to Adam — it carries the prophetic declarations and prayer with the right weight.
  4. Set the model to Eleven Turbo v2.5 to use half the credits.
  5. Tap Generate, then download the audio.
Do not choose Eleven v3. It does not handle the pause tags reliably. The "With pauses" version gives about 2–2.5 seconds between scriptures and instructions to reflect, 3 seconds after every "repeat after me" prayer line so you can speak it aloud, and 4 seconds before "Amen" to close.
