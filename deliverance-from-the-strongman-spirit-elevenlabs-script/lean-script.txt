L137 Deliverance Ministry. Pastor Kiky Ocansey.

This is a prophetic direction from a service on Clubhouse, Wednesday, September sixteenth, twenty twenty six.

Topic. Deliverance from the Strongman Spirit.

Prophetic Direction.

Luke chapter eleven, verses twenty one to twenty two. King James Version.

When a strong man armed keepeth his palace, his goods are in peace. But when a stronger than he shall come upon him, and overcome him, he taketh from him all his armour wherein he trusted, and divideth his spoils.

Jeremiah chapter twenty two, verses twenty nine to thirty. King James Version.

O earth, earth, earth, hear the word of the LORD. Thus saith the LORD, Write ye this man childless, a man that shall not prosper in his days: for no man of his seed shall prosper, sitting upon the throne of David, and ruling any more in Judah.

Leviticus chapter seventeen, verse eleven. King James Version.

For the life of the flesh is in the blood: and I have given it to you upon the altar to make an atonement for your souls: for it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul.

Take your blood of Jesus. Go outside, and stand on the earth. Pray this prayer, and pour your communion wine on the ground, the earth.

The Ground. The Earth.

Prayer.

Say. Repeat after me.

By the power of the Holy Ghost, O earth, earth, earth, hear the word of the LORD, and fight every strongman in my life fighting my destiny, in Jesus' name.

As I pour the blood of Jesus on this earth, you strongman of mind destruction, be bound, by the blood of Jesus, in Jesus' name.

You strongman of financial destruction, be bound, by the blood of Jesus, in Jesus' name.

Every strongman of bad luck, attached to my life, fall down and die, in Jesus' name.

I bind every strongman, militating against my home, by the blood of Jesus, in the name of Jesus.

I bind and paralyze every strongman of death and hell, by the blood of Jesus, in the name of Jesus.

You evil strongman, attached to my destiny, be bound, by the blood of Jesus, in Jesus' name.

Every strongman of my father's house, die right now, and set me free, in the name of Jesus.

Every strongman, assigned by the evil powers of my father's house against my life, die, in the name of Jesus.

Every strongman, assigned to weaken my faith, catch fire, in the name of Jesus.

As I pour the blood of Jesus on this earth, I bind, and I render to naught, all the strongmen that are currently troubling my life, by the blood of Jesus.

Let the backbone of the stubborn pursuer and strongman break, in the name of Jesus.

Earth, hear me. I bind every strongman, having my goods in his possession, in the name of Jesus.

I clear my goods from the warehouse of the strongman, in the name of Jesus.

I withdraw the staff of the office of the strongman delegated against me, by the blood of Jesus, in the name of Jesus.

I bind every strongman delegated against me. Fall down to the ground, and become impotent, in Jesus' name.

Oh earth, bind the strongman behind my spiritual blindness and deafness, and paralyze his operations in my life, by the blood of Jesus.

As I pour the blood of Jesus on this earth, render every strongman in my life powerless. Unless they leave me alone, they will not have peace in any area of their lives, in Jesus' name.

Earth, earth, earth, open your mouth, and swallow up the blood of Jesus, and fight on my behalf, through the power of the Holy Ghost. Amen.

Do this for three days.

May the Lord be with you.

Amen.
