About the Ministry

About L137 Deliverance Ministry International

L137 Deliverance Ministry International is a house of warfare prayer and prophetic teaching, founded on the promise of Luke 1:37 — that with God, nothing shall be impossible. Under the leadership of Pastor Kiki Ocansey, the ministry gathers the family on the prayer line every Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday at midnight GMT (7 PM EST / 6 PM CST) to contend for breakthrough, healing, and freedom in the name of Jesus.

Pastor Kiki Ocansey carries a burden for deliverance and for equipping believers to stand in their God-given authority. Every teaching is preserved and made freely available — workbooks, recall sheets, prophetic directions, declarations cards, and clean transcripts — so the word can be carried home, studied, and shared.
