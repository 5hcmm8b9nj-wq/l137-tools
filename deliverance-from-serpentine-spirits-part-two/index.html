Before you pray

Set the atmosphere

The standard L137 deliverance flow. Never skip a step and never reorder them.

  1. 1 · Praise and worshipBegin with worship. Use the service worship set or any Christ-centred worship that lifts the name of Jesus.
  2. 2 · ThanksgivingThank Him for who He is, for what He has already done, and in advance for the deliverance He is about to bring.
  3. 3 · Confession of sinsConfess by name whatever the Holy Spirit brings up, and bring it under the blood.
  4. 4 · Forgive othersForgive by name whoever the Holy Spirit brings up. Release the wound, the debt and the memory.
  5. 5 · Invite the Holy SpiritInvite the fire of the Holy Ghost over your room, your body, your home and your family.
  6. 6 · TeachingRead the module with every scripture in full KJV.
  7. 7 · Deliverance prayers and declarationsPray aloud. Declare aloud. Do not rush.
  8. 8 · SealingCover everything with the blood of Jesus and give thanks.
Pray aloud — Opening covering prayer

Father, in the mighty name of Jesus, we cover this room, this page and this reader with the blood of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. Let the angels of the Lord ascend and descend around every reader. By the power of the Holy Ghost we declare that no weapon formed against you shall prosper. Holy Spirit, take charge of this study. In the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, amen.

Salvation & Rededication Prayer

Beloved, let the Holy Spirit ask you a gentle question: have you personally received Jesus Christ as your Lord and Savior? If you have not, or if you would like to rededicate your life to Him, pray this prayer aloud.

Pray aloud — Salvation & Rededication

Lord Jesus, I repent of my sins and surrender my life to You. Wash me clean. I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, that He died on the cross for my sins, and rose again on the third day for my victory. I believe in my heart and I confess with my mouth that Jesus is my Savior and my Lord. I receive eternal life in Jesus' name. Amen.

About

Pastor Kiky Ocansey & L137

Pastor Kiky Ocansey teaches with the L137 family, leading deliverance services on Clubhouse in which the whole room prays, confesses, forgives, receives the fire of the Holy Ghost, takes communion, and is sealed with the blood of Jesus.

L137 stands for Luke 1:37 — For with God nothing shall be impossible. That verse is the ministry's name, its tagline, and the benediction on every teaching.

Every L137 teaching is preserved as a permanent ministry resource: a teaching page, an interactive course, a downloadable pack of study materials, and — when a series completes — a published book. Member names, personal prophecies, individual ministry and personal testimonies are removed from every public resource to protect the privacy of everyone ministered to.
