Deliverance from Serpentine Spirits — Part Two

A biblical deliverance teaching by Pastor Kiky Ocansey continuing the war against serpentine spirits — the enmity declared in Eden, the venom of the snake bite, the fire of the Holy Ghost, and the anointing that breaks the ancestral serpent altar.

Genesis 3:15 (KJV)
And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel.

How this course works

  • Read in order. Each module follows the deliverance flow of the service — praise and worship, thanksgiving, confession of sins, forgiving others, inviting the Holy Spirit, the teaching, deliverance prayers and declarations, and sealing with the blood of Jesus.
  • Pray the prayers aloud. Every block marked PRAY ALOUD or DECLARE OUT LOUD is meant to be spoken with your voice. Silent prayer will not carry the same fire.
  • Keep the elements near you. Communion wine (or juice, or water when wine is not available) and anointing oil are used in Module 07 and Module 09. Prepare them before you begin.
  • Write in the journaling pages. Those notes will become your testimony.
  • Return to the Prayer Point Manual daily, morning and evening.
  • Finish each module's homework before you move to the next.

Thanksgiving Before the Warfare

Before we lift a sword, we lift our voice.

Memory verse — Psalm 95:2 (KJV)
O let us come before his presence with thanksgiving, and make a joyful noise unto him with psalms.

Beloved, Pastor Kiky did not open this service with a deliverance prayer. She opened it with thanksgiving. There was a reason. Serpentine spirits work in silence, in complaint, in murmuring, in the slow drift of a discouraged heart. Thanksgiving breaks that atmosphere in one instant. It changes the temperature in the room and in the soul.

Before a single unclean spirit was called out, the pastor gathered the assembly and told them, "Let us thank the Lord." Thanksgiving is not a formality. It is the first weapon in the deliverance flow — praise and worship, then thanksgiving, then confession of sins, then forgiveness, then invitation of the Holy Spirit, then the teaching, then the deliverance, then the sealing. Skip thanksgiving and you enter the warfare from the wrong door.

We thank Him first for who He is. Then for what He has done in Part One. Then for the very fact that we lived to gather again on Monday for Part Two. Then we thank Him in advance for every deliverance He is about to bring.

Module 02 of 9

Repentance, Forgiveness & Inviting the Holy Ghost Fire

The room must be swept clean before the fire can fall.

Memory verse — Acts 3:19 (KJV)
Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord.

Beloved, the second movement of the service was confession and forgiveness. Pastor Kiky said it plainly: you cannot invite the Holy Ghost fire into a room that is full of un-repented sin and un-forgiven offense. The fire will come — but you want the fire to burn the enemy, not the flesh that has agreed with him.

So we go low. We confess by name whatever the Holy Spirit brings up. We forgive by name whoever the Holy Spirit brings up. We release the wound. We release the debt. We release the memory. And then we invite the fire.

The Bible calls this the anointing that breaks the yoke. When the Holy Ghost fire descends on a repentant, forgiving heart, every serpent that is hiding inside a memory, an offense, a lust, a bitterness, a grudge, has nowhere left to hide. That is how Part Two moves so quickly into deliverance — because Part Two begins with a swept house.

The Enmity in Genesis — Where the Battle Was Declared

God Himself declared war on the serpent. You did not start this fight — but you will finish it.

Memory verse — Genesis 3:15 (KJV)
And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel.

Beloved, Pastor Kiky went back to the beginning. Before we can cast anything out we have to remember where the enmity began. It began in the garden. It began the moment the serpent opened his mouth to a woman.

Look at his method. He did not enter the garden with force. He entered with a question. "Yea, hath God said…?" He did not tell Eve a lie first — he asked her a question first. The serpent's oldest weapon is not violence, it is subtlety. He gets you to talk with him. He gets you to negotiate. He gets you to explain God's instructions in your own words until the instructions get smaller in your mouth than they were in God's mouth. And once you have shrunk the word of God in your own conversation, the lie comes easily: "Ye shall not surely die."

Then God spoke. Genesis 3:15 is not just a promise, it is a declaration of war. God Himself put the enmity there. You did not manufacture your hatred of the serpent — your Father in heaven planted it in you the day He judged the serpent in the garden. The seed of the woman was to bruise the head of the serpent. That seed is Christ, and every believer is in Christ. That is why you can cast out serpents in His name.

This is the doctrinal ground of every prayer that follows in this book. We are not fighting to gain victory — we are enforcing a victory that was declared in Genesis and finished at Calvary.

What a Snake Bite Does — Physically and Spiritually

The same venom that swells the body will swell the destiny.

Memory verse — Psalm 58:4 (KJV)
Their poison is like the poison of a serpent: they are like the deaf adder that stoppeth her ear.

Beloved, Pastor Kiky slowed the service down here for a reason. Before you can cast out something you must recognize its signature. So the pastor walked the room through what a physical snake bite does — and then showed the assembly that a serpentine spirit does the same thing in the spirit realm.

In the body, a snake bite produces:

  • puncture wounds where the venom entered
  • swelling and pain around the bite
  • blurred or dimmed vision
  • headache, dizziness, weakness
  • rising blood pressure, then falling blood pressure
  • difficulty breathing
  • nausea and vomiting
  • confusion of the mind
  • and, when unchecked, sudden death

In the spirit, the same venom produces:

  • financial weakness that will not lift
  • physical sickness with no clear medical cause
  • backwardness — you keep starting things you cannot finish
  • confusion and forgetfulness at the exact moment you need clarity
  • marital collapse, sudden separations, spirit-spouse encounters
  • wasted destiny — years of effort with no fruit to show for it
  • disappearance of helpers just before the breakthrough
  • chronic dream imagery of snakes: a snake escaping, a snake biting, a snake in the bed, a snake in the room, snake meat being eaten in the dream
  • and, when unchecked, sudden loss and untimely death

This is why Pastor Kiky refused to move on quickly. Many believers are diagnosed with the effects of serpentine attack for years without ever recognizing the source. The Lord wants you to recognize both.

How Serpentine Spirits Enter — Family, Foundation & Familiar Voices

What is welcomed in the father's house does not leave until the son commands it to go.

Memory verse — Exodus 20:5 (KJV)
Thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them: for I the LORD thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me.

Beloved, Pastor Kiky named the doors. She said we cannot cast out what we refuse to identify. The Holy Spirit will not fight over ground the believer has never taken back. So the pastor walked through the doorways that serpentine spirits use to enter a life.

Family doors. A grandfather who worshipped a serpent idol. A grandmother who kept a snake in a shrine. A father who consulted a diviner. A mother who wore a piece of serpent jewelry as a covenant sign. Any of these open a legal claim on the descendants unless the descendants rise up and shut the door in the name of Jesus.

Foundation doors. The village altar. The dedication that was made over you at birth without your knowledge. The naming ceremony that invoked a serpent. The libation that was poured over your infancy. Polygamous witchcraft — the extra wife who used a serpent spirit to divert the affection of a husband and everything he owned. The evil altar that was built in the family compound generations before you were born.

Personal doors. Dream sex — an intimate encounter in a dream that is not with a lawful spouse. Dream eating — being fed in a dream, especially meat or foods that appear tainted. Occult books, occult jewelry, occult imagery, occult movies watched with fascination. Persistent counsel taken from mediums, "prophets" outside of Christ, herbalists, and diviners.

Voice doors. The familiar voice that tells you what God did not say. The subtle question that shrinks the Word in your mouth. The counsel of the wicked that always seems to arrive right before the breakthrough.

Every one of these doors can be shut. That is the whole point of this chapter.

The Life Under Attack — Symptoms You Cannot Ignore

Do not baptize the symptoms of a serpent as the will of God.

Memory verse — Deuteronomy 28:13 (KJV)
And the LORD shall make thee the head, and not the tail; and thou shalt be above only, and thou shalt not be beneath; if that thou hearken unto the commandments of the LORD thy God, which I command thee this day, to observe and to do them.

Beloved, Pastor Kiky moved from cause to effect. She named — plainly and without softening — what a life under serpentine attack begins to look like. Some of these signs are subtle. Some are dramatic. All of them are meant to be answered by the Lord Jesus Christ, not accepted as your portion.

Signs to watch for:

  • Riches to rags. A steady descent from provision into lack that cannot be explained by ordinary circumstance.
  • Grace to grass. A season of clear favor that suddenly collapses into humiliation.
  • The tail, not the head. A pattern of always being the follower, always being overlooked, always being the last mentioned.
  • Missed favor. Doors close the moment you arrive. Interviews fall through. Opportunities disappear.
  • Wrong positioning. You keep ending up in rooms and roles that shrink you rather than lift you.
  • Chronic error. You do good work and one hidden mistake destroys the whole project. Again. And again.
  • Refusal of counsel. You suddenly cannot receive sound advice from the people God has placed around you.
  • Disappearing helpers. Every person the Lord raises up to help you drifts away just before the breakthrough.
  • Disappearing suitors. Serious relationships that reach the threshold of covenant and then unravel for reasons no one can name.
  • Spirit-spouse encounters. Dream intimacy, dream weddings, dream children, dream houses.
  • Juniors overtaking. Peers and juniors leaping ahead of you while your effort produces nothing.
  • Wasted destiny. Years of prayer, work, giving and sowing with no visible harvest.

These are not personality flaws. These are not the will of God for a redeemed life. They are the signature of an attack, and Jesus Christ has already crushed the head of the serpent.

Paul did not argue with the viper. He shook it off into the fire.

Memory verse — Acts 28:5 (KJV)
And he shook off the beast into the fire, and felt no harm.

Beloved, Pastor Kiky brought the assembly to the crescendo of the service. Communion had been taken. The blood was applied. The confessions had been made. Now — like Paul on the island of Melita — it was time to shake off the viper.

Read what happened. Paul had just survived a shipwreck. The barbarian people showed no little kindness. They kindled a fire. Paul, still in the mercy of God, gathered a bundle of sticks to help. A viper came out of the heat, fastened onto his hand, and hung there. The islanders were sure Paul was under judgment — a murderer whom vengeance had finally caught. Paul did not argue with them. Paul did not describe the viper. He shook the beast off into the fire and felt no harm.

There is your pattern. You do not negotiate with the viper. You do not sit with the viper. You do not describe the viper. You shake it off into the fire. The same Holy Ghost fire that Pentecost fell in is the fire that receives the viper.

The Anointing, the Breath of God & the New Beginning

What God breathes into you, no serpent can breathe out.

Memory verse — Genesis 2:7 (KJV)
And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.

Beloved, Pastor Kiky closed the service the way she opened it — gently. After the fire, the viper and the altar, the pastor called for the anointing oil. Then she called for seven rounds of breathing. Then she called for thanksgiving, then the seed, then the salvation prayer. This is the chapter of settling.

First the oil. Anoint yourself. Not as a magic charm — as a sign of consecration. The oil represents the Holy Spirit given to you for protection, for discernment, for favor, for direction. Anoint your forehead, your palms, your feet.

Then the breath. God formed man from the dust of the ground, but man became a living soul when God breathed into his nostrils the breath of life. That breath is the Holy Ghost. In this service the pastor led seven rounds — seven inhales of the Spirit, seven exhales of every unclean deposit. Follow along in your own room. Breathe in deeply through your nose. Hold. Then breathe out, and speak "Out" in the name of Jesus. Every venom, every poison, every python, every cobra, every deposit that your Father did not plant — out.

Then the thanksgiving. You did not deliver yourself. Thank Him for what He has done. Thank Him for what He is still doing. Thank Him for the deliverance He has already sealed in the heavenlies.

Then the seed. In the room, the pastor invited the assembly to sow a seed on the altar of deliverance — not as a payment, but as an act of agreement, a written surname placed on the altar, a name and a family committed into the hands of the Lord.

Then the salvation prayer. If you are not yet born again, or you want to rededicate your life, that is where the book ends. There is a Word of Salvation at the back of this book for you.
