The Vessel

Pastor Kiki Ocansey

Pastor Kiki Ocansey carries the L137 altar — a midnight house of warfare prayer, tongues of fire, and the uncompromised Word of God. Three nights a week, intercessors from the US, the UK, across Africa, Asia and Australia gather to watch, pray, and decree the impossible into being.

“For with God nothing shall be impossible.”

Luke 1:37 · KJV

The ministry exists to wake sleeping watchmen, break covenants of darkness, and release breakthrough by the blood of Jesus and the power of the Holy Spirit. If you carry a burden, an unanswered cry, or a fire that will not let you sleep — you have found your altar.
