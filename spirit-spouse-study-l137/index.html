Section 2

The Four Categories

Each category is color‑coded. Click a tab to open its full teaching card.

Marine / Water Spirits

Queen of the Coast · Leviathan · Lilith · Mermaid

Definition

Kingdom‑level spirits from the marine kingdom under the Queen of the Coast, Leviathan, and Lilith — taught by MFM (Olukoya), Daniel Okpara, and Kanaan Ministries.

Signature Dream Signals

  • Swimming in seas, rivers, beaches
  • Thrones set up in the sea
  • Eating in the marine kingdom
  • Dream pregnancy — spirit‑children
  • Sudden loss of undergarments

Waking Signs

  • Near‑success syndrome
  • Repeated marriage/financial collapse
  • Chronic heaviness & oppression
  • Cyclical sickness or loss

Open Doors

  • Riverine dedications
  • Ancestral covenants with water deities
  • Occult jewelry & sacrifices
  • Marine altars in the family line

Deliverance Emphasis

Aggressive prayer warfare. Speak to the waters and command them to receive the blood of Jesus. Break marine covenants. Destroy satanic gifts. Live holy and remain vigilant.

Incubus / Succubus

Spirit husband · Spirit wife · Mare · Lilith

Definition

Unclean spirits pursuing sexual union in sleep — incubus with women, succubus with men (Kanaan Ministries). Counterfeit covenants that steal, kill, and destroy God's design for intimacy.

Signature Dream Signals

  • Recurring sexual dreams with the same figure
  • Sleep paralysis with sexual pressure
  • Wedding dreams — rings and vows
  • Wet dreams followed by oppression

Waking Signs

  • Compulsive pornography / masturbation
  • Sudden disgust toward spouse
  • Extreme jealousy or sabotage
  • Fatigue & shame after "encounters"

Open Doors

  • Sexual sin & pornography
  • Ungodly soul ties, rings, gifts
  • Abuse & unhealed trauma
  • Occult sexual practices

Deliverance Emphasis

Repent, renounce spirit‑spouse by name, sanctify the bed with the blood of Jesus, command departure, fill with the Holy Spirit, purity aftercare.

Ancestral / Familiar Spirits

Family spirits · Monitoring spirits · Generational spirits

Definition

Biblical familiar spirit language (Lev. 19:31) applied by deliverance ministry to spirits that run along a bloodline, imitating ancestors and offering counterfeit revelation.

Signature Dream Signals

  • Dead relatives offering guidance
  • Being chased in dreams
  • Eating food in dreams
  • Marketplaces & unknown crowds

Waking Signs

  • Generational cycles — divorce, addiction, poverty
  • Sense of being watched or tracked
  • Blessings appear then get stolen
  • Coldness toward prayer & Word

Open Doors

  • Consulting mediums & the dead
  • Ancestral idol worship
  • Occult heirlooms & objects
  • Unrepented bitterness

Deliverance Emphasis

Repent · Renounce · Remove · Command · Replace · Resist. Cover the family line with the blood of Jesus (Exodus 20:5–6 with Ezekiel 18:20 for hope).

Masquerading Spirits

Impersonators · Angels of light · Unfriendly friends

Definition

Deceptive spirits that impersonate known persons — living or dead — or angels, to secure trust and plant covenants (Kevin Laewing; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15).

Signature Dream Signals

  • Faces barely shown; off‑character behavior
  • Distorted height/weight/wardrobe
  • Dead relatives "talking" or giving gifts
  • Dreams accusing a real friend or spouse

Waking Signs

  • Deliverance itself is attacked or reversed
  • Friends who turn out to be agents
  • Blessings held captive at the doorway
  • Recurring rejection & bitterness

Open Doors

  • Fear‑based agreement with dreams
  • Unresolved grief
  • Occult curiosity
  • Foundational curses

Deliverance Emphasis

Test every dream. Refuse fellowship with the deceased in dreams. Cancel every word the impersonator spoke. Never confront a real person on dream evidence — cover the relationship in prayer.

Section 4

Pastor Kiky's Core Anchors

The non‑negotiable pillars from her Clubhouse spirit‑spouse services. Keep every extended teaching aligned to these.

1

Blood of Jesus Homework

Revelation 12:11 with Hebrews 9:12–14 — the conscience‑cleansing frame after every service.

2

21‑Night Prophetic Direction

Anointing oil · communion · ring‑finger anointing · invisible‑ring renunciation · four prayer sections · night tracker.

3

Standard Deliverance Flow

Praise → Thanksgiving → Confession → Forgive → Invite Holy Spirit → Teaching → Deliverance → Seal with the blood.

4

Christ‑centered Boundaries

No New Age energy language. Demons oppress, hinder, deceive — they cannot own a believer's anointing.

5

Marriage & Safety

Prayer may renounce fear, control, coercion, bitterness — without declaring a legal marriage spiritually dissolved.

6

Do Not Diagnose from One Sign

Patterns matter. One dream, one temptation, or one sleep episode never proves attachment.

Section 5

The 9‑Step Deliverance Flow

Aligned with L137 order. Read aloud. Check each step as you complete it — progress saves on this device.

  1. Enter with singing. Establish God's throne before addressing any demon.

  2. Thank Him for the cross, the blood, the resurrection, the name of Jesus.

  3. 1 John 1:9. Name every open door specifically.

  4. Mark 11:25. Unforgiveness is a wide‑open door.

  5. John 16:13. Ask Him to expose every covenant, altar, and mark.

  6. Colossians 2:14–15. Renounce by name — marine covenants, spirit‑spouse, familiar spirits, masquerading spirits, rings, garments, certificates in darkness.

  7. James 4:7; Isaiah 49:24–26. Loose from remote‑control devices, identification marks, chains, altars.

  8. Luke 11:24–26. Do not skip. Fill body, mind, dreams, sleep, marriage, womb, future.

  9. Revelation 12:11. Cover self, spouse, children, home, dreams. It is finished.
