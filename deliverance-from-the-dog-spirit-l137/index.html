Foundation

What is the dog spirit?

In Scripture the word “dog” is never a compliment when applied to a person. It is used to describe those living outside God’s covenant order, marked by uncleanness, shamelessness, sexual immorality, greedy appetite, false teaching, and hostility toward God’s people. A “dog spirit” is an unclean spirit that pushes a person to live and behave in exactly that way — often hidden, but exposed under the light of Christ.

Simple Explanation

A dog spirit is an unclean demonic influence that drives shameless, immoral, greedy, or hostile behaviour that God’s Word calls the behaviour of a dog. It attaches through sexual sin, occult covenants, generational lines, marine altars, familiar-spirit encounters, and dreams — and it is broken by the finished work of Jesus Christ.

Adult / Deeper Study

Under the Old Covenant, the wages of a “dog” were forbidden in the house of God (Deuteronomy 23:18) — the Hebrew idiom pointed to male cult prostitution attached to pagan altars. Paul warns the church in Philippi to “beware of dogs” (Philippians 3:2), meaning evil workers who corrupt sound doctrine. In Revelation 22:15 “dogs” are listed among sorcerers, whoremongers, murderers, idolaters, and every one that loveth and maketh a lie — those shut out of the New Jerusalem. Read together, the dog spirit operates on three fronts: sexual defilement, false doctrine, and shameless rebellion. Deliverance addresses all three.

Discernment

How the Dog Spirit Manifests

Manifestations are not the person — they are the exposure of an unclean spirit under the authority of Jesus Christ. The list below is a discernment guide, never a diagnosis. When any of these appear during deliverance, calmly proceed with the prayer order at the bottom of this page.

🗣️

Physical & vocal

  • Barking in different tones during prayer
  • Growling, snarling, or panting
  • Snapping the jaw, biting the air
  • Sudden loss of voice when Scripture is spoken
  • Bending low, crawling, or refusing to stand
  • Tongue hanging out uncontrollably
  • Sudden foul breath during deliverance
🌙

Dream life

  • Rabid or mad dogs chasing the dreamer
  • Being bitten by a dog
  • Owning, feeding, or being licked by a strange dog
  • A person you know turning into a dog
  • Dogs guarding a gate that will not let you pass
  • Being carried by dogs to a cliff, water, or forest
  • Waking with scratches, wetness, or a smell of dog
💔

Sexual & moral

  • Uncontrollable pornography or fornication cycles
  • Attraction to perverse or forbidden partners
  • Repeated adultery despite deep repentance
  • Shameless language and behaviour in private
  • Nocturnal emissions after dog dreams
  • Feeling “married” to an unseen partner
💵

Finances & calling

  • Repeated job loss just before promotion
  • Money leaking out with no explanation
  • Business contracts cancelled at the last moment
  • Ministry gifts stolen, mocked, or shut down
  • “Friendly” people attached to every financial setback
🩺

Body & health

  • Unexplained bites, welts, or scratches
  • Chronic infections in reproductive or urinary system
  • Insomnia beginning at 12–3 a.m.
  • Anxiety attacks when praying alone
  • Foul-smelling discharges without medical cause

If any physical symptom is present, seek qualified medical care alongside prayer. Deliverance and medicine are not enemies.

👥

Relationships

  • Cannot keep godly friendships — always driven away
  • Attracted to controlling or perverse partners
  • Cannot sit under sound teaching without agitation
  • Sudden hatred for those who correct you in love
  • Circle of “friends” who mock holiness
📚

Worship & doctrine

  • Falling asleep the moment the Bible is opened
  • Attraction to false teachers and mocking sound doctrine
  • Inability to receive correction from pastors
  • “Watchmen who cannot bark” — silent when sin appears (Isaiah 56:10)
  • Returning to the same sin like a dog to its vomit (2 Peter 2:22)
🧬

Generational & covenants

  • Family history of adultery, prostitution, or perversion
  • Ancestors involved in shrines, marine altars, or animal sacrifice
  • Dedication rituals over infants involving animals
  • Family “totem” or “clan animal” that is a dog
  • Names in the family line meaning “dog” or given as pets to a shrine
Teaching Modules

The Six-Module Journey

Move through the modules in order. Each one carries Scripture, a simple explanation, a knowledge check, a prayer, and a declaration.

Module 1

Discerning the Dog Spirit

Scripture: Philippians 3:2 · Matthew 7:6

Learn to name what Scripture names. A dog spirit is exposed by three fruits: sexual defilement, false doctrine, and shameless rebellion. Ask the Holy Spirit for discernment, not suspicion.

Knowledge Check — click to answer

Q: In Philippians 3:2, who does Paul call “dogs”?

A. Gentiles · B. Evil workers who corrupt sound doctrine · C. Roman soldiers

Reveal answer

Answer: B. Paul warns the Philippian church against evil workers — false teachers who insist on fleshly ordinances and pull believers away from Christ.

Module 2

Roots and Entry Points

Scripture: Deuteronomy 23:18 · Revelation 22:15

The dog spirit enters through sexual sin, occult covenants, generational altars, defiled food/gifts, and voluntary friendship with mockers. Every entry point must be renounced by name.

Knowledge Check

Q: Deuteronomy 23:18 forbids what from being brought into God’s house?

A. Any offering · B. The hire of a whore and the price of a dog · C. Foreign silver

Reveal answer

Answer: B. Both were abomination to the LORD — money earned through cult prostitution and shrine service.

Module 3

Dreams, Dogs, and Marine Covenants

Scripture: Psalm 22:20 · Job 33:15-16

Dog dreams are often signals — chases, bites, feeding, licking, or being carried by a dog can point to spirit-spouse or marine covenants operating in the night. Record the dream, judge it by Scripture, and bring it under the blood of Jesus in prayer.

Knowledge Check

Q: David in Psalm 22:20 prays God would deliver his soul from what?

A. His enemies at court · B. The sword and the power of the dog · C. Famine

Reveal answer

Answer: B. A prophetic prayer that Christ Himself later fulfils at the cross.

Module 4

Breaking Covenants and Altars

Scripture: Colossians 2:14-15 · Isaiah 28:18

Jesus disarmed principalities and powers at the cross and made an open show of them. Every covenant with the dog spirit — spoken, unspoken, generational, personal — is annulled through repentance, renunciation, and the blood of Jesus.

Knowledge Check

Q: According to Colossians 2:15, what did Christ do to principalities and powers?

A. He negotiated with them · B. He spoiled them and made an open show of them · C. He left them for the church to handle

Reveal answer

Answer: B. He stripped them and displayed His victory publicly.

Module 5

Casting Out and Sealing

Scripture: Mark 16:17 · Luke 10:19

In the name of Jesus, dog spirits are commanded out. Immediately seal the delivered ground with the blood of Jesus and the infilling of the Holy Spirit so the vacated place is not left empty (Matthew 12:43-45).

Knowledge Check

Q: Why is sealing after deliverance necessary?

A. To pay for the deliverance · B. So the vacated place is not left empty (Matt 12:43-45) · C. It is not necessary

Reveal answer

Answer: B. Jesus warned that an empty house invites seven worse spirits — the delivered life must be filled with the Holy Spirit and the Word.

Module 6

Aftercare and Walking Free

Scripture: 2 Peter 2:22 · Romans 12:1-2

Do not return to your vomit. Aftercare is renewing the mind, cutting off defiled friendships, disciplined worship, daily Bible reading, fellowship with sound believers, and seeking pastoral and medical support when needed.

Knowledge Check

Q: Romans 12:2 tells the believer to be transformed by what?

A. Zeal and effort · B. The renewing of the mind · C. Religious tradition

Reveal answer

Answer: B. Deliverance holds where the mind is renewed daily by the Word of God.

Prayer Point Manual

Deliverance Prayers — Pray Aloud

Follow the L137 deliverance flow in order. Do not skip a section. Pray aloud, in the name of Jesus.

1 Praise› 2 Thanksgiving› 3 Confession› 4 Forgive others› 5 Invite Holy Spirit› 6 Teaching› 7 Deliverance› 8 Sealing
1 · Praise and Worship

“I bless You Lord Jesus, King of glory, Lion of Judah, Deliverer of the captives. You are worthy of all praise. I lift Your name above every unclean name, above every dog spirit, above every altar of shame. Hallelujah to the Lamb of God.”

2 · Thanksgiving

“Thank You Jesus for the blood that was shed for me. Thank You that You have already spoiled principalities and powers on the cross. Thank You for the authority You have given me in Your name. Thank You for this hour of deliverance.”

3 · Confession of Sins
  • Father, I confess every sexual sin — in thought, word, image, and deed — known and unknown. Wash me in the blood of Jesus.
  • I confess every friendship, entertainment, and doctrine that fed the dog spirit in my life. I repent.
  • I confess the sins of my ancestors — altars, shrines, animal covenants, sexual immorality, prostitution, and every dedication to unclean powers.
4 · Forgive Others

“Father, I forgive every person who defiled me, seduced me, mocked me, or introduced me to unclean things. I release them. I remove myself from the courtroom of unforgiveness in the name of Jesus.”

5 · Invite the Holy Spirit

“Holy Spirit, come. Take Your rightful place in my mind, my body, my dreams, my finances, my ministry, and my home. Lead me through this deliverance. Show me every hidden thing.”

6 · Renunciation
  • I renounce every covenant with the dog spirit — spoken, unspoken, generational, or personal — in the name of Jesus.
  • I renounce every marine altar, spirit-spouse arrangement, and night-hunt covenant carried on the back of a dog spirit.
  • I renounce every friendship with mockers of holiness that opened a door to the dog spirit.
  • I renounce every dream contract signed while eating, drinking, or lying with unclean things.
  • I renounce every generational name, totem, and dedication that identifies me with a dog.
7 · Breaking Covenants and Altars
  • By the blood of Jesus, I break every dog-spirit covenant operating in my life — root, branch, and seed.
  • I break every altar of adultery, perversion, shamelessness, and shrine-service raised for me or against me.
  • Every legal ground the dog spirit has claimed in my life, I revoke it by the finished work of Christ (Colossians 2:14-15).
  • Every dog-spirit chain on my finances, my calling, my ministry, and my marriage — I break you now in the name of Jesus.
8 · Casting Out
  • You dog spirit — every barking, growling, biting, panting spirit — I command you: come out of my body, my mind, my dreams, and my bloodline, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every spirit of adultery, fornication, perversion, pornography, and shamelessness attached to the dog spirit, come out now.
  • Every night-visitor, spirit-spouse, or dream partner riding on a dog spirit, I evict you. Get out.
  • Every dumb dog spirit shutting my mouth from prayer, prophecy, and testimony — go, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every greedy dog spirit devouring my finances and ministry — release everything you have stolen and go.
9 · Healing and Restoration
  • Father, heal every wound the dog spirit has left in my body, my mind, and my memories.
  • Restore what has been stolen — years, opportunities, virginity of spirit, ministry, marriage, finances (Joel 2:25).
  • Rebuild me in holiness and in the fear of the Lord.
10 · Sealing with the Blood of Jesus
  • I seal every delivered part of my life with the blood of Jesus.
  • Holy Spirit, fill every place these spirits have vacated. Leave no empty room.
  • I set a watch of angels over my dreams, my home, my marriage, my finances, and my ministry (Psalm 34:7).
  • No returning dog spirit shall find a place in me — I belong to Jesus Christ. Amen.
Declare It

Fiery Declarations

Say each one aloud, boldly, in the name of Jesus.

  1. I am not a dog. I am a child of the living God, redeemed by the blood of the Lamb.
  2. The dog spirit has no legal ground in me. Every covenant is annulled by the cross of Christ.
  3. I command every barking, biting, growling spirit to be silent and to leave me forever.
  4. My dreams are cleansed. No dog shall chase me, feed me, or lick me in the night.
  5. My body is the temple of the Holy Spirit. No unclean spirit shall inhabit this temple.
  6. My marriage bed is undefiled. No dog spirit shall lie between me and my spouse.
  7. My mouth is a watchman that barks — I am not a dumb dog. I will speak truth boldly.
  8. My finances are covered by the blood of Jesus. No greedy dog spirit shall devour them.
  9. My ministry gift is protected. No dog shall trample the pearls God has given me.
  10. I do not return to my vomit. I go forward in holiness and in the fear of the Lord.
  11. The Lord is my shepherd — I follow His voice only. Every strange voice, I refuse.
  12. For with God nothing shall be impossible. L137. In Jesus’ name. Amen.
Test It

Final Quiz — Reveal Only After You Answer

1. Which verse warns the church to “beware of dogs, beware of evil workers”?

A. Romans 3:2 · B. Philippians 3:2 · C. Galatians 3:2

Reveal answer

B — Philippians 3:2. Paul warns the church against false teachers.

2. What three fruits reveal the dog spirit at work?

A. Wealth, fame, and power · B. Sexual defilement, false doctrine, shameless rebellion · C. Tradition, ritual, and law

Reveal answer

B. Drawn from Deuteronomy 23:18, Philippians 3:2, and Revelation 22:15.

3. Why does Jesus warn about an empty house in Matthew 12:43-45?

A. Because deliverance is dangerous · B. Because a vacated place must be filled with the Holy Spirit, or seven worse spirits will enter · C. Because it is not real

Reveal answer

B. Sealing and Holy Spirit infilling are non-negotiable after deliverance.

4. What is 2 Peter 2:22 warning delivered believers not to do?

A. Not to return to their vomit / folly · B. Not to fast · C. Not to pray

Reveal answer

A. Aftercare and mind-renewal keep the deliverance in place.

5. What does L137 stand for?

A. Luke 13:7 · B. Luke 1:37 — For with God nothing shall be impossible · C. Leviticus 1:37

Reveal answer

B. Luke 1:37 — the ministry’s anchor Scripture.

Aftercare

Walking Free — The 7-Day Reset

Day 1

Bible reading: Psalm 22 in full. Communion. Journal one sentence: “I belong to Jesus.”

Day 2

Bible reading: Philippians 3. Fast one meal. Pray the sealing prayer aloud.

Day 3

Bible reading: Romans 12. Cut off one defiled friendship or media source.

Day 4

Bible reading: 2 Peter 2. Confess to a trusted pastor if hidden sin remains.

Day 5

Bible reading: Ephesians 6. Put on the full armour. Anoint your door posts (Christ is the door).

Day 6

Bible reading: Revelation 22. Meditate on the promise for the pure in Christ.

Day 7

Bible reading: Luke 1:37. Testify to one person that Jesus has delivered you. Rest in Him.

When to Seek More Help

If manifestations return, or if there is any physical symptom (bites, infections, insomnia, anxiety), continue prayer and seek qualified pastoral counsel through your ministry, and qualified medical care from your doctor. Deliverance and clinical care are not enemies.
