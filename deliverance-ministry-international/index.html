About the Ministry

L137 Deliverance Ministry International

with Pastor Kiki Ocansey

L137 Deliverance Ministry International is a house of warfare prayer and prophetic teaching, founded by Pastor Kiki Ocansey on the promise of Luke 1:37 — that with God, nothing shall be impossible. Under her leadership, the ministry gathers the family on the prayer line every Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday at midnight GMT (7 PM EST / 6 PM CST) to contend for breakthrough, healing, and freedom in the name of Jesus.

Pastor Kiki Ocansey, founder of L137 Deliverance Ministry International, carries a burden for deliverance and for equipping believers to stand in their God-given authority. Every teaching is preserved and made freely available — workbooks, recall sheets, prophetic directions, declarations cards, and clean transcripts — so the word can be carried home, studied, and shared.

Dominion Hour

with Prophet Prince Amo David

A fire-branded prophetic voice called by God to teach, equip, and empower believers to walk in spiritual authority, dominion, and victory through Jesus Christ. With over two decades of ministry, he carries a distinct prophetic and teaching grace that releases deliverance, revelation, and the uncompromised Word of God with clarity and power.

Through sound doctrine, prophetic insight, and strategic warfare prayer, his ministry brings transformation, restoration, and spiritual growth to individuals and communities — establishing believers in the grace of God, the love of Christ, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit.

Prophet Prince Amo David hosts services directly with Pastor Kiki Ocansey on the L137 Deliverance Ministry International prayer line.
