Our Promise to You

Privacy & Safety

Last updated: June 7, 2026

The short version: Your prayer request stays between you and the prayer team. We do not sell your information, we do not share your name or your story without your permission, and we do everything we reasonably can to keep this website safe for you and your family to use.

1. What we collect, and why

Prayer requests

When you submit a prayer request, we collect:

  • The name you give us (you can simply write "Anonymous")
  • Your email address (so we can reply if needed)
  • The prayer request you write

These go straight to Pastor Kiki Ocansey and the L137 prayer team inbox. Nothing is posted publicly on the site without your explicit permission.

What we do not collect

  • No passwords (the site has no login)
  • No payment card numbers (giving is handled by trusted, secure processors)
  • No location tracking
  • No advertising trackers or third-party marketing cookies

2. How your information is protected

  • Encrypted in transit. Every connection to this website uses HTTPS encryption, so what you submit cannot be read in transit.
  • Bot protection. Our prayer form includes invisible spam protection to keep trolls and automated bots from flooding the prayer line.
  • Limited access. Only Pastor Kiki Ocansey and a small trusted prayer team can read incoming requests.
  • No public wall by default. Prayer requests do not appear on the website unless you specifically ask us to share them.

3. Safe browsing & downloads

  • All teaching materials (workbooks, recall sheets, declarations cards, transcripts) are standard PDF files, hosted on secure storage. They contain no scripts, no trackers, and no executable code.
  • The website is served from hardened, professionally managed infrastructure with automatic backups and isolated runtime environments.
  • You may freely download, print, and share every teaching pack with your family, your prayer group, and your local church.

4. Children's privacy

This is a public ministry site, and anyone is welcome to read teachings or watch services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has submitted information, please email us at l137prayerline@gmail.com and we will delete it.

5. Your rights

  • Right to be forgotten. Ask us to delete your prayer request and email at any time and we will do so.
  • Right to a copy. Ask us what information we hold about you and we will send it to you.
  • Right to be left alone. We will never sell, rent, trade, or share your information with marketers.

To exercise any of these rights, email l137prayerline@gmail.com.

6. Community conduct

We ask everyone who connects with this ministry — online, on Clubhouse, on WhatsApp, or in person — to treat one another with the love of Christ:

  • No harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks toward Pastor Kiki, the prayer team, or any member of the ministry family.
  • No impersonating Pastor Kiki Ocansey or the ministry on any platform.
  • No spam, scam links, or solicitations through the prayer form or the comments.
  • Disagreement is welcome; disrespect is not.

Anyone who repeatedly violates this standard will be blocked from submission forms and removed from ministry channels.

7. Reporting a problem

If you receive a suspicious email claiming to be from the ministry, see content on this site that looks broken or wrong, or experience anything that feels unsafe, please tell us right away at l137prayerline@gmail.com. We take every report seriously.

8. Terms of use (the short version)

  • Everything on this site is provided freely for personal study, prayer, and ministry. You are welcome to print and share the workbooks and teaching packs.
  • Teachings reflect the convictions of Pastor Kiki Ocansey and L137 Deliverance Ministry International. They are offered as spiritual encouragement and are not a substitute for medical, legal, financial, or mental health advice. If you are in crisis, please contact a qualified professional or emergency services.
  • This site is provided "as is." We work hard to keep it accurate and available, but we cannot guarantee it will be free from every error or interruption.

9. Changes to this page

If anything important changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be announced on the homepage so the family knows.
