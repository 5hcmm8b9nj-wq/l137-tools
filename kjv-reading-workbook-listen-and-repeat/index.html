How to Use This Workbook

Seven simple steps for every lesson.

1 · Sound Warm-Up

Tap five short Bible words. Hear each one. Say it back out loud. This warms up your voice.

2 · New Words Today

Tap each new word to hear it. Read the little meaning under it. Say the word slowly. Then say it a bit faster.

3 · Listen to the Verse

Press Play Verse. The workbook reads the whole verse to you. Just listen. Do not try to read yet.

4 · Read With Me

Now tap any word in the verse. It will speak. Say it after the voice. Take your time — one word at a time.

5 · Read Alone

Try to read the verse without help. If you get stuck on a word, tap it. That is not cheating — that is learning.

6 · Say the Declaration

Tap each line. Hear it. Then say it out loud — as a declaration over your own life. Your tongue must agree with God's Word.

7 · Remember Check & Prayer

Answer three easy questions to remember today's Word. Then say the closing prayer. Mark the lesson complete.

TIP

Slow down the voice if you need to

On every lesson page there is a Voice Speed slider. If the voice is going too fast, slide it to the left. If it is too slow, slide it to the right.
