Lesson

— King James Version

Step 1

Sound Warm-Up

Tap each word to hear it. Say it out loud after the voice.

Step 2

New Words Today

Tap each new word. Hear it. Read the meaning under it. Say it back.

Step 3 & 4

Listen to the Verse. Then Read With Me.

Press Play Verse and just listen. Then tap any word to hear it again.

Step 5

Read Alone

Now try to read the verse yourself, out loud. If you get stuck on a word, go back to Step 3 and tap it. That is not cheating — that is how you learn.

SAY IT OUT LOUD

Step 6

Say the Declaration

Repeat after me. Tap each line to hear it, then speak it out loud over your own life.

Step 7

Remember Check

Three easy questions to remember today's Word. Tap the answer you think is right.

SEAL THE LESSON

Closing Prayer
