Module 1

Recognise the Marine Spirit

Marine spirits are unclean spirits that operate out of waters — seas, rivers, streams. In the Bible, the great whore of Revelation 17 sits upon many waters. These spirits attack marriages, wombs, finances, and destinies. Jesus is stronger, and He draws His people out of the water.

The Bible uses water in two ways. Living water is a picture of the Holy Spirit (John 7:38–39). But there are also unclean waters — the "many waters" of Revelation 17 where a demonic queen sits, the "great waters" of Psalm 144, the "deep" that Jonah went down to. Marine spirits operate out of those places against people's lives.

Marine and water spirits are demonic powers whose operations are pictured in Scripture as coming out of waters. Revelation 17:1 shows a great whore that sitteth upon many waters. Psalm 18:16 shows the Lord's people drawn out of many waters. Psalm 144:7 asks God to deliver from great waters, from the hand of strange children. Isaiah 27:1 describes leviathan the crooked serpent in the sea. These spirits gain access through inherited water covenants, water dedications, sexual sin with those under marine influence, and rituals performed with river water, sea water, or well water.

Their operations include marine crowns over the glory (over destiny and calling), spirit-spouse marriages that produce dreams of sex, unknown weddings and swimming, marine serpents in the reproductive area causing barrenness and miscarriage, and destinies held down in the deep — held-back finances, held-back children, held-back callings.

Water in the biblical imagination carries two symbolic loads. In Genesis 1:2 the Spirit of God moves upon the face of the waters — the deep is the primordial place from which God calls forth order. In Genesis 7 the same deep, released, becomes the flood of judgment. Job 41 describes leviathan in the deep. Isaiah 27:1 calls leviathan "the piercing serpent, even leviathan that crooked serpent," and says the Lord will slay the dragon that is in the sea. Revelation 17 finally locates a demonic religious system upon many waters, and Revelation 21:1 concludes with a new earth where "there was no more sea" — the last hiding place of the enemy removed. Marine deliverance is therefore not folk religion; it stands on this biblical thread from Genesis to Revelation.

Revelation 17:1–2 KJV
And there came one of the seven angels which had the seven vials, and talked with me, saying unto me, Come hither; I will shew unto thee the judgment of the great whore that sitteth upon many waters: With whom the kings of the earth have committed fornication, and the inhabitants of the earth have been made drunk with the wine of her fornication.
Isaiah 27:1 KJV
In that day the LORD with his sore and great sword shall punish leviathan the piercing serpent, even leviathan that crooked serpent; and he shall slay the dragon that is in the sea.
Psalm 74:13–14 KJV
Thou didst divide the sea by thy strength: thou brakest the heads of the dragons in the waters. Thou brakest the heads of leviathan in pieces, and gavest him to be meat to the people inhabiting the wilderness.
🔑 Key point

The marine spirit is real, its access is legal, and the blood of Jesus cancels the deed. This is not superstition — Isaiah 27:1 promises the Lord Himself slays the dragon that is in the sea.

Common signs of marine attack

  • Recurring dreams of swimming, being in water, drowning, or crossing rivers
  • Dreams of sexual activity with familiar or unknown partners
  • Dreams of unknown weddings, ceremonies, or being dressed in strange clothes
  • Persistent delay in marriage, or repeated broken engagements
  • Miscarriage patterns, barrenness, or reproductive disorders with no clear medical cause
  • Repeated financial reversals when breakthrough is near
  • Attraction to marine imagery — mermaids, mermaid tattoos, seashell collections tied to spiritual meaning
  • Dreams of eating in the water or receiving food from unknown people

None of these on their own prove marine bondage. But a pattern, prayed over, submitted to Scripture, and tested by the Holy Spirit, may indicate it.

Which Scripture explicitly promises that the Lord will slay the dragon that is in the sea?
Isaiah 27:1 KJV — "In that day the LORD with his sore and great sword shall punish leviathan the piercing serpent, even leviathan that crooked serpent; and he shall slay the dragon that is in the sea." This verse is a direct promise the Lord Himself will destroy the marine dragon.
Module 2

Legal Rights — Where the Marine Spirit Gains Access

Demons do not operate by feeling — they operate by legal right. Colossians 2:14 shows the enemy holds handwriting of ordinances against people. To be free, you close the doors.

Colossians 2:14 KJV
Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross.

How marine spirits gain access

  • Inherited water covenants — parents, grandparents, or ancestors who worshipped a river, a sea, a mermaid deity, or a "queen of the coast"
  • Water dedication at birth — the child bathed with, or dedicated in, ritual water
  • Sexual covenants — sexual union with someone under marine influence (1 Corinthians 6:16 — "he which is joined to an harlot is one body")
  • Marine imagery kept in the home — objects consecrated to water deities, "mermaid" imagery kept for spiritual meaning
  • Rituals with river / sea water — bathing in a "spiritual bath" at a shrine, drinking prescribed water from a diviner
  • Personal vows made at the water — vows made at a beach, at a river, at a well, "if you give me this I will give you that"
  • Unforgiveness and pride — these two doors are named repeatedly by Pastor Kiky because they hold every deliverance back
📖 Read it

1 Corinthians 6:16 KJV — "What? know ye not that he which is joined to an harlot is one body? for two, saith he, shall be one flesh." Sexual sin transfers spirits. Repentance and renunciation cancel the transfer.

Confession & Repentance
Father, in the name of Jesus, I confess and repent for every door I have personally opened to marine spirits — every sexual sin, every vow I made at water, every ritual I permitted, every object I kept. I repent for the sins of my fathers and my mothers that opened this door in my bloodline. Wash me with the blood of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. Amen.
Renunciation
In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I renounce every marine covenant, every water covenant, every river dedication, every sea dedication, every mermaid or "queen of the coast" dedication ever made over me, my parents, my grandparents, or my bloodline — knowingly or unknowingly. I cancel every handwriting of ordinances that was against me, for it is nailed to the cross of Jesus. Amen.
What is the primary way marine spirits gain access to a life?
Demons operate by legal right (Colossians 2:14). Marine spirits enter through inherited water covenants, ancestral dedications, sexual covenants, ritual bathing, vows made at water, and unrepented sin. The blood of Jesus cancels every handwriting when we confess and renounce.

Prayer 1 · Marine crowns
Marine crowns upon my glory, catch fire, in the name of Jesus. Every crown placed on my head in the water — every mermaid crown, every queen-of-the-coast crown, every water-priest crown — be burnt to ashes by the fire of God. I am not a servant of the water; I am a servant of the Lamb. In the name of Jesus. Amen.
Prayer 2 · Marine serpents in the body
Every marine serpent in my body — in my reproductive area, in my digestive tract, in my head, in my throat, in my womb — come out now by the blood of Jesus. Every marine deposit in my system, be vomited out. Every marine altar built inside me, be dismantled by the fire of God. In the name of Jesus. Amen.
Prayer 3 · Spirit spouse (divorce)
Every spirit spouse assigned to my life, the fire of God separates us tonight. I divorce you by the blood of the everlasting covenant. I return every ring, every gift, every dowry paid in the spirit. I burn every wedding record of ours in the fire of God. Every dream of sex, every dream of eating, every dream of swimming, every dream of unknown weddings — I reject you and I burn you in the name of Jesus. Amen.
Prayer 4 · Break marine altars
Every marine altar my ancestors built that is still speaking against me, be dismantled by the blood of Jesus. Every priest at that altar, be silenced by the fire of God. Every sacrifice on that altar, be nullified by the blood of Jesus. I am under a better covenant — Hebrews 8:6. Amen.
Prayer 5 · Recover held-down destiny
Lord Jesus, You drew Peter out of the water and You draw me out tonight. Every destiny of mine held in the deep — restore it by the blood of Jesus. Every child of mine held in the deep — release them by the blood of Jesus. Every finance, every business, every marriage, every calling held under water — surface by the fire of God, in the name of Jesus. Amen.
Prayer 6 · Break the pattern in the bloodline
Every marine pattern of miscarriage, barrenness, delayed marriage, sudden divorce, financial collapse, or untimely death in my bloodline — I break you now by the blood of Jesus. You will not repeat in me, in my children, or in my children's children. Ezekiel 18:20 — the soul that sinneth, it shall die. My ancestors' iniquity does not stand on me anymore. Amen.
According to Psalm 18:16, where did the Lord draw the psalmist from?
Psalm 18:16 KJV — "He sent from above, he took me, he drew me out of many waters." This verse is a promise the marine believer prays back to God — the same Lord who drew David draws you.

Module 6

Sealing & Aftercare

After every deliverance the door must be closed and the room filled — otherwise Matthew 12 warns the last state will be worse than the first.

Matthew 12:43–45 KJV
When the unclean spirit is gone out of a man, he walketh through dry places, seeking rest, and findeth none. Then he saith, I will return into my house from whence I came out; and when he is come, he findeth it empty, swept, and garnished. Then goeth he, and taketh with himself seven other spirits more wicked than himself, and they enter in and dwell there: and the last state of that man is worse than the first.
Exodus 12:13 KJV
And the blood shall be to you for a token upon the houses where ye are: and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you, when I smite the land of Egypt.
Sealing prayer
Father, in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I seal every deliverance received tonight with the blood of Jesus. I close every window every marine spirit climbed through. I close every gate the water used. I close every dream door. I close every reproductive door. The blood of Jesus is on the doorpost of my body, my marriage, my womb, my children, my finances, and my destiny. When You see the blood, You pass over me, and the destroyer cannot enter. Amen.
Filling prayer
Holy Spirit, fill every empty room the deliverance left behind. Fill me with the Word, with worship, with holiness, with the fear of the Lord. Let my house not be empty, swept and garnished — let it be full of You. Amen.
Aftercare · the next 7 days

Read the Word daily (start with Psalm 18 and Psalm 74). Pray morning and night. Take communion at least once this week and plead the better covenant. Return to a Bible-teaching church and to your prayer group. Cut off the media, friendships and places that were the hedge-breakers. Bring persistent physical or emotional symptoms to a trusted physician alongside prayer — both are gifts from the Father of lights (James 1:17).

Module 7

Final Review & Completion

Answer the four questions below to complete the course. You may reopen this study any time.

Isaiah 27:1 KJV — the Lord Himself, with His sore and great sword, shall punish leviathan and slay the dragon that is in the sea. This is the direct scriptural promise for marine deliverance.
How do marine spirits gain legal access?
Demons operate by legal right (Colossians 2:14). Marine spirits enter through inherited water covenants, ancestral dedications, sexual covenants, ritual bathing, vows made at water, and unrepented sin. The blood of Jesus cancels every handwriting.
What must follow deliverance so the last state is not worse than the first?
Matthew 12:43–45 warns that an empty, swept, garnished house invites seven worse spirits. Seal with the blood of Jesus, fill with the Holy Spirit, walk out the 7-day aftercare. No salt baths — that is not a biblical instruction from L137.
Which verse do believers overcome the enemy by?
Revelation 12:11 KJV — "And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death." Both the blood AND the spoken word.
