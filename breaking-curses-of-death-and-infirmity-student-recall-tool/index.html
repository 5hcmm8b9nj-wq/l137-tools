How to use this tool. Work through each mode at your own pace. Your score and progress are saved on this device automatically, so you can close the page and return later. Every scripture is quoted in full from the King James Version. Start with The Teaching, then work through the recall modes. Complete all nine study modes to unlock your certificate.

Your Progress

Choose a Study Mode

Learn the teaching first, then eight ways to recall, apply, and hold on to it.

The Teaching

The full teaching, lesson by lesson, in the order it was delivered. Move through one panel at a time. Every scripture is written out in full.

Quick Quiz Recall

Thirty questions drawn from the teaching and the scriptures it stands on. Choose an answer to see why it is right.

Scripture Completion

Fill in the missing words. Spelling is forgiving, capitalisation does not matter. The full KJV verse is shown after you check.

Matching Game

Tap one item on the left, then its partner on the right. Four sets: scriptures, terms, Bible figures, and prayer categories.

Sequence Challenge

Put each order back the way it was taught. Use the arrows to move an item up or down, then check your order.

True · False · Not Stated

The hardest mode. Some statements are true, some contradict Scripture, and some were simply never taught. Do not add to the teaching.

Scenario Cards

Real situations. Apply the teaching and the Word, not instinct.

Flashcards

Tap the card to turn it over. Mark whether you knew it, and the ones you missed will come round again.

Declaration Practice

Speak each declaration aloud, exactly as it was released in the service, then tick it off. Repeat after me — say them out loud, not silently.

Scripture Library

Every passage behind this teaching, in full King James Version, grouped by theme. Search by word or reference.

Certificate of Completion

Complete the teaching and all eight recall modes to unlock and print your certificate.
