01

Opening Scripture — Isaiah 9:6-7

Tonight, we receive victory over every battle in our life and in our destiny by the power of the Holy Spirit.
As we are fasting and we are praying, beloved, we pray for victory — in the name of Jesus, over everything that we placed on the altar of God.
Let victory enter our homes, our houses. Let victory enter our finances. Let victory enter by the power of the Holy Ghost in the mighty name of Jesus. We need victory in Jesus Christ. Victory in Jesus Christ.
The Word of the Lord says in Isaiah chapter nine, verse six to seven. Isaiah chapter nine, open to that scripture for me. Isaiah chapter nine, beloved, verse six to seven. As we read the scripture, Isaiah chapter nine verse six to seven — and the word of the Lord says:

6 “For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon his shoulder: and his name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace.

7 Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end, upon the throne of David, and upon his kingdom, to order it, and to establish it with judgment and with justice from henceforth even for ever. The zeal of the LORD of hosts will perform this.”

Isaiah 9:6-7 · KJV
Jesus Christ. In First Corinthians chapter fifteen, verse fifty-seven it says, “But thanks be to God, He gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.” Beloved, we need victory in our prayer. We need victory to move forward. We need answers by the power of the Holy Ghost.

“But thanks be to God, which giveth us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.”

1 Corinthians 15:57 · KJV
Tonight, we are not praying to manufacture victory. We are standing in the victory that Jesus Christ has already obtained.
Jesus did not merely suffer; He conquered. He conquered sin, He conquered death, He conquered condemnation and the power of darkness through His resurrection. He demonstrated that even the grave cannot hold Him. We are tapping into that victory in our prayer and in our fasting.
Notice that victory is described as something God gives. Your victory is not dependent solely upon your strength. Your victory is not dependent solely upon your connection, or your finances. Your victory does not solely depend on your education. Your victory is not dependent on your ability. Your victory comes through Jesus Christ.
02

A Battle Is Not the Absence of Victory

There may be battles around you, but the presence of a battle does not mean that there is an absence of victory. I don’t know what you are going through, but what you are going through does not mean that there is no presence of victory.

“Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us.”

Romans 8:37 · KJV

“For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith.”

1 John 5:4 · KJV
Hallelujah! As you pray and you fast this week, I want you to know and have inside you that there is victory in your prayer — that there is victory; it’s going to come. Expect the victory, in the situation, in everything you place on the altar this week.
One of the things that the devil does is to speak to our minds. The devil keeps telling you that you cannot make it. He keeps speaking in your dream that you cannot make it. The enemy of your destiny always tells you that your battle is bigger than a mountain. Who says a thing, beloved, and it shall come, it shall be done, when the Lord God has not commanded it?
03

Bible Witnesses of Victory

In the Bible, do you not see that David had Goliath? But David received victory. Did you not see in the Bible that Joseph had a pit dug for him? He went to prison, but Joseph received victory.
What are you going through? Did you not also see that Moses had Pharaoh pursuing them, and even the Red Sea, but Israel received victory?
Daniel entered into the lion’s den, but God shut the mouth of the lion. He came out victoriously.
Do you remember the story of the three Hebrew men? They entered into the furnace, but the fire could not consume them. Jesus entered the grave, but on the third day, beloved, He rose victoriously.
Victory is coming for you by the power of the Holy Ghost. Wherever battles have confronted you, do not allow — do not allow it to convince you that your story is finished. I know there are some battles in your life today, but you will not allow those battles to hinder the victory that is coming. In Deuteronomy chapter twenty verse four, the word of the Lord says:

“For the LORD your God is he that goeth with you, to fight for you against your enemies, to save you.”

Deuteronomy 20:4 · KJV
In Psalm chapter one hundred and eight verse thirteen, the word of the Lord says:

“Through God we shall do valiantly: for he it is that shall tread down our enemies.”

Psalm 108:13 · KJV
Victory is coming for you by the power of the Holy Ghost. To that which you placed on the altar of God, victory is coming to that marriage. Victory is coming to that health problem. Victory is coming to those finances. Victory is coming over the attacks of witches, attacks of those that hate you, the attacks that surround your life. Victory is coming to the problems at the workplace. Victory is going to surround you in the name of Jesus.
04

The Lord Is a Man of War

The Word of the Lord says in Exodus fifteen, verse three to eight:

3 “The LORD is a man of war: the LORD is his name.

4 Pharaoh’s chariots and his host hath he cast into the sea: his chosen captains also are drowned in the Red sea.

5 The depths have covered them: they sank into the bottom as a stone.

6 Thy right hand, O LORD, is become glorious in power: thy right hand, O LORD, hath dashed in pieces the enemy.

7 And in the greatness of thine excellency thou hast overthrown them that rose up against thee: thou sentest forth thy wrath, which consumed them as stubble.

8 And with the blast of thy nostrils the waters were gathered together, the floods stood upright as an heap, and the depths were congealed in the heart of the sea.”

Exodus 15:3-8 · KJV
Many of the Israelites couldn’t sing a song of victory because they were still suffering in Egypt. God sent Moses to lead His people out of Egypt. Egypt is a place of slavery and suffering. Even when the storm followed the Israelites, they were consumed — and immediately the Israelites crossed the Red Sea, they sung a song of victory.
You are coming out of every Egypt in your life before the end of this month — that sorrow, that slavery, that suffering in your life. Somebody shout, “I am coming out! I am coming out! I am coming out!” in the name of Jesus.
In Numbers chapter thirteen from verse fourteen, Moses sent the spies to go and take the land and see if they can possess it. They came back, and most of them said, there is no way they could possess the land — and they brought evil reports to Moses. But remember Joshua and Caleb: they brought a good report to Moses, that they can possess the land, and that victory was theirs.

“And Caleb stilled the people before Moses, and said, Let us go up at once, and possess it; for we are well able to overcome it.”

Numbers 13:30 · KJV
Beloved, we will possess the land in this season, and victory will be ours throughout the rest of this year. We are in August, we are fasting, we are praying — we need victory from now. From January till now you were wondering how this year will look like; but now that God has brought you victoriously into the month of August, August it shall be the end of your problems. Victory will visit you and your family in the name of Jesus. Do not be discouraged.
05

Warfare Watch — Dreams & Steps

I want to announce to you tonight: you will sing the songs of victory. The Lord will visit you and your family. The Lord will change that situation in your life, whatever you place on the altar of God, in the mighty name of Jesus. Don’t be discouraged, beloved.
Eating in the dream can stop your destiny or your victory. Eating in the dream is an agreement with evil forces.
Having sex in the dream causes delay; it can cause you not to prosper, regardless of your gender.
If your vehicle gets an accident in a dream, it means that your destiny has crashed — the enemy from all sorts of attacks in your life. If you cannot pray, you will be defeated. You have to be a prayer machine. You have to fast in this season of fasting, in the mighty name of Jesus.
If your legs are in a satanic shoe, your legs cannot take you into your victory. You cannot get where you need to get, or you cannot get helpers to come and help you. Any attacks in your life — it’s time to rise up and fast for you to receive your victory.
Don’t pull your shoes out of nowhere. Pray and ask the leading of the Holy Ghost. Because if you pull your shoes to anyone who has the spirit of failure, you may automatically receive failure in your life. Be alert this coming month in the mighty name of Jesus as we fast and we pray.
06

Season of Supernatural Victory

Joshua chapter ten verse eight — the word of the Lord says:

“And the LORD said unto Joshua, Fear them not: for I have delivered them into thine hand; there shall not a man of them stand before thee.”

Joshua 10:8 · KJV
And God is speaking to you in this room by the power of the Holy Ghost: do not be afraid of your enemies. Pray against them in the name of Jesus. He said He has given your enemies into your hands by the power of the Holy Ghost in the mighty name of Jesus.
John chapter sixteen verse thirty-three — the word of the Lord says:

“These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace. In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.”

John 16:33 · KJV
There are seasons when you need to arise a mighty warrior in Christ — and this season is now. You need to arise for battle. Just like Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego emerged victorious from the fiery furnace in Daniel chapter three, verse twenty-five to twenty-eight, you are stepping into your season of supernatural victory.

25 “He answered and said, Lo, I see four men loose, walking in the midst of the fire, and they have no hurt; and the form of the fourth is like the Son of God.

26 Then Nebuchadnezzar came near to the mouth of the burning fiery furnace, and spake, and said, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, ye servants of the most high God, come forth, and come hither. Then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, came forth of the midst of the fire.

27 And the princes, governors, and captains, and the king’s counsellors, being gathered together, saw these men, upon whose bodies the fire had no power, nor was an hair of their head singed, neither were their coats changed, nor the smell of fire had passed on them.

28 Then Nebuchadnezzar spake, and said, Blessed be the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, who hath sent his angel, and delivered his servants that trusted in him, and have changed the word, that they should not serve nor worship any god, except their own God.”

Daniel 3:25-28 · KJV
The word supernatural — when I was studying it, it says that the “super” is the Holy Spirit, is Jesus Christ; and the “natural” is you and I. So when it comes together, it’s supernatural. The super and the natural that brings the victory. This is your season of supernatural victory in the mighty name of Jesus.
Like Joshua who commanded the sun to stand still, you carry an extra of divine authority inside you. When God’s fire falls, every Goliath must bow, every Red Sea must part, and every Jericho wall must crumble before you in this fast and in this prayer.
Receive your victory. As David defeated the armies with a sling and stone, you shall triumph victoriously. Arise in the name of Jesus.
07

Change the Language of Your Life

Our confidence is not in fear, superstitions or human strength; our confidence is in Jesus Christ and His finished works. The enemy may accuse you, but Jesus has justified you already. The enemy may oppose you, but Jesus Christ is for you already. The enemy may attempt to discourage you, but Jesus Christ is your hope already. The enemy may tell you that nothing will change, but God has the final word — life in your mind, in your hope, in your health, in the mighty name of Jesus.

“What shall we then say to these things? If God be for us, who can be against us?”

Romans 8:31 · KJV
Tonight, we lose the language of defeat in your life. Instead of saying, “I will never make it,” declare, “I can do all things through Christ that strengthens me.”
Instead of you saying, “This battle will destroy me,” declare, “I am more than a conqueror through Christ.”
Instead of you saying, “There is no way,” declare, “With God, all things are possible in my life.”
Instead of you saying, “My situation is finished,” declare, “God is able to make a way for me and my family. God is able to make a way for my son, for my daughter, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ.”

“And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it.”

Colossians 2:15 · KJV
