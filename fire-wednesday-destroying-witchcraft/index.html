Module 1

Fire Wednesday: Why We Fast Today

Psalm 138:1–2 · Psalm 100 (KJV) “I will praise thee with my whole heart: before the gods will I sing praise unto thee. I will worship toward thy holy temple, and praise thy name for thy lovingkindness and for thy truth…”

Every Wednesday, L137 fasts and prays. Today we are telling the wicked to get out of our life. We start by singing to God and thanking Him.

Fire Wednesday is the ministry's weekly day of fasting and prayer against witchcraft activities in our lives, homes, and destinies. We open by praising the Lord and thanking Him — “Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise” (Psalm 100:4). Only after praise, thanksgiving, confession, forgiveness, and inviting the Holy Spirit do we go to war.

Think of fasting like turning off the noise so you can hear God clearly. Wednesday is a spiritual training day — the ministry fasts together so we all fight together.

The deliverance flow is non-negotiable: Praise → Thanksgiving → Confession → Forgiveness → Invitation of the Holy Spirit → Teaching → Deliverance prayers → Sealing. Skipping the front-end steps leaves the deliverance stage without covering.

🔑 Key Point

You cannot skip praise and thanksgiving and jump straight to warfare. Presence first, then power.

👆 Tap-to-reveal: What is a “Fire Wednesday”?
A ministry-wide day of fasting and prayer set apart to destroy witchcraft activities against your life and destiny. Break the fast between 3, 4, 5, or 6 p.m. as the Holy Spirit leads.
🙏 Pray

Father, I thank You for this Fire Wednesday. I enter Your gates with thanksgiving and Your courts with praise. Cleanse my heart, forgive my sins, forgive everyone who has hurt me, and fill me with Your Holy Spirit as I fast today, in Jesus' name.

Repeat After Me
🔥 Declare

“Today I fast. Today I pray. Today every wicked activity against my life is destroyed by the power of the Holy Ghost, in the mighty name of Jesus.”

❓ Which step comes first before deliverance prayers?
Right — Psalm 100 tells us to enter His gates with thanksgiving. Praise builds the throne (Psalm 22:3) before the warfare begins.
🤔 Apply

What is one thing you can thank God for right now, even before you fight?

Module 2

The Sorcerer Struck Blind (Acts 13)

Acts 13:4–12 (KJV) “So they, being sent forth by the Holy Ghost, departed unto Seleucia… And there they found a certain sorcerer, a false prophet, a Jew, whose name was Bar-jesus… Then Saul, (who also is called Paul,) filled with the Holy Ghost, set his eyes on him, And said, O full of all subtilty and all mischief, thou child of the devil, thou enemy of all righteousness, wilt thou not cease to pervert the right ways of the Lord? And now, behold, the hand of the Lord is upon thee, and thou shalt be blind, not seeing the sun for a season. And immediately there fell on him a mist and a darkness; and he went about seeking some to lead him by the hand.”

A bad man tried to stop people from believing in Jesus. Paul looked him in the eye. God made him blind so he could not see the sun. That is what happens to wickedness when we stand up in the name of Jesus.

Elymas — his name literally means sorcerer in Greek — was pulling a governor away from the gospel. Paul did not negotiate; filled with the Holy Ghost, he named the spirit, addressed it directly, and released the hand of the Lord. Darkness fell instantly. When you fast today, look the beast in the eye and speak the same way against every witchcraft activity in your life.

The Bible highlights it: this guy's name meant “sorcerer.” It says something spiritual — his identity was written all over him. When you're filled with the Holy Spirit, you can see what's really going on and speak to it directly.

Note the pattern: (1) filled with the Holy Ghost, (2) set his eyes on him, (3) named the spirit accurately (child of the devil, enemy of righteousness), (4) declared the hand of the Lord, (5) pronounced the judgment (blindness), (6) it manifested (mist and darkness). Deliverance authority follows this order — always Spirit-filled, always specific.

🔑 Key Point

The hand of the Lord is laid on witchcraft. Speak it — “Every witchcraft against my life, be struck blind, in the name of Jesus.”

👆 Tap-to-reveal: What does “mist and darkness” mean?
A spiritual blinding — the sorcerer's demonic sight was shut down. When we pray, we ask the Lord to blind every witchcraft eye that is spying on our progress, our marriage, our finances, our children.
❓ True or False: Paul argued and debated with the sorcerer before speaking judgment.
False — he was filled with the Holy Ghost, looked him in the eye, and pronounced the judgment. He did not enter into an argument.
🙏 Pray

Father, in the name of Jesus, let the hand of the Lord be laid upon every witchcraft activity assigned against my life. Let mist and darkness fall on every wicked eye watching my destiny. Blind them. Silence them. Scatter them.

Repeat After Me
🔥 Declare

“Every witchcraft eye spying on me, be struck blind. Every witchcraft mouth speaking against me, be silenced. In the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.”

Module 3

Household Wickedness & Hidden Enemies

Micah 7:6–7 (KJV) “For the son dishonoureth the father, the daughter riseth up against her mother, the daughter in law against her mother in law; a man's enemies are the men of his own house. Therefore I will look unto the LORD; I will wait for the God of my salvation: my God will hear me.”
Ephesians 6:12 (KJV) “For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.”

Sometimes the people who don't want you to do well are inside your own family or friend group. God still helps you. We fight the bad spirit, not the person.

Household enemies are real. They know your goings and comings. They become envious when you are progressing — new job, marriage going well, children doing well, travelling abroad. Every family has a spiritual head — either wicked or godly. If you are the one praying, you are the godly spiritual head — you are the one dismantling what the wicked head is building.

There are also gullible adversaries — friends who pose as friends but attack your dreams from behind.

Not everyone clapping for you is happy for you. Some people close to you get quietly jealous when your life starts going somewhere. The Bible already said this would happen. You don't fight the person — you fight the spirit behind them.

Micah 7:7 is the pivot — “Therefore I will look unto the LORD.” The response to household wickedness is not vengeance, it is upward gaze. Combine with Ephesians 6:12 — the actual wrestle is against principalities, not the person. This protects your heart from bitterness while your prayer destroys the spirit.

⚠️ Warning

Do not name your family members in a rage. Name the spirit. Kill the spirit. The person may still be redeemed.

🔑 Key Point

Your neighbour is not your enemy. Your colleague is not your enemy. The battle is spiritual — and our victory is in Christ.

❓ Fill in the blank (Ephesians 6:12): “We wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against ______, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world…”
The answer is principalities. The battle is spiritual — target the spirit, not the person.
🙏 Pray

Father, in the mighty name of Jesus, I destroy every household wickedness assigned against my life, my marriage, my children, my career, and my destiny. Every wicked spiritual head in my family, be silenced by the fire of the Holy Ghost.

Repeat After Me
🔥 Declare

“A man's enemies are the men of his own house — but I look unto the Lord, and my God hears me. In Jesus' name.”

Module 4

They Shall Gather — But Not By Me

Isaiah 54:15 (KJV) “Behold, they shall surely gather together, but not by me: whosoever shall gather together against thee shall fall for thy sake.”
Isaiah 8:9–10 (KJV) “Associate yourselves, O ye people, and ye shall be broken in pieces; and give ear, all ye of far countries: gird yourselves, and ye shall be broken in pieces; gird yourselves, and ye shall be broken in pieces. Take counsel together, and it shall come to nought; speak the word, and it shall not stand: for God is with us.”

People can meet to plan bad things. But if God is with you, their meeting cannot work. It falls apart.

God does not promise there will be no gatherings — He promises the gathering will not be by Him. When they mention your name, let storms begin to thunder. When they mention your children, your husband, your business — let the fire of the Holy Ghost scatter their meeting in the mountain of Moses.

Group chats. Group meetings. Group scheming. God sees it all. And He says it can't stand — because He is with you.

Isaiah 8:10 gives us the covenant reason — “for God is with us.” That is Immanuel. Every enemy gathering fails on the Immanuel principle: God's presence is the guarantee of the outcome, not our strength.

🔑 Key Point

Gatherings will come. Immanuel guarantees they cannot prosper.

❓ According to Isaiah 54:15, what does God promise about gatherings against you?
God does not promise no gatherings — He promises the gathering will not be by Him, and it will fall for your sake.
🙏 Pray

Father, every gathering against my life, my marriage, my finances, my children — let it scatter in the mountain of Moses, in the mighty name of Jesus. Let their counsel come to nought. Let their word not stand — for God is with me.

Repeat After Me
🔥 Declare

“Every meeting called against my name — scatter. Every counsel taken against me — come to nought. For God is with me. In Jesus' name.”

Module 5

Authority in Christ — Trample Serpents

Luke 10:19 (KJV) “Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you.”
Psalm 35:1 (KJV) “Plead my cause, O LORD, with them that strive with me: fight against them that fight against me.”
Psalm 7:11 (KJV) “God judgeth the righteous, and God is angry with the wicked every day.”

Jesus gave you power. When you say His name, bad things cannot hurt you.

You are not fighting your case yourself — you are giving it to the Lord. But you exercise the authority He gave you. When wicked people argue with you, don't argue back. Silence. Go into your room, open your Bible, and pray. Every dagger they made lands back on their own head, because you refused to be provoked.

Some people just want you to argue so they can drain you. Don't take the bait. Answer them in prayer, not in words.

Two authorities operate at once: (a) positional — Luke 10:19 grants tread-authority over serpents; (b) advocacy — Psalm 35:1 asks the Lord to plead the cause. Combined, they mean we can act in authority while still committing the outcome to God as our advocate.

⚠️ Warning

“Don't waste your time arguing with certain wizards.” Silence and prayer are the response, not debate.

Module 6

Speaking to the Earth — The Blood Covenant

Jeremiah 22:29 (KJV) “O earth, earth, earth, hear the word of the LORD.”
Hebrews 12:24 (KJV) “And to Jesus the mediator of the new covenant, and to the blood of sprinkling, that speaketh better things than that of Abel.”
Revelation 12:11 (KJV) “And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.”
1 John 5:7–8 (KJV) “For there are three that bear record in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one. And there are three that bear witness in earth, the Spirit, and the water, and the blood: and these three agree in one.”

The Bible says God can talk to the earth. Bad people also try to talk to the earth against you. But when you pour the blood of Jesus, it speaks better things and God hears it.

The earth is a witness. God spoke to the earth against a man in Jeremiah 22. Wicked people also try to speak to the earth against your job, your marriage, your progress. When you pour the blood of Jesus on the ground, the blood speaks better things than the blood of Abel — restoration, new covenant, redemption. It cancels whatever the enemy has spoken to the earth about you.

The earth is more than dirt — the Bible treats it as something that can hear. That's why the blood of Jesus poured on the ground isn't a ritual — it's a witness that overrides every wicked word spoken over your location.

1 John 5:8 identifies the three earth-witnesses: Spirit, water, blood. The three heavenly witnesses are Father, Word, Holy Ghost. When we pray using the oil, the water, and the blood, we are aligning with the earthly witnesses that agree in one. This is framed entirely through Christ's finished work — never as ritual on its own.

⚠️ Boundary

The oil, water, and blood are only effective through Christ's finished work. Never treat them as ritual on their own. No salt baths. No covenant-of-salt language. Ever.

👆 Tap-to-reveal: Why the blood of Jesus, not Abel's blood?
Abel's blood cried out from the ground for vengeance (Genesis 4:10). Jesus' blood speaks better things (Hebrews 12:24) — new covenant, restoration, redemption. Where the enemy uses ancestral (ancient) blood spilled by our forefathers, the blood of Jesus overrules it.
❓ Match: The three witnesses on earth (1 John 5:8) are…
The Spirit, the water, and the blood — “and these three agree in one.” The heavenly three are Father, Word, and Holy Ghost.

Module 7

Salvation & Sealing — Cover Yourself

Romans 10:13 (KJV) “For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”
Revelation 12:11 (KJV) “And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.”

If you say “Jesus, save me,” He does. Then say “blood of Jesus, cover me,” and He does that too.

Every deliverance service ends with sealing — covering yourself and everything the Lord touched today with the blood of Jesus. If you have never given your life to Christ, today is the day. Romans 10:13 is a promise, not a suggestion.

Saying yes to Jesus isn't a religious formality. It's the biggest change of citizenship you'll ever make. And after that, cover your day every day with His blood.

Sealing is the closing step of the standard deliverance flow — praise, thanksgiving, confession, forgiveness, invitation of the Holy Spirit, teaching, deliverance prayers, sealing. Without sealing, the ground gained can be revisited.

🔑 Prayer of Salvation

“Lord Jesus, I repent of my sins and surrender my life. Wash me clean. I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, that He died on the cross for my sins and rose again on the third day for my victory. I believe in my heart and confess with my mouth that Jesus is my Saviour and Lord. I receive eternal life. In Jesus' name. Amen.”

🙏 Sealing Prayer

Father, I cover myself, my home, my family, my finances, my career, and everything You touched today with the blood of Jesus. Let the blood be my protection, my cover, and my seal, in the mighty name of Jesus.
