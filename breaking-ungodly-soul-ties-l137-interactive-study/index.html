Chapter 1 of 8

What Is a Soul Tie?

A soul tie is a spiritual and emotional bond that links two people — mind, will, emotion, and sometimes spirit.

Genesis 2:24 (KJV)“Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.”
1 Samuel 18:1 (KJV)“And it came to pass, when he had made an end of speaking unto Saul, that the soul of Jonathan was knit with the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul.”
1 Corinthians 6:16 (KJV)“What? know ye not that he which is joined to an harlot is one body? for two, saith he, shall be one flesh.”
1 Corinthians 6:17 (KJV)“But he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit.”
Chapter 2 of 8

Godly vs Ungodly Soul Ties

Not every soul tie is evil. Godly ties build you and open doors; ungodly ties formed through sin, trauma, or manipulation hold you captive.

Three God-ordained soul ties
  • Husband and wife
  • Parents and children
  • Believers and Christ
Repeat after meBy the power of the Holy Ghost, every ungodly soul tie must break in the name of Jesus.
Chapter 3 of 8

How Ungodly Soul Ties Are Formed

Pastor Kiky named five doorways an ungodly soul tie enters through.

The five doorways
  • Sexual relationships outside the will of God (1 Corinthians 6:16)
  • Toxic and abusive relationships — control, manipulation, fear
  • Past relationships never released
  • Trauma and deep emotional bonds
  • Covenants and spiritual agreements — words, rituals, dedications
Isaiah 28:18 (KJV)“And your covenant with death shall be disannulled, and your agreement with hell shall not stand; when the overflowing scourge shall pass through, then ye shall be trodden down by it.”
Chapter 4 of 8

Signs an Ungodly Soul Tie Is Operating

Nine signs to check honestly
  • Cannot stop thinking about the other person
  • Feel spiritually and emotionally drained
  • Keep returning to a toxic relationship
  • Repeated dreams about the person
  • Feel controlled, manipulated, bound, distressed
  • Spiritual sexual attack in dreams; a monitoring spirit
  • Struggle to move forward in life or any relationship
  • Feel guilty, confused, oppressed
  • Feel fear without a clear reason
Chapter 5 of 8

What Ungodly Soul Ties Do

They delay destiny, block blessing, pollute prayer, and transfer burdens — as with Samson and Delilah.

Judges 16:19 (KJV)“And she made him sleep upon her knees; and she called for a man, and she caused him to shave off the seven locks of his head; and she began to afflict him, and his strength went from him.”
Hosea 4:6 (KJV)“My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: because thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, that thou shalt be no priest to me: seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I will also forget thy children.”

Galatians 3:14 (KJV)“That the blessing of Abraham might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.”

Repeat After Me

Every line below is taken directly from the deliverance exercises of this teaching. Speak each one out loud.

Step 1I repent of every involvement in every ungodly soul tie. Blot out my sins, Lord, and let the times of refreshing come.
Step 2I renounce and I denounce every ungodly soul tie. I was with you, but now I am not part of you, in the name of Jesus.
Step 3By the blood of the Lamb I overcome. The blood disconnects me from every ungodly covenant and brings me into a new covenant.
Step 4I cut every emotional and spiritual access point into my life. Every access point the enemy has into my life, I close it.

Step 7I am joined unto the Lord and I am one spirit with Him. My identity, my power, my potential, my confidence — return to me now.
FreedomMy covenant with death is disannulled. My agreement with hell shall not stand, in the mighty name of Jesus.
