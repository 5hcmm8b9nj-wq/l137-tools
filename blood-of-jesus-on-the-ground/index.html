Blood of Jesus on the Ground

Take the blood of Jesus. Go outside. Read the scriptures. Say the prayers. Pour the blood of Jesus on the ground.

How to pray this

A short, faith-filled order of prayer for the front door, the driveway, or any piece of ground the Lord leads you to.

  1. Read the four KJV scriptures aloud.
  2. Pray the twenty prayers aloud, one by one, as you pour the blood of Jesus on the earth.
  3. Close with the sealing prayer and Amen.
  4. Pour the blood of Jesus on the ground.

On this page

Read · Four KJV scriptures Say · Twenty prayers of the blood Seal · Closing prayer

Read

Four KJV scriptures. Read each verse slowly and aloud before you begin.

Micah 5:12 · KJV

And I will cut off witchcrafts out of thine hand; and thou shalt have no more soothsayers.

Numbers 23:23 · KJV

Surely there is no enchantment against Jacob, neither is there any divination against Israel: according to this time it shall be said of Jacob and of Israel, What hath God wrought!

Revelation 12:11 · KJV

And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.

Hebrews 12:24 · KJV

And to Jesus the mediator of the new covenant, and to the blood of sprinkling, that speaketh better things than that of Abel.

Say

Twenty prayers of the blood. Pray each prayer aloud. As you pray, pour the blood of Jesus on the earth. Do not rush. Let each word come out with faith.

  1. As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, I cut myself off from every territorial spirit, in Jesus’ Name.

  2. Any association of domestic demon responsible for my present battles, O God, arise and destroy them by fire, in the Name of Jesus.

  3. Every household power praying for me to fall and never to rise up again, die, in the Name of Jesus.

  4. I loose myself from curses that activate prolonged poverty and lack, in the Name of Jesus.

  5. Whatever ancestral and household enemies have destroyed in my life and marriage, be restored by fire, in Jesus’ Name.

  6. As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, Lord, let the anointing of the Holy Ghost break every yoke of failure at the edge of breakthroughs caused by ancestral witchcraft powers, in Jesus’ Name.

  7. Altar of household wickedness in my family, catch fire now as I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth.

  8. O God, let all those using affliction to pursue my intended blessings — I command it to disappear by fire, in the Name of Jesus.

  9. Everything that household enemies have stolen or destroyed in my life, be restored now, in the Name of Jesus.

  10. Evil network of my household witchcraft, scatter by the thunder of God, in the Name of Jesus.

  11. As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, every weapon of household enemies targeted against me, turn against your owners, in the Name of Jesus.

  12. Every evil leg and evil hand walking about for my sake, catch fire, in the Name of Jesus.

  13. Every stubborn strongman trading with my goodness, submit your power and perish, in the Name of Jesus.

  14. As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, evil eyes monitoring my life, go blind now by fire, in the Name of Jesus.

  15. Demonic reverse gears installed to hinder my progress and success, roast now by fire, in the Name of Jesus.

  16. Every witchcraft handwriting in any area of my life, by the blood of Jesus, be washed off, in the Name of Jesus.

  17. Every altar of household enemies speaking hardship into my life, catch fire and scatter, in the Name of Jesus.

  18. Satanic keepers of my instruments of promotion and advancement, release them by fire, in the Name of Jesus.

  19. As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, Blood of Jesus, deliver me from every power determined to destroy me because of the wickedness of my ancestors, in the Name of Jesus.

  20. Household enemies, carry your load and go. (Name the evil load one by one: sickness, poverty, sorrow, loneliness, demotion, backwardness.)

Seal

Close with the sealing prayer, then pour the blood of Jesus on the ground.

Sealing prayer

As I pour the blood of Jesus on the earth, Lord, deliver me from every witchcraft powers, in Jesus’ Name. Amen.

Now pour the blood of Jesus on the ground.
