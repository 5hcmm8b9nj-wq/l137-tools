Section 2

The 7 Steps

Each step has a fixed time budget so the family can actually finish daily. Total: about 18 minutes.

  • 1st read: aloud, one family member.
  • 2nd read: silently, everyone.
  • 3rd read: aloud again, a different member — this time slowly, sentence by sentence.

Rule: Do not comment yet. Just receive.

On paper (or on the web form), circle / highlight:

  • THE SCRIPTURE — the anchor verse (write it out in full KJV).
  • THE LIE — what wrong thought / fear / accusation the devotional exposes.
  • THE TRUTH — the corresponding truth from God's Word.
  • THE COMMAND — what the pastor tells us to DO (renew mind, declare, stop-think-say, etc.).
  • Read the anchor verse in KJV, in full.
  • Read the verse BEFORE and the verse AFTER it (context).
  • List 2–3 cross-reference scriptures on the same theme. (The pastor's devotional almost always echoes 2–3 other scriptures — pull them out.)
  • Ask: What does this verse teach me about God's character?

Before you can receive, the ground must be clean. Say aloud, together:

“Father, I repent of every thought that has agreed with the enemy's lies of _______.” (fill in from Step 2)

“I forgive everyone who has spoken those lies over my life or family.”

“I cover my mind and my home with the blood of Jesus Christ.”

  • “I declare, in the Name of Jesus Christ, that ________.”
  • Use the pastor's own words wherever possible. Add nothing new.
  • Speak them out loud as a family. Speak them twice.

The 7 Steps above extract the juice. These 12 Layers turn today's devotional into a permanent Bible study you can teach from for the rest of your life.
