1. Preparing the Heart

Thanksgiving, confession, and forgiveness.

Thanksgiving

Read aloudPsalm 100:4; Psalm 103:1–5; 1 Thessalonians 5:16–18.
PrayerFather, in the name of Jesus, I thank You for Your mercy, goodness, and covenant love over my life.

Confession of Sins

Read aloud1 John 1:9; Psalm 51:1–4; Proverbs 28:13.
PrayerLord Jesus, I confess my sins before You. Wash me with Your blood and cleanse me from all unrighteousness.

Forgiving Others

Read aloudMatthew 6:14–15; Mark 11:25; Ephesians 4:31–32.
PrayerIn the name of Jesus, I forgive every person who has hurt me. I release them fully before God.

2. Inviting the Holy Spirit

Invite the Spirit of God to lead the session.

Read aloudRomans 8:14; John 16:13; 2 Corinthians 3:17.
PrayerHoly Spirit, take full control. Reveal what must be exposed, broken, healed, and cleansed.
Reflection promptWhat patterns or battles keep repeating in your life or family line?

3. Covenant Scriptures to Read Aloud

Use these as your scriptural foundation.

Luke 22:20This cup is the new covenant in His blood.Read aloud slowly.
Hebrews 12:24The blood of Jesus speaks better things.Read aloud slowly.
Ezekiel 16:1–6Read the full passage aloud, especially the language about nativity, navel cord, blood, and “Live.”
Galatians 3:13 and 1 Peter 1:18–19Christ redeems and His blood secures redemption.

5. Prayers for Breaking Evil Covenants

Blood of Jesus, speak against every unconscious evil covenant.
I speak destruction unto the fruits of unclean spirits in my life, in the name of Jesus.
I break every evil covenant link, by the blood of Jesus.
I dismantle every stronghold of evil blood covenants, by the blood of Jesus.
I nullify the effects of evil access to my blood, by the blood of Jesus.
I release myself from every covenanted curse, by the blood of Jesus.
I release every organ in my body from the grip of evil blood covenant, by the blood of Jesus.
I dissociate myself and my family from every territorial blood covenant, by the blood of Jesus.
I dissociate myself from every tribal blood covenant, by the blood of Jesus.
I dissociate myself from every inherited blood covenant, by the blood of Jesus.
I withdraw my blood from every evil altar, in the name of Jesus.
I release myself from every curse attached to evil covenants, in the name of Jesus.
I release myself from the grip of every enforcing power attached to evil covenants, in the name of Jesus.
Every repercussion of breaking unconscious covenants, be washed away by the blood of Jesus.
Lord, by the blood of Jesus, set me free from all evil covenants.
Let every inherited battle from my mother’s side and my father’s side be terminated in Jesus’ name.
Section A — Renouncing Evil Covenants
I renounce every evil covenant knowingly or unknowingly entered.
I reject every inherited agreement with darkness operating from my bloodline.
Section B — Applying the Blood of Jesus
Blood of Jesus, silence every accusation speaking against my life.
Let the new covenant in Christ override every evil ancestral claim.
Section C — Freedom and Peace
I receive freedom, peace, and restoration in every area affected by evil covenants.
I declare peaceful sleep and divine protection in Jesus’ name.
