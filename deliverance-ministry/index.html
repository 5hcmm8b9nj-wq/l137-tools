About Pastor Kiki

A vessel for the midnight altar

Pastor Kiki Ocansey leads L137 Deliverance Ministry — a midnight house of warfare prayer, prophetic teaching, and deliverance. Speaking on Clubhouse, she gathers intercessors across the nations to watch, pray, and contend for breakthrough in the name of Jesus.

Three nights a week the altar burns: Word, declaration, and tongues of fire poured out until heaven answers. The ministry exists to wake sleeping watchmen, break covenants of darkness, and release the impossible by the blood of Jesus and the power of the Holy Spirit.
