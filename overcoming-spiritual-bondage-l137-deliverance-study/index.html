One-Day Fasting & Prayer Schedule

Part 3

Reflective Scripture Study

Biblical symbolism — spiritual guardianship vs demonic manifestations

The Bible is filled with animal imagery, warfare imagery, and household imagery. Some symbols point to God's protection and covenant provision. Others expose the presence of unclean spirits. Learning to tell the two apart is one of the most important gifts God gives an intercessor.

1. Spiritual guardianship — God's covenant covering

God Himself is the primary guardian of His people. He assigns His angels to watch over those who fear Him. Their presence is peaceful, ordered, and always consistent with the written Word.

Psalm 91:11
For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.
Psalm 34:7
The angel of the LORD encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.
Hebrews 1:14
Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation?

Fruit of true guardianship

  • Peace that surpasses understanding (Philippians 4:7).
  • Increased love for the Word and the presence of God.
  • Freedom from fear, shame, and torment.
  • Sound mind, purity, and desire for holiness.
  • Sensitivity to sin and quick repentance.

2. Demonic manifestation — unclean spirits at work

Demonic spirits also seek to attach themselves to human lives — through inherited covenants, personal sin, trauma, wrong agreements, occult exposure, or ungodly soul ties. Their fruit is always the opposite of the Holy Spirit's.

John 10:10
The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly.
Ephesians 6:12
For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.

Fruit of demonic activity

  • Recurring compulsion, addiction, or perversion.
  • Torment, insomnia, nightmares, dread, and irrational fear.
  • Hatred of prayer, worship, and the Word of God.
  • Repeated failure at the same point despite sincere effort.
  • Physical manifestations during ministry — barking, growling, hissing, choking, coldness, heat, unnatural sleep.

Part 4

Examples of the Dog Spirit & Other Unclean Spirits

A scripture-anchored discernment reference. Every category below is drawn from the Bible.

A. The dog spirit

In Scripture, the dog is repeatedly used as a picture of that which is unclean, shameless, and outside the covenant. When this spirit attaches to a life, it drives the person toward the same behaviours the Bible warns against.

Deuteronomy 23:18
Thou shalt not bring the hire of a whore, or the price of a dog, into the house of the LORD thy God for any vow: for even both these are abomination unto the LORD thy God.
Philippians 3:2
Beware of dogs, beware of evil workers, beware of the concision.
Revelation 22:15
For without are dogs, and sorcerers, and whoremongers, and murderers, and idolaters, and whosoever loveth and maketh a lie.
Psalm 22:20
Deliver my soul from the sword; my darling from the power of the dog.
2 Peter 2:22
But it is happened unto them according to the true proverb, The dog is turned to his own vomit again; and the sow that was washed to her wallowing in the mire.
  • Repeated pull toward sexual sin, pornography, and fantasy, even after genuine repentance.
  • Shamelessness — the conscience that once trembled at sin has grown quiet.
  • Barking, growling, snarling, or biting-type behaviour during deliverance ministry.
  • Recurring dreams of dogs chasing, biting, or licking the dreamer.
  • Marriages, homes, or workplaces where quarrelling, biting words, and betrayal recur.
  • Attraction to people or environments that drag the person back into the vomit they left.
  • Persistent fear of being watched, followed, or hunted.

B. Other unclean spirits named or pictured in Scripture

Recognising these spirits is not for fear — it is so that you can dispossess them by the authority of Jesus. Every one of them must bow at His name.

Tap each spirit to open the reference.

Handle with the Word

Never name a spirit over anyone based on personality alone. Look for the biblical pattern, the fruit, and the confirmation of the Holy Spirit through the Word. Deliverance is a ministry of love, always exercised under the authority of Jesus and the covering of a spiritually mature pastor. If any manifestation frightens or overwhelms you, stop and call your pastor.
