The teaching in short

Key lessons from the service

Remember this
  • Faith without works is dead. Prayer and obedient action together bring the breakthrough (James 2:26).
  • Some attacks did not come by evil speaking alone — they came by actions. So the answer includes obedient action too.
  • God has always joined His word to a physical act: the rod, the march, the clay, the new cruse of salt.
  • The covenant of salt is a perpetual and unbreakable agreement. By it, no enemy shall prevail in your territory (2 Chronicles 13:5).
  • Salt signifies preservation and the destruction of principalities and powers.
  • God healed the waters — the salt did not. All power belongs to the Lord and to the finished work of Christ.
  • Nothing is used in prayer unless it stands with the scripture.
  • Matthew 5:13 — you are the salt. Guard your savour: repent and forgive before you fight.
  • Deliverance may leave loudly or very quietly. Everybody has a way of being delivered.
  • Seal every deliverance with the blood of Jesus, and keep a pure heart afterwards.
  • Even five minutes of honest love toward God is received by Him.

The prophetic direction

The seven-day prophetic direction

Preserved exactly as Pastor Kiky Ocansey delivered it in the service, with the scriptures she attached to it. Every element is scripture-backed obedience under the finished work of Christ — never a ritual with power of its own.

Prophetic direction

The seven-day salt-water direction

Put salt in water, pray over it, and wash your face with the salt water. Do this every day for seven days, praying the prayer points posted on the WhatsApp platform. The room first prayed with the salt together and destroyed every enchantment, every curse, every divination and every spell over their lives and their destinies.

Prophetic direction

Salt around the house

Take the salt and walk around your house. Say: this salt is for preservation and the destruction of principalities and powers. As I sprinkle this salt around my house, I destroy every principality and every power hovering around my household. Then open your door, cast them out — out of here, in the name of Jesus — and shut the door. Pastor Kiky notes you may do the same with the blood of Jesus, or anoint your home.

Prophetic direction

Salt water for an unproductive body

Standing on 2 Kings 2:19-22, put salt in water and pray: whatever is causing my vessel, my body, not to be productive, to be weak, to be sick; whatever is plaguing my system, whatever is tormenting my mind — by the power of the Holy Ghost, as I drink this salt water in the name of Jesus, may it become productive again, may I receive healing again, may I receive the power and the strength of the Lord.

Prophetic direction

Which salt to use

Any edible salt you already have is acceptable. Members in the room used Dead Sea salt and Himalayan salt, and Pastor Kiky's answer was simply to bring it and begin. Elisha's pattern also honours the vessel — bring me a new cruse (2 Kings 2:20) — so use a clean bowl.

Day 1

Repent and forgive

Scripture: Matthew 5:13

Study: Before any warfare, confess every known sin and forgive every person you are holding. Guard your savour.

Prophetic action: Set out your bowl and salt. Do the salt-water direction and pray the prayer points.

Day 2

Stand on the covenant

Scripture: 2 Chronicles 13:5

Study: Read the verse aloud three times. Write down the two words: for ever, and covenant.

Prophetic action: Salt-water direction. Declare: no enemy shall prevail in my territory.

Day 3

Faith with works

Scripture: James 2:26

Study: Ask the Holy Spirit for the one instruction you have left undone, and write it down.

Prophetic action: Salt-water direction. Then do the thing He showed you.

Day 4

Heal the source

Scripture: 2 Kings 2:21

Study: Name the area of your life that looks pleasant outside but produces nothing.

Prophetic action: Salt-water direction, praying specifically over that source.

Day 5

Walk your house

Scripture: 2 Chronicles 13:5

Study: Take the salt and walk every room. Declare preservation and the destruction of principalities and powers.

Prophetic action: Open the door, cast them out in the name of Jesus, shut the door, then worship.

Day 6

Protection and reversal

Scripture: Isaiah 54:17

Study: Pray Isaiah 54:17 over yourself, your household and your labour.

Prophetic action: Salt-water direction. Return every arrow to sender in Jesus' name.

Day 7

Seal and give thanks

Scripture: Luke 1:37

Study: Seal your deliverance with the blood of Jesus and thank God for what He has done.

Prophetic action: Salt-water direction. Then five honest minutes: Lord, I love You, thank You for Your protection.

Doctrine

How prophetic action stays biblical

Eight safeguards drawn from the teaching itself. They are the difference between scripture-backed obedience and superstition.

Safeguard 1

The element must be named in scripture

Salt (2 Chronicles 13:5; 2 Kings 2:20), water and oil and blood (1 John 5:8), the earth itself (Genesis 2:7; John 9:6). Pastor Kiky's rule: anything that stands with the scripture.

Safeguard 2

The word spoken must be a scripture God actually wrote

Never invent a verse. Never invent a doctrine. The authority is in the word, and the word is quoted, not improvised.

Safeguard 3

All the power belongs to Jesus Christ

2 Kings 2:21 credits the healing to the Lord alone: Thus saith the Lord, I have healed these waters. Salt would chemically make a bad spring worse — the sign is deliberately powerless so the glory is unambiguous.

Safeguard 4

The substance is never the agent

Pastor Kiky offers alternatives in the same breath — salt, the blood of Jesus, or anointing the home. Interchangeable signs prove the object is not the power.

Safeguard 5

The heart comes before the action

Matthew 5:13 — you are the salt. The standard deliverance flow puts praise, thanksgiving, confession of sins and forgiveness before any warfare.

Safeguard 6

It is obedience, not accumulation

Nothing builds up over seven days. Naaman dipped seven times according to the saying of the man of God (2 Kings 5:14) — the cleansing came on God's word, not on the river.

Safeguard 7

Never sourced from occult or New Age practice

L137 draws only on scripture and reputable Christian references. No divination, no charms, no spells, no astrology — the service itself renounces every enchantment, curse, divination and spell.

Safeguard 8

Medical care is not a lack of faith

The L137 aftercare standard: seek qualified pastoral and medical support when appropriate. Persistent or severe physical symptoms should be seen by a doctor.

Before anything else

Salvation & rededication prayer

No deliverance stands without Christ. If you have never given your life to Jesus, or you want to come back to Him tonight, pray this out loud.

Pray aloud
  • Lord Jesus, I come to You today just as I am.
  • I confess that I am a sinner and I need to be saved.
  • I believe that You are the Son of God, that You died on the cross for my sins, and that God raised You from the dead.
  • Forgive me for every sin I have committed, in thought, in word and in deed.
  • Wash me in Your precious blood.
  • I renounce the devil and all his works. I renounce every dark agreement made over my life.
  • Come into my heart today, Lord Jesus. Be my Lord and my Saviour.
  • Write my name in the Lamb's Book of Life.
  • Fill me with Your Holy Spirit and lead me all the days of my life.
  • Thank You for saving me. I am born again. In Jesus' mighty name. Amen.
