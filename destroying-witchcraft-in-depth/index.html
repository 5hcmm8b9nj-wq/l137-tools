Prophetic Direction

Take the blood of Jesus, go outside, read, pray, pour.

1

Take a small vessel of anointing oil or communion representing the blood of Jesus.

2

Go outside on your own property — home, driveway, yard, or property boundary.

3

Read the four scriptures aloud.

4

Pray the twenty-one prayer points, one by one, out loud.

5

Pour the blood on the ground.

6

Seal with Psalm 94:1 and say Amen.

Review, Recall, Reflect

Key Lessons

  • God Himself has promised to cut off witchcraft from His people (Micah 5:12).
  • No enchantment stands against a covenant child of God (Numbers 23:23).
  • The blood of the Lamb plus the word of your testimony defeats the accuser (Revelation 12:11).
  • The blood of Jesus on the ground speaks better things than the blood of Abel (Hebrews 12:24).
  • Household and ancestral warfare is real — and it is legally defeated at the cross.

Recall Challenge

Try to name — from memory — three household patterns this prayer targets. Then tap to compare.
