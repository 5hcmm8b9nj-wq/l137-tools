    The Closing Prayer

    Let Our Caves Become Training Grounds

    Father, in the name of Jesus Christ, transform everyone who has entered a season of distress, debt, rejection or discontentment. Let our caves become training grounds and not burial places.

    Raise among us people like Eleazar, whose hands clung to the sword; people like Shammah, who defended their assigned fields; people like Benaiah, who confronted lions; and people who strengthened David according to Your purpose.

    Give us courage without pride, loyalty without idolatry, strength without cruelty and service without selfish ambition. Let every person arise from obscurity and become useful in Your kingdom.

    May the Lord accomplish a great victory through us.

    In Jesus' Mighty Name · Amen
