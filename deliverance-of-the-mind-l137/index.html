Introduction

The battlefield is between your ears

The mind is the battleground of the believer. Before the enemy touches the body, the finances, the marriage, or the ministry, he first attacks the mind. Fear, torment, confusion, evil imagination, obsessive thoughts, doubt, unforgiveness, depression, condemnation, suicidal thoughts, filthy thoughts, mind-binding spirits, and voices — all of these are strategies to occupy the mind and rob the believer of peace, purpose, and dominion.

The good news of the gospel is this: Christ has already purchased the deliverance of your mind at Calvary. We do not fight for victory — we fight from victory.

2 Corinthians 10:3-5 · KJV

For though we walk in the flesh, we do not war after the flesh: (For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds;) Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ;

Five modules

The teaching

01The Mind as a Spiritual Battleground

What the mind is

The mind is part of the soul (mind, will, and emotions). It is the seat of thought, imagination, memory, reasoning, and decision. Because it is the control room of your life, the enemy targets it first. Whoever controls your thoughts controls your direction.

Three sources of thoughts

  • God — thoughts of life, faith, peace, love, obedience, and truth
  • Your own flesh or soul — thoughts shaped by your experiences, wounds, and habits
  • The devil and his demons — thoughts of fear, torment, lust, condemnation, suspicion, death, blasphemy, confusion

You must learn to discern the source of each thought. A thought is not automatically yours simply because it entered your head.

Romans 12:1-2 · KJV

I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service. And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect will of God.

1 Corinthians 2:16 · KJV

For who hath known the mind of the Lord, that he may instruct him? But we have the mind of Christ.

02Recognizing Demonic Oppression of the Mind

Common manifestations

  • Fear and torment — sudden, irrational fear, dread, panic, tormenting "what if" thoughts
  • Obsessive and repetitive thoughts — the same thought playing over and over that you cannot silence
  • Blasphemous thoughts — foul, accusing thoughts against God that feel forced into your mind
  • Suicidal or death thoughts — the enemy whispering that you should end your life
  • Sexual and filthy thoughts — unwanted lustful, perverse, or unclean thoughts
  • Voices — hearing voices that accuse, mock, threaten, seduce, or command
  • Confusion and forgetfulness — inability to think clearly or remember Scripture
  • Depression and heaviness — a spirit of heaviness that will not lift regardless of circumstances
  • Condemnation and self-hatred — the voice saying you are worthless or beyond forgiveness
  • Suspicion, jealousy, evil imagination — playing out betrayal or evil scenarios that have not happened
  • Nightmares and night terrors — attack on the mind during sleep
  • Mind-binding spirits — mental blockage while praying or reading the Bible; falling asleep in worship; sudden headaches during prayer
Ephesians 6:12 · KJV

For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.

Doors that give the enemy legal access

The enemy is a legalist. He looks for a legal doorway before he oppresses the mind.

  • Unrepented sin
  • Unforgiveness and bitterness
  • Occult involvement (past or present) — horoscopes, tarot, palm reading, ouija, witchcraft, spiritualism, false religions
  • Fear cultivated over time
  • Traumatic experiences never surrendered to Jesus
  • Consuming filthy media, music, and imagery
  • Word curses spoken by others or by yourself
  • Generational curses on the family line
  • Spoken agreement with lies ("I am worthless", "I will never be free")
  • Soul ties from ungodly relationships
  • Objects in the home connected to idolatry or the occult

Every door must be closed by repentance, renunciation, and the blood of Jesus before the mind can walk in lasting freedom.

03The Weapons of Mind Warfare

You are not defenseless. Christ has given the believer weapons that are mighty through God for pulling down strongholds in the mind.

Weapon 1 — The name of Jesus

Philippians 2:9-11 · KJV

Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name: That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth; And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

Every tormenting thought must bow at the name of Jesus.

Weapon 2 — The blood of Jesus

Revelation 12:11 · KJV

And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.

The blood cleanses, covers, and seals the mind from demonic access.

Weapon 3 — The Word of God

Hebrews 4:12 · KJV

For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.

Every tormenting lie must be answered with a Scripture. Jesus defeated the devil in the wilderness by quoting the Word — "It is written."

Weapon 4 — Prayer and fasting

Matthew 17:21 · KJV

Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.

Weapon 5 — Praise and worship

Psalm 22:3 · KJV

But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel.

Praise silences the enemy. Where God's presence dwells, tormenting spirits cannot stay.

Weapon 6 — Casting down imaginations

You do not have to entertain every thought. You catch it, judge it by the Word, and either receive it or cast it down. See 2 Corinthians 10:5 above.

Weapon 7 — Renewing the mind daily

Ephesians 4:22-24 · KJV

That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man, which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts; And be renewed in the spirit of your mind; And that ye put on the new man, which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness.

04The Deliverance Sequence for the Mind

Follow this sequence carefully. Do not skip a step. This is the standard L137 deliverance flow.

  1. Praise and worship — Fill the atmosphere with praise. Sing to the Lord. Enthrone Him above every tormenting thought.
  2. Thanksgiving — Thank God for salvation, the blood of Jesus, and the victory won at Calvary.
  3. Confession of sins — Repent of every sin the Holy Spirit brings to memory. Repent of agreeing with the enemy's lies.
  4. Forgive others — Release every person who has wounded you. Unforgiveness is a legal door. Close it.
  5. Invite the Holy Spirit — Ask Him to fill you and lead the deliverance. He is the Spirit of Truth.
  6. Renounce every doorway — By name, out loud, every doorway the enemy has used.
  7. Deliverance prayers and declarations — Command every tormenting spirit to go in the name of Jesus.
  8. Sealing with the blood of Jesus — Cover your mind, thoughts, imagination, dreams, and memory with the blood. Ask the Holy Spirit to fill every space vacated.
James 4:7 · KJV

Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

05Walking Daily in the Mind of Christ (Aftercare)

Deliverance is a gate. Walking in freedom is a lifestyle. After the session, the enemy will attempt to return. The following daily disciplines keep your mind swept, garnished, and occupied by Christ.

  • The Word every morning. Feed your mind on Scripture before the world feeds it noise.
  • Prayer and worship. Keep the atmosphere of God's presence over your home and your mind.
  • Guard your gates. Discipline what you watch, listen to, read, and speak.
  • Speak the promises. Declare Scripture out loud daily. Your ears must hear your mouth agreeing with God.
  • Christian fellowship. Walk with mature believers. Isolation is where the enemy re-enters.
  • Bring every thought captive as it comes.
  • Wear the whole armour of God every morning (Ephesians 6:10-18).
  • Sleep under the blood. Cover your night and your dreams before you close your eyes.
Isaiah 26:3 · KJV

Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.

Philippians 4:6-8 · KJV

Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus. Finally, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever things are honest, whatsoever things are just, whatsoever things are pure, whatsoever things are lovely, whatsoever things are of good report; if there be any virtue, and if there be any praise; think on these things.

Prayer Library

Fiery prayers for the mind

1. Praise & thanksgiving

I bless You, Lord, the God of my life, the God of my mind, the God of my thoughts. You are holy. You are worthy. You inhabit the praises of Your people. I praise You for salvation. I praise You for the blood of Jesus. I praise You for the sound mind You have given me. Every tormenting spirit is silenced under the sound of praise, in Jesus' name.

2. Confession of sins

Father, in the name of Jesus, I come with a repentant heart. I confess every sin the Holy Spirit brings to memory — known and unknown, secret and open, of thought, of word, and of deed. I repent of agreeing with the lies of the enemy against my mind, my identity, my future, and Your goodness. Wash me clean in the blood of Jesus.

3. Forgiving others

Father, I choose today to forgive every person who has wounded me — those who abused me, betrayed me, spoke curses over my mind, mocked me, abandoned me. I release them into Your hands. I break every unforgiveness cord between me and them. I walk free of them, in Jesus' name.

4. Renunciation of doorways

I renounce every involvement with the occult — past or present. I renounce every word curse spoken against my mind. I renounce every agreement I have made with the enemy's lies. I renounce every ungodly soul tie. I renounce every generational curse operating in my family line — every spirit of fear, torment, madness, suicide, depression, addiction, and mental oppression.

5. Casting out tormenting spirits

In the mighty name of Jesus Christ, I command every unclean spirit assigned against my mind to leave now. Every spirit of fear, torment, confusion, depression, heaviness, condemnation, suicide, lust, mind-binding, accusing voice, and nightmare — LEAVE NOW in the name of Jesus.

6. Sealing & aftercare

Father, I thank You for the deliverance of my mind. I cover my mind, my thoughts, my imagination, my memory, and my dreams with the blood of Jesus. Holy Spirit, fill every space the enemy has vacated. Occupy my mind. Teach me. Guide me. Guard me. It is finished. Amen.

Declare Out Loud

Fiery declarations over my mind

Speak these declarations out loud, every day. Your ears must hear your mouth agreeing with God.

  1. I have the mind of Christ — my mind is not a battlefield of the enemy, it is a sanctuary of the Holy Ghost.
  2. God has not given me the spirit of fear — He has given me power, love, and a sound mind.
  3. Every high thing that exalts itself against the knowledge of God in my mind is cast down by fire, in the name of Jesus.
  4. I refuse every tormenting thought. I refuse every accusing voice. I refuse every lying whisper. They have no home in me.
  5. My mind is stayed on Jesus. He keeps me in perfect peace, because I trust in Him.
  6. The peace of God, which passeth all understanding, guards my heart and my mind through Christ Jesus.
  7. I am delivered. I am healed. I am restored. I am the head and not the tail. I have a sound mind. It is done, in the name of Jesus.

Salvation & Rededication

Come to Jesus today

Deliverance of the mind belongs to those who are in Christ Jesus. If you have never given your life to Him, or if you have wandered from Him, pray this prayer out loud:

Lord Jesus, I come to You today. I confess that I am a sinner and I need You. I believe that You died on the cross for my sins and rose again on the third day. I ask You to forgive me, wash me in Your blood, and come into my heart as my Lord and Saviour. Fill me with Your Holy Spirit. I belong to You today. In Jesus' name. Amen.

If you prayed this prayer, welcome home. Find a Bible-believing church. Read your Bible daily. Pray. Walk with mature believers.
