Module 1

What Is a Soul Tie?

A soul tie is a bond between two people's souls. Some are godly and good — like marriage or true Christian friendship. Others are ungodly and hold you to someone the Lord never joined you to. The blood of Jesus and the sword of the Spirit cut the ungodly ones.

Your soul is your mind, will, and emotions. Every deep relationship leaves a mark on it. Some marks are from the Lord — one flesh with your husband or wife (Ephesians 5:31), covenant friendship like Jonathan and David (1 Samuel 18:1). Other marks are ungodly — the soul of Shechem "clave" to Dinah after he defiled her (Genesis 34:2–3), and Paul warns that "he which is joined to an harlot is one body" (1 Corinthians 6:16). Those ungodly ties can be cut by the sword of the Spirit.

A soul tie is a spiritual bond between two persons' inner beings — mind, will, emotions, memory, affection. Scripture speaks of soul-cleaving in several forms. In Genesis 34:2–3, after Shechem defiles Dinah, "his soul clave unto Dinah the daughter of Jacob." In 1 Samuel 18:1, "the soul of Jonathan was knit with the soul of David," and Jonathan "loved him as his own soul." In Genesis 2:24 and Ephesians 5:31, husband and wife become "one flesh." In 1 Corinthians 6:16, sexual union with a harlot makes the two "one body."

Godly soul ties — marriage, covenant friendship, spiritual mentorship in Christ — are gifts. Ungodly soul ties — sexual union outside marriage, covenants with someone under demonic influence, controlling relationships, dedications to false teachers, ungodly emotional dependence — become channels through which spirits travel, memories torment, and destinies get pulled off course. The remedy is not to hate the person; the remedy is to cut the tie, wash with the blood, and restore what was fragmented.

The Hebrew verb dabaq (to cleave, to cling, to be joined) is used in Genesis 2:24 for marital cleaving, in Genesis 34:3 for Shechem's soul cleaving to Dinah, and in Deuteronomy 10:20 for the believer's cleaving to the LORD. The same word underlies both the sacred bond and the defiled one — the difference is not the mechanism of soul-cleaving, but its object and its covenantal legitimacy. In 1 Samuel 18:1, the Niphal form niqsherâ ("was knit") describes Jonathan and David — a covenantal cleaving between two souls that becomes proverbial for holy friendship. Paul, writing to Corinth, uses σῶμα ἓν (one body) in 1 Corinthians 6:16, applying the marital "one flesh" language of Genesis 2:24 (which he explicitly quotes in the same passage) to illicit sexual union — arguing that even outside covenant the ontological bond still occurs. Soul-tie deliverance therefore stands squarely on the biblical anthropology of covenant, cleaving, and one-flesh union.

🔑 Key point

A soul tie is real. Scripture uses the same verb — cleave — for a wife cleaving to her husband, for Shechem cleaving to Dinah, and for the believer cleaving to the Lord (Deuteronomy 10:20). The bond is real. The blood of Jesus is stronger than any bond outside His will.

Module 2

Legal Rights — How Ungodly Ties Form

Demons operate by legal right (Colossians 2:14). An ungodly soul tie gives them a corridor. To be free, you name every door and close it.

Colossians 2:14 KJV
Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross.
2 Corinthians 6:14 KJV
Be ye not unequally yoked together with unbelievers: for what fellowship hath righteousness with unrighteousness? and what communion hath light with darkness?
Deuteronomy 10:20 KJV
Thou shalt fear the LORD thy God; him shalt thou serve, and to him shalt thou cleave, and swear by his name.

Doors that open ungodly soul ties

  • Sexual sin — every partner outside covenant marriage, from a single moment to a long relationship
  • Blood covenants — vows made by cutting, by mixing blood, by tribal rite, by gang initiation
  • Occult dedications — being brought to a diviner, a shrine, a "spiritual father" outside Christ
  • Controlling relationships — parents, spouses, siblings, "friends" who dominate the will
  • Unforgiven wounds — the person you refuse to forgive is a person you stay tied to
  • Hatred and bitterness — Hebrews 12:15, the root of bitterness ties the soul to what wounded it
  • Emotional idolatry — a person occupying the place only Jesus should hold
  • False teachers & cult mentors — dedications to leaders who preach another gospel (Galatians 1:8)
  • Rings, gifts, and tokens — dedicated objects that carry the memory of the covenant
Confession & Repentance
Father, in the name of Jesus, I confess and repent for every ungodly soul tie I have permitted — every sexual partner, every controlling relationship I stayed under, every unforgiveness I nursed, every dependency I chose over You, every dedication I received to anyone or anything outside of Christ. Wash me with the blood of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. Amen.
Which of these is NOT an ungodly soul tie?
A covenant marriage under God is a godly soul tie (Genesis 2:24, Ephesians 5:31) — not to be cut, but to be strengthened. Sexual sin, controlling relationships, and false-teacher dedications are ungodly ties that must be renounced and cut by the sword of the Spirit.
Module 3

Forgiveness First — You Cannot Cut What You Still Hold

Before you cut a soul tie you must release the person. Unforgiveness IS a tie. Pastor Kiky teaches this order every service, and it is exactly the Lord's order in Matthew 6:14–15.

Matthew 6:14–15 KJV
For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you: But if ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.
Mark 11:25 KJV
And when ye stand praying, forgive, if ye have ought against any: that your Father also which is in heaven may forgive you your trespasses.
Ephesians 4:31–32 KJV
Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil speaking, be put away from you, with all malice: And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ's sake hath forgiven you.
💡 Understand it

Forgiveness is not saying it was okay. Forgiveness is releasing the person into the Lord's hands so you are free. It is what you do so you can walk out of the room they hurt you in. It does not require reconciliation with an unrepentant abuser, and it does not require you to stay in an unsafe relationship.

Forgiveness prayer
Lord Jesus, in obedience to Your word, I forgive every person the Holy Spirit is bringing to my mind right now — every sexual partner, every controlling relative, every leader who abused me, every friend who betrayed me, every parent who wounded me. I release each of them into Your hands. I am not their judge. I do not carry their burden anymore. I refuse bitterness. I choose the freedom of Ephesians 4:32. In the name of Jesus. Amen.
Self-forgiveness
Lord Jesus, I also forgive myself for every door I opened. Where I have already repented, I refuse to keep condemning what You have already washed. Romans 8:1 — there is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus. I receive Your forgiveness. Amen.
Why does forgiveness have to come before cutting the soul tie?
Unforgiveness ties you to the person who wounded you (Hebrews 12:15). The Lord Himself puts forgiveness before answered prayer in Matthew 6:14–15 and Mark 11:25. You cannot cut a soul tie you are still holding in your heart. Release the person; keep the boundary.
Module 4

Cut the Tie by the Sword of the Spirit

Ephesians 6:17 gives the offensive weapon — the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God. Speak the Word aloud as you cut. Follow the L137 flow: Praise → Thanksgiving → Confession → Forgive others → Invite the Holy Spirit — then pray.

Ephesians 6:17 KJV
And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.
Hebrews 4:12 KJV
For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.
Colossians 2:14 KJV
Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross.
Prayer 1 · Renunciation
In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I renounce every ungodly soul tie — every sexual soul tie, every blood soul tie, every covenant soul tie, every hatred soul tie, every dependence soul tie, every dedication soul tie, every mentorship soul tie made outside the will of God. I renounce them all. Amen.
Prayer 2 · Cutting
By the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God (Ephesians 6:17), I cut every ungodly cord that ties my soul to anyone the Lord has not joined me to. I cut every cord to every sexual partner outside my covenant marriage. I cut every cord to every abuser. I cut every cord to every false teacher, occult leader, and controlling relative. I cut every cord to every ex-fiancé, ex-lover, and one-night partner. Blood of Jesus, wash the cut so it cannot be re-tied. Amen.
Prayer 3 · Return the pieces
Every fragment of my soul held by another person, return to me now, in the name of Jesus. Every memory, every affection, every attachment that was stolen when the covenant was broken — return to me. Every fragment of another person's soul held in me, I release now, in the name of Jesus. My soul is my own again, submitted to Christ. Amen.
Prayer 4 · Return the tokens
In the name of Jesus, I return in the spirit every ring, every gift, every letter, every ungodly token that carried the covenant. I refuse to keep them as trophies of what You have set me free from. Where I still hold physical objects that carry that memory, Holy Spirit, show me and give me the courage to remove them. Amen.
Prayer 5 · Break bloodline soul ties
Every bloodline soul tie — every ungodly cleaving that runs in my family, every generational pattern of adultery, control, codependency, occult mentorship, or dedication to false powers — I break you now by the blood of Jesus. Ezekiel 18:20 — the soul that sinneth, it shall die. My ancestors' iniquity does not stand on me. My children will not inherit it. Amen.
What does the Bible call the sword of the Spirit?
Ephesians 6:17 KJV — "And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God." Hebrews 4:12 adds it is sharp enough to divide asunder soul and spirit. Speak Scripture aloud as you cut.
Module 5

Restoration & Declarations

Once the ungodly ties are cut, the soul must be joined afresh to the Lord and to godly bonds — spouse, children, true brothers and sisters in Christ.

1 Corinthians 6:17 KJV
But he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit.
Psalm 23:3 KJV
He restoreth my soul: he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name's sake.
Joel 2:25 KJV
And I will restore to you the years that the locust hath eaten, the cankerworm, and the caterpiller, and the palmerworm, my great army which sent among you.
Restoration prayer
Holy Spirit, join my soul afresh to Jesus Christ my Lord. Let every godly bond in my life — my covenant spouse, my children, my true brothers and sisters in Christ, my Bible-teaching church — be strengthened. Let every ungodly bond stay severed forever. Restore the years the locust ate — Joel 2:25. Amen.

Module 6

Sealing & Aftercare

After the tie is cut and the fragments are returned, the door must be sealed and the empty room filled — otherwise the enemy returns.

Matthew 12:43–45 KJV
When the unclean spirit is gone out of a man, he walketh through dry places, seeking rest, and findeth none. Then he saith, I will return into my house from whence I came out; and when he is come, he findeth it empty, swept, and garnished. Then goeth he, and taketh with himself seven other spirits more wicked than himself, and they enter in and dwell there: and the last state of that man is worse than the first.
Exodus 12:13 KJV
And the blood shall be to you for a token upon the houses where ye are: and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you, when I smite the land of Egypt.
Romans 12:2 KJV
And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.
Sealing prayer
Father, in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I seal every soul tie cut tonight with the blood of Jesus. I close every door the enemy walked through. I close every memory the tie fed on. I close every dream door. The blood of Jesus is on the doorpost of my mind, my will, my emotions, my body, my marriage, and my children. When You see the blood, You pass over me. Amen.
Filling prayer
Holy Spirit, fill every empty room the deliverance left behind. Fill me with the Word, with worship, with holiness, with the fear of the Lord, with true love. Let my house not be empty, swept and garnished — let it be full of You. Amen.
Practical boundaries · the next 30 days

Boundaries are not unforgiveness — they are stewardship. Block or mute the phone number. Come off the shared social feeds. Do not visit the shared places alone. Change the passwords. Remove the physical tokens. If the relationship was abusive, involve trusted pastoral leadership and, where appropriate, civil authorities. Boundaries protect the sealed door.

Aftercare · the next 7 days

Read the Word daily (start with Psalm 51 and Ephesians 4). Pray morning and night. Take communion at least once this week and plead the better covenant. Return to a Bible-teaching church and to your prayer group. Bring persistent emotional pain to a trusted Christian counsellor as well as to prayer — professional support is a gift from the Father of lights (James 1:17).

Module 7

Final Review & Completion

Answer the four questions below to complete the course.

Which pair of Scriptures grounds cutting a sexual soul tie?
1 Corinthians 6:16 establishes the ungodly one-flesh bond; Ephesians 6:17 gives the sword of the Spirit — the Word — as the weapon by which it is cut. Speak the Word aloud as you cut, and wash with the blood of Jesus.
Why must forgiveness come before cutting?
The Lord Himself places forgiveness before answered prayer (Matthew 6:14–15, Mark 11:25). Unforgiveness ties you to the person who wounded you. Forgiveness releases them to God so you can walk out free. It does not require reconciliation with an unrepentant abuser and it does not require you to stay unsafe.
What must follow the cutting of an ungodly soul tie?
Matthew 12:43–45 warns an empty, swept, garnished house invites seven worse spirits. Seal with the blood of Jesus, fill with the Holy Spirit, set boundaries with the person, and walk out the 7-day aftercare. No salt baths — that is not a biblical instruction from L137.
Which declaration best sums up soul-tie deliverance?
This declaration captures both sides of soul-tie deliverance — you protect the godly bonds and refuse the ungodly ones. It stands on Matthew 19:6 and on the sufficiency of the blood of Jesus over every counterfeit union.
