Book 2 · Interactive deliverance course

Walk through the fire, module by module.

A nine-module deliverance sequence drawn from Book 2. Tick each module as you complete it. Your progress is saved on this device only. Anoint your Bible before you begin — this is not a race, this is deliverance.

A note on anointing your Bible: this is a simple act of consecration — setting your Bible apart with a drop of oil as a prayerful sign of faith, as oil is used throughout Scripture (James 5:14; Psalm 23:5). The oil has no power in itself; the power belongs to the Lord and to His Word. It is an aid to devotion, never a requirement for God to hear you.

A word before we begin

Discernment Is Not Suspicion

Do not accuse, confront, expose, or sever a relationship solely because of a dream, a feeling, a suspicion, a prophecy, or a prayer impression. Test every matter by Scripture, by observable conduct, by wise counsel, by prayer, and by truth. Biblical discernment produces wisdom, peace, self-control, and righteousness — never paranoia, retaliation, or reckless accusation.

Where you suspect abuse, threats, stalking, poisoning, violence, or criminal conduct, contact appropriate professional or legal help. This teaching is not a substitute for pastoral counsel, medical care, or law enforcement.

Test every matter by:

  • 1 Thessalonians 5:21 (KJV)"Prove all things; hold fast that which is good."
  • James 3:17 (KJV)"But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy."
  • Matthew 18:15 (KJV)"Moreover if thy brother shall trespass against thee, go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone: if he shall hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother."
  • Proverbs 18:13 (KJV)"He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him."
  • Proverbs 18:17 (KJV)"He that is first in his own cause seemeth just; but his neighbour cometh and searcheth him."
  • Galatians 6:1 (KJV)"Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself lest thou also be tempted."
  • Romans 12:18 (KJV)"If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men."

    Acts 3:19 (KJV)

    "Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord."

    The Holy Ghost Fire ✓

    Hebrews 12:29 (KJV)

    "For our God is a consuming fire."

    You cannot burn an assignment with cold water, a strong opinion, or human strategy. You burn it with the fire that fell on Sinai, on Carmel, and in the upper room. The fire is a Person — the Holy Spirit Himself — and it consumes hidden altars, hidden assignments, hidden objects, and hidden bondages. You are the bush that burns and is not burnt.

    Editorial expansion based on the teaching

    Pray Aloud: Lord Jesus, You baptise Your people with the Holy Ghost and with fire. I ask You to baptise me now. Consume every hidden altar, every hidden assignment, every hidden object, and every hidden bondage inside me. Let me carry Your presence into every room I enter. Amen.

    Declare: I declare that my God is a consuming fire, and every hidden altar raised against me is consumed by the fire of the Holy Ghost.

  • Module 04

    The Enemy in the Household ✓

    Micah 7:6 (KJV)

    "For the son dishonoureth the father, the daughter riseth up against her mother, the daughter in law against her mother in law; a man's enemies are the men of his own house."

    The enemy sometimes wears the face of a family member. If the attack comes from within the house, you are not disqualified — you are standing where the Word said the battle sometimes lies. Take it to the Lord in prayer before you take it anywhere else. Pray for the person, then pray against the assignment. Where the Lord finally directs separation, do it in peace.

    Editorial expansion based on the teaching

    Pray Aloud: Father, according to Micah 7:6, expose every foe within my household who is being used against my life. Sever the assignment. Break the pattern in the bloodline. Restore the bonds of my family under the blood of Jesus. Guard my mouth, that I speak only what You have told me to speak. Amen.

    Declare: I declare that every enemy in my household is exposed by the light of the Lord, and no household weapon shall prosper against my destiny.

  • Module 05

    Sanctifying the Meal ✓

    1 Timothy 4:4-5 (KJV)

    "For every creature of God is good, and nothing to be refused, if it be received with thanksgiving: For it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer."

    The friendly enemy is not always a person. It is also every word spoken over the food and drink you consume. Every meal must pass under the sanctifying word of God and the sanctifying prayer of the believer — thanksgiving, sanctification by the Word, cleansing by the blood, invocation of the fire, and blessing over the body. This is faith, not fear: the believer prays and eats, sanctifies and enjoys.

    Editorial expansion based on the teaching

    Pray Aloud: Father, according to 1 Timothy 4:4-5, sanctify every meal I will ever eat by Your Word and by prayer. I plead the blood of Jesus over every plate and cup, and I declare every word not of the Lord neutralised. Keep my spirit sensitive when the Holy Spirit whispers, "No." Amen.

    Declare: I declare that every meal I eat is sanctified by the Word of God and prayer, and no word spoken over my food shall enter my body, in Jesus' name.

Section C · Biblical Discernment or Unhealthy Suspicion?

Read each scenario and choose. The answer and a one-line teaching will appear.

A relative in your household has, over several years and in observable ways, repeatedly undermined your marriage, lied to divide you, and rejoiced at your setbacks.

Discernment. Observable conduct over time — pray for the person, then take it to Matthew 18:15 and wise counsel; guard your household in prayer.

A family member cooked a meal you did not expect, so you conclude they have poisoned it and refuse to speak to them.

Suspicion. Sanctify the meal by the Word and prayer in faith, not fear (1 Timothy 4:4-5). A surprise is not evidence of harm.

You sense a check in your spirit about a household matter; when you ask two mature believers, both independently sense the same concern and point to observable facts.

Discernment. Multiple witnesses of mature believers is a biblical pattern (2 Corinthians 13:1).

Your spouse was quiet at dinner, so you assume they are secretly working with an enemy against you.

Suspicion. One quiet evening is not evidence. Proverbs 18:13 — do not answer a matter before you hear it. Ask, do not accuse.

The Salvation Prayer

Come home to the Lord

If you have never truly given your life to the Lord Jesus Christ, or you drifted away and want to come home, this is the moment.

Pray this prayer aloud, from your own mouth, wherever you are:

Verbatim salvation prayer format used across L137 services

  • Lord Jesus Christ, I come to You just as I am. I confess that I am a sinner and have lived apart from You.
  • I believe that You are the Son of the Living God, that You died on the cross for my sins, and that You rose again on the third day.
  • I renounce every other god, every other altar, and every hidden thing that has ruled me.
  • Wash me by Your blood, make me new, and fill me with Your Holy Spirit.
  • From this day forward, I belong to You. I will follow You, read Your Word, and walk with Your people. In Your mighty name I pray. Amen.

If you prayed this prayer, all of heaven is rejoicing over you today (Luke 15:10). Reach out to the L137 team via our Linktree, find a Bible-believing local church, and begin reading the gospel of John — one chapter a day.

Your next 30 days

A simple plan to stay in the fire

  1. Days 1–7: Read one chapter of the gospel of John each morning, and thank God aloud before you rise.
  2. Days 8–14: Begin the nine modules of this course — one module a day — praying every prayer aloud.
  3. Days 15–21: Join a Bible-believing local church and the L137 WhatsApp community through our Linktree.
  4. Days 22–28: Pray the Kept-Bones declarations of Psalm 34 over your household each evening.
  5. Days 29–30: Write down what God has done, and share your testimony with one person the Lord brings to mind.

This 30-day plan is a gentle suggestion to help you build a rhythm — not a rule. Walk at the pace of grace.
