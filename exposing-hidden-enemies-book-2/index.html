A word before we begin

Discernment Is Not Suspicion

Do not accuse, confront, expose, or sever a relationship solely because of a dream, a feeling, a suspicion, a prophecy, or a prayer impression. Test every matter by Scripture, by observable conduct, by wise counsel, by prayer, and by truth. Biblical discernment produces wisdom, peace, self-control, and righteousness — never paranoia, retaliation, or reckless accusation.

Where you suspect abuse, threats, stalking, poisoning, violence, or criminal conduct, contact appropriate professional or legal help. This teaching is not a substitute for pastoral counsel, medical care, or law enforcement.

Test every matter by:

  • 1 Thessalonians 5:21 (KJV)"Prove all things; hold fast that which is good."
  • James 3:17 (KJV)"But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy."
  • Matthew 18:15 (KJV)"Moreover if thy brother shall trespass against thee, go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone: if he shall hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother."
  • Proverbs 18:13 (KJV)"He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him."
  • Proverbs 18:17 (KJV)"He that is first in his own cause seemeth just; but his neighbour cometh and searcheth him."
  • Galatians 6:1 (KJV)"Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself lest thou also be tempted."
  • Romans 12:18 (KJV)"If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men."

The heart of Book 2

What is Book 2 about?

Book 2 teaches the believer how to stand after the enemy has been exposed — by the fire, in the household, at the table, over the mouth, and through the assurance of Psalm 34.

Part 01

The Holy Ghost Fire

You cannot burn an assignment with human effort. Our God is a consuming fire, the Spirit works not by might nor by power, and His ministers are a flame of fire dispatched into every midnight prison.

Part 02

The Enemy in the Household

A man's foes shall be they of his own household. From Cain and Abel to Jacob and Esau, to Absalom, Adonijah, and Judas — the sharpest opposition can come from within the house.

Part 03

Sacred Speech and the Sanctified Meal

Every meal is sanctified by the word of God and prayer. The mouth of Zacharias was closed to guard a promise, and the ruler's discerning eye scatters evil from the room.

Part 04

Standing in Victory

Many are the afflictions of the righteous — but the Lord delivers out of them all and keeps every bone. What the enemy meant for evil, God means for good, for with God nothing shall be impossible.

Part One · Chapters 1–3

The Holy Ghost Fire

Before the believer confronts a single enemy, the fire must fall on the altar. This is where Book 2 arms the reader.

Chapter 01 · Hebrews 12:29

Our God Is a Consuming Fire

The fire falls where the altar is built. It is a Person — the Holy Spirit Himself — and it consumes hidden altars, hidden assignments, hidden objects, and hidden bondages. You are the bush that burns and is not burnt.

Chapter 02 · Zechariah 4:6

Not by Might, Not by Power

Every mountain has already been sentenced: before Zerubbabel it shall become a plain. Lay down might, lay down human power, and let the Spirit lift the standard against the flood — then shout, "Grace, grace."

Chapter 03 · Hebrews 1:7 · Acts 12

Angels of Fire and the Midnight Prison

The Lord makes His ministers a flame of fire. When the church prayed without ceasing, the angel entered Peter's prison, the chains fell, and the iron gate opened of its own accord — then delivered him back to the fellowship.

Part Two · Chapters 4–7

The Enemy in the Household

Book 2 tells the hard truth: the enemy sometimes wears the face of a family member.

Chapter 04 · Micah 7:6

A Man's Foes Shall Be They of His Own House

Micah names four patterns of household collapse. If the attack comes from within, you are not disqualified — you are standing where the Word said the battle sometimes lies. Take it to the Lord first, and separate, if you must, in peace.

Chapter 05 · Genesis 4:8–10

Cain and Abel — the First Friendly Enemy

The first murder was a household murder. Cain's real anger was not at Abel but at the God who favoured him. Hide under the blood of Jesus, which speaks better things than that of Abel, and leave vengeance to the Lord.

Chapter 06 · Genesis 25 & 27

Jacob, Esau, Rebekah — When the Home Is Divided

Parental favouritism is a doorway the enemy loves. Rebekah managed the promise instead of trusting God; Esau waited for the funeral to strike. But at Peniel Jacob was broken, and twenty years later the brothers fell on each other's necks and wept.

Chapter 07 · 2 Sam 15 · 1 Kings 1 · Matt 26

Absalom, Adonijah, Judas

Three household enemies, three weapons — betrayal at the gate, at the table, and at the kiss. Do not attend the false table; build the true altar. And for every Judas kiss, let the word "friend" rise, for the sake of your own freedom.

Part Three · Chapters 8–10

Sacred Speech and the Sanctified Meal

The friendly enemy is not always a person. It is also every word spoken over what you eat, and every promise your mouth exposed too soon.

Chapter 08 · 1 Timothy 4:4–5

The Cup and the Plate

Every creature of God is sanctified by the word of God and prayer. Give thanks, sanctify by the Word, plead the blood, invoke the fire, and bless the body — and obey the Holy Spirit when He simply says, "No, do not eat that."

Chapter 09 · Luke 1:18–22

Zacharias and the Muted Mouth

The Lord closed Zacharias's mouth to protect the miracle growing in secret. Do not expose too much of what you cherish. Guard the mouth through the season of pregnancy — and when the promise is named, let the first word be praise.

Chapter 10 · Philippians 1:10

The Ruler with Discernment

Love, then knowledge, then judgment, then the excellent, then sincerity without offence. Discernment grows in the Word and in obedience. Like the ruler of Proverbs 20:8, the trained eye scatters evil from the room without a fight.

Part Four · Chapters 11–13

Standing in Victory

Nothing is wrong with you. Many are the afflictions of the righteous — but the Lord delivers out of them all, and the mighty rise again.

Chapter 11 · Psalm 34:19–22

Many Are the Afflictions — the Kept Bones

The Lord keeps every bone — your marriage, your children, your mind, your ministry, your dignity. Not one shall be broken. The trusting soul is redeemed and shall not be desolate.

Chapter 12 · Genesis 50:20

Ye Meant It for Evil, God Meant It for Good

Joseph did not deny the evil — he named it, then held two truths at once. God brought good out of the pit to save much people alive. Weep, forgive, and do not sit in the judge's seat: "Am I in the place of God?"

Chapter 13 · 2 Samuel 1 · Isaiah 6:1

How Are the Mighty Fallen

The mighty do fall, and David wept without spiritualising the grief. But in the year that Uzziah died, Isaiah saw the Lord high and lifted up. Let the eye lift. The next David is being called from the shepherd fields.
