What This Teaching Covers

The serpent is the devil

Revelation 12:9 identifies the serpent as the devil, cast down from heaven. Because he is a defeated foe, the believer is given authority to tread upon him.

How serpentine spirits enter

Through inherited worship and ancestral covenants, ungodly ties, and the works of occult powers — the serpent looks to the bloodline.

The deceiver and his poison

The serpent works by subtlety and deception, attacking at your rising and in your wilderness, and biting where the hedge is broken.

The way out

Repentance, the blood of Jesus, and the fire of the Holy Ghost force the viper out. Then the emptied vessel is filled so nothing returns. See the five steps ↓

How to Overcome

Overcoming the Serpent Spirit

The serpent spirit — also called the serpentine, python, cobra, viper, or ancient serpent spirit — represents the biblical archetype of Satan himself (Revelation 12:9) and manifests as deception, the strangulation of destiny, financial stagnation, fear, marine and spirit-spouse attacks, and physical or emotional oppression. Overcoming it involves biblical authority, repentance, renunciation, and sustained warfare prayer — not mere emotional intensity.

Biblical Foundation for Authority Over Serpents

The core scriptural basis is Luke 10:19, where Jesus gives believers authority “to tread on serpents and scorpions” and over all the power of the enemy. Genesis 3:15 — God’s declaration that the offspring of the woman would “crush” the serpent’s head — is the pattern for total, not partial, victory. Revelation 12:9-11 identifies “that serpent, called the Devil and Satan” and teaches that believers overcome him “by the blood of the Lamb and the word of their testimony.”

Step 1

Repentance & Closing Legal Doors

Serpentine spirits gain “legal rights” through specific sins or generational involvement — idolatry (including ancestral worship of snakes or marine deities), sexual covenants with someone carrying the spirit, possessing items marked with serpent symbols, pride, unforgiveness, deception, manipulation, rebellion, and fear. Confess and repent for these doors, then explicitly renounce and cancel every covenant and agreement — knowingly or unknowingly made — with spirits such as leviathan, python, cobra, viper, and anaconda, in the name of Jesus Christ.

Step 2

Put On the Armor of God

Ephesians 6:10-17 is the standard framework before direct confrontation — the belt of truth, breastplate of righteousness, shoes of peace, shield of faith, helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit (the Word of God) used to destroy every serpentine spirit affecting your life. This reframes the battle as one fought with truth and Scripture rather than fear.

Step 3

Direct Rebuke & Command to Leave

After repentance and armor, address the spirit by name (Leviathan, Python, Cobra, Viper, Anaconda, Ancient Serpent) and command it to leave in the name of Jesus Christ. Bind the spirit, render it “venomless,” command it to “return to sender,” and decree that whatever it has swallowed — health, finances, destiny, gifts — be restored, drawing on the imagery of Moses’ rod swallowing Pharaoh’s serpents (Exodus 7:12).

Step 4

Exposure by Fire & the Blood of Jesus

Pray for the fire of the Holy Spirit and the blood of Jesus to expose hidden serpentine agents, altars, and covenants operating in dreams, foundations, or bloodlines, since much serpentine activity works through generational or ancestral channels. This is tied to Revelation 12:11 — believers overcome “by the blood of the Lamb.”

Step 5

Sustained Warfare & Watchfulness

The serpent attacked Adam and Eve specifically to separate them from God’s presence, so continued praise, prayer, and fellowship with God are ongoing protection rather than a one-time event. Remain alert (“be not ignorant of the devices of the devil”), continue in prayer and fasting as Jesus modeled in the wilderness, and seek sound teaching to recognise recurring patterns of serpentine attack.
