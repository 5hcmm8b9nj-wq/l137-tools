Breaking Ungodly Soul Ties

A biblical deliverance teaching by Pastor Kiky Ocansey on what a soul tie is, how ungodly soul ties are formed, the signs that one is operating, and what they take from a life. The teaching moves from diagnosis to freedom: repentance, renunciation spoken out loud, the blood of Jesus, cutting emotional and spiritual access, filling the void with the Holy Ghost, and being joined to the Lord as one spirit. It closes in restoration — open doors, returned identity, and recovered virtues through the finished work of Christ.

This is a full interactive multimodal course — eight modules, every KJV scripture in full, prayer library, declaration library, flashcards, recall challenge, visual story, and an end-of-teaching review with a completion badge. Learn by reading, hearing, seeing, interacting, praying and declaring.

What this teaching covers

Eight modules, drawn from the service

Every module follows the teaching as it was delivered — the scriptures in full King James Version, the prayer points, and the declarations exactly as they were spoken in the room.

Module 1

What Is a Soul Tie?

A soul tie is a deep spiritual and emotional bond that joins two people at the level of mind, will, emotion and sometimes spirit.
Module 2

Godly vs Ungodly Soul Ties

Not all soul ties are evil. God ordains bonds that build, open doors and elevate — and the pattern of the godly bond exposes the counterfeit.
Module 3

How Ungodly Soul Ties Are Formed

Five doorways named in the teaching: sexual sin outside God's will, toxic and abusive relationships, past relationships never released, trauma and deep emotional bonds, and covenants and spiritual agreements.
Module 4

Signs an Ungodly Soul Tie Is Operating

The nine signs Pastor Kiky listed for the room — the symptoms that reveal something deeper is at work.
Module 5

What Ungodly Soul Ties Do

Delay, blockage, pollution, demonic doors, transferred burdens — and the pattern of Samson, whose strength went from him through a wrong connection.
Module 6

Freedom Through the Son

The good news of the teaching: Jesus did not only come to forgive sins — He came to break every bondage, and God has declared the covenant with death disannulled.
Module 7

Seven Steps to Break Ungodly Soul Ties

Repent · renounce and denounce out loud · apply the blood · cut emotional and spiritual access · remove the objects · fill the void with the Holy Ghost · be joined to Christ.
Module 8

Restoration and Open Doors

God is the Father of open doors. What the ungodly tie shut, He opens; what was stolen, He returns; and favour surrounds the delivered believer as a shield.

Scripture highlight

The covenant with death is disannulled

Pastor Kiky built the teaching on Isaiah 28:18. Some — or all — of a person's ungodly soul ties are covenants with death, because these ties destroy a life, mislead it, and take its original glory and potential. And God declares that such a covenant shall be disannulled, and the agreement with hell shall not stand.

Salvation and rededication

Give your life to Jesus Christ

If you have never given your life to Jesus Christ, or you have wandered away and want to come back to Him, pray this prayer out loud where you are:

Salvation and rededication prayer — pray aloud
  • Lord Jesus, I come to you today just as I am. I confess that I am a sinner and I need you. I believe that you are the Son of God, that you died on the cross for my sins, and that God raised you from the dead. Forgive me of every sin. Wash me in your blood. Come into my heart and be my Lord and my Saviour. I renounce the devil and all his works. I renounce every ungodly connection and every ungodly covenant in my life. From today I belong to you. Write my name in the Lamb's Book of Life. Fill me with your Holy Spirit and teach me to walk with you. Thank you for saving me, in Jesus' name. Amen.
