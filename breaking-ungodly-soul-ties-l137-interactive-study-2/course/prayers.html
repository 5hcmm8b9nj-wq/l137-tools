Thanksgiving

The service opened in thanksgiving before any warfare began — thanking the Lord for preservation, provision and covering.

Pray aloud
  • Father, we give you thanks for our going out and our coming in.
  • We give God thanks, for He has done exceedingly, abundantly and above all for our families and our loved ones.
  • Thank you, Lord, that accidents were cancelled, evil was diverted, and death was rejected on our behalf.
  • Father, we give you thanks — for your covering has been our shield, our defence and our refuge.
  • Jesus, Son of God, who has no beginning and no end — thank you so much for what You have done in my life.

Repentance and Confession of Sins

The corporate prayer of confession, prayed aloud by the whole room before deliverance.

Pray aloud
  • Father, forgive me of my sins. Wash it away. Wash it away. Do not allow my sins to stand in the way of my breakthrough, in the name of Jesus.
  • Forgive me, Lord Jesus. In any way I have sinned against you, I repent. I confess my sins in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • Every sin of disobedience, every hidden sin tonight, every sin in my thoughts, in my words, in my actions — Lord, forgive me, in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.
  • Anything in me that is not pleasing to you, Lord, I surrender it tonight. Cleanse me. Cleanse me from every sin. Every power of sin over my life, Lord, let it be broken by the blood of Jesus.
  • Every habit that draws me away from you, O Lord, let it be destroyed tonight.
  • I receive grace to live a righteous life. I receive strength to overcome every temptation. Sin will not have dominion over my life. Sin will not stand in the way of my breakthrough.
  • Lord, cleanse me tonight. Remove every barrier to breakthrough. Let every sin standing between me and my breakthrough be removed tonight. Every spiritual blockage that is caused by sin, I command it tonight to clear away.
  • Every delay that is caused by disobedience, I ask the Lord, let it be reversed tonight, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.
  • I plead the blood of Jesus over my life. Blood of Jesus, wash me tonight. Blood of Jesus, purify my spirit. Blood of Jesus, remove every stain of sin. Let the blood speak mercy. Let the blood speak restoration. Let the blood speak deliverance.
  • I pray for restoration of my joy. I pray for restoration of my life. I pray for restoration of my spiritual fire.
  • Create in me a clean heart, and renew a right spirit within me, by the power of the Holy Ghost.
  • Lord, forgive me of every sin tonight. Wash it with your blood. Forgive and cleanse me now, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.

Inviting the Holy Spirit

The room invited the Holy Spirit to lead the prayer and to bring hidden ties to remembrance.

Pray aloud
  • Begin to speak in tongues by the power of the Holy Ghost.
  • Invite the Spirit of the Lord into this atmosphere. Invite the Spirit of the Lord in the name of Jesus. Let that Spirit lead us. Let that Spirit surround us.
  • In the name of Jesus, we lay down our plans, we lay down our thoughts, we lay down our wills. Let the Holy Spirit take control, in the mighty name of Jesus. Let His will be done in our life and in our destiny.
  • Holy Ghost, we invite you. Holy Ghost, come and have your way. Holy Spirit, lead us. Holy Spirit, take full control of us. Holy Spirit, revive us with holy fire.
  • Holy Ghost fire, fall upon us. Let every bondage be broken in our life. Holy Ghost fire, saturate our atmosphere.
  • Let nothing hinder our prayer. Let nothing hinder our deliverance. Let nothing hinder our understanding.
  • Release your power upon us tonight. Release your fresh anointing upon us tonight. Release your divine authority upon us tonight.
  • We receive power to pray. We receive power to overcome. We receive your power, Holy Spirit, to win our battles tonight.
  • Take over tonight. Lead us tonight. We call upon your name.
  • Let our prayer life catch fire. We receive fresh fire. Let the atmosphere be changed, Holy Spirit. No demon can resist the fire. We destroy every entity in our atmosphere.
  • Holy Ghost, go ahead of us, in the mighty name of Jesus. Thank you, Holy Spirit. Amen.

Deliverance Prayers Over the Room

Pastor Kiky's prayers over the whole room, prayed exactly as spoken in the service.

Pray aloud
  • I plead the blood of Jesus upon your spirit. I plead the blood of Jesus upon your soul. I plead the blood of Jesus upon your body.
  • I apply the blood of Jesus to break every demonic yoke in your life and in your destiny.
  • May the Lord have mercy and forgive you of every sin.
  • I bind every demon assigned to enforce any evil covenant in your life.
  • I command the strong force behind your relationship, behind your marriage, to catch fire tonight.
  • Let go of ungodly ties with your ex, with whom you had sex in the past. Somebody shout: “Break by fire!”
  • Every altar oppressing your life as a result of ungodly relationships, ungodly marriages — somebody say: “Break by fire!”
  • Let the power that is using your blood against your marriage be destroyed, by the power of the Holy Ghost.
  • I break and I loose you from every collective curse.
  • Blood of Jesus, break the strongholds of wickedness upon your life.
  • I abandon any evil covenant in your father's house, in your mother's house.
  • I withdraw your glory from any man, any woman that is using it. Someone shout: “My glory, be withdrawn right now!” Be withdrawn from any man, any woman that is using it.
  • All your lost virtues — somebody say: “Come back to me!” All my lost virtues, come back to me, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • I command all foundational strongmen that were transferred to your life — let them be paralysed.
  • I renounce every conscious and unconscious ungodly covenant with the dead.
  • In the mighty name of Jesus, I renounce tonight every ungodly covenant binding you as a result of your sexual relations with your ex.
  • I break every sexual agreement between you and any man or woman in your past, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • Every hold on your life and on your destiny by foundational idol powers — let it be destroyed tonight, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.
  • Every binding of your soul to the water, to the tree, to the village — somebody shout: “Expire!”

Corporate Deliverance Prayer — Breaking the Soul Tie

Prayed by the whole room, repeated after Pastor Kiky, while holding the communion wine and looking at the names written on the paper. The blood of Jesus — Christ's finished work — is what breaks the tie.

Pray aloud
  • By the power of the Holy Ghost, by the blood of Jesus, every ungodly soul tie in my life, break now, in the name of Jesus.
  • I disconnect myself from every past relationship that was not ordained by God.
  • By the blood of Jesus, every spiritual cord tying me to my past, be cut off.
  • By the blood of Jesus, I break every emotional bondage to any ungodly connection, to every covenant, to every ungodly relationship.
  • I am reconciled by the blood of Jesus. I drink the blood of Jesus.
  • I release my soul from every captivity, every evil exchange, every ungodly thought, by the blood of Jesus.
  • By the blood of Jesus, I recover my peace. I recover my identity. I recover my virtues.
  • By the power of the Holy Ghost, every invisible chain connecting me to wrong people, be sent back by the blood of Jesus.
  • By the blood of Jesus, I nullify every demonic agreement, knowingly or unknowingly.
  • By the blood of Jesus, I break free from any toxic and manipulative relationship.
  • Every spirit attached to me, I cast you out. Every ungodly soul tie, be uprooted by the blood of Jesus.
  • I command every evil influence over my mind, over my emotions — I refuse you now by the blood of Jesus.
  • By the blood of Jesus, I cleanse my soul from every pollution and defilement.
  • Every voice of my past speaking against my destiny, be silenced by the blood of Jesus.
  • I break every chain of emotional dependency by the blood of Jesus.
  • I reclaim my freedom. I walk in the power of the Holy Ghost. Every access point the enemy has into my life, I close it.
  • I declare total separation from every ungodly soul tie, in the name of Jesus.
  • I break free now, in the name of Jesus. From every ungodly soul tie — Lord, set me free now, in the name of Jesus.
  • These people whose names I have written on this paper — Lord, let any covenant created, any connection I had with them in the past, be destroyed in the name of Jesus.
  • I renounce and denounce any connection and relationship with these people.
  • I destroy any blood covenant I had with these people.
  • By the power of the Holy Ghost, as I mark myself with this blood, as I sprinkle some of this blood over them — I break every connection. I destroy every soul tie. Every connection I have with these people, in Jesus' name, by the blood of Jesus.
  • Any cord between the two of us, any cord between all of us — let that cord be cut off, in the name of Jesus Christ.
  • Any spiritual connection I have with these people, any spiritual marriage that was formed — I renounce you in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. I disconnect myself from you by the power of the Holy Ghost.
  • I call upon the name of the Lord: any connection we had, any covenant we had, any friendship we had — it is destroyed. It is broken. There is nothing between the two of us any longer. I don't want any connection with you. I don't want anything to do with you, in the mighty name of Jesus, both physically and spiritually. I divorce you spiritually, in the name of Jesus.
  • Any covenant that makes you enter into my dreams, that makes you project, that makes you follow me, monitor me spiritually — I break the covenant. I disconnect the connection. You will not be after my life anymore!
  • I disconnect the spiritual link between you and I. Wherever you have taken my name, wherever you have taken my image, and you are controlling me, manipulating me spiritually — in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I break that connection. I destroy that thing that you have done, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • I separate myself. I separate my virtues. My blessings, return to me, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • You are not going to manipulate me, in the name of Jesus. Any manipulation coming from you, I destroy it right now in the name of Jesus. You will not mind-control me by the power of the Holy Spirit. If you are mind-controlling me through any ungodly soul tie, I destroy every mind control coming from you, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • Lord, separate me spiritually from this person — mention their name. Lord, disconnect me from this person — mention their name. Lord, detach me, in the name of Jesus, by the blood.
  • As I sprinkle the blood over myself, over this person, as I drink the blood — disconnect me, detach me, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.

Breaking Covenants and Altars

Prayed as the room drank the blood of Jesus in communion.

Pray aloud
  • I cancel the verdict of the wicked upon your life and upon your destiny.
  • Every soul tie in your family background — break by fire. Any tree soul tie, let it break. Every evil soul tie with money, let it break. Amen.
  • I cancel the nickname your ex gave you, by the power of the Holy Ghost — the one that you still speak, that you still carry. I cancel that nickname right now, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • As you mark yourself with the blood, I destroy every inherited covenant from your ex — the one whose name you have written — in the mighty name of Jesus. Every inherited covenant, demonic covenant, evil covenant, idol-worship covenant, water-spirit covenant, we destroy it right now.
  • Every exchange from your ex, from your ex-boyfriend, from your ex-friendship, binding you and her or you and him together — let it break by fire. Let it break by fire. We break it by the blood of Jesus Christ that was shed for me.
  • Every curse upon this whole love affair, we break it by fire.
  • Let the Lord deliver your soul from every trauma of your life. I pronounce you free from every possession of darkness.
  • Every soul tie that is attacking your marital life, that is attacking your home, that is making you meet the wrong men, the wrong women — receive deliverance now. Every delay in your life, let it be destroyed.
  • I break the ungodly attachment between your soul and the spirit of your ex. I break it.
  • Every band that has come upon you as a result of an unbroken covenant, I command it to break.
  • I renounce every unholy bond that you made. I break it right now.
  • Every hidden and unhidden covenant troubling your destiny, troubling your career, troubling your moving forward — let it be broken by the blood.
  • I break the covenants of death. I break it right now, in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.
  • Let the blood of Jesus destroy the foundation of any evil covenant that is operating in your life.
  • Every spirit of your ex that is delaying the manifestation of your marriage — let it catch fire! You will marry. You will walk down the aisle, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • Let the fire of the Holy Ghost consume everything in you that is not of God right now.
  • Let every evil effect of any demonic covenant in your life be destroyed by the power of the Holy Ghost.
  • Every evil covenant that is working against you — break by fire!

Covenants With the Dead

For the ungodly tie to someone who has passed away — a bond that becomes like a spiritual marriage and hinders a person from moving forward.

Pray aloud
  • I break every ungodly covenant between you and a dead ex, a dead ex-husband, a dead ex-wife, a dead relative. In the mighty name of Jesus, be delivered. Be delivered by the power of the Holy Ghost.
  • We break every covenant between you and the dead. Between you and the dead. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  • I break every covenant between you and any dead ex, dead relative, dead friendship. Sometimes it might be a friend that you have.
  • I break every ungodly covenant with evil beds. With evil beds, let it be broken right now. Wherever you are, let it be broken right now, in the name of Jesus.
  • I destroy every soul-tie covenant between you and any herbalist, any occultic man, any occultic pastor. I break it right now, in the name of Jesus.
  • You have a covenant with that demon through the relations you had. You have a covenant with that spirit that follows you. You have a covenant with that spirit, that shadow, that is always with you. You have a covenant with that demon that is coming to sleep with you. That is why, no matter how you pray, they have a legal ground to be there.
  • Covenant between you and any idol and any evil spirit — break by fire! In the name of Jesus, break by fire! Break by the power of the Holy Ghost. Break now.

Open Doors and Restoration

Prayed while anointing with oil — a point of contact for faith in what Christ has already finished.

Pray aloud
  • Father, every door of blessing, every door of favour, every door of opportunity that has been shut against you because of evil — let them be open right now. We open them right now. Let them be open, in Jesus' name. Amen!
  • Every power standing at the gates of your breakthrough, and every force delaying your lifting — let it be destroyed right now.
  • Wherever you are, as you anoint yourself with this oil: every spiritual embargo placed upon your destiny, be destroyed.
  • Every ancient gate, every stubborn door resisting your progress, be lifted up right now by the power of the Holy Spirit, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.
  • Whatever has been stolen — your blessings, your career, your virtue, your beauty — let it return. Your identity — somebody shout: “My identity, return to me!” My identity, return to me, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every glory, every identity that has been withdrawn from your life, let it return to you. Let it return to you in every area of your life.
  • The Lord surrounds you with favour as a shield right now, wherever you are. Let men and women go out of their way to bless you. Whoever has taken the blessing from you — let it be returned.

Thanksgiving After Deliverance

The room gave thanks for the deliverance received.

Pray aloud
  • Tonight, we thank you, Lord, for delivering us from the ungodly spiritual ties — the ones we knew and the ones we did not know.
  • Tonight, Lord, we bless your holy name, that you are here with us tonight.
  • You have delivered us all, O God, from these ungodly spiritual ties. O God, we bind the power of this destructive activity tonight. Lord, we break free by the blood.
  • Lord, as I have written these names on this paper — release me, disconnect me, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • I remove you. I disconnect myself from you. As I flush this paper, these names, I flush it out of my life, out of my spirit, out of my soul, out of my body, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.

Sealing and Benediction

The service was sealed with the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ and the covering of goodness and mercy.

Pray aloud
  • May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the sweet fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with us now and forevermore. Surely goodness and mercy shall follow us all the days of our lives, and we shall dwell in the house of the Lord forever. Amen.
  • Whatever left should not return, in the mighty name of Jesus. Whatever covenant was broken, in the mighty name of Jesus, will not return to you, by the power of the Holy Ghost. Thank you, Father.

Salvation and Rededication Prayer

If you have never given your life to Jesus Christ, or you have wandered away and want to come back to Him, pray this prayer out loud where you are:

Pray aloud
  • Lord Jesus, I come to you today just as I am. I confess that I am a sinner and I need you. I believe that you are the Son of God, that you died on the cross for my sins, and that God raised you from the dead. Forgive me of every sin. Wash me in your blood. Come into my heart and be my Lord and my Saviour. I renounce the devil and all his works. I renounce every ungodly connection and every ungodly covenant in my life. From today I belong to you. Write my name in the Lamb's Book of Life. Fill me with your Holy Spirit and teach me to walk with you. Thank you for saving me, in Jesus' name. Amen.
