Module 4 of 8 · Breaking Ungodly Soul Ties

Signs an Ungodly Soul Tie Is Operating

The nine signs Pastor Kiky listed — the symptoms that reveal something deeper is at work.

Read it · Understand it

The teaching

How do you know a bad rope is still there? Nine signs:

  1. You cannot stop thinking of them.
  2. You feel drained.
  3. You keep going back even when it hurts.
  4. They keep showing up in your dreams.
  5. You feel controlled.
  6. You feel someone in your bed at night.
  7. You cannot move on.
  8. You feel guilty, confused or afraid for no reason.
  9. Nothing new can grow while the old rope is still tied.

These nine signs are the ones Pastor Kiky read straight to the room. If you see even three of them, do not brush it off. It is not personality; it is a bond.

When a person is tied and connected emotionally, and an ungodly soul tie is operating in their life, these are the signs Pastor Kiky named:

  1. You cannot stop thinking about the other person. Every day, every second — what they are eating, what they are drinking, where they are travelling to, what they have. Sometimes you become a stalker. It is because of the soul tie.
  2. You feel spiritually and emotionally drained.
  3. You keep returning to a toxic relationship. The person beats you, maltreats you, makes you feel worthless. You have packed and left — but by one word you have returned.
  4. You experience repeated dreams about the person.
  5. You feel controlled. Manipulated. Bound. Distressed.
  6. You experience spiritual sexual attack in dreams — a monitoring spirit that is after everything you do.
  7. You struggle to move forward in your life and in any other relationship.
  8. You feel guilty. Confused. Oppressed. Afraid without a clear reason.
Revelation before deliverance

Pastor Kiky closed the service on Hosea 4:6 — lack of knowledge makes God's people perish. There are hidden things fighting a life until the Lord reveals them.

These are diagnostic signs, not verdicts. They function the way the fruit of the Spirit functions positively in Galatians 5 — they show what is ruling. The pastoral use is careful: the presence of the signs is a call to specific prayer, not a condemnation. And the deliverance is always by the blood and the spoken word (Revelation 12:11), never by the striving of the sufferer.

Note Hosea 4:6 — the pattern is knowledge → repentance → deliverance → restoration. The nine signs supply the first step.

Pray it

Prayer points for this module PRAY

LISTEN REPEAT PRAY aloud, in your own voice
  • PRAY Every ungodly soul tie that has left me in torment, that has left me in distress and fear, that has blocked my abilities, my destiny, my career — by the power of the Holy Ghost, be disconnected.
  • PRAY Every voice of my past speaking against my destiny, be silenced by the blood of Jesus.
  • PRAY Any covenant that makes you enter into my dreams, that makes you monitor me — I break the covenant. You will not be after my life anymore.
