Declaration Library

Every corporate declaration of this service, exactly as spoken in the room. Speak them out loud — when you renounce and denounce by the power of the Holy Ghost, the bond is broken.

  • Corporate Declaration After Confession
  • Declarations Breaking the Ungodly Soul Tie
  • Declarations of Recovery
  • Declarations of Freedom
  • Corporate Declaration — Open Doors

Corporate Declaration After Confession

Declared out loud by the whole room after the prayer of confession.

Declare out loud
  • I am forgiven by the blood.
  • My sins are wiped away tonight.
  • I am cleansed and made holy again tonight.
  • No sin will stand against my breakthrough from tonight.
  • I will walk in righteousness and victory.
  • My heavens are open. My breakthrough is released over my life and over my destiny.
  • As I have repented and cried for mercy — Lord, let every closed door be opened. Let my deliverance reach me tonight. Let every delayed blessing be released. Let every spiritual barrier be destroyed, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • I will walk in purity. I will walk in power. I will walk in breakthrough, by the power of the Holy Ghost.
  • Let your blood wash me. Let your mercy stand with me, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.

Declarations Breaking the Ungodly Soul Tie

Spoken exactly as declared in the service. Freedom is voice-activated — these are said out loud.

Declare out loud
  • Every ungodly soul tie must break in the name of Jesus.
  • I renounce every ungodly soul tie.
  • I declare that every sexual agreement between me and any man is broken by the blood of Jesus Christ.
  • I renounce and denounce any connection and relationship with these people.
  • I destroy any blood covenant I had with these people.
  • I declare total separation from every ungodly soul tie, in the name of Jesus.
  • I break free now, in the name of Jesus.
  • I am reconciled by the blood of Jesus. I drink the blood of Jesus.
  • I reclaim my freedom. I walk in the power of the Holy Ghost. Every access point the enemy has into my life, I close it.
  • I remove you. I disconnect myself from you. As I flush this paper, these names, I flush it out of my life, out of my spirit, out of my soul, out of my body, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.

Declarations of Recovery

What the ungodly tie took is called back by name.

Declare out loud
  • By the blood of Jesus, I recover my peace. I recover my identity. I recover my virtues.
  • My glory, be withdrawn right now!
  • All my lost virtues, come back to me, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • My identity, return to me!
  • I separate myself. I separate my virtues. My blessings, return to me, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • Break by fire!
  • Expire!

Declarations of Freedom

Pastor Kiky's declaration of freedom over the room.

Declare out loud
  • Today is your day of freedom.
  • Freedom from past relationships.
  • Freedom from the sexual encounters you had.
  • Freedom from toxic relationships.
  • Freedom from toxic friendship.
  • Freedom from manipulation and control.
  • Freedom from trauma and pain.
  • Freedom from spiritual covenants — they must break by faith. Break by fire!
  • You are not bound! You are not owned by anybody but my God! You are not supposed to be controlled! You belong to Jesus Christ of Nazareth, by the power of the Holy Spirit!

Corporate Declaration — Open Doors

Declared while anointing the head with oil at the close of the service.

Declare out loud
  • Father, open doors that no man can shut.
  • Doors of promotion, let it be open.
  • Doors of increase, let it be open.
  • Doors of divine connection, let it be open.
  • Doors of destiny, let it be open.
  • Doors of restoration, let it be open.
  • Let it be open in my life.
  • Because of the ungodly soul tie I have been rejected — let me be accepted, in the name of Jesus.
  • Where I have been delayed, let me receive speed, in the name of Jesus.
  • Where I have been forgotten, Lord, let me be remembered again, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every spirit of delay and every spirit of rejection — I break it.
  • I reject every sickness in my life. I reject poor health in my life.
  • From today, I will move forward. I will move forward in the name of Jesus Christ, by the power of the Holy Ghost.
  • I am blessed in my home. I am blessed in my family. I am blessed in my business. I am blessed in every area of my life.
