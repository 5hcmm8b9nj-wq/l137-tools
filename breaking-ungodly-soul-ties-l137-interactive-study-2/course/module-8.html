Module 8 of 8 · Breaking Ungodly Soul Ties

Restoration and Open Doors

God is the Father of open doors. What the ungodly tie shut, He opens; what was stolen, He returns; favour surrounds the delivered believer as a shield.

Read it · Understand it

The teaching

When the bad rope is cut, God gives back what it stole. Your smile. Your confidence. Your peace.

He is the Father who opens doors that no one can close.

Deliverance is not the end of the teaching. Restoration is. God is the Father of open doors. What the tie stole — identity, favour, blessing, opportunity — He is giving back. Favour surrounds the delivered believer as a shield (Psalm 5:12).

As the covenant breaks, the blessings of the Lord and the Spirit of the Lord are invited to dwell inside you. Pastor Kiky stood on the word of the Lord and declared that God is the Father of open doors — the Father who opens the doors and no man can shut them.

When the ungodly soul tie came, some doors were shut. So those doors are opened again: every door of blessing, every door of favour, every door of opportunity that has been shut because of evil. Every power standing at the gates of a breakthrough, and every force delaying a lifting, is destroyed. Every spiritual embargo placed upon a destiny is destroyed. Every ancient gate, every stubborn door resisting progress, is lifted up by the power of the Holy Spirit.

Restored identity

Whatever has been stolen — blessings, career, virtue, beauty — let it return. Your identity, return to me, in the name of Jesus. Favour surrounds you as a shield.

Revelation 3:8 was written to the church at Philadelphia: I have set before thee an open door, and no man can shut it, for thou hast a little strength, and hast kept my word, and hast not denied my name. The open door is not a reward for strength; it is a mercy given to those with little strength who keep His word.

Psalm 5:12 supplies the covering: Thou, LORD, wilt bless the righteous; with favour wilt thou compass him as with a shield. The delivered life is not naked. Favour surrounds it. The anointing with oil in the service was the outward sign; the inward reality is Christ Himself opening what was shut.

Scenario — what would you do?

You have prayed the seven steps in Module 7 and you can feel a shift. What is the very next thing this teaching says to do — today, not tomorrow?

Outcome

Anointing with the oil is the outward sign; the inward reality is Christ opening what the tie shut. The delivered life must be filled or it becomes worse than before (Matthew 12:45).

Best response: Fill the void with the Holy Ghost and the Word of God, and stand on Revelation 3:8 for open doors.. Filling the void with the Holy Ghost and the Word of God is Step 6 for a reason. And Revelation 3:8 is God's promise over the doors the tie shut.

Apply it

Application question

PAUSE AND REFLECT

Write down one door in your life that has felt shut for years — a specific career door, a marriage door, a health door, a financial door. Speak Revelation 3:8 over it out loud. “Father, you are the God who opens doors no man can shut. Open this door in Jesus' name.”
