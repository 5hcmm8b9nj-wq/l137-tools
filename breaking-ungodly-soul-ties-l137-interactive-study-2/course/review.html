Review it · Remember it

Summary of the teaching

A soul tie is a real spiritual and emotional bond. Some ties are godly. Ungodly ties keep people bound long after a relationship ends — controlling their thinking, draining their strength, and blocking their destiny. Isaiah 28:18 is the verdict of Heaven over every ungodly covenant: your covenant with death shall be disannulled, and your agreement with hell shall not stand. Freedom comes through repentance, forgiveness, renunciation, the blood of Jesus, cutting off contact, filling the void with the Holy Ghost and the Word, and walking in the new life joined to the Lord as one spirit.

Remember it

Key lessons

  • Name it to break it. A thing that is not defined cannot be broken.
  • Godly and ungodly ties both exist. Do not throw away the godly ones (parents, children, spouse, Christ) while breaking the ungodly.
  • Prayer alone is not always enough. If a soul tie is still connecting a person, they will remain bound until it is broken by the blood and by the spoken word.
  • Freedom is voice-activated. Renunciation must be out loud.
  • Fill the void. An empty house invites seven worse spirits (Matthew 12:43-45). Fill it with the Holy Ghost and the Word.
  • Freedom in the Son is freedom indeed — not partial (John 8:36).

Test it

Final quiz

Ten mixed questions. Score at least 80% to unlock your completion badge. Answers stay hidden until you press Check my answers.

Q1 · Multiple choice

What is a soul tie, according to the teaching?

Answer. Pastor Kiky Ocansey defined the soul tie as a spiritual and emotional bond that joins two people at mind, will, emotion, and sometimes spirit.
Q2 · Multiple choice

Which is the FIRST of the seven steps to break an ungodly soul tie?

Answer. Step 1 is repentance for any involvement. Acts 3:19 — Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out.
Q3 · True or false

All soul ties are ungodly.

Answer. False. Godly ties exist — parents and children, husband and wife, believers and Christ. Jonathan and David is a godly friendship tie (1 Samuel 18:1).
Q4 · True or false

Freedom is voice-activated — silence will not break an ungodly soul tie.

Answer. True. Pastor Kiky repeated: your freedom is voice-activated. Renunciation must be spoken.
Q5 · Fill in the blank

Isaiah 28:18 — And your covenant with death shall be _____, and your agreement with hell shall not stand.

Answer. KJV wording. To disannul is to render void — Heaven's verdict over every ungodly covenant.
Q6 · Fill in the blank

John 8:36 — If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free _____.

Answer. Freedom through the Son is real freedom, not partial freedom.
Q7 · Multiple choice

Which scripture warns that a house left empty invites seven worse spirits?

Answer. Matthew 12:43-45. This is why Step 6 is to fill the void with the Holy Ghost and the Word of God.
Q8 · Multiple choice

What did Samson lose through Delilah?

Answer. Judges 16:19. Pastor Kiky said Samson lost his strength, his ability, and his destiny — an ungodly tie can cost a person everything.
Q9 · True or false

A person can pray and fast for freedom and still remain bound if an ungodly soul tie is still connecting them.

Answer. True. Not because God has not answered — but because the connection has not yet been broken.
Q10 · Fill in the blank

1 Corinthians 6:17 — But he that is joined unto the Lord is one _____.

Answer. The strongest bond a believer can have — union with Christ Himself. This is what fills the void.
