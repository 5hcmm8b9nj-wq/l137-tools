Thanksgiving and Divine Covering

Pastor Kiky opened the service in thanksgiving, calling the room to give thanks for preservation, provision, and covering before any warfare began.

Isaiah 60:1 KJV

Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the LORD is risen upon thee.

The call to arise is grounded not in feelings but in the risen glory of God upon the believer.

Isaiah 60:2 KJV

For, behold, the darkness shall cover the earth, and gross darkness the people: but the LORD shall arise upon thee, and his glory shall be seen upon thee.

Darkness covers the earth generally, but the glory of the Lord is a personal covering over His people.

Isaiah 60:3 KJV

And the Gentiles shall come to thy light, and kings to the brightness of thy rising.

The delivered believer becomes a light others are drawn toward.

Psalm 23:1 KJV

(A Psalm of David.) The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.

Quoted in thanksgiving — God as provider even when doors were closed and things were tight.

Psalm 91:1 KJV

He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.

Pastor Kiky used this to show the room they had been dwelling under a covering they did not see.

Psalm 121:1 KJV

(A Song of degrees.) I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help.

Read corporately by the room to open the heavens over the service.

Psalm 121:2 KJV

My help cometh from the LORD, which made heaven and earth.

Help is located in the Creator, not in circumstances or people.

Psalm 121:3 KJV

He will not suffer thy foot to be moved: he that keepeth thee will not slumber.

God's keeping is unbroken even while the believer sleeps.

Psalm 121:4 KJV

Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep.

The Keeper never rests — battles were fought while the room was resting.

Psalm 121:5 KJV

The LORD is thy keeper: the LORD is thy shade upon thy right hand.

God positions Himself as shade at the place of strength and action.

Psalm 121:6 KJV

The sun shall not smite thee by day, nor the moon by night.

Day and night attacks are both covered.

Psalm 121:7 KJV

The LORD shall preserve thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul.

Preservation of the soul is directly relevant to a soul-tie teaching.

Psalm 121:8 KJV

The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore.

The thanksgiving refrain Pastor Kiky returned to repeatedly: our going out and our coming in.

Confession of Sins and Cleansing

Before deliverance, the room confessed sin, because unconfessed sin blocks answers and delays deliverance.

Isaiah 59:2 KJV

But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he will not hear.

Pastor Kiky taught that sometimes the delay is not the devil but what has not been surrendered to God.

1 John 1:9 KJV

If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

God is not looking to condemn but to forgive; His mercy is deeper than the sin.

Psalm 51:10 KJV

Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me.

Prayed corporately during the confession segment — a clean heart and a renewed right spirit.

Inviting the Holy Spirit

The room invited the Holy Spirit to lead the prayer and to bring hidden ties to remembrance.

Romans 8:26 KJV

Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered.

The Holy Spirit teaches what to pray, empowers the prayer, and aligns the believer with God's will.

Covenants With Death Disannulled

The main scripture of the teaching. Ungodly soul ties function as covenants with death and agreements with hell.

Isaiah 28:18 KJV

And your covenant with death shall be disannulled, and your agreement with hell shall not stand; when the overflowing scourge shall pass through, then ye shall be trodden down by it.

MAIN SCRIPTURE. Ungodly soul ties are covenants with death — and God declares they shall be disannulled.

Galatians 3:13 KJV

Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree:

The legal ground for breaking every ungodly covenant is the finished work of Christ.

Galatians 3:14 KJV

That the blessing of Abraham might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.

Redemption from the curse opens the way to the blessing and the promise of the Spirit.

What a Soul Tie Is

A soul tie is a spiritual and emotional bond joining two people at the level of mind, will, emotion, and sometimes spirit.

Genesis 2:24 KJV

Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.

The godly pattern — a covenant bond that makes two people one flesh. This shows relationships create real spiritual connections.

1 Samuel 18:1 KJV

And it came to pass, when he had made an end of speaking unto Saul, that the soul of Jonathan was knit with the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul.

A godly friendship soul tie — proof that soul ties are real, powerful, and not always evil.

1 Corinthians 6:16 KJV

What? know ye not that he which is joined to an harlot is one body? for two, saith he, shall be one flesh.

Sexual contact outside God's will forms a one-flesh bond. Sex is not only physical; it is spiritual.

1 Corinthians 6:17 KJV

But he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit.

The strongest and most important soul tie — being joined to the Lord as one spirit.

Wrong Connections Destroy Destinies

Pastor Kiky pointed to Samson and Delilah as the pattern of a strong man losing strength, ability, and assignment through a wrong connection.

Judges 16:19 KJV

And she made him sleep upon her knees; and she called for a man, and she caused him to shave off the seven locks of his head; and she began to afflict him, and his strength went from him.

Samson's strength left him through the ungodly tie — 'that is why Samson lost his strength through Delilah.'

Hosea 4:6 KJV

My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: because thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, that thou shalt be no priest to me: seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I will also forget thy children.

Quoted at the close — hidden things fight lives, and lack of knowledge destroys. Revelation precedes deliverance.

Freedom Through the Son

Jesus did not only come to forgive sins — He came to break every bondage.

John 8:36 KJV

If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed.

The good news of the teaching: freedom through the Son is real freedom, not partial freedom.

Repentance and Renunciation

Step one of breaking a soul tie is repentance for the involvement, then renouncing and denouncing the connection out loud.

Acts 3:19 KJV

Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord;

Repentance blots out the sin and brings the times of refreshing — freedom begins here.

Overcoming by the Blood

The blood of Jesus disconnects the believer from every ungodly covenant, because the blood speaks of a new covenant.

Revelation 12:11 KJV

And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.

Quoted twice in the service — the blood and the spoken word together overcome. Freedom is voice-activated.

Do Not Leave the House Empty

After deliverance the void must be filled with the Holy Ghost and the Word of God, or worse will return.

Matthew 12:43 KJV

When the unclean spirit is gone out of a man, he walketh through dry places, seeking rest, and findeth none.

The unclean spirit does not stop seeking after it is cast out.

Matthew 12:44 KJV

Then he saith, I will return into my house from whence I came out; and when he is come, he findeth it empty, swept, and garnished.

An empty house is an invitation — deliverance must be followed by filling.

Matthew 12:45 KJV

Then goeth he, and taketh with himself seven other spirits more wicked than himself, and they enter in and dwell there: and the last state of that man is worse than the first. Even so shall it be also unto this wicked generation.

Pastor Kiky's warning: fill the space with the Holy Ghost. When God takes something, He replaces it.

Restoration and Open Doors

The service closed by anointing with oil and declaring open doors, restored identity, and returned virtues.

Revelation 3:8 KJV

I know thy works: behold, I have set before thee an open door, and no man can shut it: for thou hast a little strength, and hast kept my word, and hast not denied my name.

God is the Father of open doors — doors He opens no man can shut.

Psalm 5:12 KJV

For thou, LORD, wilt bless the righteous; with favour wilt thou compass him as with a shield.

Favour surrounds the delivered believer as a shield, replacing what the ungodly tie took.

Sealing and Benediction

The service was sealed with the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ and the covering of goodness and mercy.

Psalm 23:6 KJV

Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in the house of the LORD for ever.

Spoken in the closing benediction over the whole room.

2 Corinthians 13:14 KJV

The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Ghost, be with you all. Amen.

The grace shared corporately at the end of every L137 service.

Luke 1:37 KJV

For with God nothing shall be impossible.

The ministry scripture — L137. The whole teaching stands on this.
