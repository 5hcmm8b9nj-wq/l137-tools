Module 7 of 8 · Breaking Ungodly Soul Ties

Seven Steps to Break Ungodly Soul Ties

Repent · renounce and denounce out loud · apply the blood · cut emotional and spiritual access · remove the objects · fill the void with the Holy Ghost · be joined to Christ.

Read it · Understand it

The teaching

Seven steps to be free:

  1. Say sorry to Jesus for the wrong bond.
  2. Say it out loud: I break this bond.
  3. Use the blood of Jesus — it cuts every bad covenant.
  4. Stop talking to the one hurting you.
  5. Throw away the shirt, gift, or photo that keeps the bond.
  6. Fill your heart with the Holy Spirit.
  7. Hold on to Jesus — He is the best bond.

These are not seven suggestions. They are the order Pastor Kiky gave the room. Each step closes a specific door. Skipping one leaves it open.

Pastor Kiky gave the room a way — seven steps, in order.

Step 1 — Repentance

Repent for any involvement. Acts 3:19. Some of the ungodly ties were formed with people with whom there was something wrong. “Lord, I did not know this man very well, but I was attached to him. Deliver me. I repent of every sin.”

Step 2 — Renounce and denounce every connection out loud

Your freedom is voice-activated. Write on a paper everyone with whom you feel there is this ungodly soul tie. Begin to renounce every friendship, every connection: “I was with you, but now I am not part of you. Whatever kept a connection between us, today I destroy it, in the mighty name of Jesus.”

Step 3 — Use the blood of Jesus Christ of Nazareth

Revelation 12:11. The blood disconnects you from every ungodly covenant, because the blood speaks of a new covenant.

Step 4 — Cut emotional and spiritual access

Cut it. Stop communication if necessary with the toxic person who has disgraced you, manipulated you, beaten you.

Step 5 — Remove anything that keeps the connection alive; guard your heart

The shirt under the pillow. The dresses. The gifts. It is not just cloth — it is an emotional and spiritual asset.

Step 6 — Fill the void with the Holy Ghost

Matthew 12:43-45. Do not leave the house empty. Fill the space with the Holy Ghost and the Word of God. When God takes something, He must replace it.

Step 7 — Be joined to Christ

1 Corinthians 6:17 — he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit. The strongest and most important soul tie.

Notice the order. Repentance without renunciation leaves the covenant standing. Renunciation without the blood has no legal weight. The blood without the practical cutoff (Steps 4-5) invites the person straight back in. Steps 1-5 without Step 6 create the Matthew 12:43-45 problem — a swept house that seven worse spirits fill. Step 7 is not optional; it is the goal.

The seven steps mirror the wider deliverance flow of the ministry: confession → forgive → invite the Holy Spirit → teaching → breaking prayers → sealing.

Knowledge check

Free response — write your own answer

In your own words, why did Pastor Kiky insist that renunciation must be done out loud?

Reveal a sample answer

Because freedom is voice-activated. Revelation 12:11 pairs the blood of the Lamb with the word of their testimony. When you begin to speak, when you renounce and denounce out loud, demons manifest and leave. The spoken word is not decoration — it is the second half of overcoming.

Apply it

Application question

PAUSE AND REFLECT

Take a piece of paper. Write the names of anyone the Holy Spirit brings to your mind that you feel there is an ungodly soul tie with. Renounce and denounce those connections aloud, in your own voice. Then tear the paper and flush it, as Pastor Kiky directed — saying: “I remove you. I disconnect myself from you. In the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.”

Pray it

Prayer points for this module PRAY

LISTEN REPEAT PRAY aloud, in your own voice
  • PRAY By the power of the Holy Ghost, by the blood of Jesus, every ungodly soul tie in my life, break now, in the name of Jesus.
  • PRAY By the blood of Jesus, every spiritual cord tying me to my past, be cut off.
  • PRAY Every spirit attached to me, I cast you out. Every ungodly soul tie, be uprooted by the blood of Jesus.
  • PRAY By the blood of Jesus, I cleanse my soul from every pollution and defilement.
  • PRAY Lord, separate me spiritually from this person — mention their name. Lord, detach me, in the name of Jesus, by the blood.

Declare it

Declarations for this module DECLARE

SAY DECLARE out loud
  • I renounce every ungodly soul tie.
  • I renounce and denounce any connection and relationship with these people.
  • I destroy any blood covenant I had with these people.
  • I declare total separation from every ungodly soul tie, in the name of Jesus.
  • I remove you. I disconnect myself from you. As I flush this paper, these names, I flush it out of my life, out of my spirit, out of my soul, out of my body, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.
