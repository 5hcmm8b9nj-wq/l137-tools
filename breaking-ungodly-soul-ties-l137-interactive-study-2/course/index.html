Teaching modules

The eight modules

Module 1

What Is a Soul Tie?

A soul tie is a deep spiritual and emotional bond that joins two people at the level of mind, will, emotion and sometimes spirit.
Module 2

Godly vs Ungodly Soul Ties

Not all soul ties are evil. God ordains bonds that build, open doors and elevate — and the pattern of the godly bond exposes the counterfeit.
Module 3

How Ungodly Soul Ties Are Formed

Five doorways named in the teaching: sexual sin outside God's will, toxic and abusive relationships, past relationships never released, trauma and deep emotional bonds, and covenants and spiritual agreements.
Module 4

Signs an Ungodly Soul Tie Is Operating

The nine signs Pastor Kiky listed for the room — the symptoms that reveal something deeper is at work.
Module 5

What Ungodly Soul Ties Do

Delay, blockage, pollution, demonic doors, transferred burdens — and the pattern of Samson, whose strength went from him through a wrong connection.
Module 6

Freedom Through the Son

The good news of the teaching: Jesus did not only come to forgive sins — He came to break every bondage, and God has declared the covenant with death disannulled.
Module 7

Seven Steps to Break Ungodly Soul Ties

Repent · renounce and denounce out loud · apply the blood · cut emotional and spiritual access · remove the objects · fill the void with the Holy Ghost · be joined to Christ.
Module 8

Restoration and Open Doors

God is the Father of open doors. What the ungodly tie shut, He opens; what was stolen, He returns; and favour surrounds the delivered believer as a shield.
