Weekly assignment

Weekly Assignment — carry out the prophetic direction

This is the practical application given in the service itself. Nothing has been added to it. The communion wine and the paper carry no power of their own — the blood of Jesus, Christ's finished work, is what breaks the tie, and your spoken renunciation is how you stand on it.

  1. Take your communion wine, receiving the blood of Jesus as the new covenant that breaks every ungodly soul tie.
  2. Write on a piece of paper the names of any attachments or ungodly soul ties that you know you have had — the ones the Holy Spirit brings to your mind right now. Pastor Kiky said to write maybe three or five; the rest can be written later as the Holy Spirit reminds you.
  3. Out loud, renounce and denounce every connection, every attachment and every covenant with those names, in the mighty name of Jesus. Your freedom is voice-activated.
  4. Pray the corporate deliverance prayer over that paper, and release your life, your potential and your virtues, cancelling every ungodly soul tie with past relationships.
  5. Mark yourself with the blood, sprinkle some over the paper, and drink the blood of Jesus, entering into the new covenant.
  6. Anoint your head with the anointing oil and declare the open doors — doors of promotion, increase, divine connection, destiny and restoration.
  7. Afterwards, rip that paper into pieces and flush it out of your life, saying: “I remove you. I disconnect myself from you. As I flush this paper, these names, I flush it out of my life, out of my spirit, out of my soul, out of my body, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.”
  8. Do not do it only once. Practise it as many times as needed until you know you are completely free.
  9. If you could not write the names during the service — because you were at work, or in bed listening — you can do this afterwards, in the mighty name of Jesus.
  10. If you have not sent your prayer request to the ministry WhatsApp platform, write it there; the prayer requests are taken to the mountains of the Lord in prayer.
How to hold the physical elements

The communion wine, the anointing oil and the paper have no power in themselves. They are points of contact for faith in the finished work of Jesus Christ. It is the blood of Jesus that breaks the covenant, and it is God who opens the doors that no man can shut.

Homework

Homework — study review

Study work drawn from the eight modules of this teaching. Use the Notes panel in the course to write your answers.

  1. Write out Isaiah 28:18 in full from memory, then check it against the Scripture Library.
  2. In your own words, write the difference between a godly soul tie and an ungodly soul tie, using Genesis 2:24, 1 Samuel 18:1 and 1 Corinthians 6:16-17.
  3. List the five ways an ungodly soul tie is formed and note which ones you recognise in your own story.
  4. Read the nine signs in Module 4 slowly, and mark the ones that describe you. Bring each one into prayer.
  5. Write out the seven steps to break an ungodly soul tie in order, from memory.
  6. Read Matthew 12:43-45 and write one sentence on how you will fill the void — with the Word, with worship, with prayer in the Holy Ghost.
  7. Read the Declaration Library out loud once each day this week.
  8. Complete the knowledge checks on every module page and the quiz, then mark each module complete in the progress tracker.
