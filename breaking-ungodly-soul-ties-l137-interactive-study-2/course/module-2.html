Module 2 of 8 · Breaking Ungodly Soul Ties

Godly vs Ungodly Soul Ties

Not all soul ties are evil. God ordains bonds that build, open doors and elevate — and the pattern of the godly bond exposes the counterfeit.

Read it · Understand it

The teaching

God made some ropes to hold us up. Mum and child. Husband and wife. Believers and Jesus. Those ropes help us grow.

Some ropes were tied by mistake or by bad hands. Those ropes pull us down. Jesus wants to cut only the bad ropes — not the good ones.

Not every close bond is bad. God actually designed bonds that build you up: your parents, your husband or wife one day, your close friend in the Lord, and — biggest of all — you with Jesus.

A bond becomes ungodly when it was formed outside God's plan or through sin, trauma, manipulation, or demonic influence. That is the difference. The bond itself is not the problem; the source of it is.

Not all soul ties are evil. There are good ones. God-ordained healthy soul ties are also there — between husband and wife, between parents and their children, between believers and Christ. Those are good soul ties. There are soul ties that help you build. There are soul ties that open doors. There are soul ties that elevate you in life.

Attaching yourself with godly soul ties builds you up. It helps you. It opens doors. It elevates you. But when a connection is formed outside the will of God — or through sin, through trauma, through manipulation or demonic influence — it becomes an ungodly soul tie. It can hold a person in captivity.

The Jonathan and David pattern

The soul of Jonathan was knit with the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul. Proof that soul ties are real, powerful, and can be righteous.

Genesis 2:24 sets the archetype: they shall be one flesh. Every subsequent one-flesh language (1 Corinthians 6:16-17) is measured against that Eden covenant. The godly bond is public, covenantal, and blessed. The ungodly bond parodies the same spiritual reality — the joining is real, but the covenant is missing.

Pastoral implication: the answer to a demonic bond is not the death of intimacy; it is the recovery of covenant. Redemption from the curse (Galatians 3:13-14) opens the way to the blessing of Abraham and the promise of the Spirit — which is exactly the healthy re-bonding the delivered life needs.

True or False

All soul ties are ungodly and must be broken.

Answer: False. Not all soul ties are evil. God ordains bonds between husband and wife, between parents and children, and between believers and Christ. Jonathan and David is a godly friendship bond.

Application question

PAUSE AND REFLECT
