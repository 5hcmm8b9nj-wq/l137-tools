Quiz

Ten questions on the whole teaching

1. What is the main scripture of this teaching?

Show the teaching

Isaiah 28:18 — “And your covenant with death shall be disannulled, and your agreement with hell shall not stand.”

2. A soul tie links which parts of a person to another?

Show the teaching

Pastor Kiky defined a soul tie as a deep connection linking mind, will, emotion, and sometimes even the spirits of two people.

3. Which scripture shows a godly friendship soul tie?

Show the teaching

The soul of Jonathan was knit with the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul.

4. How many ways of forming an ungodly soul tie were named in this teaching?

Show the teaching

Sexual sin outside God's will; toxic and abusive relationships; past relationships never released; trauma and deep emotional bonds; covenants and spiritual agreements.

5. Which biblical figure was given as the pattern of a destiny lost through a wrong connection?

Show the teaching

Samson lost his strength through Delilah — he lost his strength, his ability and his destiny.

6. What is the first of the seven steps to break an ungodly soul tie?

Show the teaching

Repent for any involvement — Acts 3:19: “Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out.”

7. What did Pastor Kiky mean by saying freedom is voice-activated?

Show the teaching

You must speak out. When you renounce and denounce out loud, by the power of the Holy Ghost, the bond is broken.

8. Why must the void be filled after deliverance?

Show the teaching

Matthew 12:43-45 warns not to leave empty spaces. Fill the space with the Holy Ghost and the Word of God.

9. Which soul tie did Pastor Kiky call the strongest and most important?

Show the teaching

1 Corinthians 6:17 — “But he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit.”

10. What did Pastor Kiky call God in the closing segment on restoration?

Show the teaching

Revelation 3:8 — God sets before us an open door, and no man can shut it.
