Module 1 of 8 · Breaking Ungodly Soul Ties

What Is a Soul Tie?

A soul tie is a deep spiritual and emotional bond that joins two people at the level of mind, will, emotion and sometimes spirit.

Read it · Understand it

The teaching

A soul tie is a strong invisible rope that ties two people together on the inside. It ties their thoughts, their feelings, and even their spirit.

God made good ropes — like the rope between a mum and her child. But some ropes are bad. Bad ropes hurt the people they tie together.

Good news: Jesus can cut every bad rope.

A soul tie is a real, unseen bond that links two people at the level of the mind, the will, the emotions, and sometimes even the spirit. It is why you can break up with someone and still feel them inside, months later.

Some soul ties are godly and good. Some are ungodly — they were formed outside God's plan, and they end up controlling you instead of helping you.

You cannot argue an ungodly soul tie out of yourself. It has to be broken by prayer, by the blood of Jesus, and by the power of the Holy Ghost.

Pastor Kiky opened the teaching with a definition, because a thing that is not named cannot be broken. A soul tie is a deep connection between two people that links their mind, links their will, links their emotion, and sometimes even their spirits. It is a spiritual and emotional bond.

That is why a person can date somebody, and after the breakup their life has never remained the same again. The relationship ended physically, but the bond did not end with it.

What makes a soul tie ungodly

An agreement between two or more people bound together by lust, bound together by evil powers, bound together by monitoring spirits — a demonic covenant between a man and a woman.

Many people are praying, many people are fasting, many people are believing God for freedom — yet they remain bound. Not because God has not answered their prayers, but because there is an ungodly soul tie still connecting them to people, to past relationships and to spiritual influences.

Two Corinthian passages hold this together. 1 Corinthians 6:16 — What? know ye not that he which is joined to an harlot is one body? for two, saith he, shall be one flesh. The apostle grounds the sexual bond in the creation ordinance of Genesis 2:24 — the same one-flesh language. Sin does not create a new spiritual reality; it misuses a real one.

1 Corinthians 6:17 — But he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit. The remedy is not detachment in the abstract; it is a stronger, higher bond. The delivered believer is not asked to be alone — they are joined to the Lord as one spirit. This is why filling the void (Matthew 12:43-45, Module 7) is not optional. An empty house is an open invitation.

The doctrine here is not new: it is Pauline union-with-Christ language applied to the pastoral problem of a life that remains bound after prayer.

Tap to reveal · four layers of the bond

What does a soul tie connect?

Tap each item to reveal what Pastor Kiky said about it.

    Layer 1 — the mind

    Thoughts of the other person that will not go away — every day, every second. The mind cannot let go.

    Layer 2 — the will

    Choices are pulled toward the other person. You go back to a relationship you already left, by one word.

    Layer 3 — the emotions

    Heartbreak that will not eat, will not speak, is not itself for one week — you are still emotionally attached.

    Layer 4 — sometimes the spirit

    A shadow that lies next to you in bed. A presence you feel. Whatever follows the other person now follows you.

Apply it

Application question

PAUSE AND REFLECT

Think of one person you know you are still tied to inside — even though the relationship has ended. Which layer is the tie strongest on today: your mind, your will, your emotions, or your spirit? Write it in your journal so you can pray specifically.
