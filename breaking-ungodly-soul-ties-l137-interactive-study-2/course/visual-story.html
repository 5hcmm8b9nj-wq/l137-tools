See it · Breaking Ungodly Soul Ties

Visual Story

Ten scenes that trace one believer's journey — from carrying invisible chains, through repentance and renunciation, into freedom that is voice-activated and blood-bought. God the Father and the Lord Jesus are represented only by light, rays, and a radiant throne — never a face.

See it · Understand it

Scene by scene

Scene 1

Carrying the weight

A believer walks with heavy invisible chains — thoughts that will not stop, feelings that will not lift, a bond that will not break. Prayer alone has not been enough. Something is still connecting them.

See it

Scene 2

Hearing the Word

The believer opens the Bible to Isaiah 28:18. The Word speaks: your covenant with death shall be disannulled. Something the enemy sealed, Heaven has already declared broken.

Read it

Scene 3

Recognising the tie

The Holy Spirit shows the believer, one by one, the people, the places, the memories where an ungodly bond was formed. To name a thing is the first step to break it.

Understand it

Scene 4

Repentance

The believer kneels. Every wrong involvement is confessed — no excuse, no shifting blame. Acts 3:19 begins its work: repent, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out.

Pray it

Scene 5

Renouncing out loud

Silence will not break a soul tie. The believer speaks: “I renounce every ungodly connection in the name of Jesus.” Your freedom is voice-activated. Heaven hears; hell trembles.

Declare it

Scene 6

The blood of Jesus

The believer applies the blood of Jesus over every point of connection — mind, will, emotion, memory, body. Revelation 12:11 is not a slogan; it is a weapon.

Pray it

Scene 7

Cutting off, filling the void

Pictures, gifts, tokens — anything that keeps the tie alive is removed. The empty place is not left empty. It is filled with the Holy Ghost and the Word of God (Matthew 12:43-45).

Apply it

Scene 8

Freedom in the Son

The believer stands. Chains gone. Head lifted. John 8:36 is fulfilled — if the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed. This is not partial freedom. This is freedom indeed.

Declare it

Scene 9

Walking in the new life

The believer walks in obedience, purity, worship, and Scripture. The one-flesh union that mattered most is now the one Paul named — “he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit.” (1 Corinthians 6:17)

Apply it

Scene 10

Sealed

The service closes with thanksgiving and sealing. Every deliverance is covered by the blood of Jesus and the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ. Freedom that was declared is now sealed.

Pray it

Remember it

The story in one line

Key point

A believer cannot be talked out of an ungodly soul tie. The bond must be broken by prayer, by the blood of Jesus, and by the power of the Holy Ghost — and freedom must be spoken out loud.
