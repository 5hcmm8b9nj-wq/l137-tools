A word before we begin

Discernment Is Not Suspicion

Do not accuse, confront, expose, or sever a relationship solely because of a dream, a feeling, a suspicion, a prophecy, or a prayer impression. Test every matter by Scripture, by observable conduct, by wise counsel, by prayer, and by truth. Biblical discernment produces wisdom, peace, self-control, and righteousness — never paranoia, retaliation, or reckless accusation.

Where you suspect abuse, threats, stalking, poisoning, violence, or criminal conduct, contact appropriate professional or legal help. This teaching is not a substitute for pastoral counsel, medical care, or law enforcement.

Test every matter by:

  • 1 Thessalonians 5:21 (KJV)"Prove all things; hold fast that which is good."
  • James 3:17 (KJV)"But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy."
  • Matthew 18:15 (KJV)"Moreover if thy brother shall trespass against thee, go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone: if he shall hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother."
  • Proverbs 18:13 (KJV)"He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him."
  • Proverbs 18:17 (KJV)"He that is first in his own cause seemeth just; but his neighbour cometh and searcheth him."
  • Galatians 6:1 (KJV)"Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself lest thou also be tempted."
  • Romans 12:18 (KJV)"If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men."

  • Module 10

    Biblical Boundaries, Reconciliation, and Wise Distance ✓

    Key idea: Scripture teaches both reconciliation and, where necessary, wise distance. Not every difficult relationship calls for the same response. Test each situation carefully.

    1. Misunderstanding. Go and speak to your brother — Matthew 18:15. Most household tension is not warfare.

    2. Ordinary relational conflict. Bear with one another in love — Ephesians 4:2. Be slow to accuse, quick to forgive.

    3. Unrepentant manipulation or abuse. Have no fellowship with unfruitful works of darkness — Ephesians 5:11. Loving distance protects both parties.

    4. Criminal or dangerous behaviour. The magistrate beareth not the sword in vain — Romans 13:4. Involve law enforcement where the law has been broken.

    5. Church discipline. Follow the Matthew 18 process — one, then two or three, then the church. Do not skip ahead.

    6. Immediate physical safety. Flee — Proverbs 22:3. Seek shelter, medical help, and pastoral counsel. Do not stay to prove a point.

    Distance is a tool, not a doctrine. Reconciliation is the goal wherever it is safe and possible; separation is right where the safety of persons and the peace of the household require it. In every case, leave vengeance to the Lord (Romans 12:19).

Section C · Biblical Discernment or Unhealthy Suspicion?

Read each scenario and choose. The answer and a one-line teaching will appear.

A close friend consistently delays your progress, contradicts your gifts publicly, and takes credit for your work — over several years, in observable ways.

Discernment. Observable conduct over time — take it to Matthew 18:15 and, if needed, biblical distance.

You had a bad dream about a family member last night and now feel afraid of them.

Suspicion. A dream is not a verdict. Test by Scripture, wise counsel, and observable conduct before you act.

Someone gave you a gift and you feel a check in your spirit; when you ask two mature believers, both independently sense the same concern.

Discernment. Multiple witnesses of mature believers is a biblical pattern (2 Corinthians 13:1).

A coworker did not greet you this morning, so you assume they are secretly plotting against you.

Suspicion. One missed greeting is not evidence. Proverbs 18:13 — do not answer a matter before you hear it.

Romans 10:13 · Salvation Prayer

The Salvation Prayer

"For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved."

Say this prayer aloud, mean it, and mark today's date in your Bible:

Verbatim salvation prayer format used across L137 services
  • Lord Jesus, I repent of my sins and surrender my life. Wash me clean.
  • I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.
  • That He died on a cross for my sins.
  • And rose again on the third day for my victory.
  • I believe that in my heart and make a confession with my mouth — that Jesus is my Savior and Lord.
  • I receive eternal life in Jesus' name. Amen.

Your next 30 days

  • Read John's Gospel — one chapter each morning. It is the clearest introduction to who Jesus is.
  • Pray daily — even five minutes, aloud, in your own words. The Father hears the newborn believer.
  • Join a Bible-believing local church — a place where the Bible is preached, where the leadership is accountable, and where you can be baptised.
  • Speak with a pastor or mature believer — do not walk this out alone. Reach out to the L137 team via linktr.ee/Pastor_kiki for follow-up.
  • Learn about baptism — the New Testament pattern is that new believers are baptised in water as a public confession of their new life.

Do not depend on the repetition of words alone. Salvation is the free gift of God through genuine faith in the Lord Jesus Christ and heartfelt repentance from sin. The prayer is your first “Yes, Lord” — the walking-out is your whole life.

If you prayed this prayer, you are born again. Welcome home. Reach out to the L137 team via our Linktree so we can help you take the next steps.
