A word before we begin

Discernment Is Not Suspicion

Do not accuse, confront, expose, or sever a relationship solely because of a dream, a feeling, a suspicion, a prophecy, or a prayer impression. Test every matter by Scripture, by observable conduct, by wise counsel, by prayer, and by truth. Biblical discernment produces wisdom, peace, self-control, and righteousness — never paranoia, retaliation, or reckless accusation.

Where you suspect abuse, threats, stalking, poisoning, violence, or criminal conduct, contact appropriate professional or legal help. This teaching is not a substitute for pastoral counsel, medical care, or law enforcement.

Test every matter by:

  • 1 Thessalonians 5:21 (KJV)"Prove all things; hold fast that which is good."
  • James 3:17 (KJV)"But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy."
  • Matthew 18:15 (KJV)"Moreover if thy brother shall trespass against thee, go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone: if he shall hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother."
  • Proverbs 18:13 (KJV)"He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him."
  • Proverbs 18:17 (KJV)"He that is first in his own cause seemeth just; but his neighbour cometh and searcheth him."
  • Galatians 6:1 (KJV)"Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself lest thou also be tempted."
  • Romans 12:18 (KJV)"If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men."

The heart of the teaching

What is a friendly enemy?

A friendly enemy is someone whose actions — tested by Scripture, observable conduct, and wise counsel — consistently seek to harm, mislead, or undermine your life while wearing the mask of friendship. Sometimes such nearness is not by accident.

01

They appear helpful

They wear friendship as a costume so you keep them near, tell them your movements, and download your plans without discernment.

02

They carry veiled envy

Their smile hides resentment about the anointing, the door God is opening, or the promise being fulfilled in your life.

03

They block your breakthrough

In observable ways — over time and by pattern, not a single incident — they resist the progress the Lord is bringing to your life.

04

They are close enough to hurt

Nearness is not loyalty. Judas dipped in the same bowl. Saul kept David close. Absalom smiled at every man at the gate.

Lightly edited from the service
  • Every friendly enemy assigned to expose my glory — die by fire from tonight.
  • Anything that terminates my blessings before their manifestation — break and release me from tonight.
  • I rise, and I disappoint the expectations of my enemies, in Jesus' name.
  • Every witchcraft eye following the steps of my progress — receive blindness in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, expose my friendly enemies in my family, in my workplace, in my friendships, in my community.
  • Every sound of darkness around me — I confront by the fire of the Holy Ghost.
  • Any ancestral mark on my body attracting evil spirits — be destroyed in the name of Jesus.
  • Every hidden enemy masquerading as a friend in my life — be exposed and ashamed, in the name of Jesus.
  • Every evil veil covering the identity of my enemies — be torn apart in the name of Jesus.
  • Every secret gathering against my life — be broken by the blood of Jesus.
  • I bind every hidden enemy in my life. Lord, open my eyes to discover them, open my ears to hear them.
  • Give me the gift of discernment. Give me a heart sensitive to Your Spirit.
  • I anoint myself with the oil of the Holy Ghost. Every hidden enemy — be revealed. Every arrow — return to sender.
  • For with God nothing shall be impossible.

Note on language: phrases such as "witchcraft eye," "ancestral mark," and "return to sender" are pastoral shorthand used in this ministry tradition for spiritual influences and inherited patterns. Each is defended from Scripture in the accompanying Prayer Point Manual and Scripture Companion. If a phrase is unfamiliar, pray the plain-language equivalent — for instance, "Lord, blind every hostile eye watching me" or "Lord, remove every inherited pattern that draws evil to me."
