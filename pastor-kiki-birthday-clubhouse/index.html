Pre-Room · Read Aloud

The Founding Story of L137 Deliverance Ministry

Read this at the start or between segments · 3–4 minutes

Drawn from Pastor Kiki's own testimony shared on YouTube. Read this aloud slowly — it sets the atmosphere for the whole room.

Long before there was a ministry, there was a girl in Ghana, being raised by her grandmother. That grandmother poured faith into her the way only grandmothers can — Sunday after Sunday, prayer after prayer. When the time came, that little Ghanaian girl moved to the United Kingdom to live with her mother, and life carried on.

She was about fourteen years old when she went to church with her father and a pastor stopped, looked at her, and prophesied words she was too young to fully receive:

"You are going to be a woman of God. You are going to be a gospel singer one day."

— A prophetic word spoken over young Kiki

She said, "Oh, okay." She did not take it seriously. She was not deeply into the gospel yet. She went to church because her grandmother went. She went because her father went. She went because you do not offend your parents. Then she grew up, moved on with life, and became a hotel manager in Docklands, London.

Everything changed on April 4th, 2009.

She had finished work and was driving home on the A13. There was traffic. She parked behind another car, waiting for it to clear. And suddenly — a vehicle slammed into her from behind. Then a second severe accident on that same road. Two consecutive collisions. Multiple surgeries followed. A season in a wheelchair. A long, painful recovery. And in the middle of the pain — a deep, dark depression that tried to swallow her whole.

But God.

In the place of despair, in the place where her body was broken and her strength was gone, the Lord met her. He reminded her of the word spoken over a fourteen-year-old girl in a Ghanaian church pew. He reminded her that no matter how impossible it looked — with Him, nothing shall be impossible.

37 For with God nothing shall be impossible.

— Luke 1:37, KJV

That single verse became the foundation stone. The L for Luke. The 137 for chapter 1, verse 37. Out of a wheelchair, out of surgery rooms, out of depression, out of the ashes — God birthed L137 Deliverance Ministry International. A house of prophetic teaching. A house of deliverance. A house of warfare prayer. A house that declares over every hopeless case: with God, nothing shall be impossible.

Today Pastor Kiki also leads the L137 Clothing Line, carries a global digital pulpit of daily devotionals, and stands as a spiritual mother to sons and daughters across the nations — many of whom found their freedom through her Clubhouse rooms and recorded teachings.

The girl who was hit twice on the A13 is still standing. The woman the enemy tried to bury is preaching, prophesying, and setting captives free. The prophecy spoken at fourteen has come to pass.

Today we do not just celebrate her birthday — we celebrate every accident she survived, every surgery she recovered from, every tear the enemy meant for destruction, and every soul God has set free because she answered yes.

Long live Pastor Kiki. Long live L137.
