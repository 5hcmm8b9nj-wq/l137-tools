The Vision · Koinonia Sunday Worship

The Crocodile That Would Not Let Go

A believer at Koinonia Sunday worship on September 6, 2026 went through deliverance during the service. Her spirit was quickened. As she continued in prayer, she saw a huge crocodile. It opened its mouth. She was in a cage, deep inside its mouth.

The Lord did not show her the crocodile so she would stay in the cage. He showed her the crocodile so she would know what to pray against, and what to be delivered from, by the blood of Jesus Christ.

Psalm 74:13–14 KJV
Thou didst divide the sea by thy strength: thou brakest the heads of the dragons in the waters. Thou brakest the heads of leviathan in pieces, and gavest him to be meat to the people inhabiting the wilderness.
Job 41:1 KJV
Canst thou draw out leviathan with an hook? or his tongue with a cord which thou lettest down?

Before You Begin

Standard L137 Deliverance Flow

Walk through these eight steps before each category. Never skip, never reorder. Pastor Kiky Ocansey teaches this order for every service.

Eight steps · in order

  1. Praise & worship
  2. Thanksgiving
  3. Confession of sins
  4. Forgive others
  5. Invite the Holy Spirit
  6. Teaching
  7. Deliverance prayers & declarations
  8. Sealing (blood of Jesus)

Prayers

Renunciation
In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I renounce every marine covenant, every water covenant, every river dedication, every sea dedication, every mermaid or "queen of the coast" dedication ever made over me, my parents, my grandparents, or my bloodline. Amen.
Deliverance
Marine crowns upon my glory, catch fire, in the name of Jesus. Marine serpents in my reproductive area, come out by the blood of Jesus. Any internal altar connecting me to water demons, be dismantled by the fire of God, in the name of Jesus. Blood of Jesus, fire of God — cast out marine waters from my body. Amen.
Spirit Spouse
Every spirit spouse assigned to my life, the fire of God separates us tonight. I divorce you by the blood of the everlasting covenant. Every dream of sex, every dream of eating, every dream of swimming, every dream of unknown weddings — I reject you and I burn you in the name of Jesus. Amen.
Restoration
Lord Jesus, You drew Peter out of the water and You draw me out tonight. Every destiny of mine held in the deep — restore it by the blood of Jesus. Every child of mine held in the deep — release them by the blood of Jesus. Every finance, every business, every calling held under water — surface by the fire of God. Amen.

Declaration

Declare aloud
The Lord has drawn me out of many waters — Psalm 18:16. I am no longer married to the water — I am married to the Lamb. Every marine altar behind my delay is dismantled tonight. The captive of the mighty is delivered — Isaiah 49:25.

Prayers

Forgiveness
Lord Jesus, I forgive every person I have ever been tied to in an ungodly way — every sexual partner, every controlling relative, every false prophet, every friend who pulled me from You. I release them into Your hands. I do not carry their burden anymore. In the name of Jesus. Amen.
Renunciation
In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I renounce every ungodly soul tie — every sexual soul tie, every blood soul tie, every covenant soul tie, every hatred soul tie, every dependence soul tie, every dedication soul tie, every mentorship soul tie made outside the will of God. Amen.
Breaking
By the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God (Ephesians 6:17), I cut every ungodly cord that ties my soul to anyone the Lord has not joined me to. Every fragment of my soul held by another person, return to me now in the name of Jesus. Every fragment of another person's soul held in me, I release now in the name of Jesus. Blood of Jesus, wash the cut. Amen.
Restoration
Holy Spirit, join my soul afresh to Jesus Christ my Lord. Let every godly bond — with my spouse, with my children, with my true brothers and sisters in Christ — be strengthened. Let every ungodly bond stay severed forever. Amen.

Declaration

Declare aloud
I am joined to the Lord and I am one spirit with Him — 1 Corinthians 6:17. Every ungodly cord is cut by the blood of Jesus and cannot be re-tied. My soul is not for sale, my soul is not for rent, my soul belongs to Jesus Christ of Nazareth. What God has joined, no man puts asunder. What God has not joined, no devil holds together.
Category 6 · Sealing & Aftercare

Close the Doors. Fill the House.

After every deliverance, seal what the Lord has done and fill the empty room with the Holy Spirit.

Exodus 12:13 KJV
And the blood shall be to you for a token upon the houses where ye are: and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you, when I smite the land of Egypt.
Matthew 12:43–45 KJV
When the unclean spirit is gone out of a man, he walketh through dry places, seeking rest, and findeth none. Then he saith, I will return into my house from whence I came out; and when he is come, he findeth it empty, swept, and garnished. Then goeth he, and taketh with himself seven other spirits more wicked than himself, and they enter in and dwell there: and the last state of that man is worse than the first.
Romans 12:2 KJV
And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.
Ephesians 6:11 KJV
Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.
Luke 1:37 KJV
For with God nothing shall be impossible.

Sealing prayer

Sealing
Father, in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I seal every deliverance received tonight with the blood of Jesus. I close every door the enemy opened. I close every gate every serpent slid through. I close every window every marine spirit climbed into. I close every foundation every evil covenant was laid on. I close every path every witchcraft footprint walked. The blood of Jesus is on the doorpost of my life, my body, my home, my marriage, my children, and my destiny. When You see the blood, You pass over me, and the destroyer cannot enter. Amen.
Filling
Holy Spirit, fill every empty room the deliverance left behind. Fill me with Yourself — with the word, with worship, with holiness, with the fear of the Lord, with love. Let my house not be empty, swept, and garnished — let it be full of You. Amen.

Final declaration

Declare aloud
The crocodile has opened its mouth — and the Lord has drawn me out. The cage is broken. The blood of Jesus has broken the cage. I am delivered from the serpent, the water, the soul tie, the covenant, and the coven. I am sealed by the Holy Spirit unto the day of redemption — Ephesians 4:30. For with God nothing shall be impossible — Luke 1:37.
Aftercare · the next 7 days

Read your Bible daily · pray morning and night · take communion at least once this week and plead the better covenant · return to a Bible-teaching church and to your prayer group · avoid the friendships, media, and places that were the hedge-breakers · bring persistent physical or emotional symptoms to a trusted physician as well as to prayer — both are gifts from the Father of lights (James 1:17).
