Segment 3 · Teaching

How Jesus Showed Us To Bind Him

The Word of the Lord

— Matthew 12:29 (KJV)

Teaching

Jesus did not merely describe the strong man — He revealed the divine order of warfare. The believer who is in Christ shares His authority. We do not beg the enemy to leave; we exercise the authority Jesus purchased. “Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy” (Luke 10:19). Deliverance is not a struggle between equals; it is the enforcement of a victory already won at Calvary.

Notice the order: binding precedes spoiling. The ruling spirit — the strong man — must first be bound before his goods (the lesser spirits, the habits, the strongholds) can be plundered and removed. Derek Prince taught that many fail in deliverance because they attack symptoms while the ruling spirit still holds legal command of the house. Bind the strong man first; then his house can be emptied and kept clean.

What are the weapons that bind him? Not willpower, not religious effort, but the Name of Jesus and the Blood of the Lamb. As Frank Hammond wrote in Pigs in the Parlor, the authority is in the Name, and the cleansing is in the Blood. When you speak the Name of Jesus in living faith, heaven backs your word; when you plead His Blood, the enemy's legal ground is cancelled. Bound by the Name, judged by the Word, washed by the Blood — the strong man must release his house.

Facilitator & Group NotesSuggested time: 8 min

Let the scripture finish typing before teaching. Emphasize the three points: the believer's authority, binding-before-spoiling, and the Name and the Blood as weapons. Use the "Listen" button so the Word is heard aloud.

Group prompt: Pause here. If you're together, read Matthew 12:29 aloud once more together.

Segment 6 · Teaching

Your Three Weapons

The Blood, the Name, and the Word — these three overcome the enemy.

The Blood
Revelation 12:11
“And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb…”
The Blood of Jesus is your legal authority. It cancels every claim the enemy holds against you and washes away the guilt that gave him ground. When you plead the Blood, you stand in a covenant the enemy cannot break.
The Name
Philippians 2:10
“That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow…”
The Name of Jesus is above every name. Every demonic power must bow to it. Spoken in faith, His Name carries the full authority of heaven — you command, and the enemy must obey.
The Word
Hebrews 4:12
“For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword…”
The Word of God is the sword of the Spirit. It divides truth from lie, judges every thought, and gives you a scripture for every assault. Speak it aloud and the enemy is cut down.
Facilitator & Group NotesSuggested time: 7 min

Teach each weapon in turn. Declare together: “I overcome by the Blood of the Lamb, by the Name of Jesus, and by the Word of my testimony.” Use the declare button to lead it aloud.

Group prompt: Pause here. If you're together, declare the line aloud once, in one voice.
