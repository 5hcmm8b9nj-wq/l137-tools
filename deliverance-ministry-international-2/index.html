Session 1 · Foundation

Understanding the Strong Man

Before you can bind him, you must know who he is. This session lays the biblical foundation for your deliverance.

What is a Strong Man?

In Matthew 12:29 and Mark 3:27, Jesus taught that to plunder a strong man's house, you must first bind the strong man. In deliverance ministry, a “strong man” is a ruling demonic spirit that commands a network of lower spirits operating in a person's life.

The Strong Man of Sex — also called the spirit of lust, perversion, or sexual immorality — is one of the most common and most stubborn ruling spirits. He rarely operates alone. He commands a network including spirits of lust, fornication, adultery, pornography, masturbation, fantasy, perversion, homosexuality, incest, seduction, whoredom (Jezebel), uncleanness, soul ties, sexual addiction, and shame.

Jesus has already disarmed every principality and power at the cross. Your authority is real. Deliverance is the children's bread. — Colossians 2:15; Matthew 15:26

Session 4 · The Deliverance

Binding & Casting Out

Pray each step out loud, slowly, with faith. Do not rush. Pause to breathe and receive what the Lord is doing.

Step 1 — Repentance & Confession

Heavenly Father, in the name of Jesus Christ, I come boldly to Your throne of grace. I confess that I have sinned against You in my body, mind, and spirit. I have committed sexual sin — in thought, in word, and in deed. I name each sin before You now: (name them from your inventory in Session 2). I take full responsibility. I am sorry. I repent. I turn away completely. According to 1 John 1:9, I thank You that You are faithful and just to forgive me my sins and to cleanse me from all unrighteousness. I receive Your forgiveness now, in Jesus' name. Amen.

Step 2 — Forgiveness

Unforgiveness is the #1 hindrance to deliverance (Matthew 18:34–35).

Father, I choose by an act of my will to forgive every person who has sexually sinned against me, abused me, seduced me, molested me, raped me, manipulated me, or defiled me. I name them now before You: (name each one from Session 2). I release them from every debt I feel they owe me. I bless them in Jesus' name. I also forgive myself for every sexual sin I committed against my own body and against others. And I receive Your forgiveness, Lord, by faith.

Step 3 — Renunciations

In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I renounce:

• Every spirit of lust, perversion, and sexual immorality
• Every spirit of fornication and adultery
• Every spirit of pornography, masturbation, and fantasy
• Every spirit of homosexuality, lesbianism, and perversion
• Every spirit of seduction, Jezebel, and harlotry
• Every spirit-husband and spirit-wife (incubus, succubus)
• Every spirit of sexual addiction and uncleanness
• Every spirit of shame, guilt, and condemnation
• Every word curse I have spoken over myself
• Every covenant, oath, or agreement I made with sexual spirits, knowingly or unknowingly

I cancel every legal ground, every claim, every assignment, in the name of Jesus. Amen.

Step 4 — Breaking Soul Ties

Know ye not that he which is joined to an harlot is one body? for two, saith he, shall be one flesh. — 1 Corinthians 6:16 (KJV)
In the name of Jesus Christ, I break and renounce every ungodly soul tie formed through sexual intercourse, sexual contact, sexual fantasy, or emotional sexual involvement with every person other than my covenant spouse. I name them now before the Lord: (name every person from Session 2).

I sever every cord of soul, spirit, and body between me and them. I return to them every piece of their soul that came into me, washed in the blood of Jesus. I take back every piece of my soul that went into them, washed in the blood of Jesus and made clean. I close every door. I cancel every dream, every memory, every connection. By the sword of the Spirit, I cut myself free, in Jesus' name. Amen.

Step 5 — Breaking Generational Curses

Father, in the name of Jesus, I stand in the gap for my bloodline. I confess the sins of my fathers and mothers, my ancestors on both sides, known and unknown — every sexual sin, every adultery, every fornication, every perversion, every incest, every occult covenant involving sexuality, every dedication to false gods, every marine spirit covenant, every act of polygamy, prostitution, or sexual violence (Leviticus 26:40; Nehemiah 1:6–7).

By the blood of Jesus Christ, who became a curse for me (Galatians 3:13), I break every generational curse of sexual sin operating in my bloodline. I command every familiar spirit assigned to my family line through sexual sin to release me and depart, in Jesus' name. I declare that the curse stops with me. My children and my children's children are free, in Jesus' name. Amen.

Step 6 — Binding & Casting Out the Strong Man

Speak with authority — not loudness, but conviction.

In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, and by the authority of His shed blood, I now address you, Strong Man of Sex. I bind you. I bind every ruling spirit of lust and perversion assigned to my life. I bind you with the chains the Lord has provided (Matthew 12:29; Matthew 18:18).

I command you, Strong Man of Sex, and every spirit under your command — spirits of lust, fornication, adultery, pornography, masturbation, fantasy, perversion, homosexuality, seduction, Jezebel, spirit-husband, spirit-wife, sexual addiction, uncleanness, shame, and every related spirit — to come out of me now. Out of my mind. Out of my eyes. Out of my mouth. Out of my hands. Out of my reproductive organs. Out of my emotions. Out of my will. Out of my dreams. Out of my memory. Out of every cell of my body.

I command you to GO NOW, in the name of Jesus Christ. You have no more legal ground. The blood of Jesus has cleansed me. I belong to Jesus Christ. Leave me, and never return. Go to the place Jesus sends you. I command it, in Jesus' name. Amen.

Breathe out, cough, yawn, or sigh as the Lord brings release. Do not force manifestations — trust the Lord.

Step 7 — Cleansing

Lord Jesus, I ask You now to cleanse me by Your blood — every memory of sexual sin, every image stored in my mind, every sensation in my body, every shame in my soul. Wash me whiter than snow (Psalm 51:7). Renew my mind (Romans 12:2). Sanctify me wholly — spirit, soul, and body — and preserve me blameless until Your coming (1 Thessalonians 5:23).

Step 8 — Sealing with the Blood

I plead the blood of Jesus Christ over my mind, my eyes, my ears, my mouth, my hands, my heart, my reproductive organs, my dreams, my bed, my home, my devices, my relationships, and my entire life. I seal every door that has been closed. No spirit returns. I am bought with a price — I belong to Jesus Christ (1 Corinthians 6:19–20).

Step 9 — Infilling with the Holy Spirit

An empty house invites worse spirits (Matthew 12:43–45). Fill the cleansed space immediately.

Holy Spirit, I invite You to fill every place that has been cleansed. Fill me afresh. Fill my mind with pure thoughts (Philippians 4:8). Fill my heart with the love of God. Fill my body as Your temple. Fill my dreams with visions from heaven. Clothe me with the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness (Isaiah 61:3). I receive Your fullness now, in Jesus' name. Amen.
