        HELP

        Help and Phone Fixes

        ❦

        Tap a question to open the answer.

        SoundI pressed play but hear nothing▾
        1. Phones need one tap before they will play sound. If you see “Tap anywhere to begin listening,” tap the screen once.
        2. iPhone: check the silent switch on the side of the phone. If you see orange, flip it back. Then turn the volume up.
        3. Check that the sound is not going to Bluetooth earphones or a car you are no longer using.
        4. Still nothing? Press ■ (stop) in the player, then press ▶ Part 1 or Part 2 again.
        SoundThe reading stops when my screen locks▾
        1. Phones differ. Many keep playing after you lock the screen, but some pause the sound. Start the reading first, then lock the phone, and use the lock screen play button if it pauses.
        2. Leave this page open in your browser. Do not close the tab or swipe the browser away.
        3. Low Power Mode (iPhone) or Battery Saver (Android) can stop sound in the background. Turn it off while you pray.
        4. If it still stops, keep the screen on: iPhone Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never, then set it back afterwards.
        ReadingThe voice and the screen are not together▾
        1. Make sure “Scroll the page along with the reading” is ticked under Listening settings near the top.
        2. If you scroll the page yourself, the page stops following for a few seconds so you can read. It catches up on its own.
        3. Tap ▶ Listen from here on any point to start the voice exactly at that point.
        4. In Prayer Mode the study notes are skipped, so the voice moves from Pastor Kiky’s words and Scripture straight to the next point. In Study Mode with one level chosen, only that level is read.
        5. If nothing helps, reload the page. Your names, ticks, and quiz results are kept.
        SavingWill my names, ticks, and quiz be saved?▾
        1. Yes. Names, marks, journal answers, and quiz results are saved automatically in this browser on this phone. The page does not send them to any server.
        2. Open the page again in the same browser to continue. Use “Resume where I stopped” to continue the reading.
        3. Private or Incognito browsing does not save. Use a normal browser window.
        4. Clearing your browser history or website data erases what you saved.
        5. A different phone or browser starts fresh.
        SavingPut this study on my home screen▾
        1. iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
        2. Android (Chrome): tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to Home screen.
        CertificateMy certificate will not download▾
        1. iPhone: after you tap Download Certificate, the picture may open in a new view. Press and hold it, then tap Save to Photos.
        2. Or tap Print, then choose Save as PDF (or Save to Files).
        3. The Study Completion Certificate appears when you score 10 or more out of 12 in the optional quiz.
        OtherText too small, night mode, or starting over▾
        1. Make text bigger by spreading two fingers on the screen. On iPhone you can also tap “aA” in the address bar.
        2. Tap the Day / Night button at the top to switch the lighting.
        3. To start over, tap the progress circle at the top, then Start Over. Your family names and journal are kept.
