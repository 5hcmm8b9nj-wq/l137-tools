Module 1 · Surrounded — The Angel of the Lord Encampeth
See it
1
Scene

Morning. Believers wake in a new month. Warfare has not stopped, but the atmosphere shifts because heaven has already deployed its angels.

“The angel of the LORD encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.”Psalm 34:7 · KJV
“For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.”Psalm 91:11 · KJV
Read it — Pastor Kiky's words
“He has deployed His angels over us. We are covered, we are shielded by the angels of God and by the presence of the Lord.”

God sent His angels to walk with you today. You are not alone. You are surrounded, like a city with a wall around it.

Psalm 34 and Psalm 91 both promise angelic encampment for those who fear the Lord. This is not poetic — it is a covenant reality. Before you feel the attack, the angels are already stationed around you.

“Encampeth” (Hebrew chanah) means to pitch a military camp. The angel of the Lord takes a defensive posture around the God‑fearer. This is a corporate promise activated by the fear of the Lord — reverence, obedience, covenant loyalty — not by feeling.

Key pointFear of the Lord activates the angelic camp around your life.
Declare

I am not alone. I am covered. I am shielded by the angels of God.

Module 2 · The Word Builds You — Joshua's Charge
“There shall not any man be able to stand before thee all the days of thy life: as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee: I will not fail thee, nor forsake thee. Be strong and of a good courage: for unto this people shalt thou divide for an inheritance the land, which I sware unto their fathers to give them. Only be thou strong and very courageous, that thou mayest observe to do according to all the law, which Moses my servant commanded thee: turn not from it to the right hand or to the left, that thou mayest prosper whithersoever thou goest. This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.”Joshua 1:5, 6, 7, 8 · KJV
“And now, brethren, I commend you to God, and to the word of his grace, which is able to build you up, and to give you an inheritance among all them which are sanctified.”Acts 20:32 · KJV

Enemies will rise — kings, warriors, opposers of your season — but they cannot defeat you when the Word is in your mouth day and night. The Word is what will establish you when everything else is shaking.

Remember thisThe mind is the battleground. The Word renews the mind day by day and puts a standard in you the enemy cannot cross.
Say aloud

This book of the law shall not depart out of my mouth. I will meditate therein day and night, and my way shall be prosperous.

Module 3 · Triumph Over Principalities — The Cross
“Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross; and having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it.”Colossians 2:14, 15 · KJV

Whatever handwriting the enemy holds against you — ancestral, personal, contract of the darkness — Jesus already nailed it to the cross. He has already stripped principalities and powers and made an open show of them. Your deliverance is not being negotiated in heaven. It has been won.

Key pointEvery force of darkness bows to the name of Jesus. Even before you pray, they have already been publicly disgraced at Calvary.
Module 4 · The Angel Sent Before You — Exodus 23
“Behold, I send an Angel before thee, to keep thee in the way, and to bring thee into the place which I have prepared. Beware of him, and obey his voice, provoke him not; for he will not pardon your transgressions: for my name is in him. But if thou shalt indeed obey his voice, and do all that I speak; then I will be an enemy unto thine enemies, and an adversary unto thine adversaries. For mine Angel shall go before thee, and bring thee in unto the Amorites, and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites: and I will cut them off.”Exodus 23:20, 21, 22, 23 · KJV

Conquest means taking control of a place, a person or a problem by force, power, hard work or war. God is not asking you to fight the giants alone. He is sending His Angel — His Presence — ahead of you, and where His Presence goes, six nations of enemies get cut off: Amorite, Hittite, Perizzite, Canaanite, Hivite, Jebusite.

Your partObey His voice. Do not provoke Him. Do not rebel. Bear His name.
His partBe an enemy to your enemies. Be an adversary to your adversaries. Cut them off completely.
Listen & declare

My Father, be an enemy to my enemies. Be an adversary to my adversaries. Command Your angels to go before me and fight for me.

Module 5 · Seasons — Expectation to Manifestation
“While the earth remaineth, seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease.”Genesis 8:22 · KJV
“And the LORD visited Sarah as he had said, and the LORD did unto Sarah as he had spoken. For Sarah conceived, and bare Abraham a son in his old age, at the set time of which God had spoken to him.”Genesis 21:1, 2 · KJV

A season is a set time appointed by God for a specific purpose. You have been in a season of planting, pruning, weeping. That season has an end. God is saying: “I am taking control of your season.”

1
Scene

You feel like quitting. You want to abort. The tiredness is real.

2
Truth

Those signs are not proof God has abandoned you — they are labour signs. You are pregnant with something.

3
Word

Just as God spoke to Sarah, He speaks to you: at the set time. Expectation is turning to manifestation.

Declare

I am moving from expectation to manifestation. My expectations are turning into manifestations.

Module 6 · Pruning — The True Vine
“I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman. Every branch in me that beareth not fruit he taketh away: and every branch that beareth fruit, he purgeth it, that it may bring forth more fruit. Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you. Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, except it abide in the vine; no more can ye, except ye abide in me. I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing.”John 15:1, 2, 3, 4, 5 · KJV

The Father is a farmer. What He is doing right now in your life is purging — taking out lust, anger, selfishness, wrong attachments — so that a beautiful vessel comes out. It is not rejection. It is preparation.

Remember thisThe only way you cannot bear fruit is if you cut yourself off from the vine. Stay abiding — even in the pruning.
Module 7 · Eyes to See, Ears to Hear
“Then the disciples came, and said unto him, Why speakest thou unto them in parables? He answered and said unto them, Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given. For whosoever hath, to him shall be given, and he shall have more abundance: but whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken away even that he hath. Therefore speak I to them in parables: because they seeing see not; and hearing they hear not, neither do they understand.”Matthew 13:10, 11, 12, 13 · KJV
“But blessed are your eyes, for they see: and your ears, for they hear. For verily I say unto you, That many prophets and righteous men have desired to see those things which ye see, and have not seen them; and to hear those things which ye hear, and have not heard them.”Matthew 13:16, 17 · KJV
“For with thee is the fountain of life: in thy light shall we see light.”Psalm 36:9 · KJV

Dreams are closed visions. Open visions come while your eyes are open. Both are how God speaks. If you are not seeing yet, get closer to the Light — Jesus — because the closer you draw, the more He reveals.

Understand itThe sun rules the day, the moon rules the night. Both borrow their light from the greater light. Jesus is the greater Light. You are the lesser light — and you cannot shine one second longer than you abide in Him.

Pastor's testimony — teaching illustration
1
Scene

A believer's beautiful house. Behind the wealth, a family altar has kept sending torment for years.

2
Scene

A huge horned spirit — like a cow, bull or goat with horns all over its head — arrives at the door. The daughter recognises it: “This is the spirit that has been tormenting you.”

3
Scene

The battle moves into a river. The pastor of the flock steps into the water. The spirit flees.

4
Scene

Chasing the spirit back to the family house, they arrive to find that someone has already gone ahead and cut the cow in pieces. Deliverance.

Teaching principleWhere a covenant altar exists in a family line, torment repeats. But when the last generation stands in obedience, the Angel of the Lord goes ahead and cuts the covenant off.
Declare

I will be the last stop for that demon. That covenant is broken by the blood of Jesus.

Module 9 · One Encounter Changes Everything
“And I will give thee the treasures of darkness, and hidden riches of secret places, that thou mayest know that I, the LORD, which call thee by thy name, am the God of Israel. For Jacob my servant's sake, and Israel mine elect, I have even called thee by thy name: I have surnamed thee, though thou hast not known me. I am the LORD, and there is none else, there is no God beside me: I girded thee, though thou hast not known me.”Isaiah 45:3, 4, 5 · KJV

An encounter is a direct, life‑changing meeting with God — through a dream, a vision, His Word, or a man or woman of God He sends. One encounter can end what years of struggle could not:

🔥
Moses

Burning bush — sent to deliver a nation. (Exodus 3)

✋
Jacob

Wrestled till daybreak — became Israel. (Genesis 32)

✨
Saul → Paul

A bright light on the Damascus road — apostle. (Acts 9)

🕊️
Jesus at Jordan

Heavens opened, Spirit descended, Father spoke. (Matthew 3)

Key pointThe man at the pool waited thirty‑eight years. His deliverance was never in the pool — it was in the encounter with Jesus. Do not wait on the water. Wait on the Lord.
Module 10 · The Fast God Chooses — Reach the Poor
“Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke? Is it not to deal thy bread to the hungry, and that thou bring the poor that are cast out to thy house? when thou seest the naked, that thou cover him; and that thou hide not thyself from thine own flesh? Then shall thy light break forth as the morning, and thine health shall spring forth speedily: and thy righteousness shall go before thee; the glory of the LORD shall be thy reward. Then shalt thou call, and the LORD shall answer; thou shalt cry, and he shall say, Here I am.”Isaiah 58:6, 7, 8, 9 · KJV

Prayer needs partners. When the fast is joined to giving to the orphan, the widow and the poor, God moves suddenly. Faith is exercised through the scripture you obey — not only through the prayer you pray.

ApplyAsk the Lord what to give and to whom. Look for one orphan, one widow, one hungry family this week.

Prayer Library

Every corporate prayer in this service, organised by section.

1 · Praise & Worship
Prayer

Father, we thank You for the month of September. Your angels that watch over the month and the day recognise us and bless us. As it is written, the sun shall not smite us by day, neither the moon by night. Every element of creation that will be summoned by the evil one to contend against us shall not stand. May we conquer, may we prevail, may we overcome every satanic and demonic attack against our lives and our destinies, in the name of Jesus.

2 · Thanksgiving
Prayer

Lord, we are grateful. We show forth Your lovingkindness in the morning and Your faithfulness every night. Thank You for grace, for majesty, for covering, for angelic protection. From January until today, You have preserved us. Many were the plans of the evil one, nevertheless You have been good.

3 · Confession & Cleansing
Prayer

Have mercy upon me, O God, according to Your lovingkindness. Blot out my transgression, wash me throughly from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin. Let the blood of Jesus wash me, sanctify me and make me whole again. Let the blood atone for me and my family. In the sins of our ancestors, the sins of our forefathers, our personal sins and the sins of the land, let the blood of Jesus cleanse us. The enemy cannot hold my past against me. I am the redeemed of the Lord.

4 · Invite the Holy Spirit
Prayer

Holy Ghost, take charge over this meeting. Spirit of God, lead us. Guide us this morning. Release Your fire upon our gathering. Let any satanic projections and spells be broken through the power of the Holy Ghost. Help us to make intercession according to the will of God.

5 · Deliverance from Demonic Attacks
Repeat after me

My father, my father, every demonic spirit with horns on the head, with eyes to see and monitor my life — today, by the blood of Jesus, you that spirit that has been assigned in the family, you that power that has been deployed by my ancestors into my family, that has destroyed destinies for years and generations and wasted innocent lives unto death: today, by the blood of Jesus, I conquer you and I defeat you. You cannot conquer me. You cannot defeat me. You cannot defeat my children, my loved ones. As I receive strength from above, as I receive strength from the Holy Ghost, I conquer you and prevail over you. I dismantle you and I rescue all those you have chained and imprisoned. I liberate myself, my husband, my children, my loved ones, by the power of the Holy Ghost.

6 · Breaking Covenants & Foundations
Repeat after me

My father, by the blood of my covenant, which is the blood of Jesus, let the heavens fight back. Let the angels of God fight back against every form of darkness in my background, against every demonic power in my background that has succeeded in destroying lives and destiny. Today I will be the last stop for that demon. I come by the blood of Jesus and I declare victory. I conquer them and their kingdoms. I defeat them, and I rescue those who have been chained and imprisoned by that spirit, in the name of Jesus. I liberate my parents, my siblings, my family members. I command total deliverance.

7 · Encounter Prayer
Repeat after me

Father, in the name of Jesus, give me an encounter that will set me free forever. Give me an encounter that will terminate my long‑awaited bondage. Whatever has become an affliction in my life and destiny, today by the power of God, I am coming out in the name of Jesus. By that one encounter — an encounter in my dreams, an encounter through Your Word, an encounter through Your prophet, an encounter through the Holy Ghost — change my life for good. Let heaven descend on me. Let my prayer break through the roof and ascend before Your altar. God, hear my voice today, and give me one encounter.

8 · Restoration
Repeat after me

My father, my father, by the blood of Jesus, whatever they have taken from me — my money, my riches, my glory — I am taking them back in the name of Jesus. By the power and the anointing of the Holy Ghost, what they took from me has been restored, in the name of Jesus.

9 · Sealing with the Blood
Prayer

Father, we cover ourselves, our families, our homes, our finances, our health, our ministries, and this new season with the blood of Jesus. The blood speaks better things for us. We are sealed by the Spirit of God, in the name of Jesus.

Prophetic Dream Vault

Two prophetic instructions given by the Holy Ghost during the fast. Preserved as teaching illustrations only.

Dream 1

The President and the Mountains

The pastor is called out from a group of friends by a national president, who hands over bundles. When asked, the pastor says, “I have just come from the mountains.” On hearing this, the president goes back, retrieves more, and gives it. The dream ends with an assignment: organise the men and women of God to pray for him.

What it teachesTime spent in the secret place — the “spiritual mountains” of fasting and prayer — is what puts a believer in the position to receive great assignments and great supply. Access is granted to those who have paid the cost of intimacy.
“The secret things belong unto the LORD our God: but those things which are revealed belong unto us and to our children for ever.”Deuteronomy 29:29 · KJV
Dream 2

The Horned Spirit Defeated

The pastor visits a longtime intercessory relationship — a family already covered by years of ministry. A huge spirit shaped like a cow, bull or goat with horns all over its head knocks at the door. The daughter recognises it: “Mommy, this is the spirit that has been coming to torment you.” The battle moves into a river. The pastor enters the water and confronts the horned spirit. The spirit flees. Chasing it back to the family house, they arrive to find it has already been cut in pieces. Deliverance.

What it teaches
  • Some torment traces to a family altar, not the individual.
  • Faithfulness in covenant with a genuine shepherd matters.
  • When the last generation stands in obedience, God cuts the covenant.
Declare

That spirit with horns that has been fighting my family is defeated by the blood of Jesus. I will be the last stop.

Knowledge Check

A mix of question types. Answers stay hidden until you choose or reveal — then the explanation appears.

Apply It

Take the teaching from your ears into your week.

Scenario 1

You feel like quitting

You have been praying for a breakthrough for years. This week the tiredness hits so hard that you consider stopping. What is the biblical response?

Show a biblical response

Speak Genesis 21:1–2 over yourself: God comes to Sarah at the set time. The fatigue is a sign you are in labour, not that God has abandoned you. Refuse to go back to Egypt. Ask the Lord for one encounter that will end the long‑awaited bondage.

Scenario 2

A recurring family pattern

You notice the same sickness, poverty, or broken marriage repeating in your family line. What did the horned‑spirit dream teach?

Show a biblical response

Some torment is an assignment from a family altar, not from you personally. End‑of‑Teaching Review

Summary

God is not only a spiritual God — He is the God of all flesh. He fights spiritual, physical, mental and emotional battles for those who walk in covenant with Him. Because His Angel goes before you (Exodus 23), because the cross has already spoiled principalities (Colossians 2), and because His word is in your mouth day and night (Joshua 1), no man can stand before you all the days of your life. Your season of weeping is over. One encounter with Jesus can end what years of struggle could not.

Three things to remember
  1. The Angel of the Lord goes before you when you obey His voice.
  2. Your seasons are set by God — you are moving from expectation to manifestation.
  3. One encounter with Jesus ends long‑standing bondage. Wait on the Lord, not on the water.
