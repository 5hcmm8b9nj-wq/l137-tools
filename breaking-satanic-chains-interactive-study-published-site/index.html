Stage 1 · Prepare your heart

Come before the Lord

Before we break anything, we come humbly, thankful, forgiven, and filled with the Holy Spirit.

🙏🏾 Praise & Thanksgiving

Lift up the name of Jesus. Thank God for another day of life, and for the finished work of the cross. Praise Him for who He is before you ask Him for anything.

🕊️ Repentance & Forgiveness

Confess every known sin. Forgive anyone who has hurt you — spouse, family, boss, church member, even yourself. Unforgiveness holds chains in place. Let them go before the Father does the breaking.

✨ Invite the Holy Spirit

Say aloud: Holy Spirit, fill me afresh and strengthen me for prayer, in Jesus' name. Wait a moment. Do not rush past this — He is the One who breaks the chain.
