About the Ministry

L137 Deliverance Ministries

✠

Pastor Kiki Ocansey, Founder

L137 Deliverance Ministries International is a Christ-centered deliverance ministry founded by Pastor Kiki Ocansey. The ministry stands on the finished work of the Cross, the authority of the Word of God, and the power of the Blood of Jesus to set the captives free.

Through written prophetic directions, prayer guides, and live teaching rooms on Clubhouse, the ministry serves believers walking through deliverance — meeting them with Scripture, prayer, and prophetic counsel.
