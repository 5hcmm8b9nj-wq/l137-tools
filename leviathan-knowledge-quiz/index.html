Knowledge Quiz

Test what you’ve learned

Eight questions. Tap “Reveal answer” when you’re ready.

  1. 1 Multiple choice

    Leviathan is described in Scripture as the king over all what?

    • A. Children of disobedience
    • B. Children of pride
    • C. Children of fear
    • D. Children of strife
    Reveal answer›

    B — Children of pride.

    Job 41:34 (KJV) — “He is a king over all the children of pride.”

  2. 2 True or false

    Thanksgiving is one of the very first weapons God gives us against Leviathan.

    • True
    • False
    Reveal answer›

    True.

    Thanksgiving disarms pride. Leviathan cannot dwell where God is being magnified.

  3. 3 Fill in the blank

    Pastor Kiki teaches that “your understanding is your first ____________ for your deliverance.”

    Reveal answer›

    Open door.

    Understanding is the first open door for your deliverance. What you cannot identify, you cannot defeat.

  4. 4 Multiple choice

    Which of the following is NOT one of the five scales of Leviathan named in this workbook?

    • A. Witchcraft
    • B. Anger
    • C. Doubt
    • D. Rebellion
    Reveal answer›

    C — Doubt.

    The five scales taught here are Witchcraft, Anger, Rage, Mockery, and Rebellion. Doubt is a separate hindrance.

  5. 5 True or false

    Twisting communication, sowing pride, and dividing relationships are common ways Leviathan operates.

    • True
    • False
    Reveal answer›

    True.

    These three movements — twisting words, sowing pride, dividing relationships — are the fingerprints of Leviathan.

  6. 6 Multiple choice

    Which weapon does Pastor Kiki teach we should never neglect when contending against Leviathan?

    • A. The blood of Jesus
    • B. Persistent prayer
    • C. Worship
    • D. All of the above
    Reveal answer›

    D — All of the above.

    The blood, persistent prayer, and worship are all essential. Leviathan resists those who refuse to grow weary in well doing.

  7. 7 Fill in the blank

    The anchor scripture for L137 Deliverance Ministries is Luke 1:37 — “For with God nothing shall be ____________.”

    Reveal answer›

    Impossible.

    Luke 1:37 (KJV) — “For with God nothing shall be impossible.” The anchor of L137.

  8. 8 Reflection

    In one sentence: what is the Lord asking you to renounce today?

    Reveal answer›

    This one is between you and the Lord.

    There is no wrong answer. Bring whatever the Holy Spirit revealed into the Renunciation Prayer (Section Eleven of your workbook).

Memory verses

Tap each verse to read again

  1. I Zechariah 4:6 (KJV)

    Speak this when you grow weary of fighting in your own strength.

    Open verse›

    “Not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit, saith the LORD of hosts.”

    It is the Spirit of God — not your striving — that breaks every chain.

  2. II 2 Corinthians 10:4 (KJV)

    Speak this when the enemy attacks your mind.

    Open verse›

    “For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds.”

    Your weapons are spiritual. Pride is a stronghold. The blood, the Word, the Name of Jesus, and prayer tear it down.

  3. III John 8:36 (KJV)

    Speak this when the enemy tries to bring you back into bondage.

    Open verse›

    “If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed.”

    Your freedom is settled by Jesus. Stand in it. Do not let Leviathan re-coil what Christ has uncoiled.

Victory Declaration

  • I am set free in the name of Jesus Christ.
  • Every coil of Leviathan has been broken.
  • Every twisted word has been straightened.
  • Every divided relationship is being restored.
  • The blood of Jesus has settled my case.
  • The Holy Ghost has destroyed every yoke.
  • I walk in liberty, peace, and the favor of God.
  • What He has begun in me, He will complete.
  • I will not return to bondage. I am free indeed.
