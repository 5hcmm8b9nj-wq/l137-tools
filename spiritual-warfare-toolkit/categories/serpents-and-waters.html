The crooked serpent under water

Isaiah 27:1 · KJV
In that day the Lord with his sore and great and strong sword shall punish leviathan the piercing serpent, even leviathan that crooked serpent; and he shall slay the dragon that is in the sea.

Leviathan and the marine kingdom operate through pride, twisting, contention, and the covenants made — knowingly or unknowingly — with the waters. Sexual dreams, dream marriages, financial reversals, and the failure of covenants belong to this territory. This category exposes the serpentine and marine systems for what they are and delivers the believer through the blood of Jesus.
