Publishing policy
Every downloadable resource in this pack draws only from the clean, approved version of Pastor Kiky's teaching. Personal names, individual prophecies, personal counselling, and manifestations experienced by specific believers during the live service have been removed. The full biblical teaching, every corporate prayer, every corporate declaration, every scripture, and every deliverance principle is preserved.
