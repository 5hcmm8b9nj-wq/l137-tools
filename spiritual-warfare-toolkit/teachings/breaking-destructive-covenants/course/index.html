    Summary & completion

    Isaiah 28:18 · KJV
    And your covenant with death shall be disannulled, and your agreement with hell shall not stand.

    Key lessons

    • Every deliverance begins with thanksgiving, confession, forgiveness, and inviting the Holy Ghost.
    • A covenant is a legal, binding agreement with an attached condition — an invisible chain until it is broken.
    • Destructive covenants can be physical or spiritual, entered knowingly or unknowingly, and enter through many doorways.
    • Walking away from the person does not break the covenant. Renunciation by the blood of Jesus does.
    • The blood of Jesus cancels, breaks, cleanses, destroys legal rights, delivers from ancestral bondage, and speaks mercy.
    • Deliverance without sealing and aftercare leaves the house open — fill the house with the Word, worship, and fellowship.
