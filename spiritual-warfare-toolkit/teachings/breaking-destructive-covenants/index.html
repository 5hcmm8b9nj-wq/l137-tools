Main scripture · Isaiah 28:18 · KJV
And your covenant with death shall be disannulled, and your agreement with hell shall not stand; when the overflowing scourge shall pass through, then ye shall be trodden down by it.

What Pastor Kiky taught

  • A covenant is a legal, binding agreement — written or verbal — with an attached condition. Every covenant carries a condition, and as long as the condition is in force, the covenant is operational.
  • Destructive covenants may be entered knowingly (sexual sin, blood covenants, evil oaths, occult dedications) or unknowingly (ancestral contracts, words spoken during sex, messages sent by another person to an evil altar).
  • Demons are released to enforce the terms of the covenant. This is why some struggles are only broken through renunciation, not through general prayer.
  • Only the blood of Jesus cancels the handwriting of ordinances that stood against us. The cross has already spoiled principalities and powers openly (Colossians 2:14–15).
  • To be free, the believer must renounce, disconnect, and disannul every destructive covenant by the blood of Jesus — over themselves and over the ancestral line.
