Pastor Kiky Ocansey opens this teaching where the Lord Himself opens it — with knowledge.

Isaiah 5:13 · KJV
“Therefore my people are gone into captivity, because they have no knowledge: and their honourable men, and their multitude, and he that rejoiceth, shall descend into it.”

Her emphasis in the service is on two words: “my people.” The Lord did not say the world went into captivity. He said my people — people in church, people who pray, people who profess Christ. In this passage captivity is not caused by weak faith. It is caused by missing knowledge.

Pastor Kiky states in the teaching that studies show 7 out of 10 people in church are affected by this spirit — 7 out of 10 women who profess to be born again are involved, consciously or unconsciously, with an evil spiritual marriage, and in the same vein 7 out of 10 Christian men.

Pastor Kiky defines it plainly in the service:

  • A spirit husband or spirit wife is a demon — not a human being.
  • These spirits possess people in dreams, and can also manifest in the body or the spirit of a living person.

Pastoral safety note

Several items on this list — miscarriage, infertility, impotency, early menopause, depression, anxiety and addiction — also have medical and mental-health causes and treatments. Pastor Kiky’s teaching is spiritual warfare, not a diagnosis. Pray and also see a qualified doctor, midwife, or mental-health professional. The nightly blood-marking direction

Pastor Kiky gives this as a practical pastoral instruction, framed entirely through the finished work of Christ. The power is in the blood of Jesus — never in the liquid, never in the ritual.

  • Obtain communion wine. If it is unavailable, a red drink or water may be used.
  • Keep it beside your bed.
  • Every night before sleep, read Exodus 12:13 and Revelation 12:11 aloud.
  • Speak to the blood and give it an assignment in the name of Jesus.
  • Mark yourself — the private parts, the belly button, the body, the bedroom door and the marital room.
  • Then drink it.
  • Anoint the ring finger with oil and remove every invisible wedding ring placed on you in the spirit.
Her own instruction on persistence

She continued this nightly for over a year, until the dreams ceased completely. And she warns: the spirit knows when you are about to stop, and it returns. “Continue; don’t stop until you don’t see it anymore. Let it become your lifestyle.” As she says of communion: “Jesus says, as often as you do it, is in remembrance of me.”

Safety, privacy and about

Before You Begin

Pastoral safety note

This study is spiritual teaching and prayer ministry. It is not a substitute for medical, psychiatric or mental-health care. If you are unwell, in crisis, or having thoughts of harming yourself, seek qualified medical and pastoral help immediately. Do not stop prescribed medication. Do not fast if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, diabetic, on regular medication, recovering from illness, underweight, or have any history of an eating disorder — take medical advice first. Deliverance ministry and professional care are not enemies; wisdom uses both.

Anonymization and privacy

This is a cleaned public study. Every congregant name, personal testimony, personal prophecy, individual ministry session, location, phone number, family reference and identifying detail from the original services has been removed in line with the L137 publishing policy. Only Pastor Kiky Ocansey’s corporate teaching, corporate prayers, corporate declarations and full KJV Scripture remain. Nothing has been added to the teaching and no doctrine has been introduced.

How this study was compiled

Compiled from three Clubhouse services held by Pastor Kiky Ocansey on deliverance from spirit spouses: a phone-call deliverance service (03-05), Part Two of the teaching and prayer service (06-24), and a prayer meeting (11-03). Every teaching point on this site traces to those services. Scripture is quoted in full from the King James Version.

On physical elements

Communion wine, water and anointing oil are used here only as points of contact with the finished work of Jesus Christ. They have no power in themselves. There is no ritual, no salt bath, and no formula. The blood of Jesus is the power; the elements are only a way of agreeing with what He has already accomplished.

How to use this on your phone
