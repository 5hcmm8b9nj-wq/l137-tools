Section 05 — Genesis 3 — The Serpent Introduced

Timestamp 00:52:42

We're going to open to Genesis chapter three, beloved — verse one to five. Then we're going to move on to Genesis chapter three, verse fifteen as well. Deliverance from Serpentine Spirits.

Beloved, in this scripture, this was Eve having conversations with the serpent. And the Bible described the serpent as being more subtle than any beast of the field or than any beast that the Lord has made. And he came slowly, sat by her slowly and calmly. And he began to speak to the woman. He first asked the woman a question, then began to convince her that God is lying — that she will not die, but her eyes will open and she will be as gods. So the visitation of this serpent was to convince, was to lie, to manipulate.

So after what happened in the garden, when God came, this is the command that God gave. In all our life and generations, we will fight this serpent and spirit. It will fight us. It will try to hit us. It will try to stop us. It will try to wrestle with us. “I am declaring war between you, the serpent, and the woman; between your offspring and hers.” And the Bible says that he shall wound your head, and you will wound his heel. So there is a battle already declared between us and serpent and spirits.

Beloved, a snake is a very poisonous animal. It has the capacity to kill people once it penetrates into the human body. Snakes, serpents are known to be dangerous poisonous animals.

Scripture — Genesis 3:1-5 (KJV)

Now the serpent was more subtil than any beast of the field which the LORD God had made. And he said unto the woman, Yea, hath God said, Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden? And the woman said unto the serpent, We may eat of the fruit of the trees of the garden: But of the fruit of the tree which is in the midst of the garden, God hath said, Ye shall not eat of it, neither shall ye touch it, lest ye die. And the serpent said unto the woman, Ye shall not surely die: For God doth know that in the day ye eat thereof, then your eyes shall be opened, and ye shall be as gods, knowing good and evil.

Scripture — Genesis 3:15 (KJV)

And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel.

Repeat After Me
  • The seed of the woman shall bruise the head of the serpent.
  • I stand in the enmity God placed between me and the serpent.
  • Every deception the serpent whispered into my life — I renounce you tonight.

Section 06 — How the Serpent Attacks

Timestamp 00:59:41

Physically, when one is bitten by a snake: you suffer two puncture wounds; swelling and pain around the bite; redness and bruising; sometimes numbness of the face, especially the mouth; high blood pressure; difficulty breathing. Beloved, some people even die a premature death if a snake bites you physically. You experience dizziness, weakness, headaches, blurry vision, excessive sweating, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, fainting.

It is very dangerous. Even in the physical, when you are attacked by a snake, anybody physically that is attacked by a snake panics of death. Some people rush to get the venom out of their bodies before it starts spreading because it can kill. The presence of snakes in an environment is threatening the lives of people living in that area.

It is dangerous for you to be ignorant of a serpentine dream. Unless you rise up to the challenge of the spirit of snakes behind your dream life, you may not experience stable deliverance. A lot of people are attacked by snakes in their dream. There are many Christians today who are even afraid to kill a snake in their dream, let alone setting fire on them.

It is dangerous when you see a snake in a dream and are not able to kill it — destroy it when you wake up, rebuke it in the name of Jesus. It is dangerous when a snake bites you in the dream and you escape unhurt. It is dangerous when a snake is pursuing you in a dream — you need to pray and rebuke it. It is bad when a snake is eating something in your presence in the dream. It is even more dangerous when a snake is living with you in your house.

People who dream about snakes attacking them should know that they are under spiritual attack and manipulation of serpentine spirits. It is a sign of foundational problems in your life and in your destiny, beloved.

Some families worship serpents. Some have images of serpents in their homes. Some have jewellery that carries the images of serpents. Some have shrines and altars dedicated to the worship of serpent spirits in their families. The uprising of hatred in the yard of your father's house or mother's house can initiate evil serpents in the family. When there is a polygamous witchcraft in the family, it can work against marital and destiny of people in the family.

Scripture — Revelation 12:9 (KJV)

And the great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan, which deceiveth the whole world: he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him.

Prayer — Prayer Against Serpent Attacks

Every hidden serpent attacking my life, my mind, my dreams, and my destiny — be exposed by the light of Jesus Christ tonight. Every venom of deception injected into my thoughts — I flush you out by the blood of Jesus. Every altar of the serpent speaking against me — be silenced in the name of Jesus.
