Section 02 — Thanksgiving to God

Timestamp 00:21:45

We give God thanks and praise for all that He's done in our life and in our destiny. By the power of the Holy Ghost, we thank God tonight for our going out and our coming in — for the covering and protection of our lives, for watching over our family and children.

For waking us up this morning — it is not ordinary to just wake up in the morning. It is by His miracle; it is by His grace. Because many slept, beloved, and did not rise up again. But God has woken us up from our beds. We give Him thanks.

Thanksgiving is not just a prayer, but it is a weapon that silences complaints and unlocks our blessings. When we thank God, beloved, we acknowledge that He is the source of our life and protection on a daily basis.

The Psalm encourages joyful praise, recognizes God's greatness, and submits to His authority. But it also warns us against the dangers of disobedience and unbelief. Praise God joyfully. Give thanks in the name of Jesus for all that He has done for your life, for your family, and for your children in the mighty name of Jesus.

Section 04 — Invite the Holy Ghost & Receive the Fire

Timestamp 00:40:41

Beloved, tonight before we begin this prayer and this deliverance, we invite the Holy Ghost. We receive the Holy Ghost fire to destroy every unclean spirit. Tonight, we stand on the authority of Jesus Christ and declare: We receive the fire of the Holy Ghost to destroy every unclean spirit.

The fire of God is not just symbolic, beloved — it is real. It is powerful. It is the weapon of heaven to consume all the works of darkness in our life and in our destiny. Every unclean spirit, whether hiding in our homes, in our dreams, in our bodies, in our families, must be exposed and destroyed by the fire of the Holy Ghost. In the name of Jesus, we are not afraid, for greater is He that is in us than he that is in the world.

Just as Elijah called down fire from heaven to consume the sacrifice and silence the false prophets, we too call upon the fire. We call down the consuming fire of God tonight to bring judgment on every unclean spirit, every unclean thing working against our life and working against our peace.

We receive the fire tonight of the Holy Ghost. Let every unclean spirit, beloved, troubling our life be destroyed by the fire of the Holy Ghost. “Who the Son sets free is free indeed.”

  • In the name of Jesus, I command every evil spirit to come out of me.
