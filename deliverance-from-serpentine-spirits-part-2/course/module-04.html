Section 08 — How Serpent Spirits Infiltrate

Timestamp 01:13:59

These serpents, or shall I say serpent spirits, carry out the agenda of the devil, which is killing and stealing and destroying your life, killing your destiny, stealing the destiny, killing your potentials, destroying everything in your life.

They attack you physically and in dreams. They inject poison into your body through snake bites in the dream — anytime you see a dream of a snake bite, you need to drink the blood, begin to pray, rebuke every venom that will weaken you, bring affliction, sickness.

Serpent spirits introduce terrible sickness and affliction through eating in the dream, drinking in the dream, through sex in the dream — some of the sex in the dream is not with ordinary people but with serpents. Some attack your neck through evil deposits and plantation. They inhabit the body, moving about it, polluting the blood and biting at the same time.

Serpents swallow up good things in your life and your destiny — you're not able to live freely. They attack you when you help someone under their attack. They bring sudden captivity, destruction, slavery, and install fear in you. Some of you have so much fear and you don't know where it's coming from — you are under serpent spirit attack.

They seduce people through evil suggestion and temptations — just as with Eve, they manipulate and redirect destiny. They attack the truth, repentance, and spirit; they redesign and bring down glorious destiny by engineering setbacks and reversal of fortunes; they promote curses upon your life.

Anybody going through these attacks — even from family worship, through interactions with marine agents, having sex with them, interacting with them — can initiate serpent spirit attacks in your life.

Scripture — Luke 10:19 (KJV)

Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you.

Prayer — Closing the Doors of Infiltration

Every open door in my life through which serpentine spirits have entered — I close you tonight by the blood of Jesus. Every ungodly covenant, every generational altar, every soul-tie, every occult contact — I renounce you in the name of Jesus. Every serpent that entered through my eye-gate, ear-gate, mouth-gate, or dream life — be flushed out tonight.

Section 09 — Life Impact of Serpent Spirits

Timestamp 01:18:52

When your life is under these attacks: your destinies are swallowed up, your future turns blank, you don't know where you are going forward. Strange diseases which should only be for your enemies show up in your life.

Riches-to-rags stories, highs and lows, demotion from grace to grass — it keeps you in the tail, not the head. Living below heaven's standard and expectations for your life. You are always confused, lack focus, lack plan, no clarification of where you are going.

Your life is diverted — just like Eve, what God wants for you is diverted. You were sucked from where God placed you in comfortability. This spirit attacks your life to waste it, bringing wrong positioning — both in the dream and in the physical realm.

You miss your divine timing — absence in the day of goodness, progress, favour. You're always making errors and wondering, “Why me?” You refuse to listen to sound advice. Your helpers disappear and become uncooperative. Your pursuers vanish after the first encounter. Backwardness — others that started with you catch up or overtake you.

The Lord wants to touch you and deliver you from this spirit. In the name of Jesus, a lot of us in this room are dealing with serpent spirits, whether we like it or not.

This verse is a victory verse for you and I against these spirits.

Scripture — Deuteronomy 7:15 (KJV)

And the LORD will take away from thee all sickness, and will put none of the evil diseases of Egypt, which thou knowest, upon thee; but will lay them upon all them that hate thee.

Scripture — Exodus 15:16 (KJV)

Fear and dread shall fall upon them; by the greatness of thine arm they shall be as still as a stone; till thy people pass over, O LORD, till the people pass over, which thou hast purchased.

Scripture — Psalm 74:13-14 (KJV)

Thou didst divide the sea by thy strength: thou brakest the heads of the dragons in the waters. Thou brakest the heads of leviathan in pieces, and gavest him to be meat to the people inhabiting the wilderness.

Prayer — Prayer Against Serpent Fruit

Every fruit of the serpent operating in my life — stagnation, delay, sickness, marital confusion, financial reversal, dream attack, mental oppression — I uproot you tonight in the name of Jesus. Let the axe be laid to the root of every evil tree the enemy has planted in my life.
