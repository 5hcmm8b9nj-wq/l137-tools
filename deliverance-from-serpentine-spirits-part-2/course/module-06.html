Communion & Expelling Venom Not started

Estimated time: 25–30 min · Sections 12–13

Take Communion as the new covenant in His blood, then expel every venom of the serpent — fear, unbelief, lust, bitterness, and confusion.

“And to Jesus the mediator of the new covenant, and to the blood of sprinkling, that speaketh better things than that of Abel.”Hebrews 12:24 (KJV)

Section 12 — Communion — The Body & Blood

Timestamp 01:41:59 & 01:47:45

Take your communion wine — or your cup of water, if you have it with you. But get communion wine if you can. It says, “We overcome him by the blood of the Lamb and by the words of our testimony.”

The blood of Jesus you are holding is the new covenant. The covenant that is running through your veins in the bloodline is the old covenant — the covenant that was made with any ancestral spirit, idols, serpent spirits. In the name of Jesus, by the blood of Jesus, we cancel it. We negate against it in the name of Jesus.

As the blood enters your veins, let Him begin to mediate for you. He is the mediator of the new covenant. In the name of Jesus, He is the blood of sprinkling that speaks for better things for your life and for your destiny as He enters into your body tonight. May it begin to fight every ancestral blood of serpent covenants. May it begin to fight any serpent spirit of destruction in your bloodline. May it begin to cancel any legality between you and any serpent spirit, serpent spirit worship in the family.

Let that spirit know in the name of Jesus that you are under a new supervision. You are under a new authority. You are under a new covering. You are under a new power in the name of Jesus. As you drink the blood of Jesus, let it begin to speak of victory for your life, for your finance, for your marriage. Whatever property of yours that the serpent spirit has swallowed — as you drink the blood of Jesus, it vomits it out now in the mighty name of Jesus.

When you mark yourself with this blood in the name of Jesus — any serpent spiritual marriage, serpent sex in the dream — as you mark yourself with the blood, may it pass over you by the power of the Holy Ghost. And the plague that is meant for you would not come to destroy your life by this spirit.

Scripture — Hebrews 12:24 (KJV)

And to Jesus the mediator of the new covenant, and to the blood of sprinkling, that speaketh better things than that of Abel.

Scripture — Exodus 12:13 (KJV)

And the blood shall be to you for a token upon the houses where ye are: and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you, when I smite the land of Egypt.

Prayer — Communion Prayer — Repeat After Me

In the name of Jesus, as I drink the blood of Jesus, as I mark myself with the blood of Jesus — Serpent powers of my father's house, die in the name of Jesus. Serpent powers of my mother's house, die in the name of Jesus. Every idol of the serpent gods in my life — I fire back! Every sign against my life, because I drink the blood of Jesus, come out in the name of Jesus. Every dream animal assigned against my destiny, because I drink the blood of Jesus, come out in the name of Jesus. Sin, die in the name of Jesus. Come out from me in the name of Jesus. By the blood, by the blood, by the blood, by the blood. God of deliverance, send down Your fire right now. Come and deliver me. Come and deliver my family. In the name of Jesus. Through the blood of Jesus. I rebuke every serpent that has swallowed me — by the power of Jesus, by the blood of Jesus. Every ancestral idol, every ancestral debt collector forcing me to pay for what I did not buy — as I drink the blood of Jesus, come out of my life. Come out of my body. In the name of Jesus. I deliver myself from any serpent in my life, from any serpent that is destroying and hindering my purpose. In the mighty name of Jesus, I command you to leave this body now.

Section 13 — Expel the Venom of the Serpent

Timestamp 01:54:20

We render every serpent powerless. As you have drunk the blood of Jesus, as you have taken the mediator in the name of Jesus, the blood of sprinkling — by the power of the Holy Ghost, any serpents in your vessel, in your bloodline, that is hindering and fighting your progress, let it come out in the name of Jesus.

By every opening in your body, in the name of Jesus, every serpent spirit, serpent deposit, serpent poison and venom in your blood that is causing unknown sickness — we command you to come out now. Liquid, by every opening of the body, you vomit out. You yawn it out in the name of Jesus. Any python spirits, any cobra spirits, any unclean spirits in the form of a serpent, you vomit out now.

We silence any voice of the serpent in your mind. Whatever fear he has brought to you, we command it out of your vessel now.

Scripture — Psalm 37:14-15 (KJV)

The wicked have drawn out the sword, and have bent their bow, to cast down the poor and needy, and to slay such as be of upright conversation. Their sword shall enter into their own heart, and their bows shall be broken.

Repeat After Me
  • In the name of Jesus, every serpent in my foundation swallowing my destiny — I command you to come out of my mind, come out of my mouth.
  • In the mighty name of Jesus, every serpent in my blood, in the name of Jesus, I command you to come out now.
  • In the name of Jesus, I neutralize every serpent defilement.
  • I neutralize every serpent environment, every serpent pollution, every serpent contamination with the blood of Jesus.
  • Let the Holy Ghost arrest every serpent of darkness assigned against my life. I bind them against my family.
  • I destroy their power by the Holy Spirit.
  • Any serpent trouble in my life, any evil serpent money in my life — in the name of Jesus, come out now.
  • Any poison or substance in my body, come out now.
  • Every unholy, every ungodly, every unconscionable covenant and soul tie — reach the serpent powers and snakes.
  • Catch fire right now and burn.
  • I pull down every ancestral serpent altars militating against my life.
  • I cast out the spirits of snake and serpent working against my life.
  • I build a new altar for myself and for my family using the blood of Jesus.
