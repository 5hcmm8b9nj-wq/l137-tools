Estimated time: 35–40 min · Sections 20–24

Receive the anointing, complete the seven-breath deliverance exercise, sow at the altar, respond to the salvation call, and be sealed with the closing benediction.

“For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”Romans 10:13 (KJV)

Section 20 — Anointing & Blessings

Timestamp 02:39:52

In the name of Jesus, I anoint you. As you anoint yourself tonight, be covered, be protected in the name of Jesus. Let the protection of the Lord by the power of the Holy Ghost from any serpent spirits come upon your life right now. The peace in the name of Jesus. As you anoint yourself with this oil, let there be victory in the name of Jesus come upon your life. Full deliverance.

May the Lord strengthen you as you anoint yourself with this oil. Let the Lord's presence fill your home. As you anoint yourself right now, let Christ be exalted in your life, in your home. We break every chain in the name of Jesus. We surrender all your burdens that these serpent spirits has brought into your life unto Jesus. Let His grace sustain you. We surrender every fear to Jesus. We surrender every worry to Jesus.

Let this anointing remind you that you belong to Jesus. Every serpent preacher in your bloodline, or preacher in your home, let them be silenced and cast out by this anointing. Let the Holy Ghost lead you on a daily basis.

Receive the discernment of the Lord — the discernment for you to detect the voices, to know the difference between the voices, because the serpent can whisper, and God can also whisper. But discernment for you to detect the truth. Discernment for you to know the right direction. Discernment so you will not be diverted in the name of Jesus.

May the Lord provide your daily needs, strength to pray. May the Lord direct your steps. May the Lord's favour rest upon you. May the Lord bring positive people that would help your life moving forward. We rebuke any assignment of the serpent to redirect your helpers. May you be delivered from every serpent spirit and their powers in your life. Anoint yourself with the oil in the name of Jesus.

Scripture — Psalm 34:7 (KJV)

The angel of the LORD encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.

Scripture — Isaiah 10:27 (KJV)

And it shall come to pass in that day, that his burden shall be taken away from off thy shoulder, and his yoke from off thy neck, and the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing.

Prayer — Anointing & Blessing Prayer

Lord Jesus, You are the true Anointing. As we apply this oil, let every yoke be broken because of the anointing. Bless our health. Bless our homes. Bless our work of our hands. Bless our children. Bless our finances. Let the angel of the Lord encamp round about us and deliver us from every hidden trap of the serpent.

Section 21 — Breathing Deliverance Exercise (7 Breaths)

Timestamp 02:46:40

Before we finish, we're going to do this breathing exercise. As we breathe in and out, any oppressions of the serpent spirits in your life, in your body — may it come out by the power of the Holy Ghost. As you breathe in the Holy Ghost, may the Holy Ghost settle in your life, settle in your body. Enter into any darkness and bring light in the vessel, in your mind, in your life.

As you breathe in the Holy Ghost, every distraction of the serpent spirit come out of your body. Any venom, any poison, any deposit in the name of Jesus — let it come out of your body as you breathe. Breathe in the Holy Ghost, breathe in the peace, breathe in good health, breathe in good direction, good plans. By the power of the Holy Ghost, as you breathe in the Holy Spirit, receive strength. Receive clarity. We take away every fear this spirit has brought.

We're going to do this seven times.

For each round: (1) Breathe in deep through your nose — as deep as you can, until you feel it in your chest. Take in the Holy Ghost. (2) Hold — do not speak, do not release. Let the Holy Spirit work in your body, your mind, your intestines, your bloodline, your kidneys, your joints, your bones, your decision-making. (3) When told, breathe out through your mouth and shout “Out!” in the name of Jesus. Cough, burp, or yawn it out if you feel led — every unclean spirit come out in the name of Jesus. (4) Between rounds — anything that my Father did not plant in this body, let it come out. Any deposit in the dream, snake bite venoms, poison — let it come out.

Round 7 — Eyes Closed. Close your eyes as you breathe in the Holy Spirit. As you open your eyes on the out-breath, you open to clarity, to good vision, to good direction — in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. Wisdom to speak by the power of the Holy Ghost. Attract the right people at the right time in the name of Jesus. Shout, “Out, eyes open!” in the name of Jesus.

Begin to thank the Lord for your deliverance tonight.

Scripture — Genesis 2:7 (KJV)

And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.

Prayer — Breathing Deliverance Exercise — 7 Breaths

Breath 1 — Breathe in the Holy Ghost. Breathe out every serpent. Breath 2 — Breathe in the peace of God. Breathe out every fear. Breath 3 — Breathe in the fire of God. Breathe out every venom. Breath 4 — Breathe in the blood of Jesus. Breathe out every accusation. Breath 5 — Breathe in the healing of Christ. Breathe out every sickness. Breath 6 — Breathe in freedom. Breathe out every burden. Breath 7 — Breathe in the anointing. Breathe out every yoke. End with: In Jesus' name — I am free.

Prayer — Thanksgiving After the Breathing Exercise

Thank You, Jesus. Thank You, my God, for deliverance. Thank You, Lord, by the power of the Holy Ghost. Hallelujah. Thank You, Father, for touching me. Thank You for delivering me. Thank You, Lord, for separating me from every unclean spirit, every serpent spirit — from my father's side, my mother's side — in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. Thank You for deliverance, O Lord Jesus, from this wicked spirit. O Lord Father, deliver my children, my home. In the name of Jesus, wherever this spirit is hiding in my family, Lord, thank You for deliverance in the name of Jesus.

Section 22 — Seed & Deliverance Altar

Timestamp 02:57:56

Seed sowing is voluntary and part of the ministry's practice. It is framed as an act of faith and covenant with God, not a payment for deliverance.

As you place your seed, disconnect yourself from any ancestral worship of serpent spirit. As I place this seed, Lord, I place it for any ancestral worship of serpent spirit in my family that is tormenting my life today. That has married several people in my family, that is married to me spiritually — I break that covenant. I break that marriage in the name of Jesus.

As I place my seed, O Lord Father, I place my family name, my bloodline, in the name of Jesus, on this altar of deliverance. Deliver my family, Lord. Deliver my home, Lord. Deliver my children, Lord. Deliver me in the name of Jesus from many attacks of serpent spirit in my life that is diverting my destiny, that is diverting my peace, that is diverting my finances, my career, that is destroying in the name of Jesus.

I neutralize every serpent defilement, pollution, contamination in my life as I place a seed. Every serpent caged in my destiny — as I place this seed, let it be released. Every serpent in my blood — as I place the seed, come out of my bloodline. Come out of my family and my family members.

As I place this seed on this altar, Lord — I build a new altar and I destroy any serpent altar in my family destroying our finances, our career, our marriages, and diverting our steps. I build a new altar for myself and for my family using the blood of Jesus. I raise this altar and I pull down every ancestral altar, serpent altar that is militating against my life and against my family. In the mighty name of Jesus.

Scripture — Malachi 3:10 (KJV)

Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be meat in mine house, and prove me now herewith, saith the LORD of hosts, if I will not open you the windows of heaven, and pour you out a blessing, that there shall not be room enough to receive it.

Prayer — Seed Prayer

Father, whatever seed we release tonight, let it stand as a memorial before You. Let every seed sown in this altar bring a testimony of deliverance, healing, and open windows of heaven. Not as ritual but as worship, we align our giving to Your kingdom and Your Christ.

Section 23 — Salvation & Rededication Prayer

Timestamp 03:04:08

If you know you are not born again, or you would like to rededicate your life to Jesus Christ, then say this prayer tonight.

The word of the Lord says that whoever calls upon the name of the Lord will be saved. If you would like to know Christ, all you have to do is receive the free gift of salvation through Jesus Christ by saying this prayer.

We believe that if you pray this simple prayer, you are born again. You are starting with a clean slate. You have entered into a personal relationship with Jesus Christ. Keep God's first place in your life. Learn to pray, fast, forgive. Prayer is simply talking to God like you would talk to a friend. Mark today's date; write it down in your Bible, in your journal. This is the day of new beginnings in your life in Jesus' name.

Scripture — Romans 10:13 (KJV)

For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.

Prayer — Salvation Prayer — Repeat After Me

Lord Jesus, I repent of my sins and surrender my life. Wash me clean. I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, that He died on the cross for my sins, and rose again on the third day for my victory. I believe that in my heart and make a confession with my mouth that Jesus is my Saviour and Lord. I receive eternal life in Jesus' name. Amen.

Section 24 — Closing & Benediction

Timestamp 03:08:55

We continue to pray in the name of Jesus over this spirit, and may the Lord deliver us and cover us in our family, in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.

Scripture — Proverbs 18:10 (KJV)

The name of the LORD is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe.

Prayer — Closing Benediction

May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the sweet fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with us now and forevermore. May goodness and mercy follow us. May the Lord protect us as we go to bed tonight. We rebuke any retaliation of this prayer tonight in the name of Jesus. May the Lord protect us. May the angels encamp around us — the ministering angels, the warring angels — and give us sound sleep as we wake up in the morning in Jesus' name. Amen. Shalom.
