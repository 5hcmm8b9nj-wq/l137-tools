Estimated time: 20–25 min · Sections 10–11

The believer's response — repent, resist, and enter into the first wave of corporate deliverance prayer against every serpentine power.

“Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.”James 4:7 (KJV)

Section 10 — Call to Repentance & Resistance

Timestamp 01:24:52

Beloved, we need victory. We need deliverance by the power of the Holy Ghost. And if we need this deliverance and victory, we need to: repent and deal with any sin that made this spirit enter our life; surrender our life to Christ; deal with fear and unbelief in our life; be filled with the Holy Ghost — that serpent cannot stand the fire; pray, fast, set yourself on fire by the power of the Holy Ghost; exercise your spiritual authority over this spirit; trust God and believe in the promises of the Lord upon your life.

The Lord wants to deliver you by the power of the Holy Ghost.

Scripture — James 4:7 (KJV)

Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

Repeat After Me
  • I submit myself to God tonight.
  • I resist the devil in the name of Jesus.
  • Every serpent voice I have listened to — I silence you tonight.

Section 11 — Deliverance Prayers Begin

Timestamp 01:27:46

If you have had an encounter — if you feel like your family worships idols like snake spirits, you dated somebody and you know that this spirit is attacking your life after you've dated them, you've had friendship with certain people that this spirit has begun to attack you, you prayed with somebody and now this spirit is beginning to attack your life — and now you are feeling like, “Ah, I am always making errors. I feel like my life is being diverted. I feel like I am in the wrong positioning in life.”

Wrong positioning, wasted life. Beloved, it is all from these spirits. You cannot focus anymore; you are always confused; you are hearing voices in your ears; you are hearing things crawling on your body. We rebuke the strength of this spirit in your life and in your destiny by the power of the Holy Ghost.

Scripture — Isaiah 10:27 (KJV)

And it shall come to pass in that day, that his burden shall be taken away from off thy shoulder, and his yoke from off thy neck, and the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing.

Prayer — Opening Deliverance Prayer — Pray Aloud

By the power of the Holy Ghost, I pray for the cover, the covenant of the Lord. I cover everything within me, everything around me, and everything over me now with the blood of Jesus. It says by the blood we overcome these great serpents. By the blood. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, the serpents of impossibility in my life, in my family, I command it to die by fire. In the name of Jesus, every serpent power and scorpion power working against my life, let it be disgraced. By the power of the Holy Ghost, every serpent manipulation upon my life and my family, let it be frustrated. Let it be rendered impotent right now in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. Every serpent and all serpents, I command them to vomit my prosperity, to vomit my health, to vomit my marriage, to vomit my finances, to vomit in the name of Jesus my spiritual strength. All serpents from my mother's house, my father's house, serpents that I connected with a friend and loved one, serpents that I connected that is attacking my life, that has swallowed my health, that has swallowed my marriage, that has swallowed my finances, that has swallowed my spiritual growth — I command them to vomit them out tonight in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. I cut every soul tie with any satanic spirit. Let it be cut off right now. I cut off every soul tie of any satanic spirit that is hindering my life, that is stopping my progress, that is fighting my marriage, that is causing health problems, that is fighting my finances. I cut them off right now. Every altar of stubbornness against God, every altar of rebellion against my name and my life — in the name of Jesus Christ, let it be a fire in the name of Jesus Christ. Every serpent poison, every serpent venom in my body — come out from me in the name of Jesus.
