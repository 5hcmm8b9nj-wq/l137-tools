Section 07 — Overcoming by the Blood & Authority

Timestamp 01:09:26

Beloved, the last time we met, we talked about Revelation chapter twelve. I explained to you that the serpent spirit is the great dragon that was cast out of heaven, and the Lord says this spirit will torment us, destroy a lot of things in our life, and fight our homes and families.

He says the great dragon was cast down, the old serpent, the devil. But the Lord did not leave us empty. He says we will only overcome him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of our testimony. That is how to fight this serpent spirit from your life. You get the communion wine, pray with it, eliminate his presence in your life. It is a promise in the Bible that when you use the blood of Jesus, it will fight these spirits — it will destroy its strength, its plans.

God has given you power to tread on snakes and scorpions. Exercise that power now by the power of the Holy Ghost.

Scripture — Revelation 12:9-12 (KJV)

And the great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan, which deceiveth the whole world: he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him. And I heard a loud voice saying in heaven, Now is come salvation, and strength, and the kingdom of our God, and the power of his Christ: for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night. And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death. Therefore rejoice, ye heavens, and ye that dwell in them. Woe to the inhabiters of the earth and of the sea! for the devil is come down unto you, having great wrath, because he knoweth that he hath but a short time.

Scripture — Luke 10:19 (KJV, restated)

Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you.

Repeat After Me
  • I overcome the old serpent by the blood of the Lamb.
  • I overcome by the word of my testimony.
  • I do not love my life unto the death — I belong to Jesus Christ.

Knowledge Check

Answer from the teaching above, then check your work.

1. According to Revelation 12:11, how do believers overcome the accuser?

2. What authority does Luke 10:19 give the believer?
