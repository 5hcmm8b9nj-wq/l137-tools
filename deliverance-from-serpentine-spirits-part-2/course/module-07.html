Section 14 — Breaking Ancestral Altars

Timestamp 02:00:46

Neutralize every dream pollution and poison in my body right now, and poison in my mind right now. In the mighty name of Jesus, I bind any idol, any serpent spirit in my body, serpent spirit in my mind connected to my family.

Every serpent that has swallowed you, let him vomit you out now in the name of Jesus. Every debt collector and idol worship in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth — for mending your finances, for mending your marriage, for mending your health — by the power of the Holy Ghost, we uproot it now out of your life and stop touching your foundation.

From poison right now in your blood, right now in your veins — right now in the name of Jesus Christ, I cast out all spirits of depression from your life. By every opening in your body, any serpent tormenting your life, tormenting your mind — in the name of Jesus, I command it to go out right now.

Any venom in your body that is causing affliction, swap it out right now by the power and authority of Jesus. Serpent, come out of your life right now. Come out from the ear, the mouth, and the eyes. Come out now in the name of Jesus and speak it out. Cry it out. Vomit it out.

Serpent that has come into your finance. Serpent tormenting your mind. Serpent that has tormented your life, tormented your body, brought afflictions, sicknesses. Serpent diverting your destiny. Serpent that has wicked position in your life. I command it to come out of your life right now — every serpent that has redirected your life, every serpent from your family, your bloodline, serpent that you connected to through a man, through a woman, through a marine agent — I command them to leave you now.

If it is a serpent that has become a pain in your stomach, a pain in your back, a pain in your joints — I command it right now: spit it out. Come out now in the name of Jesus.

Section 15 — Deliverance & Freedom Decrees

Timestamp 02:07:09

Any serpent that is swallowing good things in your life, I command it to come out! I command them to vomit it out now! Any serpent that has laid any eggs and deposited in your life problems, I command it — let those problems come out now in the name of Jesus. Be delivered now. I declare deliverance in the name of Jesus.
