Module 08 of 09 · Sections 16–19

Casting Out the Viper & House Cleansing Not started

Estimated time: 30–35 min · Sections 16–19

Shake off the viper like Paul at Malta, issue ongoing deliverance commands, cleanse your house by the blood, and claim total deliverance.

“And when Paul had gathered a bundle of sticks, and laid them on the fire, there came a viper out of the heat, and fastened on his hand.”Acts 28:3 (KJV)

Section 16 — Viper Cast Out — Acts 28

Timestamp 02:11:36

Let the fire of God dry out every serpent right now from your life, according to Acts chapter twenty-eight verse three. Right now, wherever the viper is hiding in your body, let it come out now. I cast out every viper that would operate in your life, that has operated in your body, that has operated in your family.

Just as Paul was bitten in the name of Jesus on the island by the viper, and he shook it off — we shake it off right now, in the name of Jesus. I destroy the viper-like spirit by the blood, in the name of Jesus.

Scripture — Acts 28:3 (KJV)

And when Paul had gathered a bundle of sticks, and laid them on the fire, there came a viper out of the heat, and fastened on his hand.

Prayer — Shake Off the Viper Prayer

Every viper that has fastened itself to my hand, to my work, to my calling — I shake you off into the fire of the Holy Ghost tonight. Every venomous beast attempting to poison my destiny — you shall not live. I feel no harm in the name of Jesus.

Repeat After Me
  • Any serpent poison, any serpent venom in my blood, I command it to come out now in the name of Jesus.
  • By every opening of my body, serpent scales, come out now in the name of Jesus Christ.
  • Serpent from the waters assigned against my life, come out now in the name of Jesus Christ.
  • Every covenant, I rebuke the serpent's power. Satan, I bind you by the blood of Jesus.
  • By the blood of Jesus, I break that covenant.
  • I break those covenants now — from my mother's side, from my father's side — in the name of Jesus Christ.
  • I break every agreement I ever made with the enemy.
  • I bind every demon, every animal, and every angry spirit in my life to manifest and come out now.

Section 17 — Ongoing Deliverance Commands

Timestamp 02:14:24

Every inherited serpent from your bloodline, from your mother's side, from your father's side — I command it to come out now. In the name of Jesus Christ. By air or liquid, every opening of the body — burp it out, run it out, vomit it out now. Serpent powers of my place of birth, come out now. Serpent powers in my life and on the earth, come out in the name of Jesus.

In the mighty name of Jesus, every unclean spirit, every unclean serpent spirit possession to bring bondage into your life and bondage into your generation — we set it on fire right now. Every unclean animal feeding on your life, feeding on your intestines, feeding on your blood — right now, vomit out now in the name of Jesus. Every witch of serpent spirit rising against you in your dream, let it come out now — vomit out, cough it out, burp it out.

Serpent spirit assigned to hinder your progress and your breakthrough, vomit out now in the mighty name of Jesus. Every serpent spirit agreement between you and any unclean animals, vomit out. We break those covenants right now, those agreements in the name of Jesus. I paralyze the hands of every serpent spirit against your life now.

Every spirit that has access into your life, into your mind, into your business — I exorcise it right now in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. Every satanic spirit came in your marriage, catch fire right now.

I release the spirit of the King's Builder. I tap into your potential, your business, your ideas. In the name of Jesus, your plans — we set it on fire right now. Any torment of the mind, we command those spirits to leave you now. Every serpent power that wants your destiny to crawl, to be slow, to die out — let it expire right now! Every stubborn enemy in your father's house coming in your destiny — in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, Father God, we set it on fire.

Scripture — Mark 1:27 (KJV)

And they were all amazed, insomuch that they questioned among themselves, saying, What thing is this? what new doctrine is this? for with authority commandeth he even the unclean spirits, and they do obey him.

Scripture — Psalm 59:1-3 (KJV)

Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God: defend me from them that rise up against me. Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, and save me from bloody men. For, lo, they lie in wait for my soul: the mighty are gathered against me; not for my transgression, nor for my sin, O LORD.

Prayer — Ongoing Deliverance Commands

Every unclean spirit still holding on — I command you, come out and go, in the name of Jesus. Every root of the serpent in my life — dry up from the root. Every altar of the enemy still standing — fall down and never rise again in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.

Repeat After Me
  • Ancestral serpents in my body, come out now in the name of Jesus.
  • Come out from my head. Come out from my stomach. Vomit it out. Spew it out. Yawn it out.
  • Every arrow of darkness in my spirit, in my body, by the blood of Jesus, come out.
  • Every satanic power expressing terminal sickness in my body, come out now.

Section 18 — House Cleansing & Protection

Timestamp 02:23:51

In the name of Jesus, be delivered from this spirit. If this spirit is operating in your home, let it catch fire right now in the name of Jesus. Every corner it is hiding in your home, operating and bringing confusion, operating and bringing fear, operating and bringing misunderstanding —

The Lord shall deliver you from every evil work in the name of Jesus.

Scripture — 2 Timothy 4:18 (KJV)

And the Lord shall deliver me from every evil work, and will preserve me unto his heavenly kingdom: to whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen.

Scripture — Exodus 12:13 (KJV)

And the blood shall be to you for a token upon the houses where ye are: and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you, when I smite the land of Egypt.

Prayer — House Cleansing Prayer

I plead the blood of Jesus over every corner of my house — every doorway, every window, every room, every hidden place. Every plague of the destroyer, pass over my house tonight. Angels of the Lord, encamp round about my dwelling. Every serpent that has entered my home — depart now in the name of Jesus.

Repeat After Me
  • In the name of Jesus, every evil animal, I need out of my life.
  • Go blind by the fire of the Holy Ghost.
  • Every stubborn personality, every stubborn curse — you are an animal. I bring the judgment of God over you right now.
  • Lord, deliver me from those strange animals tormenting my life, constricting my life, frustrating my plans, diverting my favour, crying against my destiny. I command it to catch fire right now.
  • The enemy of my glory, destroyed by the covenant animals — be revived by the fire of the Holy Ghost from tonight.
  • In the name of Jesus, I bind any foundation of powers. I bind any animals pursuing my life. I command you to go back by fire in the name of Jesus Christ. And that the enemy has stolen from my destiny, let it return back by the fire of the Holy Spirit.
  • Any ancestral snake, any ancestral rats, any ancestral dogs — in the name of Jesus, I command you to catch fire tonight.
  • Every lion assigned to devour me — tonight, by the blood, I tear you down into pieces.
  • Any generational power casting on my marriage — I break it today in the name of Jesus. By the blood of Jesus.

Section 19 — Total Deliverance Claims

Timestamp 02:31:08

Total deliverance is claimed by faith. What Jesus has finished on the cross is received as the believer's inheritance — full, complete, unshakeable deliverance from every serpentine power.

Scripture — 2 Timothy 4:18 (KJV)

And the Lord shall deliver me from every evil work, and will preserve me unto his heavenly kingdom: to whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen.

Repeat After Me
  • I claim total deliverance from every satanic animal in the mighty name of Jesus.
  • As far as I am alive, you powers that squat over me before my helpers — die by fire in the name of Jesus.
  • Any witchcraft animal, serpent spirit destroying my progress — I set you on fire now in the name of Jesus.
  • Voice of any satanic spirit in my mind right now — I command you to leave my mind right now.
  • Any demonic hands fighting my blessings, catch fire now in the name of Jesus.
  • Any demonic smoke assigned to anoint me with difficulties, any foundational ghosts assigned to anoint me with difficulties — catch fire now in the name of Jesus.
  • The venom of the snake affecting my marriage — in the name of Jesus, I command you to come out now.
  • Any animal, any evil spirit, any force in the yoke of poverty in my life — tonight, I destroy you in the name of Jesus.
  • Any evil priest diverting my destiny to a strange person — die by fire in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, deliver me now in the name of Jesus, and I am free — I am free, I am free, I am free.
