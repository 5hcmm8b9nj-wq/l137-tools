Knowledge check

Fifteen questions from the teaching

Choose an answer for each question. The right answer will highlight in green, with the scripture underneath. Your answers save automatically.

1. According to Mark 3:27, what must happen before a man can spoil the strong man's house?

Mark 3:27 — 'except he will first bind the strong man; and then he will spoil his house.'

2. In Luke 11:21-22, what does the stronger man take from the strong man?

Luke 11:22 — 'he taketh from him all his armour wherein he trusted, and divideth his spoils.'

3. Who is the Stronger Man in this teaching?

Pastor Kiky taught that Jesus is the stronger man, and the blood is the symbol of Jesus Himself.

4. What is a strongman, as defined in this teaching?

Pastor Kiky's definition, given in the teaching section.

5. Who are we attacking in these prayers?

'We are not attacking human beings. We are attacking that strongman spirit that is fighting everything in our life.'

6. Which scripture declares that the weapons of our warfare are not carnal?

2 Corinthians 10:4 — 'the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds.'

7. Which three consecutive Bible generations faced the same struggle with childbearing?

Genesis 11:30, Genesis 25:21 and Genesis 30:1.

8. What did Joshua declare in Joshua 24:15?

Joshua 24:15 — the declaration that ends a generational pattern.

9. According to Leviticus 17:11, why was the blood given upon the altar?

Leviticus 17:11 — 'for it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul.'

10. Which scripture shows the Lord Himself speaking to the earth?

Jeremiah 22:29 — 'O earth, earth, earth, hear the word of the LORD.'

11. What does James 2:26 teach about prophetic action?

Pastor Kiky used James 2:26 to explain why the day's prayer needed a prophetic action to seal it.

12. In Matthew 12:43-45, what happens when the unclean spirit returns to an empty house?

This is why Pastor Kiky insisted on consistency rather than stopping after a few days.

13. What did Jesus say after Lazarus came out of the grave still bound?

John 11:44 — the graveclothes picture of foundational patterns that must still be loosed.

14. What was the instruction for breaking the fast?

The prophetic action seals the fasting and the prayer, whenever the fast is broken.

15. According to this teaching, who are you fighting for?

'You are not fighting the battle for yourself. You are fighting for a whole generation.'
