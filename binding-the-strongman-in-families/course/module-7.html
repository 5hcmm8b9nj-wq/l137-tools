The Blood of Jesus — the Stronger Man in Your Bloodline

Teaching

This was the heart of the deliverance session. Pastor Kiky explained why a family strongman targets a person: because of the blood running through their veins. The bloodline carries the covenants of the family. So the answer is a new covenant — and the new covenant is the blood of Jesus.

Leviticus 17:11 says the life of the flesh is in the blood, and God gave it upon the altar to make an atonement for our souls. Atonement means the soul is bought back — redeemed out of the hand of the enemy and the wicked.

Then Luke 11:21-22 was applied directly: Jesus is the stronger man. As He enters your bloodline, He overcomes the strongman and takes from him all the armour wherein he trusted. Whatever covenant, sacrifice or agreement was made over a bloodline, the blood of Jesus speaks a better and newer covenant over that same bloodline. The ancestral blood may speak of poverty and destruction; the blood of Jesus answers that it has redeemed this vessel.

Pastor Kiky's physical instruction was framed entirely through Christ's finished work: take the communion wine if you have it; if not, take a cup of water or a red drink and pray over it. Mark yourself, drink, and remember. The element is never a ritual on its own — the power is in Christ's blood and Christ's covenant, and the act is remembrance and appropriation, exactly as the Lord instructed: as often as you do this, in remembrance of Me.

She was firm that this must be done consistently, not for three days and then abandoned. Matthew 12:43-45 was the warning: the unclean spirit goes out, walks through dry places, returns, finds the house empty, swept and garnished, and comes back with seven worse than himself. Her illustration was of a house full of strangers you invited in years ago and made comfortable — the first time you tell them to leave, they will not believe you. Some leave the second time. The strongest and most stubborn will stay ten days, twenty days, a month. But when you keep coming, and they know you are serious, they go — and because the protection has not left you, they do not come back.

The instruction for members of the ministry was also practical: get the communion wine and keep it, because the ministry has been taught the power in the blood of Jesus and it is meant to be used, not admired.

Scripture — read aloud

Leviticus 17:11

For the life of the flesh is in the blood: and I have given it to you upon the altar to make an atonement for your souls: for it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul.

Luke 11:21-22

When a strong man armed keepeth his palace, his goods are in peace: But when a stronger than he shall come upon him, and overcome him, he taketh from him all his armour wherein he trusted, and divideth his spoils.

1 Corinthians 11:25-26

After the same manner also he took the cup, when he had supped, saying, This cup is the new testament in my blood: this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of me. For as often as ye eat this bread, and drink this cup, ye do shew the Lord’s death till he come.

Exodus 12:13

And the blood shall be to you for a token upon the houses where ye are: and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you, when I smite the land of Egypt.

Matthew 12:43-45

When the unclean spirit is gone out of a man, he walketh through dry places, seeking rest, and findeth none. Then he saith, I will return into my house from whence I came out; and when he is come, he findeth it empty, swept, and garnished. Then goeth he, and taketh with himself seven other spirits more wicked than himself, and they enter in and dwell there: and the last state of that man is worse than the first. Even so shall it be also unto this wicked generation.

Prayer

Pray aloud
  • Lord Jesus, You are the stronger man. Enter my bloodline and take from every strongman all the armour wherein he trusted.
  • By the blood of Jesus, I break and I terminate the power and the influence of family idols over my life.
  • By the power of the Holy Ghost, I break myself free from the bondage of ancestral spirits as I drink this blood of Jesus.
  • Let the blood of Jesus wash away every mark of witchcraft in my life.
  • As I drink the blood of Jesus, I separate myself and my generation from every negative pattern in my family.
  • Every ancestral covenant speaking poverty and destruction over my bloodline — the blood of Jesus answers you: I have been redeemed.

Declarations

Say after me
  • Jesus is the stronger man, and the blood is the symbol of Jesus Himself.
  • By the blood of Jesus, every strongman in my family falls down to the ground and becomes powerless.
  • Every ancestral strongman, all your promises fail.
  • Every sacrifice exposing me to the evil ancestral powers of my father's house — be destroyed and die by the blood of Jesus.
  • By the blood of Jesus, every area of financial problems — strongman, be destroyed in the name of Jesus.
  • Every oppression in the house where I am living, be destroyed by the blood of Jesus.
  • Every ancestral strongman attacking me from one place to the other — fire now, in the name of Jesus, by the blood of Jesus.
  • Evil family trees, be uprooted in the name of Jesus.
  • I stand against every destroyer in my life. I bind it in the name of Jesus Christ, and I cast it out in the name of Jesus Christ, and I command it to go from my life right now in the name of Jesus Christ.
  • There is ancestral blood running through my veins, but the blood of Jesus contends for me: I am redeemed, and I am not part of them.
