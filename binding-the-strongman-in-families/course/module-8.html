The Prophetic Action: Speaking to the Earth of Your Foundation

Teaching

Pastor Kiky gave the room one prophetic action to seal the day's fasting, to be done whenever the fast is broken — six to six, six to three, six to one, or wherever your strength can take you. Fasting was flexible; the sealing was not optional.

She taught first why the action uses the earth. The earth is the foundation. When you pray to the earth you are speaking to the foundations — even the foundations of your bloodline, the roots of where you came from. We are all connected to the earth: the water we drink, the clothes we wear, the medicine we take, and the body that returns to dust. Psalm 24:1 — the earth is the LORD's, and the fulness thereof.

Then she gave the biblical precedent. In Jeremiah 22:29-30 the Lord Himself speaks to the earth: O earth, earth, earth, hear the word of the LORD. Thus saith the LORD, Write ye this man childless. God addressed the earth, and the earth carried the word. Pastor Kiky's application: the wicked also use the earth — dust, water, drink poured on the ground — against people, and a child of God has been given dominion and does not have to allow it. The earth must obey the word of the Lord, not the word of the enemy.

Her third scripture was Leviticus 17:11 again — the blood is the redeemer, given upon the altar to buy your soul back from the enemy. So the action combines the two: you stand on the earth, you pour the communion wine (or the prayed-over red drink or water) on the earth, and you speak the word of the Lord over your foundation, commanding every strongman in your foundation and family to be destroyed.

She also taught the doctrine behind prophetic action, and she used James 2:26 to do it: faith without works is dead. There are prayers you can pray that do not move anything until an act of obedient faith is added to them. She said plainly: you have changed the strategy of your prayer with your fasting; now add your prophetic action.

The final instruction was on how to do it without drawing a crowd: walk casually on the ground, read the prayer and the scriptures from your phone, and pour as you go. And if the Holy Spirit leads you to continue it beyond one day, continue it.

Scripture — read aloud

Jeremiah 22:29-30

O earth, earth, earth, hear the word of the LORD. Thus saith the LORD, Write ye this man childless, a man that shall not prosper in his days: for no man of his seed shall prosper, sitting upon the throne of David, and ruling any more in Judah.

Psalm 24:1

The earth is the LORD’S, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein.

Leviticus 17:11

For the life of the flesh is in the blood: and I have given it to you upon the altar to make an atonement for your souls: for it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul.

Luke 11:21-22

When a strong man armed keepeth his palace, his goods are in peace: But when a stronger than he shall come upon him, and overcome him, he taketh from him all his armour wherein he trusted, and divideth his spoils.

James 2:26

For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.

Psalm 141:9

Keep me from the snares which they have laid for me, and the gins of the workers of iniquity.

Psalm 35:7-8

For without cause have they hid for me their net in a pit, which without cause they have digged for my soul. Let destruction come upon him at unawares; and let his net that he hath hid catch himself: into that very destruction let him fall.

Seven-day prayer and fasting plan

Ends with communion, thanksgiving, and a broken fast — Christ at the centre.

Prayer

Pray aloud
  • Every strongman in my foundation, every strongman in my family — be destroyed by the blood of Jesus.
  • O earth, hear the word of the Lord: whatever has been buried in my name, whatever has been done against me in my foundation, release it now in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, keep me safe from the snares which they have laid for me, and from the gins of the workers of iniquity.
  • Let their net that they have hid catch themselves; let destruction come upon them at unawares.
  • All my oppressors fall by their own snares. I release all my blessings from the house of my enemies — release!
