Module 2 · At a glance
"First bind the strong man; and then he will spoil his house."
Mark 3:27 · KJV
Jesus is the stronger man. Binding must come before spoiling.
  • The strongman is a spiritual power, not a person.
  • Bind first — then the goods are recovered.
  • Jesus in Luke 11 is the stronger one who overcomes.
  • Mount Zion is the place of deliverance.

Order restored in your prayer: bind first, then possess.

Teaching

The main text of the teaching was Mark 3:27 — no man can enter into a strong man's house and spoil his goods, except he will first bind the strong man. The order in that verse is not negotiable. First the binding, then the spoiling. Pastor Kiky repeated the question the verse forces on us: except he first does what? Bind. Arrest. Only then can he go in and take back the goods.

Luke 11:21-22 gives the second half of the revelation. When a strong man armed keepeth his palace, his goods are in peace — that is the season when nothing is being disturbed and the family simply accepts the situation as normal. But when a stronger than he shall come upon him and overcome him, he taketh from him all his armour wherein he trusted and divideth his spoils. There is a Stronger Man. That is Jesus.

Obadiah 1:17 was declared over the room as the place they were standing: upon mount Zion shall be deliverance, and there shall be holiness, and the house of Jacob shall possess their possessions. Pastor Kiky said, this place is our mount Zion — and as we are here to pray, we are possessing every possession.

This is the doctrine of the teaching: deliverance is not achieved by shouting at a problem. It is achieved by binding the power that supervises the problem, in the name of Jesus Christ, who is Himself the Stronger Man.
