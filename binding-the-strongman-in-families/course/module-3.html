Teaching

Pastor Kiky defined it directly: a strongman is a demonic resistance in families, holding people down and keeping them in perpetual states of poverty, stagnation, pain, setbacks, limitation and struggle. A strongman is an agent of darkness — a demon that steals, kills and destroys lives inside a family.

They cause delays. They frustrate your moving forward. They frustrate marriages, children, careers, breakthroughs. They are the enemies of your progress and your success. In every family there is a principality that commands them, and they are rarely alone — sometimes they operate as a group, and whenever they see progress they regroup and gather to destroy it.

They are also stubborn. Pastor Kiky said strongmen are very stubborn spirits, and they can only be subdued by aggressive prayer, fasting and deliverance. That is why this teaching was delivered on a fasting day.

One of the hardest parts of the teaching: hindrances and barriers are not always invisible. Sometimes they are visible relatives — a mother, a father, an uncle, an aunt, a cousin — someone who is always opposing and blocking good things, or someone using curses, spells and manipulation to control the family against God's plan for them. But the target of our prayer is never the human being. Pastor Kiky said it twice: we are not attacking human beings; we are attacking the strongman spirit that is fighting everything in our life.

Many families cannot receive deliverance from captivity because there is a foundational strongman supervising the problem. Until that strongman is bound, there is no headway, no freedom, no all-round breakthrough — and there are prayerful, good people still suffering for exactly this reason.

Prayer

Pray aloud
  • Every strongman in my family, every strongman that is dealing with me, every strongman that has been my hindrance and my barrier — be destroyed in the name of Jesus.
  • Every strongman watching my life and my family so that we do not move forward in wealth, riches and peace — be scattered by the power of the Holy Ghost.
  • Lord, today destroy that strongman, uproot that strongman, scatter that strongman.
  • Every strongman in my household, in my mother's house, in my father's house — I destroy your power over my life and over my destiny in the name of Jesus.
  • I withdraw the control of my life and my destiny from the hands of any strongman in the name of Jesus.
