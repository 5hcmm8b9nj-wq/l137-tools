Teaching

Joshua 24:15 was the turning point of the teaching: as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD. Pastor Kiky pointed out that there was a reason Joshua had to say it. Whatever had been worshipped in the generations before him, Joshua drew a line — not Baal, not Ashtaroth, not the gods of his fathers. He said, I will change certain things in my family.

There must come a point when somebody stands before God and says, Lord, let a new pattern begin in this family. And God has chosen you. Even if it does not happen in the whole family, it happens from your house, from you and from your children.

Where there was hatred, let there be love. Where there was fighting, let there be reconciliation. Where there was addiction, let there be freedom. Where there was poverty and financial irresponsibility, let there be wisdom to navigate finances. Where there was prayerlessness, let there be prayer, so that you can raise your successors in prayer. Where there was darkness, God has called you to bring light — Joshua brought the light into a family that had a lot of darkness.

Everything you want to change in your family begins with you. As you raise your own family you are also creating a generation, and one day it will be your children's children's children. Pastor Kiky asked the room the searching question: as you are setting a generation, what are you imparting into them? What did you see before you that was negative, that you must change moving forward?

She also gave a wise instruction about how change spreads. You do not have to tell or force the family to change; it does not work like that. But as they begin to see the change in you — the peace that surrounds you, your children growing in peace, the negative pattern no longer landing in your house — that is when they ask questions, and that is when they say, I too will serve this God.

Finally, a warning: when you allow the strongman to defeat you, he will defeat your children and your children's children. Somebody must stop it. Somebody must fight it.

Prayer

Pray aloud
  • Lord, let a new pattern begin in this family, and let it begin with me.
  • Where there was hatred in my family, let there be love. Where there was fighting, let there be reconciliation. Where there was addiction, let there be freedom. Where there was poverty, let there be wisdom. Where there was prayerlessness, let there be prayer. Where there was darkness, let there be light.
  • Lord, whatever I have seen with my parents, my uncles and my grandparents that was negative shall not happen in my household in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, make me the one who stops it, so that it does not enter into my generation.

Declarations

Say after me
  • As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD.
  • It cannot proceed with me. It cannot continue with me. It will not enter into my generation.
  • I will not stay where my mother stayed at. I will not stay where my father stayed at. What happened to my uncle will not happen in my house.
  • In my house there shall be a change.
  • My children will not beg for money. I will not be disgraced.
