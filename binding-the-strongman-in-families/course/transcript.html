Binding the Strongman in Families

**Service Date:** August 12, 2026

**Platform:** Clubhouse

**Main Scripture:** Mark 3:27

**Pastor Kiky Ocansey · L137 Deliverance Ministry International**

Praise and Worship

**Pastor Kiky:** Good morning, family. Welcome to this morning’s service in the mighty name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. We thank the Lord for another day. We take this room and our bodies in the blood of Jesus, and we ask for the leading of the Holy Spirit as we pray. Please share the room and invite people to join us.

Thanksgiving

**Pastor Kiky:** Give God thanks for your going out and your coming in, for waking you this morning, for protection, and for the breath of life.

**2 Corinthians 4:15 (KJV)** — For all things are for your sakes, that the abundant grace might through the thanksgiving of many redound to the glory of God.
**Jeremiah 30:19 (KJV)** — And out of them shall proceed thanksgiving and the voice of them that make merry: and I will multiply them, and they shall not be few; I will also glorify them, and they shall not be small.

**Pastor Kiky:** Give Him praise and thanksgiving. He has done exceedingly, abundantly, and above all. Open to Psalm 104, verses 1 through 24, and let us give God thanks.

**Psalm 104:1-24 (KJV)** — Bless the LORD, O my soul. O LORD my God, thou art very great; thou art clothed with honour and majesty. Who coverest thyself with light as with a garment: who stretchest out the heavens like a curtain: Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters: who maketh the clouds his chariot: who walketh upon the wings of the wind: Who maketh his angels spirits; his ministers a flaming fire: Who laid the foundations of the earth, that it should not be removed for ever. Thou coveredst it with the deep as with a garment: the waters stood above the mountains. At thy rebuke they fled; at the voice of thy thunder they hasted away. They go up by the mountains; they go down by the valleys unto the place which thou hast founded for them. Thou hast set a bound that they may not pass over; that they turn not again to cover the earth. He sendeth the springs into the valleys, which run among the hills. They give drink to every beast of the field: the wild asses quench their thirst. By them shall the fowls of the heaven have their habitation, which sing among the branches. He watereth the hills from his chambers: the earth is satisfied with the fruit of thy works. He causeth the grass to grow for the cattle, and herb for the service of man: that he may bring forth food out of the earth; And wine that maketh glad the heart of man, and oil to make his face to shine, and bread which strengtheneth man’s heart. The trees of the LORD are full of sap; the cedars of Lebanon, which he hath planted; Where the birds make their nests: as for the stork, the fir trees are her house. The high hills are a refuge for the wild goats; and the rocks for the conies. He appointed the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth his going down. Thou makest darkness, and it is night: wherein all the beasts of the forest do creep forth. The young lions roar after their prey, and seek their meat from God. The sun ariseth, they gather themselves together, and lay them down in their dens. Man goeth forth unto his work and to his labour until the evening. O LORD, how manifold are thy works! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.

**Pastor Kiky:** We thank God for protecting us throughout the night, shielding us from dangers and from arrows sent by the wicked; for being our Provider and Deliverer.

**Psalm 124:7 (KJV)** — Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers: the snare is broken, and we are escaped.
**Psalm 107:8-9 (KJV)** — Oh that men would praise the LORD for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men! For he satisfieth the longing soul, and filleth the hungry soul with goodness.

**Congregation:** We thank You, Lord Jesus, for waking us, for protection, for our children and families, and for every trap destroyed by Your power. We give You praise. [Prayer in tongues and corporate thanksgiving followed.]

Confession of Sins

**Pastor Kiky:** Ask the Lord to forgive every sin. We do not want sin to stand between us and the favour, breakthroughs, testimony, signs, miracles, and helpers appointed for this day.

**Acts 3:19 (KJV)** — Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord;
**Psalm 32:5 (KJV)** — I acknowledged my sin unto thee, and mine iniquity have I not hid. I said, I will confess my transgressions unto the LORD; and thou forgavest the iniquity of my sin. Selah.
**Psalm 51:10 (KJV)** — Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me.

**Pastor Kiky:** Ask the Lord for mercy. Say: “Forgive me of everything I have committed, O Lord. Wash and cleanse me. The sins I do not know, wash me thoroughly from every iniquity. Let the blood of Jesus cleanse me from all unrighteousness. Forgive bitterness, anger, pride, and every careless word. Remove every stain of sin from me by the blood of Jesus.”

Forgiveness of Others

**Pastor Kiky:** Forgive us, Lord, of every sin and let nothing hinder us this day.

Inviting the Holy Spirit

**Pastor Kiky:** Invite the Holy Spirit into your prayers. If you woke with anxiety, depression, worry, or oppression, invite the Comforter.

**John 14:26 (KJV)** — But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.
**Romans 8:26 (KJV)** — Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered.

**Pastor Kiky:** Without the Holy Spirit we can do nothing. Every great victory, breakthrough, and deliverance requires His presence. Let the Holy Spirit strengthen you, ignite your fire to pray, comfort you, reveal truth, and expose hidden battles and traps of the enemy.

**Congregation:** Holy Spirit, fill us in the name of Jesus.

**Pastor Kiky:** Holy Spirit, take absolute possession of our lives, prayer, homes, and surroundings. Expose every hidden work of darkness. Break every chain, cancel wicked dreams, destroy every stronghold, and give us peace, joy, and restoration.

Teaching

**Pastor Kiky:** As we continue fasting this week, today we are binding the strong man in families.

**Mark 3:27 (KJV)** — No man can enter into a strong man’s house, and spoil his goods, except he will first bind the strong man; and then he will spoil his house.
**Matthew 12:29 (KJV)** — Or else how can one enter into a strong man’s house, and spoil his goods, except he first bind the strong man? and then he will spoil his house.
**Obadiah 1:17 (KJV)** — But upon mount Zion shall be deliverance, and there shall be holiness; and the house of Jacob shall possess their possessions.

**Pastor Kiky:** We must first bind the strong man and then spoil his house. This is our Mount Zion, and as we pray we are possessing every possession. We cannot stop praying against evil strong men and evil patterns in families. As we grow and have children, anything that hinders us can move onto our children.

**Pastor Kiky:** A strong man is demonic resistance in families: holding people down and keeping them in poverty, stagnation, pain, setbacks, limitation, and struggle. As you fast and pray, confront every strong man fighting your life and family. Be prayerful and do not be ignorant of the devices of your enemies.

**Pastor Kiky:** There are negative recurring patterns in families—untimely death, sickness, marital breakdown, poverty, children outside marriage, delay in marriage, delayed conception, barrenness, miscarriage, and affliction. Some battles are indoors, in the family. Strong men are stubborn spirits and are subdued through aggressive prayer, fasting, and deliverance.

**Luke 11:21-22 (KJV)** — When a strong man armed keepeth his palace, his goods are in peace: But when a stronger than he shall come upon him, and overcome him, he taketh from him all his armour wherein he trusted, and divideth his spoils.

**Pastor Kiky:** We are not attacking human beings. We are attacking the spiritual stronghold fighting our lives. A family strong man may influence people outside the family to oppose progress. A foundational strong man can supervise captivity in a family. Until that strong man is bound, there may be no headway. When the strong man is defeated, there is freedom.

*[Personal testimony redacted per publishing policy — teaching principle preserved below.]*

**Pastor Kiky:** A woman spoke publicly before her death about the same illness that took her mother and her sister. The teaching principle is that destructive patterns can recur through a family line; they must be confronted in prayer.

**Pastor Kiky:** Say after me: “I declare this morning that every strong man tormenting my life, tormenting my family, has fallen down and died in the name of Jesus.”

**Congregation:** “I declare this morning that every strong man tormenting my life, tormenting my family, has fallen down and died in the name of Jesus.”

**Pastor Kiky:** Every evil pattern is an evil cycle, and it can be broken. Go to the root and uproot it. Do not necessarily mention people by name; pray against the strong men of the family and let the Holy Spirit expose what needs to be exposed.

**Psalm 118:5-6 (KJV)** — I called upon the LORD in distress: the LORD answered me, and set me in a large place. The LORD is on my side; I will not fear: what can man do unto me?

**Pastor Kiky:** Do not fear the strong man; fight the strong man. As you fast, pray: “Every strong man in my life, destroying my life, fighting my marriage and career: Lord, destroy that strong man, uproot that strong man, and scatter that strong man.” We serve a prayer-answering God.

**Pastor Kiky:** Every prayer is warfare. Strong men are responsible for evil family patterns. Consider barrenness: Sarai was barren; Isaac’s wife Rebekah was barren; and Rachel also had no child.

**Genesis 11:30 (KJV)** — But Sarai was barren; she had no child.
**Genesis 25:21 (KJV)** — And Isaac intreated the LORD for his wife, because she was barren: and the LORD was intreated of him, and Rebekah his wife conceived.
**Genesis 30:1 (KJV)** — And when Rachel saw that she bare Jacob no children, Rachel envied her sister; and said unto Jacob, Give me children, or else I die.

**Pastor Kiky:** Reject negative patterns. What happened before you will not automatically determine what happens to you.

**Joshua 24:15 (KJV)** — And if it seem evil unto you to serve the LORD, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the flood, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell: but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD.
**Romans 12:2 (KJV)** — And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

**Pastor Kiky:** There must be a point at which someone says, “Lord, let a new pattern begin in this family.” It can begin with you and your household. Where there was hatred, let there be love. Where there was addiction, let there be freedom. Where there was financial irresponsibility, let there be wisdom. Where there was prayerlessness, let prayer begin. As you raise your family, you are creating a generation. If you allow the strong man to defeat you, it will seek to defeat your children and your children’s children. Somebody must stop it.

**2 Corinthians 10:4 (KJV)** — (For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds;)

**Pastor Kiky:** Our weapons include prayer, the Word, truth, righteousness, obedience, and the authority in Jesus Christ. Jesus must take His rightful place in our families. Every destructive stronghold must bow and every cycle of evil patterns must end with you. Do not try to force relatives to change. Let them see the change that Christ has brought in you: peace, faithful work, and children growing in peace. Then they can see that your house serves the Lord.

*[Personal testimony redacted per publishing policy — teaching principle preserved below.]*

**Pastor Kiky:** Evil patterns can be broken. Continue in prayer and fasting, and determine that the pattern will not attach to you or enter your generation.

**Pastor Kiky:** Fast through the day as your strength allows—some until noon, some until three, and some longer. Pray: “Every strong man in my house and family, dealing with my marriage, finances, business, worth, and riches, Lord Jesus, destroy the strong man wherever it is, in the name of Jesus.” See a different future for your children. They shall finish school, grow, progress, work, and live without disgrace. Fight the strong man.

Deliverance Prayers and Declarations

**Pastor Kiky:** I pray that the control of every strong man over your life and destiny is withdrawn in the name of Jesus.

**Congregation:** Amen.

**Pastor Kiky:** Every strong man in my life, every strong man in my family, every strong man in my foundation: be destroyed in the name of Jesus.

**Congregation:** Every strong man in my life, every strong man in my family, every strong man in my foundation: be destroyed in the name of Jesus.

**Pastor Kiky:** Power of God, expose every pattern. Expose every strange pattern. I am coming out.

**Congregation:** I am coming out. I am coming out today in the name of Jesus Christ and by the blood of Jesus Christ.

**John 5:8 (KJV)** — Jesus saith unto him, Rise, take up thy bed, and walk.

**Pastor Kiky:** Rise, take up your bed, and walk. Walk out of that bed. Walk into greatness, victory, and prosperity by the power of the Holy Spirit. You do not need sickness or joblessness any longer. It is time for a great manifestation. Say: “I am coming out of this calamity today.”

**Congregation:** “I am coming out of this calamity today.”

**Psalm 35:7-8 (KJV)** — For without cause have they hid for me their net in a pit, which without cause they have digged for my soul. Let destruction come upon him at unawares; and let his net that he hath hid catch himself: into that very destruction let him fall.

**Pastor Kiky:** Where is the strong man or strong woman hindering your progress? Let every trap plotted for you fall upon itself. When our obedience is fulfilled, we are new creatures in Christ. Old things, old troubles, and negative family patterns are passed away.

**2 Corinthians 5:17 (KJV)** — Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.
**2 Corinthians 6:17 (KJV)** — Wherefore come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing; and I will receive you,

**Pastor Kiky:** Come out from every negative family pattern. You cannot remain in misery, shame, or disgrace. When I say, “Come out,” say, “I am coming out.”

**Congregation:** I am coming out. I am coming out. I am coming out now in the name of Jesus Christ and by the blood of Jesus Christ.

Communion and the Blood of Jesus

**Pastor Kiky:** If you have communion wine, take it. If you do not have it, take a cup of water or anointed oil. The blood speaks of the new covenant. The reason a strong man may pursue a family is because of bloodline covenants; we need the new covenant, the blood of Jesus. Jesus is the stronger man.

**Luke 11:21-22 (KJV)** — When a strong man armed keepeth his palace, his goods are in peace: But when a stronger than he shall come upon him, and overcome him, he taketh from him all his armour wherein he trusted, and divideth his spoils.
**Leviticus 17:11 (KJV)** — For the life of the flesh is in the blood: and I have given it to you upon the altar to make an atonement for your souls: for it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul.

**Pastor Kiky:** Say after me:

**Pastor Kiky / Congregation:** “In the name of Jesus, every strong man delegated against me, by the blood of Jesus, fall down to the ground and become powerless. Every ancestral strong man attacking my life, fall down and die in the name of Jesus. By the blood of Jesus, I break and terminate the power and influence of family idols over my life. Every area of financial problems, every government oppression in my present house, be destroyed by the blood of Jesus. Every ancestral power assigned to destroy me and crush me to death, I destroy you in the name of Jesus. I cast down your plans and traps in the mighty name of Jesus.”

**Pastor Kiky:** Speak in tongues and pray.

**Psalm 141:9 (KJV)** — Keep me from the snares which they have laid for me, and the gins of the workers of iniquity.

**Pastor Kiky:** Make this your prayer: “Lord, keep me from the traps set by evildoers in my family, from the snares they have laid to destroy my life and hinder the goodness and blessing in my life.”

**Pastor Kiky / Congregation:** “By the power of the Holy Ghost, I break myself free from the bondage of ancestral spirits as I drink the blood of Jesus. I declare divine judgment in the camps of my oppressors. Let the blood of Jesus wash away every mark of witchcraft. I stand against every destroyer and everything not of Jesus; I bind it and cast it out in the name of Jesus Christ. As I drink the blood of Jesus, I separate myself and my generation from every negative family pattern.”

Prophetic Action (Sealing)

**Pastor Kiky:** When you break your fast, carry your communion wine or another red drink you have prayed over. Go outside, walk on the ground, read the assigned scriptures and prayer, and pour the drink on the ground as you pray against every strong man in your family and foundation. Do this to seal the day’s fast and prayer. You may continue on further days as led by the Holy Spirit.

**James 2:26 (KJV)** — For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.
**Psalm 24:1 (KJV)** — The earth is the LORD’S, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein.
**Jeremiah 22:29-30 (KJV)** — O earth, earth, earth, hear the word of the LORD. Thus saith the LORD, Write ye this man childless, a man that shall not prosper in his days: for no man of his seed shall prosper, sitting upon the throne of David, and ruling any more in Judah.

**Pastor Kiky:** The earth is the Lord’s. As you pray, speak against every strong man in your foundation and family. You may add your own prayer points. We are not attacking human beings; we are confronting the spiritual strongholds that hinder life and destiny.

**Pastor Kiky:** Let us read the scriptures for the assignment.

**Congregation:** By the power of the Holy Ghost, every strong man in my life and family, be destroyed by the blood of Jesus Christ.

Thanksgiving and Sealing

**Pastor Kiky:** Let us thank the Lord. Sovereign King of kings and Lord of lords, conquering Lion of the tribe of Judah, Adonai: we thank You. As you place your seed over this prayer, stand on Luke 11:21–22. Let Jesus, the stronger One, take the armour of every strong man in your family and destroy its force. May you discover the strong man; may the Lord lead you and disconnect you as you pray and fast. It is time for breakthrough, blessing, and testimony, in Jesus’ name.

**Congregation:** Amen.

**Pastor Kiky:** Fast today as the Lord leads and as your strength permits. When you end your fast, follow the written ministry directions and do the prophetic action.

**Salvation Prayer Leader:** If you prayed this prayer, you are born again. You have entered a personal relationship with Jesus Christ. Mark today’s date as a day of new beginnings, learn to pray, and give God first place in your life.
