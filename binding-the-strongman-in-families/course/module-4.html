Teaching

Pastor Kiky described evil patterns as negative, recurring events in a family that have run from generation to generation. Some families always experience untimely death at an early age. Some develop the same sickness at the same stage of life. Others never remain in marriage. Some have children outside marriage, delay in marriage, delay in conception, or barrenness. Some have poverty, financial irresponsibility, addiction, prayerlessness, hatred, unforgiveness, disgrace, or a repeated inability to keep anything that has been built.

*[Personal testimony redacted per publishing policy — teaching principle preserved below.]* The illustration given was of a woman who spoke publicly before her death about the same illness that had already taken her mother and her sister — the same diagnosis, moving down the same bloodline. Pastor Kiky's point was that a pattern like that is not coincidence; it is enforced into the bloodline, and a foundational strongman needs to be destroyed with prayer and fasting by the power of the Holy Ghost.

The Bible shows the same phenomenon of a repeating family struggle. Abraham waited years for a child (Genesis 11:30). Isaac faced the same struggle with Rebekah (Genesis 25:21). Then Jacob's household carried it again — Rachel envied her sister and said unto Jacob, Give me children, or else I die (Genesis 30:1). The same difficulty appeared in three consecutive generations.

Pastor Kiky also warned about a pattern that touches destiny helpers. In families with a pattern of poverty, when a person moves to take a destiny helper, the enemy substitutes someone who will not protect what they carry — so instead of a helper, the family keeps receiving people who spill, steal and destroy.

There is one more layer to this: a family strongman can give an assignment to somebody outside the family who is close to you, to carry out the deed. That is why the prayer must go to the root, not to the symptom.

Prayer

Pray aloud
  • Every evil pattern in my family — untimely death, sickness, poverty, delay in marriage, delay in conception, barrenness, divorce, addiction, prayerlessness, disgrace — I command you to break at the root in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, keep me safe from the snares which they have laid for me in my family, and from the gins of the workers of iniquity.
  • Every assignment given to any person close to me by a strongman of my family — be cancelled by the blood of Jesus.
  • Every substitute the enemy has placed where God has appointed a destiny helper — be exposed and removed in the name of Jesus.
