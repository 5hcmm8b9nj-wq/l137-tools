Teaching

The teaching closed with a call to come out. 2 Corinthians 6:17 — come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing. Pastor Kiky applied it to the family patterns: come out from among them, come out of every negative pattern in the family. You cannot be poor, you cannot be disgraced, you cannot go through what they have gone through, because it is not your portion.

2 Corinthians 5:17 was the ground of it: in Christ we are a new creature; old things are passed away. The negative patterns of the family cannot follow a new creation. We no longer have anything in common with the enemy, but eternal life with Christ by the power of the Holy Spirit.

John 5:8 became the instruction for the one who has been lying in the same condition for years: Rise, take up thy bed, and walk. It cannot remain the same again. Walk out of that bed. Walk into your victory. Walk into your prosperity. You do not need that sickness anymore. You do not need that joblessness anymore.

And then the Lazarus principle, which is one of the most important pictures in the whole teaching. Jesus called Lazarus out of the grave and he came forth alive — but he came out still wrapped in grave clothes, and Jesus said, Loose him, and let him go. Pastor Kiky's application: our grave clothes are the evil patterns and the things attached to us from our foundation. We have risen. Our eyes are open. We have come into Christ. But now the grave clothes must come off, because without removing them we cannot move forward freely or prosper. Coming into Christianity is not the end of deliverance — it is the beginning of it. There are covenants and attachments that still have to be broken.

Matthew 10:37 was given as the discernment scripture for the hardest cases: he that loveth father or mother more than me is not worthy of me. When the Lord reveals that a battle is connected to someone very close, the response is not accusation — it is prayer: Lord, if this is the revelation You have given me, deliver me, disconnect me, and deliver me from every bondage.

The last word of the teaching was generational: you are not fighting this battle for yourself. You are fighting for your children and your children's children. You are opening a new generation.

Prayer

Pray aloud
  • Lord, I come out from among every negative pattern in my family, and I refuse to touch the unclean thing.
  • Loose me from every grave cloth of my foundation, and let me go, in the name of Jesus.
  • Lord, if this is the revelation You have given me, deliver me and disconnect me from every bondage attached to it.
  • Lord, let the disconnection be complete, so that what battled my parents will not battle me, and what battles me will not battle my children.
