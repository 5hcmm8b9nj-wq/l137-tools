Teaching

Pastor Kiky anchored the warfare in 2 Corinthians 10:4 — the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds. Our weapons are prayer, the word of God, truth, righteousness, obedience, and the authority we have in Jesus Christ.

She was explicit about the target: we are not attacking human beings. We are attacking the strongman spirit that is fighting everything in our life. You are not killing the man and you are not killing the woman — you are dealing with the spirit, so that it must die and leave you alone. Even if relatives continue to do things against you, and even if the pattern is still running in the family, it will not attach to you and it cannot do anything to you.

She also taught the persistence principle. Every prayer you pray is a prayer of warfare. If there are ten, you may deal with five the first time; the next time you pray the same prayer, you deal with more. It will get to the point where they can still do things against you but it cannot harm you, because you have rendered them powerless. That is why one teaching becomes part one, part two, and why the same prayer is prayed again and again.

And she declared Jesus Christ into His rightful place: we are declaring that Jesus Christ takes His rightful place in our families, and every destructive stronghold must bow in the mighty name of Jesus.
