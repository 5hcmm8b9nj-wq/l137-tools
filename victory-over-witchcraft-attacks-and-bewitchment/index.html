THREE TRUTHS TO REMEMBER

Stand in Christ, not in fear.

  1. God condemns sorcery and spiritual deception; His people do not practice or imitate it.
  2. Victory is through Jesus Christ, the blood of the Lamb, God’s Word, prayer, repentance, and faithful obedience.
  3. Freedom is maintained through thanksgiving, Scripture, Christian fellowship, forgiveness, renewed thinking, and wise pastoral care.
